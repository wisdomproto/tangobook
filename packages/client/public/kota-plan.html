# 시리즈 16 — 코타와 오늘의 손님

> 창작동화 **시리즈 16** (25권 · 250쪽). 무대 = **일본 온천 골짜기 마을의 작은 여관**.
> 이 시리즈를 가르는 축은 **낯선 손님**이다. 다른 열다섯은 전부 아는 사람들 사이에서 벌어지는데,
> 여기만 **매권 처음 보는 이가 하나 온다.** 그래서 이 라인에서 유일하게 **아이가 더 많이 아는 시리즈**다.

## §0. 축 — 무엇으로 갈리나

| | 시리즈 14 유키 (같은 일본) | 시리즈 08 피포 (같은 페파형) | **시리즈 16 코타** |
|---|---|---|---|
| 무대 | 산촌 · 집과 산길 | 아일랜드 돌담 목장 | **온천 여관 한 채와 그 마당** — 뜨거운 물이 도랑으로 흐른다 |
| 시간축 | **사계절**(넉 달마다 다른 길) | 계절 없음 | 🔴 **계절이 없다. 하루가 축이다** — 손님이 오고, 씻고, 자고, 간다 |
| 캐스트 구성 | 아이 + 말 못 하는 동무 + 어른 | 아이 + 이웃 어른 셋(고정) | **아이 + 아빠 둘뿐 + 매권 바뀌는 이름 없는 손님** |

| 아이의 위치 | 배우는 쪽 | 배우는 쪽 | 🔴 **알려 주는 쪽** — 단, 말로 가르치지 않고 데려가 보여 준다 |

🔴 **유키와 겹치지 않는 이유는 「일본이 아니라 다른 일본」이 아니라, 축 다섯이 전부 반대편이라서다.**
유키는 한 아이가 계절을 도는 이야기이고, 코타는 한 자리에서 **사람이 지나가는** 이야기다.
그래서 유키에 **손님이 한 명도 없고**, 코타에 **계절 표시가 한 줄도 없다**(눈 오는 날 두 권은 「눈 오는 날」이지 겨울 절이 아니다).

## §1. 캐스트 — 고정 둘

| | 이름 | 영문 토큰 | 종 | 관계 · 말 | 몸 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 주인공 | **코타** | `Kota tanuki` | 아기 너구리 | 여관집 아이 · 말한다 | 이족보행 · 옷(짧은 겹옷 + **분홍 수건** 목에) · 앞다리는 「손」 · 나막신 |
| 어른 | **아빠** | `Dad tanuki` | 너구리 | 🔴 **첫 등장에 「아빠」라고 낱말로 부른다** · 한 권에 두세 마디 | 이족보행 · 옷(감색 겹옷 + 허리에 마른 수건) · 앞다리는 「손」 |

**손님 — 이름을 주지 않는다.** 매권 하나, 「기다란 아저씨」·「지팡이 짚은 할아버지」·「나보다 작은 아이」처럼
**보이는 것으로 부른다.** 아이가 외울 이름이 늘지 않게 하려는 것이고(브리프 §첫 쪽 셋째), 25권 내내
새 사람이 온다는 이 시리즈의 축이기도 하다.

| 토큰 규칙 | `Guest <종>` — `Guest crane` · `Guest boar` · `Guest turtle` · `Guest owl` · `Guest hippo` · `Guest frog` · `Guest heron` · `Guest swan` · `Guest ox` · `Guest tanuki` |
|---|---|

- 🔴 **의인화 등급은 하나다** — 코타·아빠·손님 전부 **이족보행 + 옷 + 「손」**. 14 가 동무만 사족으로 쪼갠 것은
  그 시리즈의 캐스트 축이 「말 못 함」이라 그랬고, 여기 축은 「낯섦」이라 몸으로 가를 것이 없다.
- 🔴 **시그니처 = 코타 수건의 복숭아 분홍 하나.** 여관 수건은 **전부 흰 무지**다. 그래서 07 에서 코타가
  제 수건을 손님 아이에게 건네면 **화면의 유일한 분홍이 손에서 손으로 옮겨 간다** — 25권 중 그 권 하나만이다.
- **손님 종은 열 가지 안에서 돌리고, 다섯 종이 두 번 온다**(01↔25 두루미 · 04↔15 부엉이 · 05↔22 개구리 아이 ·
  09↔20 백조 · 14↔23 멧돼지 아이). 🔴 **재방문은 「지난번 그」로만 부른다** — 이름을 붙이면 고정 캐스트가 된다.

## §2. 뼈대 — 10쪽 고정 (페파형)

```
p1      판을 깐다 — 오늘 손님이 온다                     (🔴 인물 둘 · 관계어 「아빠」 · 산문으로 연다)
p2      코타의 원함 — 코타의 문장으로
p3      손님이 끼어든다 — 🔴 손님도 원한다. 원함을 손님 입으로
p4~p5   두 원함이 어긋난다
p6~p7   틀린다 — 대개 손님이(마을을 몰라서), 넉 권은 아빠가, 아홉 권은 코타가
p8      아이의 방식 — 🔴 말로 가르치지 않는다. 데려가거나 놀이로 바꾼다
p9      먹힌다 — 🔴 상대의 원함이 여기서 움직인다
p10     착지 — 🔴 인물의 입에서 한 문장. 대개 손님의 입
```

🔴 **p8 에서 코타가 설명하면 이 시리즈는 죽는다.** 아이가 어른을 가르치는 책이 되고, 그건 설교보다 나쁘다.
코타는 **자기가 늘 하던 대로 해 보일 뿐**이고 손님이 그걸 보고 따라온다.
🔴 **p10 은 대개 손님의 입이다**(19권). 코타의 입은 4권(03 · 11 · 20 · 25), 아빠의 입은 2권(18 · 24).

## §4. 그림체 방향 — 니혼가(암채 · 가루 안료)

**광물을 빻은 가루를 아교에 개어 얹는다. 불투명하고, 입자가 굵어 표면이 까슬까슬하고, 덧얹을수록 두꺼워진다.**
고른 이유는 셋이다. ①**14 수묵 담채의 정확한 반대편**이다 — 저쪽은 투명·번짐·한 획이고 **빛이 안 칠한 종이**인데,
이쪽은 불투명·쌓기이고 **흰 것도 칠해서 만든다**(김을 호분으로 얹는다). 같은 일본 무대 두 권이 서가에서
형제로 보이지 않으려면 매체가 계열째 갈려야 하고, 인쇄 계열(01~13 이 다 썼다)로는 김을 못 그린다.
②**매체와 무대의 물성이 같다** — 온천의 김은 물이 아니라 **떠 있는 알갱이**이고, 가루 안료가 바로 그 물성이다.
젖은 나무·돌·수건이 전부 까슬까슬한 면으로 선다. ③**색이 저절로 제한된다** — 광물색은 몇 가지뿐이라
팔레트를 규칙으로 묶지 않아도 좁아진다.

- **악센트 = 복숭아 분홍**, 코타 목의 수건 한 장. 라인 15시리즈가 한 번도 안 쓴 색이다. 여관 수건은 전부 흰 무지.
- 🔴 **김(호분 흰 면)과 악센트가 화면에서 싸운다** — 김은 회백까지만 올리고 **순백은 안 쓴다.** 안 그러면
  분홍 한 점이 흰 덩어리에 먹힌다.
- 🔴 **캐릭터를 읽히게 하는 축 = 얼굴의 「무늬」다.** 코타는 너구리라 눈가에 검은 띠가 있고, 그 띠의 각도와
  두께로 표정이 선다(눈동자에 연기를 안 시킨다). 이 축은 15시리즈 어디에도 없다 —
  🔴 **점 눈으로 가지 마라.** 앵커를 쓸 때 수상작의 `character` 필드에서 가져올 것(`_series-slate-asia.md` §지시서에 이름 안 붙인 축).

> 앵커 문안은 **art-director 소관**이라 여기 쓰지 않는다. 위 넷은 방향과 금지선이다.

STYLE ANCHOR - kota-mineral   (Kota's hot-spring inn / opaque ground pigment, built up in passes)

Style: ground mineral pigment bound in warm glue and laid OPAQUE on a toned sized surface, for a
  picture book read aloud to four- to six-year-olds. 🔴 NOTHING HERE IS FINISHED IN ONE PASS. Every
  field is built by laying the same colour down again over itself - two passes for a far thing, four
  or five for the thing the story is about - and the earlier passes are meant to show through the
  last one as grain rather than be covered by it. 🔴 THE SUPPORT IS NEVER LEFT BARE: there is no
  reserved white, no empty corner and no place where the paper is doing the work of light. If a
  thing is pale it is pale BECAUSE IT WAS PAINTED PALE. Water thins nothing here.

RENDERING (finish hierarchy): the dial of this book is GRIND, not brightness. One pigment is dark,
  gritty and very slightly raised off the surface when ground coarse, and pale, smooth and dead flat
  when ground fine, and the three steps of finish are read straight off that. (1) THE FIGURES AND
  THE ONE THING THEY ARE TOUCHING - coarsest grind, four to five passes, and a thin dry ink contour
  drawn first and still showing at the end. (2) THE PLACE THEY STAND IN - medium grind, two or three
  passes, NO contour: a wall meets a floor because the two fields stop, not because a line says so.
  (3) ANYTHING PAST THE EAVES, AND ALL STEAM - finest grind, one pass, no contour. 🔴 SO A FIGURE
  CAN BE FOUND ON ANY GROUND IN THIS BOOK BY ONE TEST: it is the only thing on the page with a drawn
  edge. 🔴 EVERY EDGE IS A CUT EDGE - grain lives inside a field and never at its border, and two
  fields never blend into each other. AT MOST TWO THINGS ON A PAGE GET THE COARSEST GRIND.

PALETTE (mineral, and there are only these): GROUND #C9BEA8, a warm clay tone that is painted on,
  never bare · SHELL WHITE #EDE7DC for steam, wet-stone shine and the inn's towels - 🔴 THIS IS THE
  LIGHTEST THING PERMITTED AND IT IS NOT WHITE; pure white appears nowhere, because a white mass of
  steam swallows the one pink on the page · AZURE #3B5A72, deep and coarse, spent on night, water
  and the father's robe · GREEN #6E8A6A · OCHRE #B98F52 for wood, straw and matting · BURNT EARTH
  #8A4E3C for roof tile and hot stone · INK #2A2622 for every contour and every marking · 🔴 PEACH
  PINK #E9A6A0, the one accent in twenty-five books, on Kota's neck towel and nowhere else ever -
  not on a flower, not on a lantern, not on a guest. AT MOST FOUR OF THE MINERAL COLOURS ON ONE
  PAGE, plus ink and shell white. The inn's own towels are shell white and plain, all of them.

STAGE CLAUSES (one inn and its yard; the place sets the grind and the number of steam passes):
  🔴 STEAM IS COUNTED IN PASSES OF SHELL WHITE at the finest grind, laid over a field already
    finished so that field still reads through it: a cold morning is four passes and the far side of
    the valley goes under, an ordinary day is two, a rainy day is ONE and it lies low along the
    ground instead of rising, and indoors there is none.
  UPPER POOL - hottest, highest up the slope. Stone here is burnt earth at the coarsest grind in the
    book. Water is not transparent: one flat field of azure, and nothing under the surface is drawn.
  LOWER POOL AND CHANNEL - the same water further down and cooler, so the same azure is ground finer
    and paler and the steam is one pass or none. 🔴 THE CHANNEL RUNS DOWNHILL ACROSS THE PAGE AND
    ITS FALL IS ALWAYS VISIBLE: a floating thing sits nearer the bottom edge of the page than the
    place it went in. A stone in the channel is pale and dry on top, burnt earth where water reaches.
  YARD - the widest place, ochre ground. When snow falls it is shell white over everything EXCEPT a
    hand's width along the channel, which stays bare ochre.
  CORRIDOR AND CHANGING FLOOR - wood indoors, ochre at medium grind with the grain running the
    length of each board. 🔴 A WET BOARD IS THE SAME OCHRE WITH ONE PASS OF AZURE OVER IT and a hard
    edge where the wet stops. Steam gathers high, so an upper shelf takes that same azure pass.
  GUEST ROOM - the quietest page, matting in one flat ochre field with no grain at all. Night here
    is azure at the coarsest grind over everything except the lamp's reach, and that reach is a
    hard-edged shape, never a glow.

CHARACTER DESIGN LANGUAGE: 🔴 A MARKING IS WHO SOMEBODY IS - NOT THE EYES. Every face carries flat
  ink shapes laid down last, on top of a coat that is already finished, and those shapes do all the
  work of feeling. For the raccoon dogs who keep this inn it is THE BAND ACROSS BOTH EYES: its
  angle, its thickness and where its two ends stop are the whole expression of that page - it tilts
  down at the outer ends and thickens when he is downcast, thins and lifts at the outer ends when he
  is pleased, and breaks into two short pieces when he is startled. THE EYE INSIDE THE BAND IS FIXED
  FOREVER - one small round opening of shell white with an ink centre, the same size on every page
  of twenty-five books, open or shut. THERE ARE ONLY THREE INK MARKINGS ON A RACCOON DOG AND NEVER A
  FOURTH: the eye band, the dark forehands, the ringed tail.
  A COAT IS A BUILT FIELD AND NOT HAIR - no hair strokes, no whisker lines, no drawn fur edge, no
  drawn folds in cloth. The coat ends where the field ends.
  GRADE: everyone here stands upright, wears cloth and has hands, guests included whatever animal a
  guest happens to be. 🔴 A GUEST HAS NO EYE BAND: each guest species is given ONE flat ink shape of
  its own on the face instead, in ink and never in colour.
  🔴 HOUSE OR ROAD, READ OFF THE OUTLINE: the two who live here WEAR cloth that is not clothing -
  the child a towel at the neck, the father a folded towel at the waist - and carry nothing. A guest
  wears no cloth at neck or waist and instead has ONE travelling thing on the outline: a bundle on
  the back, a wide hat, a stick, a basket. A house towel in a guest's hands is carried, not worn.
  🔴 AN ADULT IS NOT A BIGGER CHILD: an adult's robe closes at mid-calf so no shin is ever seen and
  the sleeves hang past the hands, a child's stops above the knee with both shins bare. Height never
  tells two figures apart.

CANVAS: 16:9 double-page spread. 🔴 NOT ONE WRITTEN MARK ANYWHERE - the door curtains, lanterns,
  towels, wooden tags and paper doors of this inn are all blank: no writing, no numerals, no crest.

NOT: no airbrush, no soft gradient, no glow, no 3D render / 🔴 NO FIRE ANYWHERE IN THIS BOOK - no
  hearth, no stove, no lit wood,
  no flame, no smoke: this water is hot because it comes out of the ground / 🔴 THE CHILD IS NEVER
  IN THE WATER - his hands and his feet only, and only at the lower pool and the channel edge; hot
  water is carried and poured by the adult on every page where it moves / a slip lands sitting on
  the floor, never head first and never on a stair.

RENDERING (finish hierarchy): the dial of this book is GRIND, not brightness. One pigment is dark,
  gritty and very slightly raised off the surface when ground coarse, and pale, smooth and dead flat
  when ground fine, and the three steps of finish are read straight off that. (1) THE FIGURES AND
  THE ONE THING THEY ARE TOUCHING - coarsest grind, four to five passes, and a thin dry ink contour
  drawn first and still showing at the end. (2) THE PLACE THEY STAND IN - medium grind, two or three
  passes, NO contour: a wall meets a floor because the two fields stop, not because a line says so.
  (3) ANYTHING PAST THE EAVES, AND ALL STEAM - finest grind, one pass, no contour. 🔴 SO A FIGURE
  CAN BE FOUND ON ANY GROUND IN THIS BOOK BY ONE TEST: it is the only thing on the page with a drawn
  edge. 🔴 EVERY EDGE IS A CUT EDGE - grain lives inside a field and never at its border, and two
  fields never blend into each other. AT MOST TWO THINGS ON A PAGE GET THE COARSEST GRIND.

PALETTE (mineral, and there are only these): GROUND #C9BEA8, a warm clay tone that is painted on,
  never bare · SHELL WHITE #EDE7DC for steam, wet-stone shine and the inn's towels - 🔴 THIS IS THE
  LIGHTEST THING PERMITTED AND IT IS NOT WHITE; pure white appears nowhere, because a white mass of
  steam swallows the one pink on the page · AZURE #3B5A72, deep and coarse, spent on night, water
  and the father's robe · GREEN #6E8A6A · OCHRE #B98F52 for wood, straw and matting · BURNT EARTH
  #8A4E3C for roof tile and hot stone · INK #2A2622 for every contour and every marking · 🔴 PEACH
  PINK #E9A6A0, the one accent in twenty-five books, on Kota's neck towel and nowhere else ever -
  not on a flower, not on a lantern, not on a guest. AT MOST FOUR OF THE MINERAL COLOURS ON ONE
  PAGE, plus ink and shell white. The inn's own towels are shell white and plain, all of them.

STAGE CLAUSES (one inn and its yard; the place sets the grind and the number of steam passes):
  🔴 STEAM IS COUNTED IN PASSES OF SHELL WHITE at the finest grind, laid over a field already
    finished so that field still reads through it: a cold morning is four passes and the far side of
    the valley goes under, an ordinary day is two, a rainy day is ONE and it lies low along the
    ground instead of rising, and indoors there is none.
  UPPER POOL - hottest, highest up the slope. Stone here is burnt earth at the coarsest grind in the
    book. Water is not transparent: one flat field of azure, and nothing under the surface is drawn.
  LOWER POOL AND CHANNEL - the same water further down and cooler, so the same azure is ground finer
    and paler and the steam is one pass or none. 🔴 THE CHANNEL RUNS DOWNHILL ACROSS THE PAGE AND
    ITS FALL IS ALWAYS VISIBLE: a floating thing sits nearer the bottom edge of the page than the
    place it went in. A stone in the channel is pale and dry on top, burnt earth where water reaches.
  YARD - the widest place, ochre ground. When snow falls it is shell white over everything EXCEPT a
    hand's width along the channel, which stays bare ochre.
  CORRIDOR AND CHANGING FLOOR - wood indoors, ochre at medium grind with the grain running the
    length of each board. 🔴 A WET BOARD IS THE SAME OCHRE WITH ONE PASS OF AZURE OVER IT and a hard
    edge where the wet stops. Steam gathers high, so an upper shelf takes that same azure pass.
  GUEST ROOM - the quietest page, matting in one flat ochre field with no grain at all. Night here
    is azure at the coarsest grind over everything except the lamp's reach, and that reach is a
    hard-edged shape, never a glow.

CHARACTER DESIGN LANGUAGE: 🔴 A MARKING IS WHO SOMEBODY IS - NOT THE EYES. Every face carries flat
  ink shapes laid down last, on top of a coat that is already finished, and those shapes do all the
  work of feeling. For the raccoon dogs who keep this inn it is THE BAND ACROSS BOTH EYES: its
  angle, its thickness and where its two ends stop are the whole expression of that page - it tilts
  down at the outer ends and thickens when he is downcast, thins and lifts at the outer ends when he
  is pleased, and breaks into two short pieces when he is startled. THE EYE INSIDE THE BAND IS FIXED
  FOREVER - one small round opening of shell white with an ink centre, the same size on every page
  of twenty-five books, open or shut. THERE ARE ONLY THREE INK MARKINGS ON A RACCOON DOG AND NEVER A
  FOURTH: the eye band, the dark forehands, the ringed tail.
  A COAT IS A BUILT FIELD AND NOT HAIR - no hair strokes, no whisker lines, no drawn fur edge, no
  drawn folds in cloth. The coat ends where the field ends.
  GRADE: everyone here stands upright, wears cloth and has hands, guests included whatever animal a
  guest happens to be. 🔴 A GUEST HAS NO EYE BAND: each guest species is given ONE flat ink shape of
  its own on the face instead, in ink and never in colour.
  🔴 HOUSE OR ROAD, READ OFF THE OUTLINE: the two who live here WEAR cloth that is not clothing -
  the child a towel at the neck, the father a folded towel at the waist - and carry nothing. A guest
  wears no cloth at neck or waist and instead has ONE travelling thing on the outline: a bundle on
  the back, a wide hat, a stick, a basket. A house towel in a guest's hands is carried, not worn.
  🔴 AN ADULT IS NOT A BIGGER CHILD: an adult's robe closes at mid-calf so no shin is ever seen and
  the sleeves hang past the hands, a child's stops above the knee with both shins bare. Height never
  tells two figures apart.

CANVAS: 16:9 double-page spread. 🔴 NOT ONE WRITTEN MARK ANYWHERE - the door curtains, lanterns,
  towels, wooden tags and paper doors of this inn are all blank: no writing, no numerals, no crest.

SHEET LAYOUT: one sheet painted ground colour edge to edge - 🔴 THE SHEET HAS NO BARE MARGIN
  EITHER. Three figures stand in a row on a single ground line at their true relative heights: the
  father, the child reaching the father's hip, and one guest of middling size between them. Each is
  drawn THREE times - front standing, three-quarter walking, and the face alone large enough that
  both ends of the eye band are clear. Hands are visible and empty in every drawing, so the hand
  shape is fixed before any prop exists.
🔴 TEST ON THE THREE LARGE FACES: paint the eyes out entirely and the feeling must still be readable
  from the band alone. If it is not, the band was not doing the work and the sheet is wrong.
🔴 ONE EXTRA ROW AT THE FOOT OF THE SHEET: the same three again, side by side, filled solid in flat
  ink with 0 interior marks and no pink at all. THREE THINGS ARE READ OFF THAT ROW AND THE SHEET IS
  WRONG IF ANY ONE FAILS: the child is told from the father by his two bare shins, his two hanging
  towel ends and the gap under each raised sandal - NOT by being smaller; the father is told by his
  unbroken mid-calf hem, his sleeves hanging past the hands, and his widest point sitting at the
  hip; and the guest is told from both by carrying one thing and wearing no cloth at neck or waist.
🔴 A SECOND SMALL ROW: the child's face three times at thumbnail size - downcast, pleased, startled
  - WITH THE EYES PAINTED OUT. The three must still be told apart from the band alone.
  Nothing else on the sheet: no props beyond the guest's one travelling thing, no scenery, no ground
  beyond the single line, and no lettering, numerals, labels or name tags anywhere.
