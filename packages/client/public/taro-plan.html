4. 앵커 — 한 시리즈 = 한 그림체

시리즈 전권이 한 그림체입니다. 무대·계절 차이는 앵커 안 무대 조항이 처리합니다 — 매체·색은 그대로 두고 「두 색을 어디에 쓰고 무엇을 종이로 남기나」만 바뀝니다. 🔴 악센트 타로 허리끈 · 무무 머리끈 에만 닿습니다.

STYLE ANCHOR - taro-batik   (Taro and Mumu's island village / wax resist, dipped twice)

Style: wax-resist dyeing on woven cotton, ONE indigo dye bath used twice and one small second dye,
  4-6 year old picture book. 🔴 THE CLOTH STARTS PALE AND ONLY GETS DARKER - nothing is ever painted
  light. Wax, dip, more wax, dip again - exactly THREE values and no fourth. Every value shows the
  weave running through it. SHADING IS ZERO - no modelling, gradient or glow. 🔴 EXCEPT a shadow
  LYING ON THE GROUND: ONE flat DEEP silhouette, hard edge, nothing shaded inside it, never on a
  body and never on a face.

RENDERING (finish hierarchy): wet leaves and the shine on water are CLOTH areas with a hard edge,
  never a soft halo. 🔴 RAIN IS DRAWN WITH THE WAX PEN, at most 13 hairline CLOTH lines all leaning
  one way, and it is the only thing allowed to cross another shape. Cracked wax lets dye in as
  hairline DEEP lines: at most 9 per page, only inside CLOTH areas, never across a face, never in a
  row. Repeats are capped: leaves at most 9 of one stamped shape · houses behind at most 4 flat DEEP
  silhouettes with 0 windows; no repeat mirrors its neighbour. 🔴 A RUN OR A BAND (stones in a line,
  a ring of shells, a bed of leaves) IS EXEMPT FROM THESE CAPS - the nearest six or seven are
  separate stamps, the rest carries on as one joined line. FINISHED THINGS PER PAGE = 2.

PALETTE: CLOTH #EFE3CC, waxed before any dip - faces, light, rain, steam, ripe fruit, bare earth,
  anything woven from grass, the brightest thing anywhere · MID #4C6E8C, one dip - sky, water, walls,
  most of the world · DEEP INDIGO #22406B, two dips - animal backs, shade, tree masses, night,
  the ground of the whole book · ACCENT ORANGE #E2711D, 🔴 a separate small dye on nothing but Taro's
  waist cord and Mumu's head band. No white, no black, no green, no pink.

STAGE CLAUSES (the stage changes where the wax goes, not the dyes):
  VILLAGE - walls and ground MID, roofs and tree masses DEEP, the yard left as one broad CLOTH area
    the two of them stand on.
  RAIN AND AFTER - MID covers the whole page including the ground, everything under a roof stays MID
    and everything out in it is DEEP, the rain lines are CLOTH. Once it has passed the ground stays
    DEEP because it is soaked and CLOTH steam rises off it, at most 5 hard-edged shapes; puddles are
    flat CLOTH areas with 0 reflections.
  WATER - one unbroken MID area, 0 ripples, 0 glints; a thing floating on it keeps its whole
    outline, a thing under it is DEEP with no edge of its own. Sand is CLOTH.
  NIGHT - DEEP covers everything, MID exists only where a light reaches and the light itself is
    CLOTH. A thing being looked for is the one CLOTH shape in the page's DEEP field.

CHARACTER DESIGN LANGUAGE: animals are built from the same dipped areas as the world. GRADE:
  bipedal, upright, forelimbs are HANDS with no hooves, bare feet, 🔴 NO CLOTHING - ONE cord each is
  all either of them wears, so no sleeve, pocket, hem or collar anywhere.
  🔴 SOMETHING ON EVERY ANIMAL IS DRAWN LONGER THAN IT COULD BE, AND THE STRETCH IS THE JOKE. What
  runs long goes at least half again past what the build allows, holds the same narrow width the
  whole way with no taper and no elbow or knee showing, and bends in smooth arcs instead of at joints.
  🔴 IT IS NOT THE SAME PART ON EVERY ANIMAL, AND WHICH PART IT IS IS HOW ONE IS TOLD FROM ANOTHER
  ACROSS A ROOM. A YOUNG ONE RUNS LONG IN THE LIMBS AND KEEPS A SMALL ROUND BODY; A GROWN ONE RUNS
  LONG IN THE BODY OR THE NECK AND KEEPS ITS ARMS AND LEGS SHORT AND FOLDED IN, so a grown one is
  never a bigger child - the two are apart as OUTLINES, with no grey hair, no glasses and nothing
  worn to say which is which. 🔴 THE STRETCH FOLLOWS THE PAGE - a reaching limb is longer than a
  resting one, and the longest thing in any picture belongs to whoever is doing the thing the page
  is about.
  🔴 THE WHOLE FACE IS WAXED CLOTH, so a face is the palest thing on any page and
  the eyes, nose and mouth are small DEEP marks left inside it. An eye is a NARROW UPRIGHT OVAL, not
  a round dot: it stretches taller when the eyes go wide and squashes to a flat lying oval when they
  narrow or shut, but covers the same area of face either way. 🔴 THERE ARE NO EYEBROWS - the deep
  marks inside a waxed face are exactly three, two eyes and one mouth, and a fourth is never added.
  🔴 AN EYE CANNOT POINT - an oval has no pupil and no direction, so where a character is looking is
  said by the head, the neck and the stretched limb, never by the eye. The mouth is ONE curve and
  carries the feeling; nothing crosses a face, not even rain.
  TARO is a monkey child, small and round-bodied, the stretch in his thin arms and one long tail
  that ends in a closed curl; MUMU is a deer child, a head taller, the stretch in long straight legs
  and two tall ears that turn, a stub tail. The two grown animals sit or are seen from behind, run
  long in the back and the neck instead, and carry no accent colour anywhere.

CANVAS: 16:9 double-page spread. 🔴 No lettering, numerals or signs anywhere.

NOT: no airbrush, gradient, glow or 3D render / no soft, feathered or blurred edge anywhere.
