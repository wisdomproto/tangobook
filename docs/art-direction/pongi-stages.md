## §0. 🔴 이 시리즈의 매체 번역 한 줄 — 둘이다

**① 어두운 색이 따로 없다. 겹쳐야 생긴다. 그러니 무대 시트가 정하는 것은 「어디가 겹인가」다.**

앵커: `Where the two overlap a third, darker colour appears - that is the only way a dark exists`.
칠하는 게 아니라 **판 두 장이 만나는 자리**라, 「저기를 좀 어둡게」가 이 매체에는 없는 말이다.
🔴 SCENE 이 이미 그 규칙으로 쓰여 있다 — **물에 잠긴 것은 겹(OVERLAP), 물 위에 뜬 것은 제 윤곽 그대로**
(17 p5 큰 종이배가 뜬다 ↔ 12 p10 물 표면에 별이 비친다). → 자리 시트에 `WHERE THE OVERLAP FALLS` 절.

**② 얼음은 그리는 것이 아니라 안 찍는 것이다 — 그래서 하늘과 같은 것이다.**

앵커: `Ice is the same area left as PAPER instead of pulled`. 24권은 「얼어붙은 운하가 미색 한 판」으로
열린다. 🔴 **즉 24권은 새 무대가 아니라 `Canal` 시트의 물 면적을 안 찍은 것**이고, 시트가 그 면적을
확정해 놔야 물과 얼음이 **같은 운하**로 읽힌다. 면적이 권마다 다르면 얼음이 딴 데가 된다.
타로의 「모래가 CLOTH 라 조개는 CLOTH 일 수 없다」와 같은 자리다.

⚠️ **셋째로 지킬 것** — `Registration is one hair off on every page`. 시트에도 어긋남을 그린다.
안 그리면 시트가 「정판이 맞는 그림」이 되어 컷이 그걸 따라간다.

### §1.1 Canal — 실제 프롬프트 (가장 먼저)

```
STAGE SHEET - Canal   (pongi-screenwater · SCENE token: Canal · bake FIRST)

The canal the village is built along. Six books stand on it and one book freezes it, so this
drawing decides the water, the bank and the ice all at once.

FIXED PARTS - two screens only, pulled flat, one pass each:
  THE WATER is ONE unbroken pull of INK1 across the picture, never lighter or darker inside itself.
    0 ripples, 0 glints, 0 reflections. Its far edge is a hard straight line.
  THE NEAR BANK runs along the bottom in INK2 - a grass lip, a low mooring post every so often,
    and a worn earth path on top of it. Reeds at the water's edge, at most 7 strokes.
  THE FAR BANK is at most 5 flat INK2 silhouettes of houses with 0 windows, standing on the far
    edge of the water. Nothing behind them.
  THE SKY IS NOT PULLED - it is bare PAPER, and it is the brightest thing on every page.
  ONE MOORED BOAT lies against the near bank, INK2, seams at most 4 lines.

🔴 WHERE THE OVERLAP FALLS - this is what the sheet is for:
  A thing FLOATING ON the water sits on top of the INK1 pull with its WHOLE outline showing and is
    NOT overlapped. A thing UNDER the water is INK1+INK2 overlap lying inside that pull, hard edge,
    no distortion, and it has no outline of its own. An animal's back and head are overlap; its
    chest and belly are INK2. Night is overlap over everything except what is left as paper.

🔴 THE ICE STATE: winter is not a different place. THE SAME WATER AREA IS SIMPLY NOT PULLED - it
  becomes bare PAPER, edge for edge identical to the INK1 area, so that the ice and the sky are the
  same cream and the canal is recognisably the same canal. The bank, the posts, the reeds and the
  far houses do not change at all. A scratch on the ice is ONE thin INK2 line and nothing else.

REGISTRATION: the two screens sit one hair out of true, and that misfit shows at 2 or 3 edges on
  every panel of this sheet - a sliver of paper on one side of the bank, a sliver of overlap on the
  other. Do not correct it anywhere.

SPOTS:
  A ALONG THE BANK, wide, eye level: water filling the lower half, far houses on the top edge.
  B FROM THE BANK LOOKING ACROSS, medium: the near lip in the foreground, water, far bank.
  C THE WATER'S EDGE, close, high: where INK1 meets INK2, and one moored post.
  D ON THE ICE, wide, low: the same frame as A with the water area left as bare paper.

PLATE: A, B, C, D once each, plus A DRAWN TWICE side by side - water and ice - at exactly the same
  size, so the two can be laid over each other and the area checked as identical.

NOT: no character of any kind, no red anywhere on this sheet (the red plate touches only Pongi's
  neck cord), no lettering, numerals or signs, no third mixed colour that is not an overprint, no
  airbrush, gradient, glow or 3D render, no soft or feathered edge, no ripple, glint or sparkle,
  and nothing on the water is ever mirrored or flipped.
```

### §2.1 PaperSheet — 실제 프롬프트 (🔴 3권 공유)

```
PROP SHEET - PaperSheet   (pongi-screenwater · SCENE token: PaperSheet)

A sheet of drawing paper. Three books use it - drawn on at the table, carried down the road, and
folded into a boat - so its one size and one colour are fixed here.

🔴 THE SHEET IS BARE PAPER. It is not pulled with either screen, which makes it the same cream as
  the sky and the brightest thing indoors. Its edges are read only by what is pulled AROUND it -
  the INK2 table under it, the INK1 water beside it. NEVER outline the sheet.
FORM: a rectangle about as wide as a child's two hands. One book needs TWO sizes side by side and
  the larger must be plainly larger, not slightly.
STATES - draw all five at consistent scale:
  1 BLANK, lying flat on an INK2 table, seen from above.
  2 DRAWN ON - the marks are INK2 only, crooked and child-made: a long horizontal line for the
    canal with one boat above it. 🔴 The drawing inside is NOT in the book's own style - it is a
    four-year-old's hand.
  3 SMEARED - rake-like drag marks crossing the sheet in a row.
  4 FLAPPING, held up in the wind, one corner turned over so the back shows.
  5 FOLDED INTO A BOAT, floating: the hull keeps its WHOLE outline on top of the INK1 pull and is
    NOT overlapped, because it is on the water and not under it.
🔴 WET PAPER: a sheet that has been in the water becomes a lump - state 5 gone soft. That lump IS
  pulled, in INK2, because it is no longer bright. This is the only time the sheet takes ink.

PLATE: the five states, plus one panel with the two sizes side by side.

NOT: no character, no hands, no red anywhere, no lettering, numerals or written words on the sheet
  (the drawing on it has no letters), no outline around the sheet, no third mixed colour, no
  gradient, glow or soft edge.
```

### §2.2 Skates — 실제 프롬프트

```
PROP SHEET - Skates   (pongi-screenwater · SCENE token: Skates)

Two strap-on skates - a wooden footplate with an iron blade screwed under it and two straps over
the top. One book only, but seven of its ten pages have them on the page.

FORM: the footplate is INK2, one flat shape, at most 4 seam lines. The blade under it is the
  OVERLAP colour - a long thin curve rising to a small hook at the toe. Straps are INK2, two per
  skate, with a small buckle each. No metal sheen, no highlight, no rim light.
STATES:
  1 THE PAIR, lying on the bank side by side, seen from above.
  2 ONE SKATE, side on, so the blade curve and the toe hook are clear.
  3 ON THE FOOT, strapped, seen from the side at ground level.
🔴 ON THE ICE the blade sits ON bare PAPER, so it is the darkest thing on the page and the only
  overlap in that frame. A cut in the ice behind it is ONE thin INK2 line - NOT overlap, because
  nothing is under the surface.

PLATE: the three states, plus one close-up of the blade and toe hook at large size.

NOT: no character, no red anywhere, no lettering or numerals, no metal shine or sparkle, no snow
  spray, no third mixed colour, no gradient, glow or soft edge.
```

## §3. 🔴 미결 — 「현관」 3갈래 · 「문간」 3갈래

> 🔴 **이 §3 은 판정 근거만 적는다. 이름→토큰 변환의 SSOT 는 `_stage-tokens.json` 이다.**
> 표 출력·검사 = `node packages/client/scripts/extract-series-stages.mjs --tokens pongi` (**미매칭 0** 이어야 한다).
> 🔴 **쪽 목록은 폐기했다** — 목록과 변환표를 따로 두니 어긋났다(변환표엔 있는데 목록엔 없는 이름이 나왔다).
> 규칙이 전 이름을 덮으므로 목록이 필요 없고, 「목록 밖이라 안 붙인다」가 생기지 않는다.
> 🔴 **SPOT**: `_stage-tokens.json` 의 `spots` 가 `null` 인 시트는 **시트명만** 붙인다. 값이 있는 시트도
> **A/B/C/D 는 카메라가 정하므로 경로표(§4)에서 정한다** — §3 에서 비워 둔 건 빈칸이 아니라 경로표 몫이다.
> 🔴 **일부 이름은 권을 알아야 정해진다**(`byBook`). 앞 쪽을 읽어야 하는 종류이고, 규칙 파일이 그걸 들고 있다.
