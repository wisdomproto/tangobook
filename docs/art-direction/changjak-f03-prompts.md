## F-03 §1. 앵커 배정

**권**: `f03` 「할머니 부엌의 소리」 (12쪽 · 4~6세 · 주제군 **F 집·가족의 작은 사건** · 엔진 **관찰과 성장** · 무대 폴란드 시골 부엌 · 주인공 새끼 여우 + 할머니 여우)

**한 줄**: 민속 오려내기 문양이 벽·선반·앞치마의 **반복 띠**이고 그 안은 평칠. 🔴 **그 쪽에서 소리를 내는 것 하나만 마감한다 — 주인공은 마감하지 않는다.** 앵커 슬러그 `changjak-folkband` — **신규 민팅**(배정표 지정, 변경 없음).

**이 권이 그림에 요구하는 것** 셋.

1. 🔴 **마감 위계를 서사가 직접 정한다 — 그리고 뒤집혀 있다.** §2.16 의 3단은 「주인공 = 마감」인데, 이 권의 주인공은 **눈을 감고 있고 독자만 본다**. 화자가 「할머니가 감자를 썰고 있었어요」라고 앞질러 말하지 않으므로 **그림이 답하는 유일한 자리**가 소리원이고, 눈은 화면에서 가장 마감된 것으로 간다(§2.16) → **소리원 하나만 세밀하게 마감하고 새끼 여우는 평칠 + 윤곽으로 둔다.** 마감 위계가 곧 장치다.
2. 🔴 **부엌은 소품이 가장 많은 무대라 반복으로 눌러야 한다.** 벽·선반 앞자락·앞치마·도자기를 **오려내기 반복 문양 띠**로 만들면 화면은 꽉 찼는데 정보량이 0 이 된다(§2.16 「반복하기」). 문양 띠는 열두 쪽 내내 **같은 자리에 같은 무늬**라 눈이 한 번 훑고 다시는 안 본다.
3. 🔴 **검정이 이 책에 딱 둘뿐이다 — 빗자루 솔과 까만 씨.** 그래서 p6 의 오답(「그건… 빗자루다!」)이 말이 아니라 **화면에서 검정 둘이 나란히 놓인 것**으로 설명된다. 윤곽선까지 검정으로 그으면 이 장치가 즉시 죽는다.

**왜 이 공정인가.** 🔴 **어길 방법이 없게 만들었다**(§2.16.3): **윤곽 잉크를 검정이 아니라 짙은 코발트로 정하면**, 「까만 것이 화면에 둘뿐」이 지시가 아니라 팔레트 사실이 된다. 아무리 잘 그려도 셋째 검정이 생길 수가 없고, p1 선반 끝의 까만 알갱이가 **어른 눈에 조용히 걸리는 유일한 이물**이 된다. 그리고 접힌 앞발가락(1→2→3→4)이 눈금이라 「네 개를 다 맞혔다」를 화자가 안 세도 되는데, 그 발가락은 **마감 2번 자리**(그 쪽에서 몸이 하는 일)에 정확히 앉는다 — 위계와 눈금이 같은 자리다.

## F-03 §2. STYLE ANCHOR

```
STYLE ANCHOR - changjak-folkband   (a fox cub and its grandmother / a Polish country kitchen)

Style: CUT-PAPER FOLK MOTIF BANDS with flat colour inside them, on chalk-white plaster paper
  #F4F0E6, 4-6 year old picture book. Walls, shelf fronts, apron and crockery carry REPEAT BANDS
  cut from paper - the same motif over and over along a strip, 0 variation within a band - and
  everything else is a flat shape with a fine drawn contour.

RENDERING (finish hierarchy): 🔴 THE MOST FINISHED THING ON EACH PAGE IS NOT THE HERO. ONE object
  per page - THE THING MAKING THE SOUND - is drawn in full detail, and the cub stays flat colour
  with a contour and no interior detail, because its eyes are shut.
  FINISHED THINGS PER PAGE = 2 - that one sound source + the ONE part of the cub that is doing the
  listening (ears, folded toes, tilted head, shut mouth, groping paw, open eyes). Everything else
  is a repeat band or a flat shape. 🔴 THE CONTOUR INK IS DARK COBALT #26386E, NEVER BLACK - black
  exists only as the broom bristles and the black seed, and that is all of it.
  Bands: wall frieze = one motif repeated at least 9 times - shelf front = one motif 7 times -
  apron = one flower repeated, 0 shading, 0 variation in any band. Herb bunches = 3 flat shapes.
  Crockery = at most 8 pieces, dots only. Push back by WITHHOLDING FINISH, never by blurring.
  DENSITY RATION = none; all twelve pages stay at 2.

PALETTE: paper #F4F0E6 (plaster, tile stove, flour) · contour dark cobalt #26386E · cobalt blue
  #2A4C9B (the folk bands and the dotted crockery) · fox red #C4552B (both foxes and the
  firelight) · honey wood #C99A55 (table, floor, benches, shelves) · 🔴 BLACK #14110E,
  used for two things only: the broom bristles and the black seed. No grey, no green, no purple,
  no gradient anywhere.

CHARACTER DESIGN LANGUAGE: both foxes are one flat red shape with a cobalt contour, so they read
  at thumbnail size by silhouette and EAR ANGLE - the ears are the acting and are drawn large.
  Eyes are drawn, with a SEPARATE contour brow above; 🔴 the cub's eyes are two shut
  curved lines on eleven of twelve pages. FIXED GRADE: both stand on two legs with forepaws as
  hands and wear simple cloth - a headscarf and flowered apron, a small waistcoat - and their
  heads stay fox (long muzzle, big triangular ears). 🔴 The cub never touches anything that is
  making a sound.

CANVAS: 16:9 double-page spread. One Polish country kitchen and nothing else on all twelve pages:
  white tile stove and long bench LEFT, heavy honey table centre, counter and arched brick oven
  RIGHT with a banded shelf of dotted crockery above, a small back window, a broom and wooden
  shoes by the door, a lidded glass jar in the corner. Positions never change. NO LETTERING, NUMERALS OR SIGNS.

NOT: no digital slickness of any kind - airbrush, gradient, glow, 3D CG, cel-shading, photographic,
  or a paper-texture filter over flat colour / no light rays, beams, sparkle or lens bloom - the
  fire is a flat shape with a cut edge / not blurred, hazy, faded or spotlit / one sheet thick - no
  stitching, no thread, no wool fibre, no felt, no sculpted material.
```

## F-03 §3. 캐릭터 시트 (🔴 FolkBands 를 가장 먼저)

```
CHARACTER SHEET - FolkBands   (bake FIRST - this sheet is the entire background budget)

Four repeat bands of cut folk paper, drawn as long horizontal strips on a plain plaster ground
#F4F0E6. 🔴 EACH BAND IS ONE MOTIF REPEATED WITHOUT VARIATION - identical spacing, identical size,
0 detail inside any single unit. They are wallpaper for the eye: the whole point is that a reader
takes them in once and never looks again.

1 WALL FRIEZE: a cobalt #2A4C9B cut motif - a symmetrical bird-and-leaf shape - repeated AT LEAST
  9 times along a strip, all facing the same way, 0 interior marks in any of them.
2 SHELF FRONT: a scalloped cobalt strip with a smaller repeated cut shape, 7 units, hanging along
  the edge of a honey shelf.
3 APRON: one flower shape repeated over a plaster ground in cobalt, 0 shading, 0 outline drawing -
  a printed cloth, not a painting of cloth.
4 CROCKERY DOTS: cobalt dots on plaster-white bowls and jars, evenly spaced, 0 highlight,
  0 reflection, 0 rim shine.
🔴 CHECK ON THIS SHEET: cover the four bands and the page must lose no information at all. If any
  single unit is more finished than its neighbours, or if a band is shaded or lit from one side,
  the band has stopped being background and the book breaks.
🔴 ALSO: one strip of the BLACK #14110E broom bristles and one small heap of the BLACK seed, side
  by side, so the two - and only these two - are established as the only blacks in the book. All
  drawn contours on this sheet are dark cobalt #26386E, not black.
0 lettering, 0 numerals. SCENE token: FolkBands.
```
