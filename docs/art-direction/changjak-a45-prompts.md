## A-45 §1. 앵커 배정

**권**: `a45` 「물에 비친 나를 지워 줘」 (13쪽 · 4~6세 · 주제군 **A 마음·감정** · 엔진 **소원의 대가** · 무대 할슈타트 소금호수의 나무 선착장 한 자리 · 주인공 새끼 수달 + 새끼 흰 거위)

**클러스터**: **C7** · 앵커 슬러그 `changjak-foldpress` — **신규 민팅**(배정표 지정, 변경 없음).

**한 줄**: 위 절반만 그린 뒤 **수평선에서 종이를 접어 눌러** 아래 절반을 만든다. 아래는 언제나 위가 옮겨진 것이고, 어긋나게 누르면 늘어나고 갈라지며, 널판이 덮은 자리는 아예 안 옮겨진다.

**이 권이 그림에 요구하는 것** 셋.

1. 🔴 **비친 것을 흉내 내지 않고 재료로 참이 되게 한다.** 아래 절반이 위 절반의 거울상인 것이 그림 솜씨가 아니라 **누른 결과**여야, 「물결이 지나가면 얼굴이 늘어난다」가 화가의 선택이 아니라 물리가 된다.
2. 🔴 **계기판 = 정합(register)이다.** 쪽마다 **위아래 대칭이 얼마나 성립하나**를 값으로 준다 — 정확히 맞음 / 한 띠만 옆으로 밀림 / 통째로 깨짐 / 마스크로 아예 안 옮겨짐. 이 네 단 말고 중간이 없다.
3. 🔴 **덮으면 손으로 칠할 수밖에 없다.** 널판이 마스크로 가린 자리는 눌러도 아무것도 안 옮겨지므로 **손으로 평면 한 장을 채운다** — 이 책에서 손으로 칠한 유일한 물이고, 「또 하나의 화면이 없어졌다」가 표면의 종류로 읽힌다.

## A-45 §2. STYLE ANCHOR

```
STYLE ANCHOR - changjak-foldpress   (an otter pup and a white gosling / one plank dock on a salt lake)

Style: gouache on soft cream paper #F6F4EE, 4-6 year old picture book, MADE BY FOLDING. 🔴 Only the
  upper half of the sheet is painted; while it is wet the sheet is folded on ONE straight horizontal
  line - the waterline - and pressed, so the LOWER HALF IS A MIRROR PRINT of the upper half and never
  a second drawing. The fold runs unbroken across the full width and is never tilted.

RENDERING (finish hierarchy): 🔴 THE GAUGE IS THE REGISTER OF THE PRESS - how much of the water is a
  true mirror - and it has FOUR STATES AND NO IN-BETWEEN. (1) SQUARE: the print matches the top edge
  to edge. (2) SLIPPED: one horizontal band of the print slides sideways, so whatever crosses it
  prints tall and doubled at its cut; outside the band the print stays exact. (3) BROKEN: the
  sheet moved under the press and the image comes apart into at most 9 patches that do not touch,
  bare lake colour between them. (4) MASKED: a paper slip covers the plate where the floating plank
  lies, nothing transfers, and that region is then FILLED BY HAND with one flat slab of #21372E
  holding no image, no edge and no grain - the only hand-painted water in the book. Nothing is ever
  blurred or faded to mean water; it is MIS-PRINTED instead. 🔴 AFTER the press only three things are
  laid on top: the boat prow, the floating plank, and the gosling's body on open water - and the
  gosling gets a short second fold of its own so its mirror sits directly beneath it. FINISHED THINGS
  PER PAGE = 2, the otter and the one thing it touches. Far mountains = at most 5 silhouettes, 0
  windows. Roofs = at most 4. Rope = at most 6 turns. Plank grain = at most 4 lines. DENSITY
  RATION = none; all thirteen pages stay at 2.

PALETTE: paper #F6F4EE · lake green #2C4A3C, and #21372E for the hand-filled slab under the plank ·
  dock grey-brown #8C7C68 · far roofs dull red #9E5A4A, at most 4 and never on an animal · wet
  crusted salt #C9C4B4, grey and never bright. 🔴 THE ONLY PURE WHITE IN THE BOOK IS THE GOSLING AND
  ITS MIRROR PRINT - not the salt, not a splash, not a highlight. No sky blue, no purple, no pink.

CHARACTER DESIGN LANGUAGE: flat gouache shapes with a soft brush edge, no ink outline. An eye is a full round dark iris with a lid line over it that can lower; no eyebrow, no
  human mouth, no cheeks. 🔴 Acting is done by NECK LENGTH and by how far a forepaw leaves the boards.
  Silhouettes separate at thumbnail size: heavy low body and thick tail against a light round body
  and a long neck. FIXED GRADE: four-legged otter, waterbird gosling; no clothes, no
  shoes, nothing held. The otter raises ONE forepaw only when it is using it.

CANVAS: 16:9 double-page spread. Same dock corner on all thirteen pages. 🔴 No lettering, numerals, signs or name boards anywhere.

NOT: no digital slickness - airbrush, gradient, glow, 3D CG, cel-shading, photographic or texture
  filter / never a blurred or double-exposed reflection, only mis-printing / never a second white,
  never a white highlight or white spray / one sheet thick: no wool, no felt fuzz, no stitching.
```

### 시트 1 — OtterPup

```
CHARACTER SHEET - OtterPup   (bake FIRST, before any scene)

Same make as the book: flat gouache shapes on cream paper #F6F4EE, soft brush edges, no ink outline.

FACE: a river otter pup - broad flat muzzle, small rounded ears set low and wide, a dark nose pad, a
  full round dark iris with a lid line above it. No eyebrow, no human mouth, no cheek blush. Whiskers
  = at most 7 per side, single strokes.
BODY: low and heavy, dark grey-brown #6B5A48 on the back grading to #A08A70 on the throat, with a
  thick tapered tail as long as the body. Fur is NOT drawn - at most 5 wet clumping strokes along the
  spine when it has been in the water, 0 otherwise. Webbed forepaws, five short toes, no fingers.
GRADE (fixed, all thirteen pages): four legs on the boards. No clothes, no shoes, nothing held. ONE
  forepaw is raised only while it is being used - to tap, to smooth, to point, to push.
REFERENCE SHEET: (a) full body standing four-square in three-quarter view; (b) lying flat on its belly
  with the chin pushed out past a board edge and both forepaws stacked under the chin - this is the
  posture of p1 and p13; (c) neck stretched long and low, head turning, one forepaw off the boards;
  (d) three head close-ups - eyes round and open, eyes screwed shut with the mouth wide, eyes narrowed
  into a dark opening with the mouth closed.
🔴 THE MIRROR TEST: paint this sheet, fold the page under the chin and press. The printed head must be
  readable as the same head upside down. If the face only works one way up, simplify it until it works both.
```
