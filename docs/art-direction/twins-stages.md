## §0. 🔴 매체 번역 한 줄 — 날씨는 드럼이 돈 방향이다

앵커: 🔴 `THE SEA AND SKY OF ONE VOLUME ARE ONE PULL RUNNING THE SAME DIRECTION ON EVERY PAGE OF
THAT VOLUME - sideways for a calm day, down for rain, flat for fog - and the weather lives in that
direction and nowhere else.`

**그러므로 이 시리즈의 첫 시트는 그림이 아니라 「방향 세 장」이다.** 잉크가 고르지 않게 얹혀 드럼이 돈
쪽으로 결과 얼룩이 생기는데, **그 결이 곧 날씨**다. 🔴 그리고 그 방향은 **한 권 안에서 안 바뀐다** —
시트가 방향을 확정해 놓지 않으면 권마다·쪽마다 결이 달라지고, 그 순간 **이 시리즈에 날씨라는 것이 없어진다**.
구름·빗줄기를 더 그리는 것으로는 못 메운다(그건 다른 매체의 해법이다).

🔴 **둘째 — 어촌인데 노랑이 금지다.** 앵커: `A FISHING VILLAGE IS FULL OF YELLOW AND NONE OF IT IS
ALLOWED HERE` — 기름옷·부표·등불·그물·뜸·등대 몸통·종이꽃·연이 전부 INK1 또는 INK2 를 받는다. 노랑은
리리 모자와 롤로 장화뿐이다. **무대·사물 시트에서 가장 어기기 쉬운 자리**라 모든 `NOT` 첫 줄에 둔다.

⚠️ 셋째 — `THE TWO DRUMS NEVER REGISTER`. 시트에도 색이 형태 밖으로 한 올 삐져나간 자리를 두세 곳
남긴다. 안 그리면 시트가 「정합이 맞는 그림」이 되어 컷이 그걸 따라간다.

### §1.1 SeaAndSky — 실제 프롬프트 (🔴 가장 먼저 · 이건 자리가 아니다)

```
STAGE SHEET - SeaAndSky   (twins-risoshore · SCENE token: SeaAndSky · bake FIRST)

Not a place - the WEATHER INSTRUMENT of the whole series. The sea and the sky of one volume are ONE
pull of ATLANTIC, and the direction that pull runs is the only place the weather lives. This sheet
fixes the three directions so that a volume can pick one and hold it for ten pages.

THREE DIRECTIONS, drawn as three panels of the SAME horizon at the SAME size:
  1 SIDEWAYS - a calm day. The mottle and the streak of the ink run left to right, parallel to the
    horizon, unbroken across the whole width. 0 ripples, 0 glints, 0 foam lines.
  2 DOWN - rain. The same pull, the same colour, the same amount of ink, but the streak runs top to
    bottom. Rain itself is at most 11 straight strokes all leaning one way, and 🔴 THE STREAK IS
    ALREADY DOING THE WORK - do not add cloud, spray or darkening.
  3 FLAT - fog. The roller has laid the ink evenly with almost no streak at all, and the horizon
    line is where the pull simply stops against bare PAPER. Nothing is blurred and nothing fades.

🔴 RULES THAT COME OFF THIS SHEET:
  - ONE VOLUME = ONE DIRECTION, on every page of it, including the pages that are indoors and show
    the sea through a window.
  - The sea is never lighter or darker inside itself. Distance does not pale it.
  - The sky is the same pull as the sea, or it is bare PAPER - never a second, lighter blue.
  - Anything ON the water sits whole on top of the pull. Anything UNDER it is OVERLAP lying inside
    the pull with a hard edge. 🔴 NOTHING IS EVER MIRRORED IN THE WATER - there are no reflections.

MISFIT: on each panel the ATLANTIC runs past the horizon by about a hair at two or three places.
  Leave it.

PLATE: the three direction panels, plus one strip showing the same small boat sitting on all three,
  so a reader can confirm the boat does not change - only the ground it sits on does.

NOT: 🔴 no yellow anywhere on this sheet (the buoy yellow touches only Riri's hat and Lolo's boots)
  / no character / no lettering or numerals / no third drum or mixed colour / no ripple, glint,
  foam line, sparkle or reflection / no cloud drawn as a shape / no gradient, glow, blur or soft
  edge / nothing paler with distance.
```

### §1.2 Shore — 실제 프롬프트

```
STAGE SHEET - Shore   (twins-risoshore · SCENE token: Shore · bake SECOND)

The wide sand below the village. Six books stand here, so this drawing decides it once - and it
decides which of its several places is which.

FIXED PARTS:
  THE SAND IS BARE PAPER from edge to edge and it is the light of the page. It is not printed at all.
  THE SEA is one pull of ATLANTIC across the top, running the direction that volume's SeaAndSky
    sheet gives it.
  🔴 WET SAND left by a retreating wave is a single flat ATLANTIC pull with a HARD EDGE against the
    bare dry sand - it is not a gradient and not a darker sand. This is a STATE of the shore, and it
    is the only state it has.
  ROCKS at the left in INK2, a low group, one flat shape each, 0 texture.
  THE HILL rises at the right and carries the lighthouse; a single pine stands beside it.
  THE HARBOUR WALL closes the far right of the sand.
  AN UPTURNED HULL lies on the sand toward the middle, INK2, its underside OVERLAP.
  Footprints and dug holes are at most 9 OVERLAP marks - never more, and never in a row.

🔴 FOUR NAMED PLACES ON ONE SAND - the books call them different things and they are all here:
  BY THE ROCKS (left) · UNDER THE HILL (right) · UNDER THE LIGHTHOUSE (far right, the tower visible)
  · IN FRONT OF THE HARBOUR (far right, the wall visible). Fix on this sheet WHERE EACH ONE IS, so
  that a page which names one of them is standing in a decided spot and not a new beach.

SPOTS:
  A THE WHOLE SAND, wide, eye level: rocks left, hill and lighthouse right, sea across the top.
  B BY THE ROCKS, medium: the group at frame left, sand running away right.
  C THE SAND, close, high: bare paper and whatever is on it.
  D THE WATER LINE, medium: the hard edge where the wet ATLANTIC pull meets bare sand.

PLATE: A, B, C, D once each, plus 🔴 A PLAN DIAGRAM of the sand with the four named places marked,
  plus A drawn TWICE - dry and with the wet pull - at the same size.

NOT: 🔴 no yellow anywhere / no character / no lettering or numerals / no third drum / no foam,
  glint or reflection / no seaweed drawn strand by strand / no gradient, glow or soft edge / the
  sand is never printed.
```

### 🔴 갈랐다 — 이름은 같은데 다른 물건이다 (작업표의 「공유」가 틀린 자리)

### §2.1 Net — 실제 프롬프트 (🔴 한 권 전체가 이 물건이다)

```
PROP SHEET - Net   (twins-risoshore · SCENE token: Net)

The father's fishing net. One book is ten pages of two children untangling it, so its FOUR STATES
are the instrument and are fixed here.

FORM: INK2. 🔴 THE MESH IS AT MOST 11 CROSSINGS anywhere it is drawn clearly, and everywhere else
  the net is a flat INK2 shape with NO interior detail at all. This is the whole trick: the near
  patch has mesh, the rest is a shape. Do not draw a net full of holes - the cap is the design.
  Floats along one edge are INK1, at most 7, all the same, none mirroring a neighbour.
  🔴 THE FLOATS ARE NOT YELLOW. In a real harbour they would be; here they take INK1.
FOUR STATES, drawn at the same size and the same distance:
  1 TANGLED HEAP - taller than a child, one flat INK2 mass, mesh readable at only ONE small patch
    near the viewer.
  2 ONE SPAN FREED - the same heap with a hand's width of proper mesh at its edge, and the mass
    unchanged behind it. 🔴 The freed part must look tiny against the mass.
  3 A KNOT - one small hard lump of OVERLAP, tighter and smaller than anything else on the page.
  4 SPREAD FLAT - the net laid out square across the ground, mesh readable across it at the 11-
    crossing cap, and 🔴 THE MASS ENTIRELY GONE. The page is emptier than page one, not busier.

PLATE: the four states in a row, plus one close-up of the mesh at the cap so the crossings can be
  counted, plus one panel of a single float.

NOT: 🔴 no yellow anywhere / no character, no hands / no lettering or numerals / no third drum / no
  net drawn as a full field of holes / no rope shading, sheen or highlight / no gradient, glow or
  soft edge.
```

### §2.2 Shells — 실제 프롬프트 (🔴 2권 공유)

```
PROP SHEET - Shells   (twins-risoshore · SCENE token: Shells)

Shells from the sand. One book gathers them, another threads them on a string.

FORM: one fan shape with a straight hinge, at most 5 rib lines. 🔴 A SHELL IS INK2 - it can never
  be bare paper, because the sand it lies on IS bare paper and it would disappear. A shell lying on
  WET sand (the ATLANTIC pull) keeps the same INK2 and reads as the lighter thing there instead.
STATES:
  1 ONE SHELL from above, hinge left.
  2 HALF BURIED - only the fan edge showing above the bare paper.
  3 SCATTERED - at most 9 (the cap), no two the same angle, none mirroring a neighbour.
  4 THREADED - a line of them on a cord, the cord INK2, the shells hanging at even spacing; the
    near six or seven separate and the rest carried on as one joined line (a RUN is exempt).

PLATE: the four states at consistent scale, plus one shell large enough to count its ribs.

NOT: 🔴 no yellow anywhere / no character, no hands / no lettering or numerals / no third drum / no
  pearl sheen, wet highlight or sparkle / no gradient, glow or soft edge.
```
