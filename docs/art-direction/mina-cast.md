## 🔴 왜 이 파일이 생겼나 (2026-08-17 신고)

> 「가운데 아이 미나 — 가족들이 너무 비슷하게 생김. (엄마 빼고)」

렌더 넷을 보면 미나·라주·소누가 **같은 그림**이었다. 원인은 앵커가 셋을
`POCKET · GOLD ANKLE BAND · round belly` **작은 표식 하나씩**으로만 갈라 놓은 것이다.
카드 한 장엔 한 명만 나오니 키 계단 `5:4:3` 은 아예 안 보이고, 주머니·발찌는 손톱만 해서
축소하면 사라진다. **엄마만 갈린 이유가 답이다 — 옷이 발끝까지 내려와 윤곽이 통째로 달랐다.**
(그 옷은 규격에 없었다. 모델이 알아서 입힌 것이라 다시 뽑으면 사라진다.)

### 그래서 넷을 **윤곽**으로 갈랐다 — 카드 한 장에서 혼자 있어도 읽힌다

| | 멀리서 본 윤곽 | 머리 : 키 | 눈 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **라주** 맏이 | 어깨가 제일 넓고 아래로 좁아지는 **기둥** · 무릎 위로 걷어붙인 바지라 **정강이가 맨살** | 1 : 5 | 붙고 높다 |
| **미나** 가운데 | 위아래가 같은 폭 · 바지가 **발목 바로 위**까지 내려와 맨살은 발찌 자리뿐 | 1 : 4.5 | 가운데 |
| **소누** 막내 | **배가 몸에서 제일 넓은 종** · 큰 윗옷이 한쪽 어깨에서 흘러내렸다 | 1 : 3 | 벌고 낮다 |
| **엄마** | **어깨부터 바닥까지 끊기지 않는 한 덩어리 — 다리 사이 틈이 없는 유일한 인물** | 1 : 6 | 붙고 높다 |

🔴 **앵커의 축 안에서 갈랐다.** 이 그림체의 캐릭터 축은 「몸엔 윤곽이 없고 점이 몰린 자리가 곧 몸,
정확히 놓이는 것은 눈뿐」이다. 그래서 개체를 가르는 것도 **점이 어디에 몰리는가**(어깨 / 고름 / 배 /
바닥까지)와 **그 한 점을 어디에 찍는가**(눈 간격·높이) 둘뿐이다. 새 축을 만들지 않았다.

🔴 **SCENE 250쪽의 표식은 그대로 살아 있다** — 주머니 86쪽 · 금색 발찌 161쪽 · 동그란 배 82쪽 ·
큰 머리 87쪽. 아래 규격은 그것들을 **지우지 않고 윤곽을 덧댄 것**이라 SCENE 을 한 글자도 안 고친다.
🔴 **귀는 크기만 정하고 방향은 안 정한다** — SCENE 이 귀로 감정을 그린다(「두 귀가 번쩍 섰다」·
「두 귀가 뒤로 눕고」). 방향까지 박으면 250쪽이 안 그려진다.

## Mina elephant

BUILD: middle of three, height 4 on the 5 : 4 : 3 ladder. Head is 1/4.5 of her height.
  Shoulders and hips the SAME width - she is the one with no exaggeration anywhere, and her dots sit
  evenly from top to bottom. Read alone, she is the plain one between a column and a bell.
CLOTH: the short top ends at the hip, and the loose trousers run FULL LENGTH and stop one hand above
  the ankle. So the only bare skin below her top is the ankle itself - the two hard hem edges on her
  body sit LOW (hip, ankle), the opposite of the eldest.
GOLD: one GOLD ankle band on bare skin at that hem, on one ankle only. It is the only solid filled
  shape on any page and it belongs to her. She wears no other gold.
EYES: the two eye-dots sit at the middle setting - neither close nor wide, halfway up the head.
EARS: ears are medium against her head, between the eldest's and the youngest's.

## Raju elephant

BUILD: eldest, tallest, height 5 on the 5 : 4 : 3 ladder. Head is 1/5 of his height - the SMALLEST
  head against the body of the three. His dots crowd at the SHOULDERS, which are the widest part of
  him, and thin downward, so his outline is an upright tapering column with long straight legs and a
  clear gap between them.
CLOTH: the short top ends ABOVE the waist and the loose trousers are ROLLED TWICE ABOVE THE KNEE, the
  roll a band of touching dots. His shins and feet are bare. Both hard hem edges sit HIGH on the body.
  A POCKET on the top - it is a detail that confirms him, never the thing that identifies him.
EYES: the two eye-dots sit CLOSE together and HIGH on the head.
EARS: ears are the smallest against his head of the three children.

## Sonu elephant

BUILD: youngest, height 3 on the 5 : 4 : 3 ladder. Head is 1/3 of his height, so his head is as big
  in absolute size as the eldest's. His dots crowd LOW: the round BELLY is the widest part of the
  whole body and there is no waist, so his outline is a bell standing on two short legs.
CLOTH: the short top is one size TOO BIG - it has slipped off ONE shoulder and hangs to mid-thigh, so
  only a little of the loose trousers shows below it. That fallen shoulder is an outline nobody else
  in the book has. He wears no gold.
EYES: the two eye-dots sit WIDE apart and LOW on the head.
EARS: ears are the largest against his head of the three children.

## Mother elephant

BUILD: the adult. ADULT : ELDEST = 8 : 5. Head is 1/6 of her height, the smallest head against the
  body of anyone in the book.
CLOTH: 🔴 ONE LONG PLAIN CLOTH, wrapped over one shoulder and falling unbroken to the ankles, so her
  dots run in ONE MASS from shoulder to ground and SHE IS THE ONLY FIGURE WITH NO GAP BETWEEN THE
  LEGS. Every child shows two legs and a gap; she never does. The hem stops at the ankle and her feet
  show. The cloth is plain - no pattern, no border, no jewellery, and no gold anywhere on her.
EYES: the two eye-dots sit CLOSE together and HIGH on the head, like the eldest's.
EARS: ears are large but small against her head, the smallest head-to-ear ratio in the book.
