# 붕이네 물 위 장터 — 개체 규격 (cast sheet)

> art-director 산출물 (2026-08-17). 시리즈 11 `bung-woodblock` · 캐스트 4인.
> 🔴 **그림체 SSOT 는 `bung-anchor.md`.** 여기는 **한 사람씩 무엇이 다른가**만 적는다.
> 🔴 빌더(`build-series-html.mjs`)가 `## ` 제목을 `_series-config.mjs` 의 `aliases[1]` 로 찾아
> 인물별 시트 프롬프트에 `[이 인물만의 규격]` 으로 덧쓴다. **제목 문자열을 바꾸면 조용히 안 붙는다.**

### 왜 생겼나 (2026-08-17)

첫 렌더 넷을 나란히 놓으니 **넷이 서로 다른 그림체**였다 — 셋은 전신에 나뭇결이 깔린 나무 인형이고
할아버지만 결이 0에 사람 얼굴, 뿔은 넷이 다 다른 모양, 주둥이는 엄마에게만 있었다. 앵커의
`CHARACTER DESIGN LANGUAGE` 는 **그 세계 전체**를 말하지 한 사람을 말하지 않으므로, 넷이
**이름만 다른 같은 지시**를 받고 각자 빈칸을 메운 결과다. 개체를 가르는 값은 여기 있어야 한다.

🔴 **키·뿔 폭은 이 문서에만 있다.** 앵커에는 `HORN SPAN IS THE AGE` 라는 규칙만 있고 숫자는 없다 —
숫자를 앵커에 넣으면 네 사람 치가 매 쪽에 따라다닌다.

| | 키 (머리 개수) | 뿔 폭 (머리 폭의 배수) | 실루엣을 가르는 것 | 청록 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **엄마** | 5 | **1.5** | 뿔 사이로 두른 머릿수건 | 없음 |
| **할아버지** | 5 (목이 굽어 머리가 더 낮다) | **1.5** (더 길고 가늘고 끝이 아래로) | 동그란 모자 + 굽은 등 | 없음 |
| **붕이** | 3.5 (엄마 허리) | **1** | 목에 걸린 끈 한 줄 | 목끈 |
| **또리** | 2.5 (붕이 허리) | **2/3** | 발목의 방울 | 방울 끈 |

🔴 **뿔은 모양이 아니라 크기만 다르다** — 초승달 모양이 네 사람 다 같아야 「같은 종」으로 읽히고,
폭이 나이를 말한다. 모양까지 갈면 네 마리가 서로 다른 짐승이 된다(첫 렌더가 그랬다).

🔴 **청록 코끈 = 한 줄의 끈이다**(SCENE 214쪽). 주둥이 앞 작은 고리에 매여 뺨을 지나 **목에 걸려
늘어진다** — 대본이 부르는 「목에 청록 코끈」이 그 늘어진 부분이다. 🔴 **주둥이에 구멍을 뚫지 않는다**
(첫 렌더가 네모 구멍에서 굵은 고리를 늘어뜨렸다).

## Bung buffalo

```
BUNG - the older brother, about six. 3.5 heads tall, up to Mom's waist.
HORNS: crescent span = ONE head width. Same crescent as the adults, smaller.
HEAD: dome OVERLAP, muzzle and horns RIVER, one KEY-INK eye disc, one brow bar, one mouth curve.
CLOTHES: short LEAF top ending above the hips, RIVER trousers to the knee, nothing on the head.
🔴 ACCENT: ONE thin TEAL line - tied to a small ring at the front of the muzzle, running back along
  the cheek and HANGING AT THE NECK, where most of its length is seen. Even thickness, never filled,
  never a thick loop, and 🔴 THE MUZZLE IS NOT PIERCED AND HAS NO HOLE IN IT. This is the only teal
  on him. Nothing else he wears or carries is teal.
🔴 SILHOUETTE: a child-sized buffalo whose horn crescents are wider than his shoulders, with one
  thin cord hanging at the neck. That cord and his height are what tell him from the little one.
HANDS: four fingers and a thumb, drawn as one shape, never planks.
```

## Ddori baby buffalo

```
DDORI - the little brother, about two, who barely speaks. 2.5 heads tall, up to Bung's hip - 🔴 THE
  SMALLEST STANDING FIGURE IN THE SERIES, and his head is the biggest piece he has (a toddler's
  proportion: head about 2/5 of his height, where Bung's is about 2/7).
HORNS: crescent span = 2/3 of a head width. 🔴 Still the full crescent shape, out and back - never
  a bud, never a stub, never a bump. Smallest horns in the book, but they are still horns.
HEAD: dome OVERLAP, muzzle and horns RIVER, one KEY-INK eye disc, one brow bar, one mouth curve.
  The muzzle is SHORTER front to back than Bung's but just as wide - that is what makes him a baby.
CLOTHES: short LEAF top, RIVER trousers to the knee, nothing on the head. 🔴 NO NECK CORD.
🔴 ACCENT: a small bell tied at ONE ankle with a TEAL cord. The bell body is RIVER; only the cord is
  teal. It is his only teal and it must be visible in every full-figure drawing, including the back
  view - it is the single clue that finds him in book 13.
🔴 SILHOUETTE: the shortest figure, head-heavy, tiny crescents, and one bell breaking the line at the
  ankle. The bell at the foot is what tells him from Bung at a glance.
HANDS: same hand as Bung, smaller.
```

## Mom buffalo

```
MOM - sells noodles from the boat. Fast hands, few words. 5 heads tall, the tallest head in the book.
HORNS: crescent span = 1.5 head widths, thick at the base. The widest horns Bung sees every day.
HEAD: dome OVERLAP, muzzle and horns RIVER, one KEY-INK eye disc, one brow bar, one mouth curve. The
  muzzle is longer front to back than a child's.
CLOTHES: a loose LEAF top over RIVER trousers, sleeves pushed up above the elbow (she is always
  working), 🔴 A CLOTH WRAPPED OVER THE CROWN AND BETWEEN THE HORNS, tied at the back - LEAF, flat,
  0 folds. The horn crescents come out from under the cloth on both sides.
🔴 ACCENT: NONE. No teal anywhere on her, ever - the teal belongs to the two children only.
🔴 SILHOUETTE: tall, wide crescents, and the wrapped cloth filling the space between the horns. The
  cloth is what tells her from Grandpa, whose hat sits on top instead.
HANDS: same hand shape as the children, larger and wider.
```

## Grandpa buffalo

```
GRANDPA - owns the rowing boat, carries things, mostly shows instead of telling. Old.
BUILD: the same 5 heads of total height as Mom, but 🔴 THE NECK PIECE IS SET FORWARD AND DOWN so his
  head sits lower than hers and his back reads as a stoop. He is never drawn standing straight.
HORNS: crescent span = 1.5 head widths, but the crescents are LONGER, THINNER and their tips DIP
  BELOW the line of the eye - old horns. Same sweep out and back, cut thinner.
HEAD: dome OVERLAP, muzzle and horns RIVER, one KEY-INK eye disc, one brow bar, one mouth curve. The
  longest muzzle in the book.
CLOTHES: a loose LEAF top to mid-thigh with sleeves to the wrist, RIVER trousers, 🔴 A ROUND FLAT-TOPPED
  HAT SITTING ON TOP OF THE HEAD BETWEEN THE HORNS - RIVER, flat, 0 folds, 0 brim decoration.
🔴 ACCENT: NONE. No teal anywhere on him, ever.
🔴 SILHOUETTE: a stooped figure with long thin down-dipping crescents and a round hat between them.
  The stoop plus the hat is what tells him from Mom at any distance.
HANDS: same hand shape, with thicker knuckles - SCENE calls them 「마디 굵은 손」.
```

### 🔴 이 문서를 고칠 때

1. **제목 문자열 = `_series-config.mjs` `cast[].aliases[1]`** (`Bung buffalo` · `Ddori baby buffalo` ·
   `Mom buffalo` · `Grandpa buffalo`). 안 맞으면 빌더가 조용히 빈 문자열을 넣는다.
2. **그림체 규칙(결·판·눈·어긋남)은 여기 쓰지 마라** — 앵커가 SSOT 이고 여기 쓰면 두 곳이 갈라진다.
   여기 쓰는 것은 **숫자·의상·악센트 자리·실루엣** 넷뿐이다.
3. 🔴 **키·뿔 폭을 고쳤으면 `bung-anchor.md` 의 `CHARACTER SHEET` 줄 세우기도 같이 본다**(단체 시트가
   그 비례를 다시 말한다).
