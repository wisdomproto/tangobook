## G-08 §1. 앵커 배정

**권**: `g08` 「내 그림자가 따라와」 (12쪽 · 4~6세 · 주제군 **G 용기·두려움** · 엔진 **소원의 대가** · 무대 스페인 흰 마을 골목 하나 · 주인공 새끼 당나귀 + 새끼 염소)

**한 줄**: 차가운 흰 종이(= 석회 회벽) 위, 🔴 **손가락으로 문질러 만든 목탄 덩어리가 그림자이고, 그것 하나만 지울 수 있다.** 앵커 슬러그 `changjak-drypaper` — **e01 과 공유**.

**🔴 e01 과의 공유 조건 — 공정이 같고 방향이 반대다.**

| | e01 「빵집에서 재채기하면」 | **g08 (이 권)** |
|---|---|---|
| 방향 | **THE UNDRAWN AREA GROWS** — 안 그린 데가 그린 데를 먹는다 | 🔴 **THE RUBBED MARK IS TAKEN AWAY** — 문질러 만든 그것 하나가 지워진다 |
| 변수 | **흰 것의 면적**(한 줌 → 몸통 → 방 전체) | 🔴 **검은 덩어리 하나의 유무와 길이**(작음 → 없음 → 아주 큼) |
| 안 그린 종이 = | 밀가루, **자란다** | 석회 회벽, 🔴 **열두 쪽 내내 같은 양** |
| 손 | 굵고 뭉툭한 콩테 획 하나 | 🔴 **획과 문지른 덩어리 둘이 갈라져 있다** |
| 종이 | 따뜻한 크림 #EFE7D6 | 🔴 **차가운 흰 #F6F6F4** |
| 악센트 | 황토 반죽(안 덮이는 것) | 🔴 **레몬 노랑 두 알** |

🔴 **첫 렌더에서 즉시 판정되는 축 = 「획과 문지른 것이 갈라져 보이나」.** e01 은 화면의 모든 것이 같은 손(굵은 콩테 획)이고, g08 은 **사물은 획이고 그림자는 획이 하나도 없는 매끈한 가루 덩어리**다. 획은 지울 수 없고 가루는 지울 수 있다 — 그래서 「그늘에 들어가면 없어지는 것」이 그림자 하나뿐인 것이 매체의 사실이 된다.

**이 권이 그림에 요구하는 것** 셋.

1. 🔴 **그림자가 화면에서 유일하게 어두운 것이어야 한다.** 회벽이 **안 그린 종이**라 화면이 통째로 눈부시고, 사물의 획을 **중간 회색**으로 두면 새까만 것은 문지른 덩어리 하나뿐이 된다. 팔레트가 「발밑 얼룩 하나에 눈이 곧장 붙는다」(대본 p1 톤)를 대신 짓는다.
2. 🔴 **「대가」가 매체 안에 있다.** 그림자를 없애려면 **가루를 한 자리에 뭉치는 대신 화면 전체에 아주 옅게 고르게 펴야** 한다(= 아치 밑 그늘). 그러면 화면에 값이 하나뿐이라 눈이 갈 데가 없어진다. 🔴 **같은 재료인데 뭉치면 그림자이고 고르게 펴면 그늘이다** — p7 의 무서움이 설명 없이 그림에서 나온다.
3. 🔴 **지운 자리가 「지운 것」으로 보여야 한다.** p4 는 앞발 하나만, p6 은 통째로 지운다. 지우개로 걷어낸 자리에는 가루가 조금 남고 가장자리가 부슬거려서, **없어진 것이 원래 있던 자리**가 화면에 남는다.

**🔴 C1 넷 분리 4축 표 (§7.2 — C1 은 가장 위험한 클러스터다)**

| | 종이색 | 얼굴 | 악센트 | 매체 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| b01 | 크라프트 갈색 = 상자 | 그린 눈 + 별개 눈썹선 | 빨간 테이프 한 줄 | 흑연 선 + 얇은 평워시 |
| d15 | 흰 + 거친 크라프트 두 장 | 그린 아몬드 눈 + 눈꺼풀선 | 새빨간 실 한 오라기 | 흙 물감 + 색연필 |
| e01 | 따뜻한 크림 = 밀가루 | 작은 눈 + 별개 눈썹 | 황토(안 덮이는 것) | 굵은 콩테 가루, **덮는다** |
| 🔴 **g08** | 🔴 **차가운 흰 = 석회 회벽** | **긴 속눈썹 없는 큰 눈 + 별개 눈썹**, 긴 귀가 연기한다 | 🔴 **레몬 노랑** | 🔴 **획 + 문지른 가루, 지운다** |

- 🔴 **종이가 무엇을 뜻하나**(§7.24 부수의 다섯째 줄): 점눈이의 크림은 **햇빛**, e01 의 크림은 **밀가루**, 여기 흰 것은 **석회 회벽**이다. 셋 다 다르다.
- 🔴 **빨강이 한 점도 없다.** 대본이 b04 와의 분리에서 빨간 제라늄을 빼고 **노란 레몬**으로 갈아 두었고(note ③), 그것이 그대로 이 권의 악센트다.

**🔴 b04 와의 분리 — 같은 스페인 흰 마을이라 이 권의 최대 위험이다.** 대본 note 가 여섯 축으로 이미 갈라 뒀고(주인공 종 · 인물 수 · 소품 · 그림자의 역할 · 착지 · 크기 문법), 그림에서 굳힌 것은 셋이다: ① b04 = **하드에지 불투명 통짜 도형**(음영 0, C4) ↔ 여기는 **문질러 만든 가루 덩어리**(가장자리가 부슬거린다) ② b04 의 그림자는 **앞장서서 움직인다** ↔ 여기 그림자는 🔴 **한 번도 저 혼자 안 움직인다** ③ b04 는 빨간 제라늄 ↔ 여기는 **노란 레몬**.

**🔴 라인 충돌**: 호리 니들펠트 ✕(가루 매체, 섬유·바늘땀·보풀 0 — NOT 절 명시) · 점눈이 ✕(위 4축 표).

**🔴 대본 SCENE 처방 3건** — 대본은 고치지 않고 컷에서 분기한다(§2.16.4 표: 안 칠한 종이 + 가루 → **획 수를 줄인다, 워시 금지**).

## G-08 §2. STYLE ANCHOR

```
STYLE ANCHOR - changjak-drypaper / g08   (a donkey foal and a kid goat / a whitewashed alley)

Style: charcoal and conte DUST on COLD WHITE paper #F6F6F4, ages 4-6. 🔴 THE PAPER IS THE
  LIMEWASHED WALL: the walls of this alley are never drawn on at all.

RENDERING: 🔴 THE DIRECTION OF THIS VOLUME IS THE RUBBED MARK BEING TAKEN AWAY. There are exactly
  two hands in this book. 1) STROKES - broad, blunt, crumbly conte in MID GREY #6A6A70 for
  everything that is a thing: bodies, the door, the pot, the basket, the cobbles. A stroke cannot
  be lifted; it stays. 2) 🔴 ONE RUBBED MASS - dust smeared flat with a fingertip into near-black
  #1E1E22, WITH NO STROKE IN IT ANYWHERE, and this is the only thing in the book that is a shadow.
  🔴 IT IS ALSO THE ONLY THING THAT CAN BE LIFTED: a kneaded rubber takes it back to bare paper,
  leaving a few loose grains and a crumbly rim where it was.
  🔴 SHADE IS THE SAME DUST SPREAD THIN AND EVEN OVER A WHOLE FRAME - one value edge to edge, and
  🔴 INSIDE IT THERE IS NOT ONE RUBBED MASS ANYWHERE. Rubbed together = shadow; spread out = shade.
  FINISHED THINGS PER PAGE = 2 (the donkey + the one thing it is doing). DENSITY RATION = none.
  Counts: wall = 0 marks (0 plaster cracks, 0 stone joints, 0 tiles) - cobbles = at most 11 short
  arcs - door = 3 plank strokes - lemon tree = 5 leaf strokes - roof line = 1 stroke - fur = 0
  hairs. Push a thing back by MAKING FEWER STROKES, never by blurring, fading or softening it.

PALETTE: paper #F6F6F4, and every white wall in this book IS this bare paper - stroke grey #6A6A70
  - 🔴 SHADOW BLACK #1E1E22, rubbed, and it is THE ONLY DARK THING IN THE BOOK - shade veil
  #B9B6AE, thin and even, arch passage only - door brown #7A5230 - donkey #8E8880 - goat #DCD6C8.
  🔴 ACCENT: LEMON YELLOW #E8C33A, two lemons on one potted tree, and 🔴 IT IS THE ONLY COLOUR IN
  THE BOOK. No red, pink, blue sky, green shutters, terracotta roofs or geraniums. On the last
  pages the light goes low but 🔴 NOTHING TURNS ORANGE OR GOLD - the sun is shown by where the
  shadows point, never by tinting the paper.

CHARACTER DESIGN LANGUAGE: animal faces, no clothes, no shoes. A large drawn eye with one dark
  pupil and a SEPARATE brow stroke above it; no dot-eyes, no catchlight. FIXED GRADE: both go on
  four legs and lift ONE foreleg only when a forepaw works; neither stands or points like a person.
  🔴 THE DONKEY'S TWO LONG EARS AND THE GOAT'S TWO SHORT HORNS ARE THE WHOLE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN
  THEIR SHADOWS - make both readable in silhouette at thumbnail size.

CANVAS: 16:9 double-page spread. One narrow alley, never left, its props never moving. Bottom 18%
  quiet for a caption. No lettering, numerals, house numbers or signs.

NOT: no digital slickness of any kind - airbrush, gradient, glow, 3D CG, cel-shading, photographic,
  or a paper-texture filter over flat tone / 🔴 NO WHITE PAINT, WHITE CHALK OR HIGHLIGHT - if a
  thing is white it is bare paper / 🔴 the shadow never leaves the ground it belongs to, never
  stands up, never leads, never has a face / nothing blurred or out of focus - a rubbed edge is
  powder, and no felt, wool, stitching or sculpted clay anywhere.
```

## G-08 §3. 캐릭터 시트 (🔴 ShadowPlate 를 가장 먼저)

```
MATERIAL SHEET - ShadowPlate   (bake FIRST - this book is these four states of one dust)

A test board, no characters. Charcoal and conte dust on cold white paper #F6F6F4.

A · 🔴 TWO HANDS SIDE BY SIDE: one clay pot drawn with broad blunt conte STROKES in mid grey
  #6A6A70 - every stroke's start and end visible - and beside it the pot's SHADOW, dust rubbed flat
  with a fingertip into near-black #1E1E22 with 🔴 NOT ONE STROKE INSIDE IT. The mass is much
  darker than any stroke on the page.
B · 🔴 LIFTED: the same pair, with the shadow taken out with a kneaded rubber back to BARE PAPER -
  and 🔴 the place it was is still findable: a few loose grains and a crumbly rim. The pot's
  strokes are completely untouched, because strokes cannot be lifted. Put A and B side by side.
C · 🔴 PART-LIFTED: one animal's four feet, three with a small rubbed mass under them and one with
  its mass lifted away. One body, two states, in one picture.
D · 🔴 SHADE, WHICH IS NOT SHADOW: a panel with the same dust SPREAD THIN AND EVEN across the whole
  panel, one value edge to edge. Inside it, draw the same pot in strokes - 🔴 AND GIVE IT NO
  SHADOW AT ALL. The panel is darker than bare paper and yet has nothing dark in it. Set this panel
  right beside A so a child can see: rubbed together = shadow, spread out = shade.
E · 🔴 LONG: one mass pushed out with a fingertip into a long tapering streak - dense where it
  starts, 🔴 running out into scattered loose grains at the far end, never into a soft gradient.
  Show it once on flat ground and once bending up a vertical wall at a hard crease.
No characters, no lettering. SCENE token: ShadowPlate.
```

```

Same make. The alley and its props never change in this book.

  be touched at once. 🔴 BOTH WALLS ARE BARE PAPER - 0 marks on them: no cracks, no stones, no
  tiles, no windows. Underfoot, worn round cobbles (at most 11 short arcs, never every cobble). On
  the LEFT wall, one low brown door #7A5230 (3 plank strokes) and beside it on the ground a POTTED
  LEMON TREE with 🔴 TWO YELLOW LEMONS #E8C33A, the only colour in the book. On the RIGHT wall, a
  straw basket on a nail and a water pot below it. 🔴 AT THE INNER END, A LOW ARCH ROOFS THE ALLEY
  OVER, and everything under it is in shade. Above, one strip of sky between the roofs - 🔴 drawn
  as bare paper, never blue.
🔴 THREE LIGHT STATES, drawn in a row: (i) NOON - the sun straight overhead, every prop's shadow a
  small mass pooled directly under it, and 🔴 ONE STRIP OF SHADE ONLY A HAND'S WIDTH WIDE along the

  even veil #B9B6AE, 🔴 with not one rubbed mass anywhere in it, and a hard straight line on the
  ground where the veil begins; (iii) LATE - the sun low in the alley's mouth, every prop's shadow
  pushed out into a long streak, 🔴 ALL OF THEM POINTING THE SAME WAY, and the ones that reach the
  far wall bending up it at a hard crease.
0 lettering, 0 numerals, no animals. SCENE token: AlleySet.
```

```
CHARACTER SHEET - DonkeyFoal   (bake THIRD)

Same make: broad blunt conte strokes in mid grey #6A6A70 on cold white paper. Plain paper behind.

BODY: a young donkey, soft grey #8E8880 built from 6 to 8 broad strokes, 0 hairs drawn. 🔴 TWO VERY

  no lashes. 🔴 THE EARS DO THE ACTING - draw four states large and side by side: laid flat back
  along the neck (afraid) / pricked straight up and forward (interested) / spread out sideways and
  drooping (lost) / 🔴 STRAINED UP AS HIGH AS THEY GO, the tips almost touching (the last page).
🔴 THE SHADOW PLATE: the same standing figure four times, with its own shadow shown as a RUBBED
  MASS each time - (1) a small round mass pooled right under it at noon, one contact point per

  ANYWHERE, the ground round all four hooves bare paper; (4) the mass pushed out behind it into a
  long streak several body-lengths long, 🔴 with the two long ears unmistakable at its far end.
GRADE: four legs; it lifts one foreleg only when using a forepaw, and 🔴 it never rears, never
  walks upright and never puts a hoof over its eyes like a person.
SHEET: backing away stiff-legged, head twisted down to look under itself / running flat out, both
  forelegs reaching / jumping with all four feet gathered and the belly toward us / neck stretched
  right out to push its nose past a line on the ground / body hunched small, legs drawn in under
  it, ears flat, tail clamped / bursting forward with the ears up and the mouth wide open.
SCENE token: DonkeyFoal.
```

