## 🔴 갈라 놓은 축 = 실루엣 (미나 사고에서 배운 것)

「가족들이 너무 비슷하게 생김」 신고의 원인은 셋을 **작은 표식 하나씩**으로만 갈라 놓은 것이었다.
카드 한 장엔 한 명만 나오니 키 계단은 아예 안 보이고, 주머니·발찌는 축소하면 사라진다.
**엄마만 갈린 이유가 답이다 — 옷이 윤곽을 통째로 바꿨다.**

> **판정 = 통째로 까맣게 칠해도 셋이 다른 모양인가.** 아래 값은 전부 그 줄에서 읽힌다.

| | 멀리서 본 윤곽 | 머리 : 키 | 가장 넓은 곳 | 발밑 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **코타** 아이 | 몸통 양쪽으로 **수건 끝 두 자락**이 늘어져 튄다 · **정강이 둘이 맨살** | 1 : 3.5 | 어깨 | 🔴 **발바닥과 땅 사이에 굽 두 개 만큼 틈**이 있는 유일한 인물 |
| **아빠** 어른 | **어깨부터 종아리 중간까지 끊기지 않는 사다리꼴** · 정강이가 한 번도 안 보인다 · 소맷단이 손끝을 지나 늘어진다 | 1 : 6 | **허리**(접은 수건이 가로 막대로 윤곽을 벌린다) | 땅에 붙는다 |
| **손님** 매권 | 목·허리에 **천이 하나도 없다** · 대신 윤곽 밖으로 **든 것 하나**가 튀어나온다 | 종마다 | 종마다 | 종마다 |

🔴 **어른을 크기로 가르지 않았다.** 여섯 시리즈가 「다 같은 방식으로 짓는다」류 문장 때문에 아이와
어른 사이에 크기만 남았고, 유키는 두 여우를 가르는 값이 「가장 작다」 하나였는데 **시트엔 인물이
하나뿐이라 비교 대상이 없어 판정 자체가 불가능**했다. 그래서 아래 값은 전부 **혼자 있어도 잴 수
있는 것**이다 — 정강이가 보이나 · 소맷단이 손을 지나나 · 발밑에 틈이 있나 · 가장 넓은 곳이 어디냐.

🔴 **앵커의 축 안에서 갈랐다.** 이 그림체의 캐릭터 축은 「무늬가 정체를 말한다」이므로, 개체를 가르는
것도 **걸친 천의 자리**(목 / 허리 / 없음)와 **먹 무늬 세 개의 굵기**뿐이다. 새 축을 안 만들었다.

🔴 **분홍은 코타 수건 하나다.** 아빠 수건도 여관 수건도 전부 흰 무지 — 그래서 07권에서 코타가 제
수건을 손님 아이에게 건네면 **화면의 유일한 분홍이 손에서 손으로 옮겨 간다**(설계 §1).

## Kota tanuki

BUILD: the child, head 1/3.5 of his height, reaching the father's hip. Shoulders are his widest
  point and the body tapers a little downward. 🔴 HIS ROBE STOPS ABOVE THE KNEE SO BOTH SHINS ARE
  BARE - he is one of only two figures in the book with bare shins, and the guest is the other only
  when the guest happens to be a child.
CLOTH: a short lined robe in OCHRE, plain, no pattern, sleeves ending at the forearm so his hands
  are always clear of cloth. 🔴 A PEACH PINK #E9A6A0 TOWEL ROUND HIS NECK WITH BOTH ENDS HANGING
  LOOSE DOWN HIS CHEST - those two hanging ends are part of his outline on every page and they swing
  when he moves. It is the only pink anywhere in twenty-five books.
FEET: 🔴 RAISED WOODEN SANDALS - two blocks under each sole, so THERE IS A GAP OF DAYLIGHT BETWEEN
  HIS FOOT AND THE GROUND. Nobody else in the book is lifted off the ground. Read alone and filled
  solid in ink, that gap and the two towel ends are what say this is the child.
MARKING: the ink band across both eyes is the THINNEST of the raccoon dogs and sits high, close
  under the brow, with its two ends stopping short of the cheek. Dark forehands stop at the wrist.
  Tail rings: three.

## Dad tanuki

BUILD: the adult, head 1/6 of his height. 🔴 HIS WIDEST POINT IS THE WAIST, NOT THE SHOULDERS - the
  folded towel at his hip pushes the outline out sideways there, so filled solid in ink he is a
  wide-hipped trapezoid where the child is shoulder-heavy.
CLOTH: a lined robe in deep AZURE #3B5A72, plain, closing at MID-CALF so 🔴 NO SHIN OF HIS IS EVER
  SEEN, in any pose, on any page. Its sleeves hang PAST HIS HANDS when his arms are down, so with
  the arms lowered he has no hands in the outline at all - that is his second read. A WHITE folded
  towel tucked at the waist, shell white and plain like every towel in this inn. No pink on him.
FEET: flat straw sandals, sole on the ground, no lift and no gap.
MARKING: the ink band across both eyes is the THICKEST and BROADEST of the raccoon dogs and runs
  wider than his face, its two ends reaching past the outer edge of each cheek. Dark forehands run
  up past the wrist. Tail rings: five.

## Guest

BUILD: a different animal every book and never named - crane, boar, turtle, owl, hippo, frog, heron,
  swan, ox, raccoon dog. Bipedal, upright, wearing cloth, with hands, the same grade as everyone.
  Size is whatever the species is; size is never what identifies a guest.
🔴 CLOTH - THIS IS THE RULE THAT MAKES A GUEST A GUEST: A GUEST WEARS NO CLOTH AT THE NECK AND NONE
  AT THE WAIST. The two who live at this inn wear cloth that is not clothing (the child's towel at
  the neck, the father's folded towel at the waist); a guest's neck and waist are bare of it. The
  robe itself is one plain muted field in GREEN #6E8A6A, OCHRE #B98F52 or BURNT EARTH #8A4E3C - 🔴
  never azure, which belongs to the father, and never pink, which belongs to the child.
🔴 CARRIED - ONE TRAVELLING THING ON THE OUTLINE, and exactly one: a bundle on the back, a wide flat
  hat, a walking stick, or a basket. It sticks out past the body so it is there in the silhouette.
  When the guest has set it down, the guest is then the only figure on the page with NOTHING at the
  neck, NOTHING at the waist and NOTHING in the hands. A house towel lent to a guest is HELD or laid
  over an arm - never tied at the neck and never tucked at the waist.
MARKING: 🔴 NO EYE BAND - that belongs to the raccoon dogs of this inn. Instead ONE flat ink shape
  of the species' own, laid last on a finished coat: a crane's crown patch, a boar's snout stripe,
  an owl's facial disc rim, an ox's muzzle, a turtle's brow-to-neck stripe. 🔴 IT IS ALWAYS ON THE
  FACE - never on a shell, a wing, a tail or the robe, because ITS ANGLE AND ITS LENGTH ARE THE
  WHOLE EXPRESSION OF THAT PAGE (straight = unmoved, its far end curling up = pleased). A guest
  whose shape sits anywhere but the face has no face left to feel with, and the feeling then leaks
  into the eyes - which never change size for anybody in this book.
  INK ONLY, never colour, and never more than one shape.
  Eyes are the same fixed shell-white opening with an ink centre that everyone in this book has.
REPEAT VISITS: five species come twice. 🔴 A RETURNING GUEST IS DRAWN EXACTLY AS BEFORE, down to the
  one carried thing, because the book calls them only "the one from last time" and the picture is
  the only place that says so.

### 이 문서를 고칠 때

- 여기 쓰는 것은 **숫자(키·비례)·의상·악센트 자리·실루엣** 넷뿐이다. 그림체 규칙은 앵커가 SSOT.
- 🔴 **손님 종은 열 가지가 돌고 다섯이 두 번 온다**(설계 §1). 권별 종·재방문 짝은 설계서가 SSOT라
  여기 옮겨 적지 않는다 — 두 곳에 손으로 적으면 갈라진다.
- 🔴 **`## Guest` 절은 종을 안 정한다.** 권마다 다른 종에 얹히는 **일반 규격**이고, 회차 시트를 구울
  때 그 권의 종 한 줄(`Guest crane`)만 앞에 붙인다.
