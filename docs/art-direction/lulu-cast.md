# lulu — 캐스트 규격

> 시리즈 10 「룰루네 올리브 언덕」. 앵커 = [lulu-anchor.md](lulu-anchor.md) · 설계 = [_design.md](../changjak-books/lulu/_design.md)
> 🔴 **`##` 제목은 별칭 그대로** — `build-series-html.mjs` 가 이 문자열(`aliases[1]`)로 찾아 시트 프롬프트에 넣는다.
> 🔴 **`##` 아래에 쓴 것은 전부 프롬프트로 나간다.** 해설은 이 머리말에만 쓴다.
> 🔴 **각 규격은 자족적이어야 한다** — 시트는 한 사람만 받으므로 키 사다리를 다섯 곳에 다 적는다.
>
> **가르는 축은 앵커의 축 하나 — 「겹침」이다.** 앵커가 이미 답을 적어 두었다:
> *"a donkey inside that mass is read only by **what sticks out of it**."*
> 그래서 다섯을 가른 것은 ① **몸에서 제일 넓은 자리** ② **덩어리에서 삐져나오는 부분** 둘뿐이다.
> 🔴 둘 다 **혼자 있어도 윤곽으로 읽히고, 겹쳐 있으면 더 잘 읽힌다** — 축과 안 부딪힌다.
>
> | | 제일 넓은 자리 | 덩어리에서 삐져나오는 것 | 덩어리의 어느 높이에서 읽히나 |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | **룰루** | **머리**(몸보다 넓다) | 제 머리보다 긴 **두 귀** | 맨 위 |
> | **엄마** | **어깨** — 아래로 같은 폭 | **곧은 수평 어깨선** (혼자 직선) | 어깨 높이 |
> | **니노** | **가슴** — 그런데 폭 자체는 제일 좁다 | **뒤로 젖힌 머리**(제일 높다) + **팔과 옆구리 사이 빈 종이 둘** | 덩어리 위 |
> | **로사** | **치맛단**(바닥) | **옆으로 뻗는 치맛자락 쐐기** | 바닥 |
> | **큰아버지** | **배**(몸 한가운데) | **하나의 큰 부풀음** | 허리 높이 |
>
> ## 🔴 렌더 실물에서 본 것 (`comic-assets/lulu-plan/*.jpg` 다섯 장)
>
> 다른 시리즈만큼 똑같지는 않았다 — 로사(종 모양)·큰아버지(통)는 이미 갈려 있다. **문제는 다른 데 있었다.**
> ① **니노와 엄마가 같은 몸**이다. 앵커가 니노를 「tall and narrow」라 했는데 렌더의 니노는
> 엄마와 같은 폭에 젖힘도 없다 — **모자를 빼면 앞치마 대신 조끼 입은 엄마**다.
> ② 🔴 **갈라 놓은 것이 전부 떼어낼 수 있는 물건이다.** 그리고 SCENE 이 그것을 실제로 뗀다 —
> 01 p7 밀짚모자가 가지에 걸려 **p10 까지 안 내려오고**, 04 p7 은 바닥에 떨어져 있고,
> 03 p8 은 지팡이가 손에서 자루 위로 옮겨 가 **끝까지 안 돌아온다**.
> ③ 250쪽 실측 — **니노 등장 56쪽 중 모자가 적힌 쪽 23(41%) · 큰아버지 61쪽 중 지팡이 19(31%) ·
> 로사 58쪽 중 치마·앞치마 12(20%)**. 즉 **셋의 절반~80%는 몸만으로 읽어야 하는데 몸이 규격에 없었다.**
> (룰루만 **220/220쪽 100%** 파란 방울 목끈 — 그래서 룰루는 원래 안 흔들린다.)
>
> 🔴 **SCENE 은 근거를 딱 한 줄 갖고 있다** — 15 p1 「니노는 **좁고 긴 몸**… 베포는 **두꺼운 몸**이
> 그보다 한 뼘 넓게 퍼졌으며」. 아래 규격은 그 한 줄을 다섯으로 늘린 것이라 SCENE 을 한 글자도 안 고친다.
> 🔴 **귀는 길이만 정하고 방향은 안 정한다** — SCENE 이 귀로 감정을 그린다(14 p7 「룰루의 두 귀만 위로」·
> 22 p6 「귀가 옆으로 눕는다」). 방향까지 박으면 250쪽이 안 그려진다.
> 🔴 **엄마의 팔은 안 묶는다** — 엄마 22쪽이 전부 밀대·냄비·칼·대야로 **두 앞발을 쓰는 일**이다.

## Lulu donkey

BUILD: the smallest standing figure. On the family ladder LULU 1.00 / ROSA 1.55 / MAMA 1.65 /
  BEPPO 1.85 / NINO 1.95 she is 1.00. Head is 1/3.4 of her height and 🔴 THE HEAD IS THE WIDEST PART
  OF HER - wider than her shoulders and wider than her hips - so her outline is a big round head on a
  short straight tube with no waist and short legs. No adult in this book is ever built that way.
EARS: her two ears are LONGER THAN HER HEAD IS HIGH and narrow - the longest against the head of
  anyone. 🔴 THEIR DIRECTION IS NOT FIXED: pages turn them up, sideways and back to carry feeling.
  Only the LENGTH is fixed, so whichever way they lie they are the part of her that comes out of the
  top of a shared mass.
CLOTH: one plain sleeved suit in DRY EARTH. No apron, no hat, no pocket, no tool - 🔴 nothing about
  her can be put down or left behind.
BLUE: a BLUE cord with ONE BLUE BELL at the throat. It is the only blue in the world and she wears it
  on every page.

## Mama donkey

BUILD: the RECTANGLE among curves. On the family ladder LULU 1.00 / ROSA 1.55 / MAMA 1.65 /
  BEPPO 1.85 / NINO 1.95 she is 1.65 - a full-grown adult, not a big child. Head is 1/4.5 of her
  height and never the widest part of her. 🔴 HER SHOULDERS ARE THE WIDEST PART AND THEY ARE A STRAIGHT
  HORIZONTAL LINE - the only flat top in the family - and the body drops from them at THE SAME WIDTH
  to the hem: no waist, no flare, no lean. Standing, she is plumb vertical.
APRON: a BIB APRON in OLIVE LEAF from the chest to below the knee as ONE FLAT PANEL with STRAIGHT
  VERTICAL SIDES and NO POCKET, over sleeves and trousers in DRY EARTH. Her front edge is therefore a
  straight line from chest to knee - 🔴 nobody else in this book has a straight edge on the body.
WORKING: she is the one bent over the work. When she rolls, lifts, pours or cuts she bends AT THE
  WAIST while her legs stay vertical, and her back becomes a FLAT HORIZONTAL SHELF at shoulder height.
  🔴 Both forelimbs work freely in front of her - her arms are never held in a fixed pose.
EARS: shorter than her head is high, set wide.

## Nino donkey

BUILD: THE TALLEST AND THE NARROWEST. On the family ladder LULU 1.00 / ROSA 1.55 / MAMA 1.65 /
  BEPPO 1.85 / NINO 1.95 he is 1.95 - his head goes higher than anyone's. Head is 1/5 of his height,
  and his widest point, THE CHEST, is only 1.1 of his own head widths, so the whole standing figure is
  a tall thin band. 🔴 He is HALF the width of Beppo and clearly narrower than Mama.
THE BACKWARD LEAN: his chest is pushed FORWARD and his shoulders and head go BACK, so the line from
  hoof to crown is a shallow BACKWARD C and his chin rides above everyone. In a pile of bodies HIS
  HEAD IS THE HIGHEST THING AND IT IS TIPPED BACK - that is how he is found, hat or no hat.
THE TWO HOLES: his arms are held AWAY from his ribs - hands in pockets, thumbs in the waistcoat, or
  hands on hips - leaving a WEDGE OF BARE PAPER between each arm and his body. 🔴 He is the only
  figure in this book with holes wiped through the middle of his silhouette.
HAT: a broad STRAW HAT in DRY EARTH. 🔴 IT COMES OFF - one volume leaves it in a tree for four spreads
  and another drops it on the ground. It confirms him; it never identifies him.
EARS: shorter than his head is high, narrow, and they follow the tilt of the head.

## Rosa donkey

BUILD: the roundest adult and the SHORTEST adult. On the family ladder LULU 1.00 / ROSA 1.55 /
  MAMA 1.65 / BEPPO 1.85 / NINO 1.95 she is 1.55. Head is 1/4 of her height. 🔴 THE WIDEST PART OF HER
  IS AT THE FLOOR: the skirt hem is 2.4 of her own head widths, wider than her shoulders, so her whole
  outline is a TRIANGLE STANDING ON ITS BASE.
NO GAP: the skirt reaches the hooves and 🔴 SHE IS THE ONLY FIGURE IN THE BOOK WITH NO OPEN PAPER
  BETWEEN THE LEGS - every other donkey shows two legs and a gap; she never does.
THE SWINGING HEM: she is always mid-stride, and the hem swings out to one side and trails behind, so a
  WEDGE OF SKIRT sticks out AT FLOOR LEVEL from any mass she is part of. 🔴 She is read at the bottom
  of a pile, where nobody else is read.
APRON: a WAIST apron in OLIVE LEAF, from the waist down only, flaring with the skirt, with ONE DEEP
  POCKET. It confirms her; it never identifies her.
EARS: shorter than her head is high, set close together.

## 큰아버지

BUILD: THE WIDEST THING IN THE BOOK. On the family ladder LULU 1.00 / ROSA 1.55 / MAMA 1.65 /
  BEPPO 1.85 / NINO 1.95 he is 1.85 - 🔴 he is NOT the tallest, Nino's head goes higher, but he is the
  biggest because he is TWICE ANYONE ELSE'S WIDTH. Head is 1/5.5 of his height, the smallest head
  against the body of anyone, and it sits on top of the barrel looking small on it.
THE BARREL: shoulders, chest and belly are ONE UNBROKEN CURVE and the widest point is THE BELLY, at
  the middle of his height, 2.6 of his own head widths. Legs are short and thick under it, with a gap
  between them. From behind he is one round mass. 🔴 In a pile of bodies HE IS THE BULGE - the shared
  silhouette swells out at waist height wherever he is, and that swelling is him.
CLOTH: a WAISTCOAT in OLIVE LEAF that stops ABOVE the belly, so the DRY EARTH belly is the widest
  thing showing, over sleeves and trousers.
STICK: a CROOKED STICK in DRY EARTH. 🔴 IT LEAVES HIS HAND - it rides on the sacks for half of one
  volume and leans on a chair in another. It confirms him; it never identifies him.
EARS: shorter than his head is high, thick, set wide.
