# pipo — 캐스트 규격

> 🔴 **앵커(`pipo-anchor.md`)는 이 세계 전체를 말하고, 이 파일은 한 사람을 말한다.**
> 빌더(`build-series-html.mjs`)가 `##` 제목을 **영문 별칭(`aliases[1]`)** 으로 찾아 그 사람 시트
> 프롬프트에 `[이 인물만의 규격]` 으로 끼운다 — 제목 문자열을 바꾸면 조용히 안 들어간다.
> 이 시리즈의 `aliases[1]` 은 `Pipo puppy` · `Mom dog` · `할아버지` · `아줌마` · `아저씨` 다.

## 🔴 가르는 축 = **자세(stance)** — 발명이 아니라 앵커가 흘린 것을 되찾은 것이다

앵커의 출처인 `butler-borders`(Kate Greenaway 숏리스트 2022)의 캐릭터 조항은 셋을 든다 —
**「짐(load) · 자세(stance) · 발 위치(feet)」**. 그런데 앵커는 그중 **둘만** 가져왔다:

```
THE ONLY THINGS THAT SAY WHO A FIGURE IS ARE WHAT ITS HANDS HOLD AND WHERE ITS FEET ARE PUT
```

**자세가 빠졌다.** 그리고 빠진 그 하나가 하필 **유일하게 살아남는 축**이다:

| 축 | 시트 카드(마젠타·바닥 없음) | 23권(소품이 서로에게 넘어간다) | 13 p6·p7 · 25 p6·p7(셋이 엉켜 누움) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 발 위치 | 🔴 **원리상 없음** | 있음 | 있음 |
| 짐 | 있음 | 🔴 **뒤바뀜** | 🔴 **지팡이가 셋 위에 가로놓임** |
| **자세** | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |

그리고 자세는 이 시리즈에서 **각 어른의 결점 그 자체**다(`_design.md` §1) — 할아버지=앞장서는
옛날 방식 · 아줌마=목을 빼고 다 본다 · 아저씨=서두름. 몸이 접히는 방식이 곧 그 사람이 틀리는
방식이라, 그림체와 내용이 같은 것을 가리킨다.

| | 몸 | 자세 | 까맣게 칠했을 때 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **피포** | 짧고 두툼한 통 하나, **머리가 몸의 1/3** | **내려간다** — 무릎을 접고 두 손이 가슴 앞으로 | 낮고 뭉툭한 덩어리, 늘 웅크림 |
| **엄마** | 길고 가늘다, **몸 한가운데 허리** | **허리에서 접힌다** — 팔은 늘 아래로 늘어짐 | 긴 선 하나가 가운데서 한 번 꺾임 |
| **할아버지** | **목 없는 달걀**, 머리는 몸에 비해 작다 | **뒤로 젖히고 앞장선다** | 다리 짧은 달걀 |
| **아줌마** | **목이 몸 길이만큼**, 몸은 셋 중 제일 작다 | **몸은 붙박이고 목만 겨눈다** | 어깨에서 한 번 꺾이는 긴 목 |
| **아저씨** | **어깨가 이 책에서 제일 넓다** | **이미 움직이는 중** — 무게가 앞으로 쏠림 | 앞으로 기운 넓은 덩어리 |

🔴 **소품으로 가르지 않는다.** 모자(23 p5·p6 에서 **피포가** 큰 모자를 안아 얼굴이 덮인다) ·
지팡이(23 p10 에서 셋이 주고받는다, 13 p7·25 p6 에서 바닥에 뒹군다)는 250쪽 안에서 주인이
바뀐다. **소품을 다 빼앗아도 다섯이 갈려야 한다.**

🔴 **다섯 중 개가 둘**이고 그 둘이 **11쪽에서 나란히 선다**(01 p2 · 04 p10 · 05 p2 · 05 p10 ·
07 p6 · 07 p10 · 09 p10 · 10 p10 · 19 p1 · 23 p10 · 25 p10). **일곱 쪽이 p10 = 그 권의 마지막
쪽**이라, 여기서 둘이 안 갈리면 25권 중 일곱 권이 마지막 장에서 무너진다.

## Pipo puppy

```
HE IS A CHILD BY BUILD, NOT BY SCALE. THREE HEADS TALL: the head is a THIRD of him and sits straight
on the shoulders with NO NECK SHOWING. The body is ONE BARREL - chest and belly are a single curve
and there is NO WAIST, so nothing on him folds in the middle. Arms and legs are SHORT AND THICK and
hold the same width from shoulder to paw. EARS ARE SHORT AND SET HIGH ON TOP OF THE SKULL. The
muzzle is BLUNT AND SHORT, no longer than one eye is wide. The tail is a STUB.
STANCE: HE GOES DOWN TO THINGS. The knees bend and the whole body drops, and BOTH HANDS COME UP IN
FRONT OF HIS CHEST onto whatever he is pressing, digging, rolling, hugging or carrying - his arms
are never left hanging at his sides. HE IS THE ONLY FIGURE THAT CROUCHES.
His outline is ONE CLEAN PRESSED LINE while every other edge on the page is furry.
He wears a YELLOW SCARF and he is the only yellow thing in the book.
```

## Mom dog

```
SHE IS NOT A BIGGER PIPO, AND THE HAT IS NOT WHAT TELLS THEM APART - TAKE THE HAT OFF AND SHE MUST
STILL READ AS A DIFFERENT ANIMAL. SIX HEADS TALL: the head is SMALL on her and THERE IS A NECK
between it and the shoulders. The body is NARROW and has a WAIST at the middle - she is the only
figure in the book with a place to fold. Arms and legs are LONG AND THIN and hold the same narrow
width the whole way down. EARS ARE LONG AND HANG LOOSE PAST THE JAW and swing when she moves. The
muzzle is LONG AND NARROW. The tail is LONG AND THIN and hangs down.
STANCE: SHE FOLDS AT THE WAIST. With Pipo she bends her upper body down toward him while her legs
stay straight - THE BEND IS IN ONE PLACE, HALFWAY UP HER. Her ARMS HANG STRAIGHT DOWN and whatever
her hands hold HANGS OFF THEM: a basket handle, a cart handle, a pail. SHE NEVER CROUCHES and she
never holds anything up against her chest - that is Pipo's way, not hers.
Her one prop is a WIDE SOFT FLOPPY BRIMMED HAT, THE SAME HAT EVERY TIME - never a flat stiff boater,
never two different hats in one drawing.
NO YELLOW ANYWHERE ON HER, on the hat or on anything she carries.
```

## 할아버지

```
HE IS AN EGG THAT WALKS. The woolly body is ONE OVAL, widest low at the belly, and it takes up
almost his whole height; the head sits STRAIGHT ON TOP OF IT WITH NO NECK AT ALL and is SMALL
against it - the opposite of Pipo, whose head is a third of him. Legs are SHORT and come straight
out of the bottom of the oval, and the arms are short too and barely clear the wool. He is the
ROUNDEST FIGURE AND THE SHORTEST OF THE THREE NEIGHBOURS.
🔴 HE IS NOT ONE OF THE FLOCK. The sheep out in the fields walk on FOUR legs with plain blank faces;
he stands on TWO and has the two pressed brow strokes over his eyes. That must hold even when he is
standing in among them.
STANCE: HE LEANS BACK AND GOES FIRST. The belly leads, the shoulders sit behind it, and one leg is
always half a step out ahead of the other - he is the one out in front of everybody, doing it the
old way.
🔴 THE CROOK IS NOT WHAT IDENTIFIES HIM - it changes hands in volume 23 and lies on the ground
across all three of them in 13 and 25. The egg and the missing neck do that work.
NO YELLOW.
```

## 아줌마

```
SHE IS A NECK WITH A BODY UNDER IT. THE NECK IS AS LONG AS THE BODY IS LONG and runs up out of the
shoulders as one unbroken line, so she is the TALLEST FIGURE IN THE BOOK BY THE NECK ALONE while her
BODY IS THE SMALLEST OF THE THREE NEIGHBOURS - a plain teardrop. Two thin stick legs with flat
webbed feet and no knees showing. The head is small and carries ONE BEAK, a hard wedge.
🔴 WINGS AND NO HANDS. She has no arms, no fingers and no hands anywhere. She points, fans, flaps
and reaches with a WING EDGE, and anything she does hold is PINCHED UNDER A WING EDGE, never gripped.
STANCE: THE NECK AIMS AND THE BODY STAYS PUT. Her feet are planted flat and square while the neck
goes somewhere - out, up, round a corner, over a wall. THE LINE FROM HER FEET TO HER BEAK BENDS
ONCE, AT THE SHOULDER, and everything she is claiming to have seen is in that bend.
NO YELLOW.
```

## 아저씨

```
HE IS WIDE BEFORE HE IS TALL. THE SHOULDERS ARE THE WIDEST THING IN THIS BOOK and the chest is a
heavy slab under them. The head is LONG AND STRAIGHT, a plain wedge of a muzzle, with a MANE running
from between the ears down the back of the neck as one pressed mass. He is the BIGGEST FIGURE, but
his mark is the WIDTH, not the height - Goose Auntie's beak still goes higher than his ears.
🔴 TWO LEGS ONLY. He stands and walks upright on two legs like everybody else. A HORSE'S FOUR-LEGGED
BODY, A HORSE'S RUMP AND A LONG HORSE TAIL NEVER APPEAR - not in silhouette, not seen from behind,
not far off in a field. Feet are two blunt hooves; the arms are heavy and end in hands.
STANCE: HE IS ALWAYS ALREADY MOVING. HIS TWO FEET ARE NEVER LEVEL WITH EACH OTHER and never both
flat on the ground - one is out ahead and lifting - and his weight is thrown FORWARD past his front
foot so his whole outline leans. He is the only figure that is off balance on purpose.
NO YELLOW.
```
