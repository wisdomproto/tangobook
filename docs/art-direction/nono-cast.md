# nono — 캐스트 규격 (개체)

> 시리즈 09 「노노네 겨울 골목」. 앵커 = [nono-anchor.md](nono-anchor.md) · 설계 = [_design.md](../changjak-books/nono/_design.md)
> 🔴 **`##` 아래는 전부 프롬프트로 나간다.** 해설은 이 머리말에만 쓴다(표·굵은 글씨도 그대로 새어 나간다).
> 🔴 빌더(`build-series-html.mjs`)가 `## ` 제목을 `_series-config.mjs` 의 **`aliases[1]` → 없으면 `name`** 으로 찾는다.
> 그래서 넷은 영문 별칭이지만 **오소리 할아버지만 한국어 제목**이다(그의 `aliases[1]` 은 `Grandpa Badger` 가
> 아니라 `할아버지`이고, 그걸 제목으로 쓰면 사람이 못 읽는다 — `name` 으로 잡히게 두었다).
> 🔴 앵커는 **그 세계 전체**를 말한다. 여기는 **한 사람이 남과 무엇이 다른가**만 쓴다.

## 🔴 왜 생겼나 — 이 시리즈의 위험은 「참새와 비둘기」다

셋 중 둘이 새다. SCENE 250쪽이 세 아이를 구별하는 유일한 표식이 **초록 목도리 / 초록 천 가방 /
초록 방울** 세 낱말인데, 카드에서 그것들은 손톱만 하다 — 타로·미나가 무너진 자리와 같다.

### 그래서 실루엣을 **무게가 어디 있나**로 갈랐다 (통째로 까맣게 칠해도 셋이 다르다)

| | 통째로 칠했을 때 | 머리 : 키 | 몸을 가로지르는 것 | 초록의 모양 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **노노** 참새 | **위가 무겁다** — 큰 원 아래 작은 원 | 1 : 2.5 (가장 크다) | **가로줄 둘**(소매 단·옷단) | 늘어진 끈 **둘** |
| **구구** 비둘기 | **아래가 무겁다** — 작은 공 아래 큰 배 | 1 : 5 (가장 작다) | **대각선 하나**(가방끈) | 대각 **띠 하나** |
| **키키** 고양이 | **뾰족하다** — 귀 둘 + 가는 꼬리 | 1 : 4 (귀 빼고) | 아무것도 없다 | **점 하나** |

🔴 **어른은 크기 말고 「어긋남이 없다」로 갈린다.** 이 그림체의 캐릭터 축이 「얼굴 조각이 서로 안 맞는다」
이므로, **안 맞는 것이 아이의 표시**다. 할아버지는 눈 둘이 같은 크기로 나란하다 — 잘려 나오거나 멀리
있어서 키를 못 잴 때도 이건 읽힌다. (타로에서 어른·아이가 크기로만 갈렸던 것이 진범이었다.)

🔴 **앵커가 자기와 모순돼 있었다** — `THE MISMATCH IS NEVER REPEATED · the pieces are torn fresh for
every page, so the wrongness lands somewhere new each time` 는 **인물을 고정하지 말라는 말**이다.
매 쪽 다르게 어긋나면 같은 아이로 안 읽힌다. 앵커를 고쳤다: **어긋나는 방향은 인물마다 고정, 매 쪽
새로 찢기는 것은 섬유(가장자리)뿐.** 아래 다섯이 저마다 **한 가지 어긋남**을 들고 간다.

🔴 **렌더 실물이 세 가지를 알려 줬다**(`comic-assets/nono-plan` 5장, 실제로 받아서 봤다).
①노노의 앞발이 **손가락 없는 벙어리장갑** = 앵커가 금지한 날개로 읽힌다 ②노노 부리·발이 **주황**,
구구 발이 **살구색** — 팔레트에 없는 다섯째·여섯째 종이다(앵커가 부리·발 종이를 안 정해 둬서 모델이
지어냈다) ③노노의 **큰 머리와 짧은 코트**는 잘 됐는데 규격에 없다 — 미나 엄마의 긴 옷처럼 **다시
뽑으면 사라진다**. 셋 다 아래에 못 박았고, ①②는 앵커 쪽 뿌리도 같이 고쳤다.

🔴 **키·귀 방향은 안 정한다** — 셋은 키가 같고(앵커), SCENE 이 귀로 감정을 그린다(「귀가 아래로」·
「두 삼각 귀만 할아버지 쪽으로」). 크기와 자리만 고정하고 방향은 열어 둔다. **SCENE 은 한 글자도 안 고쳤다.**

## Nono sparrow

BUILD: THE TOP-HEAVY ONE. Torn from TWO pieces only - one round head and one smaller round body
  touching it with NO NECK between them. 🔴 THE HEAD IS 1/2.5 OF HIS HEIGHT AND IS THE WIDEST PART OF
  HIM; his body is narrower than his own head and narrower than the pigeon's chest. Short legs.
  Blacked out at thumbnail size he is a big circle sitting on a smaller circle.
CLOTH: ONE short plain coat, hem at the hip, a cuff at each wrist - 🔴 SO THE ONLY THINGS CROSSING HIS
  BODY ARE HORIZONTAL TORN EDGES. TORN2 WOOD BROWN. It is cut NARROWER THAN HIS HEAD so it never
  becomes his widest part, and he wears nothing else, ever.
GREEN: a GREEN SCARF round the throat with BOTH ENDS HANGING FREE, long enough to reach his hip. The
  ends are the part of him that moves - they hang, blow back, flick up - and two loose green strips
  stay readable when the whole figure is thumbnail-sized.
BEAK AND FEET: 🔴 TORN2 WOOD BROWN, never orange. The beak is SHORT AND BLUNT, no longer than one eye
  is wide - half the pigeon's. Feet wood brown with three splayed toes.
HANDS: hands with THREE SEPARATE FINGERS on every ordinary page. Wings replace the arms ONLY in
  volumes 01, 04, 07, 10, 18, 19 and 25, and on those pages he has no arms at all.
🔴 HIS FIXED MISMATCH IS SIZE: the RIGHT eye is clearly bigger than the left, and the head is one size
  too big for the body. Same direction on every page - only the fibre of the tear is new.
🔴 AGAINST THE PIGEON, and the drawing fails if any of the three is missing: big head against small
  head · horizontal hems against one diagonal strap · a short brown beak against a long slate one.

## Gugu pigeon

BUILD: THE BOTTOM-HEAVY ONE. 🔴 HER HEAD IS 1/5 OF HER HEIGHT - THE SMALLEST HEAD OF THE THREE
  CHILDREN - and it sits on a chest and belly torn as ONE DEEP PIECE TWICE THE WIDTH OF THAT HEAD,
  widest low down. Two straight legs with a clear gap between them. Blacked out she is a small ball
  standing on a big pear.
🔴 WHEN SHE HUDDLES SMALL (volume 05) the whole outline shrinks and that is the page; what identifies
  her is the RATIO of head to chest, never how much room she takes up.
CLOTH: 🔴 NONE. The bag is the only thing she wears - no coat, no shawl, nothing laid over the chest,
  because the chest is the whole of what tells her from the sparrow.
GREEN: a GREEN CLOTH BAG on a strap worn across one shoulder, the bag hanging at the opposite hip.
  🔴 THAT STRAP IS THE ONLY DIAGONAL ON ANY CHILD IN THE BOOK.
BEAK AND FEET: 🔴 OVERLAP DEEP SLATE, never pink or orange. The beak is LONG AND TAPERING, about twice
  the sparrow's, and being the same paper as her head it half disappears into it. Feet the same slate.
HANDS: hands with THREE SEPARATE FINGERS. Wings appear ONLY in volume 05, where she spreads them
  across the chair; there she has no arms.
🔴 HER FIXED MISMATCH IS HEIGHT: the two eyes are about the same size but sit at CLEARLY DIFFERENT
  HEIGHTS on the head, the left one a scrap lower. Same direction on every page.

## Kiki cat

BUILD: THE SPIKED ONE. Two sharp triangle ears, each about half a head tall, are the tallest and
  widest thing about her; the head without ears is 1/4 of her height. 🔴 HER BODY IS THE NARROWEST OF
  THE THREE, straight from shoulder to hip with NO BELLY AT ALL, and ONE LONG THIN TAIL as long as her
  body, torn as a single piece and NEVER BUSHY. Blacked out she is two spikes, a narrow trunk and a
  whip.
CLOTH: 🔴 NOTHING AT ALL. The cord at her throat is everything she wears - a bare narrow body and a
  thin tail are what keep her from the two birds at any size, and a coat would take both away.
  In ONE volume she has a hat; it is the only hat any child wears in this book and for nine of that
  volume's ten pages it is not on her head.
GREEN: a small GREEN BELL on a thin cord at the throat - 🔴 THE SMALLEST GREEN OF THE THREE, a single
  dot, against two hanging strips and one diagonal band. In one volume she wears no green at all and
  her throat is bare with no cord.
FACE AND PAPER: no beak - a small torn nose. Head, body, limbs and tail are all OVERLAP DEEP SLATE;
  only a chest patch is TORN2 WOOD BROWN. No claws are drawn.
HANDS: hands with THREE SEPARATE FINGERS. She never has wings and never flies.
🔴 HER FIXED MISMATCH IS THE EARS: ONE EAR IS CLEARLY BIGGER THAN THE OTHER AND SET LOWER on the head,
  while her two eyes match. 🔴 THE SIZE AND THE SETTING ARE FIXED, THE DIRECTION THE EARS POINT IS NOT -
  the ears carry her feeling and they turn, flatten and prick up freely.

## 오소리 할아버지

BUILD: the only grown-up. Twice a child's height and the widest figure in the book, standing still,
  weight low, shoulders sloping.
🔴 HIS PIECES ALMOST MATCH, AND THAT IS WHAT SAYS GROWN-UP. His two eyes are the same size and sit
  level, his two ears are a pair. The children are torn wrong on purpose; he is not. This must hold
  when he is cropped at the waist or standing far down the alley, where his height cannot be compared
  to anything.
FACE: 🔴 A WHITE FACE WITH ONE DARK BAND TORN STRAIGHT ACROSS BOTH EYES, ear to ear. He is the only
  figure in the book with white on his face. The eyes are two small round scraps inside that band.
CLOTH: ONE apron from the chest to the ANKLE, so 🔴 HIS OUTLINE IS UNBROKEN FROM CHEST TO FLOOR AND HE
  IS THE ONLY FIGURE WITH NO GAP BETWEEN THE LEGS - every child shows two legs and a gap and he never
  does. Sleeves rolled above the elbow on every page, standing still or not: he is drawn mid-work.
  His hands are always busy with the same work the children are doing.
🔴 NO GREEN ANYWHERE ON HIM - not the apron, not a cap, not the shop awning - and he never carries a
  green thing for a child. He has no errand of his own.

## Baby squirrel

🔴 SHE IS NOT ONE OF THE THREE. She stands in five pages of ONE volume, and 🔴 SHE CARRIES NO GREEN
  ANYWHERE - that absence is her whole part in the book: she is the child who is not in the group
  yet. Even on the last page of that volume, where she plays with them, the greens are three.
BUILD: 🔴 THE SMALLEST FIGURE IN THE SERIES - she comes up to a child's shoulder. ONE TAIL TORN AS A
  SINGLE PIECE, as tall as she is and wider than her body, standing up behind her: blacked out she is
  more tail than animal, and that is what separates her from the cat, whose tail is one thin whip.
  Round ears set low on the head, nothing sharp anywhere on her.
CLOTH: one coat that is TOO BIG - it reaches BELOW THE KNEE so almost no leg shows, the opposite of
  the sparrow's hip-length coat. TORN2 WOOD BROWN. Nothing else.
HANDS: hands with THREE SEPARATE FINGERS, usually held together in front of her. No wings ever.
🔴 HER FIXED MISMATCH IS SCALE: BOTH eyes are too big for the head and they are equally too big -
  the only face in the book that is wrong in a symmetrical way.
