## §0. 🔴 매체 번역 한 줄 — 밝은 것은 칠한 게 아니라 닦아 낸 것이다

앵커: `THE INK IS ROLLED ONTO A PLATE, THE FORM IS WIPED OUT OF IT WITH A RAG AND A FINGER, AND THE
PLATE IS PRINTED ONCE. Light is made by TAKING INK AWAY.`

**그러므로 자리 시트가 정하는 것은 「무엇을 그리나」가 아니라 🔴 「어디를 닦아 내나」다.** 지중해 볕·마른
길·식탁보·회벽이 전부 **판이 안 닿은 종이**다. 마당이 최다 무대인데(마당 10권 + 농가 마당 9권),
🔴 **마당 바닥이 「닦아 낸 넓은 자리」라는 걸 시트가 못 박지 않으면 매번 칠해 버린다** — 그러면 이 시리즈가
지중해에서 북유럽으로 간다.

🔴 **둘째 — 판 자국(plate mark)이 이 책의 유일한 부드러운 가장자리다.** 인쇄가 판 테두리에서 잦아드는
그 자리를 **매 쪽에 남긴다.** 시트에도 남긴다 — 안 남기면 컷이 화면 끝까지 꽉 찬 그림이 된다.

⚠️ 셋째 — 🔴 `THE CHILDREN ARE ALWAYS EXACTLY FIVE` 이고 **사촌 셋은 한 덩어리로 움직인다**(실루엣 하나).
자리 시트의 SPOT 은 **다섯이 다 들어가는 폭**을 하나는 갖고 있어야 한다.
⚠️ 넷째 — `DEPTH IS SPACING`. 언덕 아래 나무는 **좁게 선다**. 흐리게 금지. `NO WHITE INK, EVER`.

### §1.1 FarmYard — 실제 프롬프트 (가장 먼저)

```
STAGE SHEET - FarmYard   (lulu-monotype · SCENE token: FarmYard · bake FIRST)

The farm yard. Ten of the eighteen named places in this series are somewhere in it, so this drawing
decides it once - and above all it decides WHAT IS WIPED.

🔴 THE WIPE MAP - light is made by taking ink away, so name every bright thing as a wiped shape,
  not a painted one. Fix them here:
  THE YARD FLOOR is the biggest wiped area in the series - dry beaten earth, rag-wiped down to bare
    PAPER across most of the picture, with a CLEAN HARD EDGE where the ink starts again. It is
    never inked and never toned.
  THE HOUSE WALL, whitewash, wiped.
  THE LONG TABLE'S CLOTH, wiped, and it is the brightest thing on any page it appears in.
  THE SUN AND THE SKY, wiped to nothing at the top of the picture.
  WHAT STAYS INKED: the olive tree at the yard's edge (OLIVE LEAF, its trunk DRY EARTH), the vine
    pergola overhead, the barn wall, the doorway (the two inks mixed on the plate = the only dark),
    tools, jars, sacks.
  🔴 A WIPED SHAPE HAS A HARD EDGE BECAUSE A RAG MADE IT. Nothing fades into anything.

FIXED PARTS: ONE olive tree at the left edge of the yard, low branches · THE VINE PERGOLA over the
  right half, at most 5 posts · THE LONG TABLE under it · THE BARN closing the back · THE HOUSE
  DOOR at the right · a water trough by the barn.

🔴 THE PLATE MARK: the print runs out at the border of the plate, and that soft fade is the ONLY
  soft edge in the book. Leave it on every panel of this sheet, a hand's width inside the frame.

SPOTS:
  A THE WHOLE YARD from the road, wide: 🔴 wide enough to hold five children, tree left, pergola
    and table right, barn behind.
  B UNDER THE PERGOLA, medium: the table, the wiped cloth, posts.
  C THE FLOOR, close, high: bare wiped paper and one inked thing lying on it.
  D FROM THE BARN DOOR looking out, medium: the dark doorway framing the wiped yard.

PLATE: A, B, C, D once each, plus 🔴 A WIPE DIAGRAM - the yard drawn as flat areas, each marked
  INKED or WIPED, so nobody paints the floor.

NOT: no character of any kind, no accent colour anywhere on this sheet, no lettering or numerals,
  🔴 NO WHITE INK EVER - every bright thing is wiped, no third ink, no graded area, no soft edge
  except the plate mark, no shading, gradient, glow or cast shadow, nothing paler or bluer with
  distance.
```

### §2.1 Nets — 실제 프롬프트 (🔴 「빈 것」이 닦아 낸 자리다)

```
PROP SHEET - Nets   (lulu-monotype · SCENE token: Nets)

The wide net spread under an olive tree to catch the fruit. Two books use it, and in both the
EMPTY net is the picture.

🔴 THE EMPTY NET IS A WIPED AREA, not a drawn mesh. Spread on the ground it is one broad shape
  rag-wiped down to bare PAPER with a clean hard edge, and that big bright emptiness is what says
  "nothing has fallen yet". Do not draw a mesh across it. A few crossings may be lifted with a
  finger at the NEAR edge only, at most 11, and nowhere else.
FORM: the net's border is a rope of DRY EARTH; the corners are weighted with small stones.
STATES:
  1 SPREAD AND EMPTY - the wiped area at its largest, the tree's inked branches above it.
  2 FIRST OLIVES - a scatter of separate berries, at most 12 ever drawn as individuals, the rest
    of any quantity being ONE inked mass. 🔴 The mass eats the wiped area, and that is the ruler:
    how much white is left is how much is still to come.
  3 HEAPED - the net's centre one dark mass (the two inks mixed on the plate), the wiped area
    reduced to a rim.
  4 GATHERED UP - the net lifted by its corners into a bundle, no wiped area left at all.

PLATE: the four states at the same size and distance, so the wiped area can be compared shrinking.

NOT: no character, no hands, no accent colour, no lettering or numerals, no white ink, no drawn
  mesh across the wiped area, no third ink, no graded area, no soft edge except the plate mark, no
  shading, gradient or glow.
```

### §2.2 Olives — 🔴 이 시리즈에서 제일 자주 나오는 것 (전권)

> 알 하나가 그물에도 · 빵에도 · 체에도 · 탑에도 · 통에도 있다. 한 장으로 못 박지 않으면 권마다 크기와
> 모양이 달라지고, 그러면 **12권의 「굵은 알과 가는 알」**과 **37권의 「올리브인가 아닌가」**가 통째로 안 읽힌다.

```
PROP SHEET - Olives   (lulu-monotype · SCENE token: Olives)

The olive itself - the fruit this whole family lives on. It is on more pages than any character
except Lulu, so its one shape and one weight of ink are fixed here.

🔴 A RIPE OLIVE IS PLATE MIX - the darkest print in the book, made where the two inks meet on the
  plate. It is never OLIVE LEAF (that is the tree) and never outlined. On the bare-paper net it is
  the darkest thing on the page; that contrast is the whole of volumes 01 and 21.
FORM: an OVAL, longer than it is wide, with one small stem scar - never a circle, because volume 37
  tells a round berry from this one and the difference must survive at thumbnail size.
🔴 THE CAP IS THE DESIGN: at most 12 olives are ever drawn as separate berries. Any quantity beyond
  that is ONE inked mass with a wiped edge, and nothing in the mass is a mirrored copy of its
  neighbour.
STATES - draw all at the same distance so sizes can be compared:
  1 ONE OLIVE, large, alone.
  2 FAT AND THIN SIDE BY SIDE - a plainly fat one and a plainly thin one touching. 🔴 The gap between
    the two sizes is the ruler of volume 12; if they are only slightly different that book fails.
  3 A SCATTER of 12 separate berries on bare paper, none touching.
  4 ONE MASS - a heap where only the 6 at the rim read as separate and the rest is a single field.
  5 THE STONE - the pit left after eating: a small pale almond shape, wiped, NOT inked, and plainly
```
