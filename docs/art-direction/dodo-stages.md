## §0. 🔴 매체 번역 한 줄 — 둘이다

**① 가장자리를 그리는 게 아니라, 붓이 못 미쳐 남은 종이가 가장자리다.**

앵커: `strokes that STOP SHORT of the outline, so a rim of bare paper is left around most shapes and
THE PAPER DRAWS THE EDGE INSTEAD OF A LINE - there is no outline anywhere in this book.`
🔴 **그러므로 두 물건이 붙어 있으면 사이에 종이 테가 있어야 갈린다.** 자리 시트가 정할 것은 형태가 아니라
**어디에 테를 남기나**다. 테를 안 남기면 물레방아와 벽이 한 덩어리가 되고, 다 남기면 만화가 된다.
→ 자리 시트에 `WHERE THE PAPER RIM IS LEFT` 절.

**② 초크는 색이 아니라 「튜브에 든 종이」이고, 칠한 면 위로 튀어 오른 것에만 쓴다.**

앵커: `CHALK IS NOT A FIFTH COLOUR, IT IS PAPER IN A TUBE ... ONLY for what is thrown UP off a
coloured field: splash, spray, flour dust, falling snow, mist over water. At most 9 ... never a
highlight on a form, never an outline, never blended, and never laid on bare paper.`
🔴 **물방앗간 시리즈라 물보라·밀가루가 매 권 나온다** — 이 규칙을 시트에 안 적으면 초크가 곧장
**형태 위 하이라이트**로 새어 나가고, 그 순간 이 매체가 수채가 된다. 15시리즈 중 **가장 새기 쉬운 자리**다.

⚠️ 셋째 — 🔴 **물은 파랑이 아니다**(MILLPOND). 파랑은 도도 머리 리본과 무무 목수건뿐.

🔴 **넷째(2026-09-04 사물 시트를 쓰며 드러났다) — 밝은 것은 세 갈래인데 이름이 하나다.**
「하얀 것」이 이 시리즈에 세 종류 있고 **셋이 다른 물건**이다. 사물 시트마다 어느 것인지 못 박아 뒀다.

| 갈래 | 무엇 | 어떻게 |
|---|---|---|
| **안 칠한 종이** | 고드름 · 눈오리 · 눈밭 · 화덕 아가리 · 창빛 · 밀가루가 내려앉은 바닥 · 마른 자리 | 둘레가 칠해져서 **남은** 자리. 칠하지 않는다 |
| **CHALK** | 튀는 물 · 나는 밀가루 · 내리는 눈 · 물 위 안개 | 칠한 면 **위로** 얹는 불투명 한 획, 쪽당 최대 9 |
| **없는 것** | 반짝임 · 서리 결정 · 광택 · 하이라이트 | 안 그린다. 앵커 NOT 이 금지한다 |

### §1.1 MillStream — 실제 프롬프트 (가장 먼저)

```
STAGE SHEET - MillStream   (dodo-gouachemill · SCENE token: MillStream · bake FIRST)

The stream in front of the watermill. Seven of the thirteen named places in this series are on it,
so this drawing decides it once - and it decides the stones, which are the ruler.

🔴 SEVEN STEPPING STONES, NUMBERED. Exactly seven stones stand above the water and they never
  change: THREE SMALL on the left (1-2-3), 🔴 THE LARGEST IN THE MIDDLE (4), THREE on the right
  (5-6-7). A page that stands on a stone is standing on a NUMBERED one, and a page that shows fewer
  shows a numbered run. Each stone carries one white mark where the current parts against it.

FIXED PARTS:
  THE STREAM is ONE sweep of MILLPOND running across the picture, never lighter or darker inside
    itself. 🔴 IT IS NOT BLUE.
  THE MILL stands at the RIGHT, WHEAT timber, with the great wooden wheel on its near wall - at
    most 8 paddles, never more.
  THE FAR FIELD across the water, WHEAT, with the far bank as one edge.
  REEDS at the near bank, at most 7 strokes.
  THE SKY IS BARE PAPER and is the light of the page.

🔴 WHERE THE PAPER RIM IS LEFT - this is what the sheet is for. Every brush sweep stops short of
  its own outline, and the bare paper between two areas is what separates them. Decide it here:
  a rim IS left between STONE and WATER · between WHEEL and MILL WALL · between MILL and SKY ·
  between REEDS and BANK. A rim is NOT left where one thing sits inside another as a hole. Never
  draw a line to help; if two areas read as one mass, widen the rim, do not outline.

🔴 CHALK ON THIS STAGE: splash and spray thrown up off the MILLPOND sweep, at most 9 separate
  marks, each ONE opaque stroke with a hard edge. Mist over the water, the same. CHALK IS NEVER
  laid on the bare-paper sky, never used as a highlight on a stone or a bill, never blended, and
  never used to draw the water's edge.

SPOTS:
  A ACROSS THE STREAM, wide, eye level: all seven stones, mill right, field beyond.
  B ON A STONE, medium low: water either side, the numbered neighbours visible.
  C THE WATER, close, high: one sweep of MILLPOND and what breaks it.
  D FROM THE MILL SIDE looking back, medium: the wheel entering at frame edge.
  🔴 E STANDING AT THE WATER'S EDGE, medium, eye level: the near bank underfoot, shallow water
     running past it, and NO STEPPING STONES IN FRAME - this is the spot a duckling stands on when
     it has not crossed. Three books need it (07 p3 · 13 p6 · 14 p6) and A/B/C/D all show stones.

PLATE: A, B, C, D once each, plus 🔴 A NUMBERED DIAGRAM of the seven stones with the largest marked
  as 4, plus one CHALK panel showing spray done correctly beside spray done as a highlight (wrong).

NOT: no character of any kind, no ribbon blue anywhere on this sheet, no lettering or numerals, no
  outline of any kind, no third tube, no thinned wash, no blue water, no chalk on bare paper, no
  chalk as highlight, no shading, gradient, glow, cast shadow or soft edge, no reflection in the
  water.
```

### §1.3 Meadow — 실제 프롬프트 (신설 · §5 ① 의 답 · 9쪽 · 4권)

> 12 p5(안개 속 버드나무) · 16 p9(버드나무 그늘) · 18 p2·p4·p5·p6·p7·p8(딸기 덤불) · 19 p1(방앗간 뒤 언덕
> 여름밤). 🔴 **네 권이 같은 풀밭의 다른 구석을 쓴다** — 그래서 한 시트이고, 그 셋(버드나무 · 덤불 ·
> 언덕)의 **서로에 대한 자리**가 이 시트가 정하는 전부다. 18권은 여섯 쪽이 덤불 하나에 걸려 있으므로
> 덤불의 **안팎**이 여기서 확정돼야 §2.25 `BerryBush` 가 그 위에서 성립한다.

```
STAGE SHEET - Meadow   (dodo-gouachemill · SCENE token: Meadow)

The rough grass across the stream from the mill - not the wheat field and not the water. Four books
stand here and each one uses a different corner of it, so the three corners and where they sit
relative to one another are fixed once.

FIXED PARTS:
  THE GROUND is ONE sweep of WHEAT, 0 blades picked out, running to a low horizon.
  🔴 ONE GREAT WILLOW at the LEFT, MILLPOND: a thick knotted bole with THREE branches splitting off
    it low down, one of them low enough for a duckling to walk into. Its long trailing branches are
    ONE repeated stroke, all leaning the SAME way, and that lean is the wind of the page. Its shade
    on the ground is ONE flat MILLPOND sweep with a HARD edge and 0 strokes inside it.
  🔴 ONE BRAMBLE at the RIGHT, a broad low MILLPOND mass (its own build is PROP SHEET BerryBush).
    🔴 THE BUSH IS OPAQUE - a duckling inside it shows as ONE part only (two webbed feet under the
    hem, a ribbon end through the leaves) and never faintly through the mass.
  🔴 ONE HILL BEHIND, rising between the meadow and the mill, so that FROM ITS TOP THE MILL ROOF IS
    BELOW AND SMALL. Its crest is one long WHEAT edge and nothing stands on it.
  THE STREAM leaves the frame at the bottom edge; the mill is on its far side. Trees at most 5 in
    the whole stage, willow included.

🔴 WHERE THE PAPER RIM IS LEFT: a rim IS left between WILLOW and GROUND · between BRAMBLE and
  GROUND · between HILL CREST and SKY. A rim is NOT left between the willow's shade and the ground
  it lies on - the shade sits INSIDE the ground sweep as a hole, hard edged. Never draw a line to
  separate anything; if two masses read as one, widen the rim.

STATES - the weather and hour each book brings:
  1 CLEAR DAY - sky bare PAPER, willow shade a hard flat sweep, bramble fruit on its sunlit edge.
  2 🔴 MIST - everything but the nearest thing is bare PAPER; the willow arrives at FULL strength
    with a hard edge and NOTHING behind it, so it reads as a wall. There is no pale version of it,
    no fading and no haze. The bramble and the hill are simply not on the page.
  3 🔴 SUMMER NIGHT ON THE HILL - the sky is OVERLAP laid as one flat sweep filling most of the
    frame; the hill is a WHEAT edge along the bottom with the mill roof a small outline below it.
    Stars are BARE PAPER points, each cut on its own, at most 14, none a mirrored copy of another.
    No moon glow, no halo, no star of a different size.
  4 LEAF SHADE ON A BODY - the willow's leaf shade falling across something lying on the ground is
    the SAME flat MILLPOND with hard edges, cut as separate patches, at most 9, never a soft dapple
    and never a gradient.

🔴 COLOUR: the berries the script calls red are OVERLAP, not red - there is no red tube in this
  book. The one berry a volume ends on is a single OVERLAP round with bare paper all round it.

SPOTS:
  A ACROSS THE MEADOW, wide, eye level: willow left, bramble right, hill behind, stream at the
    bottom edge - the one panel that fixes the three corners against each other.
  B UNDER THE WILLOW, medium low: the bole close, the three branches overhead, the shade edge
    crossing the ground.
  C AT THE BRAMBLE, medium: its outer face straight on, ground in front of it.
  D ON THE HILL TOP, wide, low: sky filling most of the frame, the crest a band along the bottom
    with the mill roof below and beyond.

PLATE: A, B, C, D once each, plus 🔴 A DRAWN TWICE at one size - clear and in mist - so the mist
  panel can be checked as the SAME meadow with things simply absent.

NOT: no character of any kind, no ribbon blue anywhere on this sheet, no red berry or any third
  tube, no lettering or numerals, no outline of any kind, no thinned wash, no chalk on bare paper,
  no chalk as a highlight, no blade-by-blade grass, no darker green for shade (density only), no
  figure showing faintly through the bramble, no shading, gradient, glow or soft edge, no soft
  dapple of leaf shade, no moon glow or halo around a star.
```

### §2.3 FlourFloor — 🔴 안 칠한 종이 위에 자국을 어떻게 내나 (3권)

> 33권은 조약돌 자국이 사라지는 것이 결말이며, 42권은 하얀 발자국이 둘 사이에 남는다.

> 선을 그으면 이 책에 없는 윤곽선이 된다. **밀려난 테로 그린다.** 이 시트는 그 답을 못 박는 것이다.

```
PROP SHEET - FlourFloor   (dodo-gouachemill · SCENE token: FlourFloor)

The mill floor under a settled layer of flour, and the marks made in it. Three books turn on marks
appearing in this floor or being swept out of it.

🔴 THE FLOOR IS BARE PAPER. Settled flour is not painted - it is the unpainted sheet, and it is the
  brightest thing in the mill. The room around it is OVERLAP; the walls, beams and sacks are WHEAT.
  The floor reads as floor because everything else is painted and it is not.
🔴 A MARK IN THE FLOUR IS NOT A LINE. Where a foot or a wingtip or a pebble has pushed the flour
  aside, the flour is GONE there and the dark boards show - so a mark is a NARROW WHEAT-OR-OVERLAP
  SHAPE with the bare paper heaped along BOTH its edges as a raised rim. Draw the mark as the thing
  it exposes, never as a stroke drawn on top. 🔴 NEVER OUTLINE A MARK.
MARK VOCABULARY - all at one size so they can be compared:
  1 A WEBBED FOOTPRINT - three toes forward, one back, the flour ridged around it.
  2 A PEBBLE'S DIVOT - a small round pit with the ridge on the far side only.
  3 A SLIDE - one long smear, widest where the foot began.
  4 A SWEPT BAND - the flour dragged into parallel combs by a broom, all in ONE direction.
  5 A DRAWN LINE - a shallow narrow furrow made by a wingtip, thinner than any of the above.
🔴 THE FOUR DRAWINGS (volume 44, drawn IN the flour with a wingtip, all in mark type 5, all seen
  from straight above, all child-made and crooked): A FOOT-FLOWER, six webbed prints set in a ring ·
  A WHEEL, one circle with EIGHT short spokes, no more · A SNOWMAN, three stacked circles · A HAT
  with a brim wider than its crown. Nothing else is ever drawn in the floor.
STATES:
  A CLEAN - unbroken bare paper edge to edge, one shallow drift along the wall.
  B SCATTERED - divots and slides all over, the floor read as messy.
  C SMOOTH AGAIN - clean as A, so A and B and C can be laid over one another.
  D THE FOUR DRAWINGS together, seen from straight above.
  E ONE NEW PRINT alone on a smooth floor - a single mark is the whole picture.

PLATE: the five mark types at one size, then A-E, plus one panel showing a mark drawn correctly
  beside the same mark drawn as an ink line (wrong, marked).

NOT: no character, no ribbon blue, no lettering or numerals anywhere in the flour, no outline
  around any mark, no third tube, no chalk on this sheet at all (settled flour is bare paper, and
```
