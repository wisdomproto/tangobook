## B-12 §1. 앵커 배정

**권**: `b12` 「지도에 없는 골목」 (14쪽 · 4~7세 · 주제군 **B 상상·변신** · 엔진 **여정과 귀환** · 무대 리스본 계단골목의 모퉁이 한 자리 · 주인공 새끼 고양이 + 정어리 굽는 늙은 개 아저씨)

**클러스터**: **C1** · 앵커 슬러그 `changjak-gapsheet` — **신규 민팅**(배정표 지정, 변경 없음).

**한 줄**: 한 화면에 **지지면이 둘**이다 — 바깥은 거친 크림 종이, 골목 안은 매끈한 파란 종이. 둘째 종이는 벽의 틈 안에서만 보이고 **p12 에 화면에서 없어진다.**

**이 권이 그림에 요구하는 것** 셋.

1. 🔴 **「그냥 다른 것」을 색이 아니라 재료로 말한다.** 안쪽이 파랗다고만 하면 배경 설정이지 그림체가 아니다. **종이를 바꾸면** 같은 붓 같은 물감이 다르게 앉으므로, 다름이 그림 실력이 아니라 물성이 된다.
2. 🔴 **계기판 = 화면의 지지면 개수다.** p1 한 장 · p2~p10 두 장 · p11~p14 한 장. 착지(「벽이 이어져 있다」)가 **종이가 한 장으로 돌아온 것**으로 그려진다.
3. 🔴 **카메라가 모퉁이를 안 뜬다**(대본 규칙). 골목 안은 언제나 **입구 틀을 액자 삼아 밖에서 본 그림**이고, 크림 종이가 늘 테두리와 화면 아래 연기를 붙잡고 있다.

**형제 권과 갈린 축**(첫 렌더에서 세어진다).

| 대상 | 갈림 |
|---|---|
| **d15** `changjak-twopapers`(같은 C1 · 두 종이) | **둘째 지지면이 화면에서 없어지나.** d15 는 열세 쪽 내내 두 장이고 물감이 다르게 물린다. b12 는 **1 → 2 → 1** 이고, 그 개수가 곧 사건이다 |
| **b01** `changjak-cardboard` · **e01·g08** `changjak-drypaper`(같은 C1) | 셋 다 **지지면이 한 장**이고 그 위에서 안 그린 자리가 자라거나 줄어든다. 여기는 지지면이 **둘**이다 |
| **f02** `changjak-delft` | **파랑이 무엇인가** — f02 는 흰 유약면 위 코발트 평칠(타일 문법). 🔴 b12 의 파랑은 **종이 색**이고, 아줄레주 무늬는 그 위 얇은 한 겹이다. **타일 문법으로 그리면 그 책이 된다** |

**🔴 대본 SCENE 처방 5건** — 대본은 고치지 않고 컷에서 분기한다.

**밀도 배급**: 🔴 **p6 · p7 · p8 · p9 — 골목 안쪽만** 마감 4~5개로 올린다(빨래·화분·전차·양말·타일 무늬). 🔴 **같은 쪽의 크림 테두리는 2 로 둔다.** 안이 바깥과 똑같이 비면 「그냥 다른 것」이 안 보인다. 나머지 열 쪽은 두 종이 다 `FINISHED THINGS PER PAGE = 2`.

**의인화 등급**: 둘 다 **두 발로 서서 걷는다.** 옷은 하나씩만 — 고양이는 헐렁한 빨간 스카프, 아저씨는 회청색 앞치마. **신발·바지·모자 없음.** 앞발은 앞발이고(손가락 안 갈라짐) 물건은 **두 앞발로** 잡는다. 고양이는 **들여다보거나 뻗을 때만 네발로 내려선다.** 컷마다 반복해 적는다.

## B-12 §2. STYLE ANCHOR

```
STYLE ANCHOR - changjak-gapsheet   (a kitten and an old dog / one corner of a Lisbon stair alley)

Style: gouache and coloured ink, 4-7 year old picture book, and 🔴 THERE ARE TWO SUPPORT PAPERS IN ONE
  FRAME. OUTSIDE = coarse toothy cream stock #EDE3CE, where every line skips, every wash breaks into
  speckle and edges crumble. INSIDE THE GAP = smooth sized blue stock #4A79B4, where the same paint
  lies flat, edges cut clean and pool slightly at the end of a stroke. Same hand, same paint - what
  differs is the paper, never the drawing.

RENDERING (finish hierarchy): 🔴 COUNT THE SHEETS - p1 one, p2-p10 two, p11-p14 one. The blue stock is
  only ever seen INSIDE the torn opening in the cream stock; that opening works as a picture frame,
  and the cream stock always keeps a border and the brazier smoke across the bottom, because the
  camera never leaves this corner. FINISHED THINGS PER PAGE = 2, the kitten and the one thing it
  touches. Wall = 0 cracks. Stairs are the only repetition in the book: at most 7 treads, one ruled
  line each. Azulejo = at most 9 tiles carry pattern, the rest is plain blue stock. Laundry = at most
  5 pieces. Roofline and windows above the wall = 0. 🔴 DENSITY RATION = p6, p7, p8, p9 - INSIDE THE
  GAP ONLY, raise to 4-5 finished things (laundry, flowerpot, tram, sock, tile pattern); the cream
  border on those same pages stays at 2. Every other page keeps both stocks at 2.

PALETTE: cream stock #EDE3CE · blue stock #4A79B4, with #2F5480 for tile pattern and for the bare
  unpainted gap on p2-p3 · ochre #E6B93C - 🔴 the same yellow on both stocks but never the same
  shape: on cream it is ONE ruled continuous band at shoulder height, on blue it appears only as
  single isolated objects, one tram and one sock, and NEVER as a line · smoke and sardine grey
  #A8A49B · stair stone #C9BFAE · dog brown #8A6A4C with a slate-blue apron #6E7F8C · kitten grey
  tabby #8C8B86. 🔴 #C0392B IS THE SCARF AND NOTHING ELSE - coals stay dull ember brown, sardines
  stay grey. No green, no purple, no black.

CHARACTER DESIGN LANGUAGE: animals walk upright on two feet and wear one garment each. Paws stay
  paws - no split fingers - and anything held is held between two forepaws. 🔴 An eye is a round iris
  with a lid line above it, never a bare dot on a blank face. No eyebrows; acting is done by EARS and
  TAIL and by how far the neck reaches out. The two silhouettes separate at thumbnail size: a small
  upright kitten with a straight tail against a heavy stooped dog. FIXED GRADE: no shoes, no
  trousers, no hats. The kitten drops to all fours only to peer or to reach.

CANVAS: 16:9 double-page spread. The same street corner on all fourteen pages, the alley always seen
  through its entrance. 🔴 No lettering, numerals, shop signs, house numbers or tram route boards.

NOT: no digital slickness - airbrush, gradient, glow, 3D CG, cel-shading, photographic or texture
  filter / never blurred, hazy or spotlit - depth is made by leaving the ink line off / never a tile
  grid stamped across a wall, and never one paper faked with texture on the other / one sheet thick:
  no wool, no felt fuzz, no stitching, no fibre edge.
```

### 시트 1 — KittenScarf

```
CHARACTER SHEET - KittenScarf   (bake FIRST, before any scene)

Same make as the book: gouache and coloured ink. Bake this figure TWICE, once on the coarse cream
stock #EDE3CE and once on the smooth blue stock #4A79B4 - on cream the outline skips and the fur wash
speckles; on blue the same outline runs unbroken and the wash lies flat. Same cat, two papers.

FACE: a kitten - short round muzzle, small triangular ears, a round amber iris with a lid line above
  it. 🔴 Never a bare dot eye. No eyebrows, no human mouth. Whiskers = at most 5 per side.
BODY: small and upright, grey tabby #8C8B86 with at most 6 stripe strokes on the back and 3 on the
  tail; belly a shade lighter. Fur is NOT drawn. Forepaws are paws, no split fingers.
CLOTHES: one loose red scarf #C0392B knotted at the throat, ends hanging to the chest. 🔴 The ONLY red
  in the book, and it is the same red on both papers - it tells the reader which side he is standing
  on. Nothing else worn: no shoes, no trousers, no hat.
REFERENCE SHEET: (a) standing upright in three-quarter view, one forepaw laid on a wall; (b) frozen
  mid-step with the forepaw stopped in the air, ears pricked, tail stiff and up - this is p2; (c)
  down on all fours with the neck stretched right out, peering; (d) jumping with both feet off the
  ground and the scarf flying up; (e) sitting with its back against a wall, holding a sardine between
  two forepaws; (f) three head close-ups - eyes wide, eyes creased shut, one ear down.
```

### 시트 2 — OldDogSardine

```
CHARACTER SHEET - OldDogSardine   (bake SECOND)

Same make as the book. 🔴 This character is only ever drawn on the CREAM stock - he never enters the
gap - so his outline always skips and his wash always speckles.

  and a heavy upper lid. Grey around the muzzle. No eyebrows, no human mouth.

  no trousers, no cap.
🔴 FIXED RULE OF THIS BOOK: he never turns toward the wall. In every reference the head is turned to
  the brazier, to the plate or to the kitten - never past his own shoulder to the gap.
REFERENCE SHEET: (a) seated on the stool with tongs, chin lifted toward the kitten; (b) seen from
  behind over his shoulder, turning a sardine, the wall beyond him; (c) standing with a plate held in
  two forepaws; (d) crouching to set the plate on a stair tread; (e) head close-up, half-lidded eyes.
```

DELIVER THREE STATES OF THIS PLATE: (1) ONE SHEET - the band unbroken, no aperture anywhere = p1, p12,

  marks, one flat #2F5480 = p2, p3. (3) TWO SHEETS, APERTURE OPEN - the blue stock painted and filling
  most of the frame, cream held to a border plus the smoke at the bottom = p6.
