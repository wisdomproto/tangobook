# A-04 「뒤로 걷는 오리」

주제군 A 마음·감정 / 엔진 **규칙 깨기** / 무대 네덜란드 운하 / 주인공 오리 / 본문 191자 · p7 무텍스트

## A-04 §1. 앵커 배정

### 이 권이 그림에 요구하는 것 (판정의 전제)

본문이 191자다. **글이 못 하는 일을 그림이 전부 한다.** 구체적으로 세 가지다.

1. **발자국 방향이 플롯이다.** 매 쪽에서 발가락이 어디를 향하는지가 읽혀야 한다 → 발자국이 화면에서 **가장 진하고 가장 또렷한 표시**여야 한다. 지붕 기와·벽돌 이음매까지 같은 밀도로 그리면 플롯이 화면 소음에 묻힌다.
2. **"아무도 못 본 것"이 소재다.** 굴뚝부터·다리 아래부터·집의 뒷면·물속 마을. 전부 *뒤집힘·아래·뒤*다 → 형식이 그 뒤집힘을 물고 있어야 필연성이 생긴다.
3. **12쪽 중 밀도를 쓸 수 있는 쪽은 2쪽뿐**(§2.10). 그리고 그 2쪽은 정해져 있다 — **p6(물속 마을)과 p7(무텍스트)**. p7 은 글이 없어서 그림이 유일한 정보원이고, p6 은 반전 장치라 정보가 있어야 성립한다. 나머지 10쪽은 덜 그린다.

### 🔴 추천 = 후보 ① — 흑연 단색조 + 앰버 1점

근거 세 줄로 요약하면:

- **앰버의 양이 이야기다.** 이 권의 감정선은 *토라짐 → 혼자 다 봄 → 돌아옴*이고, 앰버는 **"보이는 것 / 집"**이다. 오리는 하루 종일 앰버가 없는 세계를 걷다가, **물속(뒤집힌 세계)에서 처음 앰버를 보고**, 마지막에 그 앰버 안으로 뒷걸음쳐 들어간다. 색을 먼저 고르고 이야기를 끼운 게 아니라 이야기에서 색이 나왔다(§2.9).
- **매체가 발자국이다.** 흑연은 눌러서 남기는 물질이다. 화면에서 가장 진한 흑연 = 발자국. 이건 문구로 위계를 부탁하는 게 아니라 매체가 위계를 만드는 것이다.
- **뒤집힘을 팔레트가 한 번 더 말한다.** 앰버의 첫 등장이 **거꾸로**(p6 수면 반사)다. 형식이 내용을 되풀이한다.

## A-04 §2. STYLE ANCHOR

```
STYLE ANCHOR - changjak-a04  (duck / Dutch canal / walking backwards)

Style: a hand-drawn picture-book page for 4-6 year olds. Quiet, cool, observant.
  Almost wordless - the drawing has to carry the whole story, so it must be legible before it is pretty.

MEDIUM: soft graphite pencil and diluted ink wash on cool grey-white paper with a fine tooth.
  Every mark is PRESSED into the paper: the graphite is dark and waxy where it is pushed hard,
  and skips over the tooth where it is dragged light. Edges are made by the pencil running out,
  never by a clean vector line. Water and air are one flat pass of grey ink wash, laid once and
  left alone, with the brush edge still visible where it dried.
  One single thin colour plate of warm amber is laid over the finished drawing, like a second
  print run - it sits ON TOP of the graphite and does not blend with it.
  No blending, no soft airbrush gradient, no digital glow.

PALETTE: a cold blue-leaning graphite monochrome covers the entire book.
  Hex anchors: paper #E9EAE7 / mid graphite #6E7A80 / deep graphite #2E3438 /
  pressed-hard darkest (footprints, feet, wet brick joints) #1F2427.
  EXACTLY ONE other colour exists in this book: warm amber #E5A23C, deepening to #C9762A.
  🔴 AMBER IS LAMPLIGHT AND BEING-SEEN, AND ITS AMOUNT IS THE STORY. It appears ONLY where
  a per-page instruction says so. On every other page there is no amber anywhere at all.
  Nothing else is ever amber - not the duck, not its bill, not the bricks, not the tulips,
  not the sun. Brick, roof tile, tulip and sunset are all rendered in graphite only.

COMPOSITION: low-information field first, subject second.
  The duck is small in frame (about 1/7 of page height) except p2, p5, p9 and p10.
  Canal, quay and rooflines are horizontal bands; the eye is led by diagonals, never by a
  centred symmetrical subject - except p6 and p10, which are deliberate mirror compositions.
  🔴 Keep the bottom 18% of the image quiet and free of key subject matter (a caption band is
  laid over it later). Where the ground fills the frame, keep the bottom strip plain wet brick.

FINISH HIERARCHY - read this twice. This is about how FINISHED each area is, NOT about opacity.
  1. THE DUCK AND THE FOOTPRINTS = finished. Line and tone, worked, pressed dark.
  2. WHAT THE DUCK TOUCHES OR LOOKS AT on that page (the one bridge beam, the one chimney,
     the one shard of reflected village) = half-finished: contour plus one pass of tone.
  3. EVERYTHING ELSE = A LOOSE UNFINISHED PENCIL SKETCH. A few open contour lines and direction
     strokes, barely any tone, bare paper showing through, corners of shapes left unclosed,
     deliberately not filled in - like the rough underdrawing on a sketchbook page.
  🔴 The background is NOT faded, NOT hazy, NOT blurred and NOT desaturated. It is simply not
  drawn to completion. A rough dark pencil line is correct; a soft pale finished line is wrong.
  Never draw every roof tile, every brick, every window pane, every tulip petal.
  EXCEPTION - exactly two pages carry density: the page of the reflected village and the
  wordless page of the backs of the houses. On those two, level 2 finish extends to the props.

CHARACTER DESIGN: eyes are DRAWN, not dotted - a small dark almond with a SEPARATE drawn
  eyebrow line above it, so the face can sulk, be startled, and be quietly content.
  Bodies read as one soft mass plus a neck and a tail. Bills are two or three pencil strokes.
  The silhouette must be readable at thumbnail size.

SETTING: a Dutch canal town - narrow tall stepped-gable brick houses shoulder to shoulder along
  the water, tulip pots on window ledges, bicycles laid on their sides, mooring rope and
  bollards, a white wooden drawbridge, a windmill at the end of the road, clogs by a door.
  European, no Asian architectural motifs.

CANVAS: 16:9 double-page spread, 4-6 year old picture book.

NOT: NOT digital airbrush / NOT smooth gradients / NOT glossy 3D CG render / NOT cel-shaded
  anime / NOT a texture filter laid over flat digital colour / NOT photographic /
  NOT a fully rendered background / NOT every roof tile, brick, leaf or window drawn to
  completion / NOT a uniform finish across the page / NOT a hazy, blurry or faded background
  (that is blur, not un-drawn) / NOT a second accent colour / NOT amber anywhere except where
  the page instruction places it / NOT any lettering, numerals, shop signage or house numbers
  anywhere in the image / NOT wool felt, NOT stitched fabric, NOT sculpted clay (another line
  owns those).
```

### 🔴 이 앵커의 두 불변 규칙 (매 컷 반복 확인)

**규칙 A — 발자국 방향.** 뒤로 걷는 발자국은 이렇게 생겼다:

> 자국은 오리가 지나온 쪽에서 시작해 **오리 쪽으로** 자란다. 그런데 **발가락은 전부 자국이 시작된 쪽(오래된 끝)을 향한다.** 즉 자국을 처음부터 끝까지 눈으로 따라가면 **발가락이 전부 나를 마주 보고 있다.** 오리의 부리도 같은 쪽을 본다.
> 영어 지시문(컷에 붙일 것):
> `FOOTPRINT RULE: the trail grows toward the duck, but every toe points back toward where the trail began. Follow the trail from its oldest end and all the toes face you. The duck's bill faces the same way the toes do - i.e. away from the direction it is moving.`

**규칙 B — 앰버 스케줄.** 컷마다 `AMBER:` 줄을 반드시 읽는다. `none` 이면 화면에 앰버가 **한 점도 없다.**

## A-04 §3. 캐릭터 시트 (🔴 이것부터 굽는다)

```
CHARACTER SHEET - DuckBack   (bake this FIRST, before any scene)

🔴 THE SHEET IS DRAWN IN THE SAME MEDIUM AS THE BOOK. Soft graphite pencil and diluted ink wash
  on cool grey-white toothed paper #E9EAE7. The pencil grain must be visible on the body, the
  contour must be left open in places, and the graphite must be pressed dark on the feet.
  Do NOT render this character smoothly just because there is no background behind it.
  There is NO amber anywhere on this sheet.

FACE: a long flat bill, the tip turned very slightly DOWN, so a small sulk is built into the
  anatomy and survives every mood - this is the single most important design decision.
  Eye = a small dark almond #1F2427 with a SEPARATE drawn eyebrow stroke above it, set low and
  close to the bill. No dot eye, no blush, no highlight dot, no glossy catchlight.
FEATHERS: body a soft mid graphite grey #6E7A80 with the paper showing through on the breast;
  wing coverts a darker pressed grey #2E3438 in three or four short strokes, not feather by
  feather; throat and cheek left as bare paper.
BILL & FEET: bill in mid graphite. 🔴 FEET ARE THE PLOT - webbed feet drawn deliberately large
  for the body, pressed the darkest on the whole character #1F2427, with the three toes clearly
  separated so a footprint can be recognised as this duck's foot.
BUILD & SILHOUETTE: a fat teardrop body about two and a half heads tall, carried LOW and level
  on a long neck that it holds forward and down rather than up - most storybook ducks hold the
  neck high, this one does not, because it is always looking at the ground and behind itself.
  Legs set well back so it leans.
SIGNATURE DETAIL: 🔴 ONE crooked tail feather that sticks out the opposite way from all the
  others. It is the whole book in one feather, and it is how the reader tells this duck from
  the five others. Keep it in every single drawing, including the back views.
THE OTHER FIVE DUCKS: identical species and silhouette, ALWAYS seen from behind, never given a
  face, never given the crooked feather. They exist only as a row of same-shaped backs.
REFERENCE SHEET: full-body side view standing / three-quarter turn stepping BACKWARDS with one
  webbed foot reaching behind / back view showing the crooked tail feather / a large detail of
  one webbed foot and, next to it, the wet print that foot leaves /
  three expression close-ups: sulking (brow low and level, eye half lidded, bill tipped down),
  startled (brow high, eye wide and round, bill open a crack),
  quietly pleased (eye a downward crescent, brow relaxed, neck loose).
  Plain paper background, no scenery, no amber.
SCENE token: DuckBack.
```

## A-04 §4. 12컷

각 컷은 `STYLE ANCHOR + FOOTPRINT RULE + @image1(시트) + 아래 블록` 으로 합성한다.

### p1 — 무리는 앞으로, 오리만 등을 돌린다
```
CAMERA: wide, low angle at 40cm above the ground. Reverse composition - the flock walks away
  into the depth of the frame at the left, the duck alone faces us in the right foreground.
SUBJECT: DuckBack stands still in the right foreground, bill tipped down, brow low and level,
  eyelids half down. One webbed foot has already stepped half a pace BACKWARD onto wet brick.
  Its eye is on its own feet, not on the flock. Mid-ground to far: five identical ducks in a
  single file, backs only, no faces, all necks angled the same way, walking away.
SETTING: brick canal quay at seven in the morning, mist lying at ankle height on the water.
FOOTPRINTS: none yet, except one single half print appearing under the foot that just moved back.
FINISH: DuckBack finished. The wet brick directly under its feet half-finished. The five ducks,
  the gabled houses, the tulips, the two bicycles and the mooring rope are all ROUGH UNFINISHED
  PENCIL SKETCH - open contours and direction strokes, no tone, paper showing through.
TONE: low back-light. Only the rim of each duck's back catches light; faces are in shade.
AMBER: none.
```

### p2 — 오리는 뒤로 걸었다 (후렴 1/3)
```
CAMERA: extreme close-up, frog's eye pinned to the ground. The bottom two thirds of the frame
  is wet brick paving.
SUBJECT: only DuckBack's two webbed feet, large in frame. The right foot is stretched BACKWARD
  feeling for the ground, toes spread and tense; the left foot still bears weight in front.
  Above the top crop line, only the underside of the breast and the crooked tail feather.
FOOTPRINTS: 🔴 five wet prints, this is the page that teaches the rule - the trail runs from the
  far side of the frame toward the feet, and every toe points AWAY from the feet, back down the
  trail. Draw them as the darkest, most pressed marks on the page.
SETTING: water standing in every brick joint, moss in the cracks, one blown tulip petal.
FINISH: feet and the five prints finished and pressed dark. The brick joints they sit in
  half-finished. Everything beyond - the far feet of the flock, the quay edge - rough sketch
  lines only.
TONE: reflected light coming up off the wet ground from below.
AMBER: none.
```

### p3 — 빵집이 굴뚝부터 지나갔다
```
CAMERA: high angle from third-floor window height, strong diagonal, DuckBack very small at
  lower right (about 1/9 of page height).
SUBJECT: DuckBack moving backward, its back toward the direction of travel, neck stretched UP
  to look at the chimney, bill parted, brow raised in surprise. Its feet are far apart, which
  is what shows it is walking backwards.
SETTING: 🔴 the building reads in reverse order from top to bottom - brick chimney and white
  smoke fill the top of the frame, then the tilted roof, then the attic window, and only at the
  very bottom the bakery front with its window and its hanging board.
  🔴 THE SHOP BOARD CARRIES NO LETTERING - draw it as a plain painted board or a simple pretzel
  shape hanging on an iron bracket. No letters anywhere in this image.
FOOTPRINTS: a short trail behind and above the duck, all toes pointing back up the slope.
FINISH: DuckBack and the chimney it is looking at are finished. The smoke is one pass of grey
  wash. The roof, attic window, shopfront, crates and milk churn are rough unfinished sketch -
  do not draw the loaves, the panes or the tiles.
TONE: morning light from above; the smoke reads bright, the street reads dark.
AMBER: none. 🔴 The bakery window is NOT lit - this is the page where the reader first wants
  warmth and does not get it.
```

### p4 — 오리는 뒤로 걸었다 (후렴 2/3)
```
CAMERA: medium, over the shoulder - we look past the back of DuckBack's head at the road it has
  already covered. Shallow depth.
SUBJECT: foreground lower left, the back of the head, neck and shoulders of DuckBack, large and
  out of focus, the crooked tail feather just in frame. The neck tips slightly left for balance.
FOOTPRINTS: 🔴 THIS IS THE PAGE WHERE THE PRINTS ARE IN FOCUS AND THE DUCK IS NOT. The trail
  runs from deep in the frame straight toward the duck, and every toe faces the camera.
  They are the sharpest, darkest thing in the picture.
SETTING: the rising quay toward the drawbridge, a lamp post, a rowing boat tied at the water,
  the reflection of the far houses on the canal.
FINISH: the trail finished and pressed dark. The wet brick around it half-finished. Everything
  else - the lamp post, the boat, the far houses, the duck's own out-of-focus head - rough
  unfinished sketch strokes. The out-of-focus head is drawn with FEWER, looser lines, not with
  a blur filter.
TONE: soft side light, the depth of the frame slightly brighter than the foreground.
AMBER: none.
```

### p5 — 다리가 아래부터 지나갔다
```
CAMERA: low angle, looking straight up from beneath the bridge. The timber structure crosses the
  whole frame in near symmetry.
SUBJECT: DuckBack at bottom centre, backing into the shadow under the bridge. Head thrown fully
  back to look at the underside, eye wide and round, brow high, bill tip pointing straight up.
  Its tail and crooked feather are still out in the light.
SETTING: heavy timber beams and blackened wet grain, nail marks, ONE bird's nest wedged in a
  joint, a chain wound on a pulley, a green algae line where the water reaches.
FINISH: DuckBack finished. The one beam directly above its head and the nest half-finished -
```

# A-11 「내가 안 그랬어」

주제군 A 마음·감정 / 엔진 **누적·반복** / 무대 체코 붉은 기와 지붕 / 주인공 까치 / 본문 870자

## A-11 §1. 앵커 배정

### 이 권이 그림에 요구하는 것

1. **목격자 수가 정보다.** 1→2→3→4→6→전부. 독자가 **셀 수 있어야** 한다. 셀 수 있으려면 목격자가 **같은 도형의 반복**으로 나타나야 한다 — 창문 하나하나를 다르게 그리면 개수가 안 읽히고 그냥 복잡해진다.
2. **p8과 p12가 같은 앵글의 대구다.** 열린 창 전부 ↔ 닫히는 창 전부. **두 쪽이 한 장의 판에서 찍힌 것처럼 보여야** 대구가 성립한다.
3. **범인의 색 = 증거의 색.** 까치는 검정, 흙도 검정, 발자국도 검정. 까치는 자기 몸의 색으로 잡힌다.
4. 지붕 기와를 한 장씩 다 그리면 창에서 고개 내민 이웃이 안 보인다. 밀도는 **얼굴에만.**

## A-11 §2. STYLE ANCHOR

```
STYLE ANCHOR - changjak-a11  (magpie / Czech rooftops / accumulating witnesses)

Style: a hand-printed picture-book page for 4-6 year olds. Graphic, countable, a little stiff -
  the stiffness is the point, because this is a book about being caught.

MEDIUM: a two-block relief print - linocut. The image is CUT, not drawn.
  Wide blunt gouge marks. The ink sits unevenly and skips where the block did not take, so
  every flat area has small bare specks in it. Contours are chipped and slightly wobbly because
  a knife made them, and corners of shapes are cut square rather than rounded.
  White lines exist only where material was carved AWAY from a block - never as a painted
  highlight. Where the two blocks overlap, the ink simply gets darker; that overprint is the
  ONLY shading that exists anywhere in this book. No modelling, no light-source shading.
  Slight registration drift between the two blocks is welcome.

PALETTE: two inks on oat-grey printing paper. That is the whole book.
  Hex anchors: paper #E4E0D6 / terracotta ink #C4552F (a light half-covered pass reads #E0A88E) /
  black ink #1E1A18 / overprint of terracotta under black #5A2A22.
  TERRACOTTA IS THE WORLD: roof tiles, the flowerpot, its seven shards, the chimneys, the
  geranium. It covers large areas and is never a small accent.
  🔴 BLACK IS THE EVIDENCE AND THE WITNESSES: the magpie, the spilled soil, the footprints, and
  every OPEN WINDOW. An open window is a black rectangle cut into the terracotta roof field, and
  a closed window is left as bare paper with a black outline. That is the whole visual engine of
  this book - the number of solid black rectangles is the number of eyes on the magpie.
  Paper is the sky. There is no blue, no green and no third ink anywhere.

COMPOSITION: the roofscape is a field of terracotta bands stepping across the frame.
  🔴 EVERY DORMER WINDOW IS THE SAME RECTANGLE, DRAWN AT THE SAME ANGLE. They must be countable
  at a glance, the way beads on an abacus are countable. Do not vary them decoratively.
  Keep the bottom 18% of the image quiet and free of key subject matter (caption band).

FINISH HIERARCHY - read this twice. This is about how FINISHED each area is, NOT about opacity.
  1. THE MAGPIE AND THE FACES IN THE WINDOWS = finished. Cut line, solid ink, worked.
  2. WHAT THE MAGPIE TOUCHES ON THAT PAGE (the shard under its foot, the sill, the soil) =
     half-finished: one contour cut plus one ink pass.
  3. EVERYTHING ELSE = A LOOSE UNFINISHED SKETCH ON THE BLOCK: a few open gouge lines and
     direction strokes indicating where the roofs and chimneys go, barely any ink, bare paper
     showing through, shapes left unclosed, deliberately not carved to completion.
  🔴 The roofs are NOT faded, NOT hazy, NOT blurred and NOT desaturated. They are simply not cut
  to completion. 🔴 NEVER CARVE EVERY ROOF TILE. If the tiles are all drawn, the neighbours
  leaning out of the windows disappear into the pattern, and the whole book stops working.
  The density lives in the FACES, always.

CHARACTER DESIGN: heads are solid masses of ink; the eye is a small WHITE DOT CUT OUT of that
  mass, with a SHORT SEPARATE GOUGE LINE above it for a brow, so a stiff printed face can still
  act. Open beaks are cut out as bright wedges of bare paper, which makes an open beak readable
  from across the page. Bodies are single silhouettes; posture and tail angle do the acting.

SETTING: the old town of a Czech city seen from the rooftops - steep terracotta pantile roofs
  stepping in layers, dormer windows, low chimneys, green-copper gutters (rendered in
  terracotta+overprint, not green), stone steps, a cobbled lane below, a square clock tower far
  off. European, no Asian architectural motifs.

CANVAS: 16:9 double-page spread, 4-6 year old picture book.

NOT: NOT digital airbrush / NOT smooth gradients / NOT glossy 3D CG render / NOT cel-shaded
  anime / NOT a woodcut texture filter laid over flat digital colour (the cut must MAKE the
  shapes, not sit on top of them) / NOT photographic / NOT a fully rendered background /
  NOT every roof tile, brick or leaf carved to completion / NOT a uniform finish across the
  page / NOT a hazy, blurry or faded background (that is blur, not un-carved) /
  NOT a third ink colour / NOT any lettering, numerals or signage anywhere in the image /
  NOT wool felt, NOT stitched fabric, NOT sculpted clay (another line owns those).
```

## 두 권 나란히 보기 (라인 정체 점검)

🔴 라이브러리 카드가 **가로로 이웃**하므로, 두 권의 첫 렌더를 **나란히 놓고** 마지막으로 한 번 더 본다.
- 종이색이 다른가(회백 ↔ 오트) · 지배색이 다른가(무채 ↔ 테라코타) · 획이 다른가(눌러 그린 연필 ↔ 칼자국) · 얼굴 문법이 다른가(아몬드 눈+눈썹 선 ↔ 오려낸 흰 점+눈썹 홈).
- 넷 중 셋 이하만 다르면 앵커 하나를 다시 고른다. **"권마다 다 다르다"가 이 라인의 유일한 정체다.**
