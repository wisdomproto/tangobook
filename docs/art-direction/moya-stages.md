## §0. 🔴 이 시리즈의 매체 번역 — 셋이다

**① 사물의 기본값은 「선이 없는 것」이다.**

앵커: `(2) THE GROUND UNDERFOOT AND WHATEVER GROWS OUT OF IT - painted on DAMP paper: 4 to 6 touches per
thing, edges feathering together, NO LINE ANYWHERE`. 물감과 선이 만나는 것은 **아이와 그 쪽이 도는 것
하나**뿐이다(`FINISHED THINGS PER PAGE = 2`). 그러니 사물 시트가 정할 것은 모양이 아니라
**「이 사물이 선을 갖는 쪽이 어디인가」**다 — 가시는 11권 p8에서만, 새순은 20권 p9에서만, 금은 19권
p9에서만 선을 갖는다. 나머지 쪽에서 같은 물건은 축축한 종이 위 터치 넷이다.

**② 색을 섞지 않으므로 「흙탕물」도 「새순」도 없는 색이다.**

앵커: `NO COLOUR IS EVER MIXED TO MAKE ANOTHER ONE … AND THERE IS NO GREEN IN THIS PALETTE`.
25권을 통틀어 이 시리즈가 계속 부딪히는 물건이 셋인데 셋 다 **팔레트에 없는 색**이다 —
흐려진 물(갈색) · 새순(초록) · 연기(회색). 답은 전부 같다: **옆에 놓는다.**
흐린 물 = RED EARTH 터치가 DEEP BLUE 옆에 · 새순 = LEMON 옆에 SKY 하나 · 그림자와 선 = 전부 VIOLET.
🔴 **검정과 회색은 없다** — 16권의 「크고 뾰족한 것」이 검정이 되는 순간 이 그림체가 아니게 된다.

**③ 개수를 세게 하는 쪽은 권마다 하나뿐이다.**

대본이 그렇게 못 박아 뒀다(19 p9 `이 쪽에서만 세게 한다` · 21 p6 `개수를 또렷이 세게 하는 쪽은 여기
하나`). 그러니 사물 시트는 **그 한 쪽의 개수를 확정하고, 나머지 쪽에서는 못 세게** 해야 한다 —
겹쳐 놓거나, 화면 밖으로 흘리거나, 「한 무리」로 뭉친다. 안 그러면 아이가 매 쪽 다시 세다가
19권이 무슨 이야기였는지를 잃는다.

⚠️ **넷째로 지킬 것** — `MAGENTA #D6417A` 는 스물다섯 권을 통틀어 **모야 목의 땋은 끈 하나**다.
열매도, 진흙도, 새도, 새순도, 노을도 그 색이 아니다. 아래 열한 장 전부 `NOT` 에 박아 뒀다.

### 🔴 「무리」와 「나무」 — 한 글자에 두 낱말이 있다

퐁이의 「의자 다리 / 다리(橋)」와 같은 함정이 이 시리즈에 **둘** 있다. 접었으면 두 권이 죽는다.

- **무리** = ①15·09·10 의 **지나가며 물 마시는 떼**(등의 줄무늬가 **전부 곧다** — 모야 이마의 구불구불한
  줄 하나가 그것과 달라야 15권이 성립한다) ↔ ②06·16·25 의 **둥글게 누워 자는 고리**(안쪽에 아이를 둔다)
  ↔ ③12·18 의 **누 떼**(다른 종 · 손님 · 길을 가로질러 지나간다). 배치도, 뜻도, 개수도 다르다.
  → 한 시트 안에서 **세 배치로 갈라 두고 이름을 붙였다.**
- **나무** = ①앵커의 **아카시아**(납작한 우듬지 · 그늘이 이 책의 가장 큰 보라) ↔ ②04 의 **열매 나무**
  (아카시아보다 낮고 잎이 뭉쳐 있다) ↔ ③20 의 **작은 아카시아**(가지가 부러지고 새순이 난다).
  → 사물 시트로 접지 않고 **자리 시트의 구멍으로 신고**했다(§1). 사물은 그 나무가 떨군 것(`Branch` ·
  `Fruit`)뿐이다.

### §2.1 Herd — 🔴 15권은 무리의 줄무늬가 전부 곧아야만 성립한다

> 이 권의 그림 하나는 **모야 이마의 구불구불한 줄**이고, 그건 **둘레가 곧을 때만 구불구불하다.**
> p2·p5·p9 가 전부 「무리의 등 — 줄무늬가 모두 곧다」로 쓰여 있다. 무리를 대충 그리면 이 권에 사건이 없다.

```
PROP SHEET - Herd   (moya-daylight · SCENE token: Herd)

The grown-up zebras and the other animals that cross behind the children. Ten books put them on the page
and they are never the subject - but in one book the whole story is that THEY ARE ALL STRAIGHT and one
child is not, so this sheet decides them.

🔴 EVERY FIGURE ON THIS SHEET IS FINISH (3): painted on SOAKING paper, one bloom each, 0 countable
  touches, NO VIOLET LINE ANYWHERE, no face, no eye, no muzzle, no hoof. The anchor allows two finished
  things a page and neither of them is ever a herd animal.
🔴 THREE ARRANGEMENTS, AND THEY ARE NEVER MIXED IN ONE FRAME:
  A PASSING LINE - backs only, overlapping, walking one way across the far middle of the picture. Read as
    a row of BACKS: a soft top edge with SUN OCHRE, and 🔴 THE STRIPES ON THEM ARE STRAIGHT - short
    VIOLET touches running straight down each back, all of them, with no bend, no wave and no exception.
    One child in another book has ONE bent stripe on her forehead and it is the only bent stripe that
    exists in twenty-five books.
  A SLEEPING RING - grown zebras lying in a closed circle, heads inward, seen from above. Backs only, no
    faces, no legs drawn. 🔴 THE RING IS ALWAYS CLOSED AND THE CHILD IS ALWAYS INSIDE IT - that enclosure
    is the picture, and a gap in the ring would say the opposite of what the page says.
  A CROSSING OF STRANGERS (wildebeest) - one long single bloom drawn as a horizontal smear across the
    middle distance, darker than the zebra line, with NO stripes at all, no horns, no eyes, no faces, and
    no countable touch anywhere in it. It reads as many only by being long.
🔴 NEAR AND FAR ARE THE SAME PAINT. A herd animal never comes to the front of the picture. If a page
  needs an adult near the child, that adult is the mother and she is a cast figure, not this sheet.
STATES:
  1 A PASSING LINE across the middle distance, backs, straight violet stripes, walking left to right.
  2 THE SAME LINE FURTHER OFF - one bloom with the stripe touches almost gone.
  3 A SLEEPING RING from above, closed, a small bright gap at the centre for the children.
  4 A SLEEPING RING from ground level between two bodies - two soft backs filling the left and right
    edges and the sky between them.
  5 THE WHOLE RING VERY FAR - one small closed loop on an empty plain, nobody identifiable.
  6 A CROSSING SMEAR of strangers on a road, one long dark bloom, the near ground quiet and empty.
  7 A HERD COMING TO DRINK - a line of backs lowering, the tops of the backs stepping down.

PLATE: states 1 and 2 side by side, then 3 4 5, then 6 and 7, plus one large panel of three backs at
  close range showing that the stripes on them are dead straight.

🔴 WHAT IS BANNED: no flame, no orange, no ember, no glow at the base, no ash, no burnt ground, no
  running animals, no smell lines, no birds fleeing. The only thing that says "we should go" on those
  pages is one adult stopping and turning her head, and that is a cast drawing, not this sheet.
🔴 DISTANT SMOKE AND NEAR DUST ARE DIFFERENT THINGS. Dust (see GroundMarks) is at hoof height, in the
  foreground, and RED EARTH; smoke is at the horizon, in the sky band, and VIOLET. They are never in one
  frame together.
STATES - all three drawn at ONE size and ONE horizon height so they can be compared:
  1 FIRST - a small violet stain sitting on the horizon, sky above it a single wash, plain below it.
  2 SPREAD - the same stain twice as wide, the same height, the same value.
  3 GOING - the stain drawn out sideways and thinning to nothing at both ends.
  4 THE SAME HORIZON WITH NOTHING ON IT, so the three can be checked against a clean sky.

PLATE: the four states stacked one above the other at one size, plus one large panel of state 2 showing
  that there is not a single countable touch in it.

NOT: no character, 🔴 no fire, flame, ember, spark or orange of any kind, no glow at the base, no ash or
burnt ground, no magenta, no black and no grey, no countable touch, no drawn edge or outline, no violet
line, no column that rises or darkens between states, no fleeing animals or birds, no airbrush, gradient,
glow or 3D render.
```

### §2.11 Bird — 24권 (점인 것과 마감되는 것)

```
PROP SHEET - Bird   (moya-daylight · SCENE token: Bird)

Birds over the reeds. In most books they are two or three dots and nothing else; in one book a big bird
sits on the reeds through the whole story and a young one lands beside a meerkat at the end - so this
sheet decides when a bird gets a body.

🔴 TWO KINDS, AND A PAGE HAS ONE OR THE OTHER, NEVER BOTH:
  A DOT BIRD - finish (3): one small VIOLET touch with a hint of a spread, at most 3 of them in a frame,
    high and far, no head, no beak, no legs, no eye. This is what a bird is on nineteen pages of this
    series and it must stay that cheap.
  🔴 A FINISHED BIRD - finish (1), the only pages where a bird is one of the two finished things: 9 to 12
    separate touches, SUN OCHRE body with SKY touches in the wings and paper between them, and the thin
    VIOLET line on the LEGS AND THE BEAK ONLY. 🔴 Paint and line never describe the same part - the body,
    the wings and the tail are paint with no line; the legs, the toes and the beak edge are line with no
    paint. The eye is ONE violet almond as wide as its own nostril, the anchor's eye, unchanged.
🔴 A BIG BIRD PERCHED ON REEDS bends the reeds it stands on: the reed touches under it lean and the
  bird's weight is read from that, never from a drawn shadow.
🔴 THE YOUNG BIRD IS THE SAME DRAWING, SMALLER AND ROUNDER - shorter beak, shorter legs, wings half
  folded, and it is drawn AT THE SAME DEPTH as the meerkat it faces so their heights compare. That
  comparison is the point of the page.
🔴 A BIRD DOES NOT DO ANYTHING FRIGHTENING. No hooked beak, no talons, no diving, no prey, no open beak
  full of anything. Wings open is a resting spread, not a strike.
STATES:
  1 THREE DOT BIRDS high over reeds, at most 3 violet touches, nothing else.
  2 A FINISHED BIG BIRD perched on reed tops, wings folded, the reeds beneath it leaning.
  3 THE SAME BIRD, wings spread wide and still, perched.
  4 THE SAME BIRD IN THE AIR, wings out, seen from below, legs tucked - still 9 to 12 touches.
  5 THE YOUNG BIRD landing on a rock top, wings half folded, one foot just down.
  6 🔴 THE YOUNG BIRD AND A MEERKAT facing each other at the same depth, standing, so the two heights
    compare. Nothing else in the frame.
  7 A DOT BIRD AND A FINISHED BIRD drawn on one sheet panel at their true relative sizes, so the two
    kinds are never confused.

PLATE: state 7 first, then 1-6, plus a large close-up of one wing showing ochre and sky touches with
  paper between them and no line.

NOT: 🔴 no magenta anywhere, no black or grey, no hooked beak, talon, prey, dive or strike, no open beak
with anything in it, no feather detail, barbs or drawn plumage pattern, no violet line on a body, wing
or tail, no motion lines behind a flying bird, no cast shadow under a perched bird, no airbrush,
gradient, glow or 3D render.
```

### §2.12 Sun — 🔴 앵커에 해가 없다 (6권)

> `THE BIG SKY` 조항은 하늘의 **색**만 정하고(`dry: lemon meeting sky blue high up`), **해는 한 번도 안
> 나온다.** 그런데 06권은 열 쪽 중 여섯 쪽이 해의 높이로 시간을 재고(「반쯤 넘어간」→「넘어가기 직전」→
> 「해 뜬 직후」), 07·22·25 도 해를 등지거나 낮게 걸어 둔다. 흰 물감이 없고 글로우가 금지된 팔레트에서
> **해는 그리는 게 아니라 남기는 것**이라는 판단이 아무 데도 없다.

```
PROP SHEET - Sun   (moya-daylight · SCENE token: Sun)

The sun on the horizon, and the stars. Six books use the sun's height as their clock - one book walks
from late afternoon to sunrise across ten pages - and the anchor has no clause for it, so this sheet
decides it.

🔴 THE SUN IS BARE PAPER. It is not painted; it is the one round area the brush went around, so it is
  exactly the same warm rag as everything else that is bright in this book. Its edge is read only by the
  sky wash laid up to it. 🔴 NEVER outline it, never paint it lemon, never paint it orange, and 🔴 NEVER
  put a glow, halo, ray, beam or lens flare anywhere near it - the anchor bans glow outright and this is
  where an illustrator will reach for one.
🔴 ITS SIZE NEVER CHANGES between books or between pages. Its height above the horizon is the ONLY thing
  that moves, and that height is the clock: HIGH (morning and midday, the disc small against a big pale
  sky) · LOW (late afternoon, the disc sitting a disc's width above the land) · HALF DOWN (the horizon
  cutting the disc, the paper still one clean shape) · GONE (no disc at all, and the sky wash a deeper
  drop at the top edge). Draw the four at one size on one sheet - that row IS the clock.
🔴 A SKY WITH THE SUN IN IT IS STILL FINISH (3): one bloom, 0 countable touches, no line. The sun does
  not get its own touches; it gets the absence of them.
🔴 BACKLIT IS THE HARD PAGE AND THERE IS NO BLACK TO DO IT WITH. When a figure stands between the reader
  and a low sun, it is NOT a silhouette: it is the same animal at its usual finish, and what says
  "backlit" is that 🔴 THE EDGE OF THE BODY ON THE SUN SIDE IS BARE PAPER - the touches stop short of the
  outline and let the paper run round it - while the far side carries an extra VIOLET touch laid beside
  it. No rim light, no halo, no darkened body.
🔴 STARS ARE THE SAME DECISION, SMALLER: unpainted specks left in a deep-blue soaking sky, at most 20,
  scattered without pattern, never five-pointed and never drawn as a shape.
🔴 THE SUN IS NEVER IN THE WATER. The anchor forbids anything drawn upside down in water - a sun on the
  surface is horizontal touches of the sky's own colours and nothing round.
STATES - the first four drawn in one row at ONE size and ONE horizon height:
  1 HIGH - a small paper disc in a pale sky, the plain bright beneath it.
  2 LOW - the same disc a disc's width above the land, the sky wash warmer at that edge.
  3 HALF DOWN - the horizon cutting the disc, the remaining paper one clean shape.
  4 GONE - no disc, the sky wash dropped deeper at the top edge.
  5 BACKLIT - a plain zebra standing between the reader and state 2, its sun-side edge bare paper, one
    violet touch beside its far side. No silhouette, no rim light.
  6 STARS - a deep soaking night sky with unpainted specks, at most 20.
  7 THE SUN BEHIND A ROCK HILL - the disc half hidden by the hill's shape, the hill's top still bare
    paper as the anchor's rock clause requires.

PLATE: states 1-4 in one row at one size, then 5 6 7, plus one large panel of the sun's edge showing the
  wash laid up to bare paper with no outline.

NOT: 🔴 no glow, halo, ray, beam, corona, lens flare or sunburst of any kind, no orange, gold, crimson or
magenta anywhere, no outline round the disc, no lemon or ochre painted INSIDE the disc, no black or grey,
no silhouetted figure, no rim light, 🔴 no sun or star reflected in the water, no five-pointed stars, no
airbrush, gradient or 3D render.
```
