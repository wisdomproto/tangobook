## §0. 🔴 매체 번역 한 줄 — 둘이다

**① 깊이가 어둡기가 아니라 간격이다. 그러므로 골목 시트가 정하는 것은 「집이 몇 채, 어떤 간격으로」다.**

앵커: `DEPTH IS SPACING, NEVER DARKNESS - houses further down the alley stand CLOSER TOGETHER at the
same weight of paper. Nothing recedes by getting paler, bluer or softer.`
🔴 멀리 있는 집을 흐리게·푸르게·작게 흐리면 이 매체가 통째로 부정된다. 골목 시트는 **원근이 아니라
간격표**다 — 앞에서 뒤로 가며 집 간격이 좁아지는 수열을 시트가 확정한다. 그게 없으면 권마다 골목 깊이가
달라진다.

**② 바닥은 그리는 게 아니라 종이를 얹었다 떼는 것이다.**

앵커: 눈 = 둘째 흰 종이를 **찢어 얹은 것** / 쓸거나 미끄러진 자리 = 그 종이를 **찢어 낸 것**(밑의 회색이
드러나고 그 가장자리도 섬유질). 🔴 **그러니 이 시리즈에서 「자국」은 그린 선이 아니라 종이가 없는 자리다.**
01권이 통째로 그 이야기다(자국 하나 없는 눈 → 끊긴 발자국 → 온통 밟힌 눈 → 다시 덮인 눈).
→ `Alley` 시트는 **바닥 종이 여덟 장**을 함께 굽는다(온전 · 자국 · 쓸어 냄 · 다시 덮임 · 언 바닥 ·
녹는 중 · 🔴 **파낸 구멍** · 🔴 **눈에 그은 자국**).

🔴 **뒤의 둘은 나중에 붙였고, 붙인 이유가 같다 — 종이가 없는 자리의 종류가 하나가 아니다.**
발자국은 **밟아서** 생긴 작은 구멍이고, 12권은 **손으로 파서** 낸 큰 구멍이며(p3 「파헤쳐진 구멍 둘」·
p5·p6), 16권은 **손가락으로 그어서** 낸 가는 고리다(p4 「동그라미 셋」·p5 「잔뜩」). 셋 다 그린 선이
아니라 뜯긴 자리인데 **모양과 크기가 달라서**, 한 상태로 묶으면 화가가 셋을 같은 것으로 그린다.

⚠️ 셋째로 지킬 것 — `not one mechanically straight edge in this book`. 벽·계단·덧문·문틀이 **다 조금씩
휜다**. 시트가 자로 잰 듯 반듯하면 컷이 그걸 따라간다.

### §1.1 Alley — 실제 프롬프트 (가장 먼저)

```
STAGE SHEET - Alley   (nono-tornpaper · SCENE token: Alley · bake FIRST)

The old stone alley the whole series lives in. Ten of the twenty-five books stand here, so this
drawing decides it once.

🔴 DEPTH IS SPACING. The alley runs away from the reader and the houses down it stand CLOSER
  TOGETHER as they go - at the SAME weight of paper, the SAME grey, the SAME size of tear. Set the
  spacing here and never change it: the four nearest doorways are a hand apart, the next three half
  that, the last two almost touching, and the alley ends at a low wall. NOTHING recedes by getting
  paler, bluer, smaller or softer, and there is no sky colour at the far end - it is bare GROUND
  SHEET, and that is the light of the page.

FIXED PARTS - every one torn by hand, edge fibrous, and NOT ONE MECHANICALLY STRAIGHT EDGE:
  WALLS both sides in STONE GREY, wobbling slightly as they go up.
  SHUTTERS down the alley, at most 8, one torn shape each, no two the same, none mirroring a
    neighbour. DOORS and beams in WOOD BROWN.
  ROOF CAPS of TORN WHITE snow along every eave. Icicles at most 7.
  THREE STEPS up at the near end, each step's edge wobbling.
  DOORWAYS are OVERLAP - two papers pasted over each other, and that overlap is the only dark.

🔴 THE FLOOR IS A SHEET THAT COMES AND GOES - draw all four states:
  1 UNTOUCHED - the alley floor is ONE unbroken piece of TORN WHITE from wall to wall, and the only
    torn edges crossing it are none at all.
  2 TRACKED - the same white piece with footprints; a footprint is a small torn hole where the
    STONE GREY beneath shows, at most 14 of them, in ONE line. 🔴 WHERE THE LINE STOPS IS THE
    SHARPEST EDGE ON THE SHEET.
    🔴 THE COUNT OF 14 IS FOR PRINTS YOU CAN COUNT. When the book says the snow is trampled ALL
    OVER (01 p7) or the running is SCRIBBLED ACROSS IT (03 p5), that is not 30 prints - it is ONE
    AREA torn away in small ragged bites, and it is drawn as one shape with grey showing through,
    never as a tally. 🔴 IF IT CAN BE COUNTED IT OBEYS 14; IF IT IS A MESS IT IS ONE PIECE.
  3 SWEPT - the white piece TORN AWAY in a curve so a broad area of STONE GREY cobbles is bare, the
    fibrous edge of the tear left showing, and the swept snow heaped at one side as a second white
    piece.
  4 COVERED AGAIN - back to state 1, with nothing of state 2 or 3 remaining anywhere.
  🔴 5 FROZEN (volume 06) - the white piece is GONE and the floor is ONE flat piece of STONE GREY,
    smooth, with TORN WHITE only at the very edges of the alley. A reflection on it is ONE MORE
    PIECE OF THE SAME PAPER laid below the wall with 0 interior detail - a smear, never a mirrored
    copy. 🔴 THE SMOOTH AREA IS THE DANGEROUS AREA, and ash thrown down on it is at most 11 WOOD
    BROWN specks in ONE BAND - that band is the safe path and it must read as rough against smooth.
  🔴 6 THAWING (volumes 23, 24) - the white piece has been torn away in PATCHES so wet STONE GREY
    shows through in the middle and white survives only along the walls. 🔴 THE AREA OF WHITE IS THE
    CLOCK: it shrinks page by page and never grows back within a volume. Puddles are one flat piece
    of the darker overlap, 0 reflections.
  🔴 7 DUG (volume 12) - holes torn out of the white piece BY HAND, not by feet. A dug hole is
    THREE TO FOUR TIMES the width of a footprint, its torn edge raggeder, and the snow taken out of
    it lies beside it as small white scraps ON TOP of the sheet. At most 6 holes. 🔴 THEY
    ACCUMULATE ACROSS THE BOOK AND NEVER CLOSE - the last page of that volume still has them, and
    at dusk they are the only thing left of the digging, read as bare grey with no scraps visible.
  🔴 8 SCORED (volume 16) - circles a finger drew in the snow. A circle is a THIN TORN RING, one
    finger wide, grey showing through it, the ring never closing perfectly and never twice the same
    size. AT MOST 3 WHEN THEY ARE BEING COUNTED (16 p4 says three); when the book says the snow is
    covered in them (16 p5) it is ONE AREA of overlapping rings, not a tally. 🔴 NOT A DRAWN LINE -
    if it reads as ink or crayon on top of the paper the medium is broken. It is paper missing.
    🔴 STATE 8 SITS ON THE HALF OF THE ALLEY THAT IS STILL WHITE while state 3 (swept) holds the
    other half - 16 p5 splits the screen down the middle and both must be legible in one frame.

SPOTS:
  A FROM THE ENTRANCE, wide, eye level: both walls, the spacing running away, the far low wall.
  B THE MIDDLE, medium: two facing doorways, floor across the bottom.
  C THE FLOOR, close, high: the torn white and whatever is or is not on it.
  D FROM THE WALL TOP looking down: the alley floor seen whole, so a track can be read as a line.

PLATE: A, B, C, D once each, plus the EIGHT FLOOR STATES drawn as eight panels of the same frame,
  and one spacing diagram of the doorway intervals.

NOT: no character of any kind, no wool green anywhere on this sheet (the green touches only the one
  small thing each child carries), no lettering, numerals or shop signs, no fourth paper, no cut or
  scissored edge - every edge is torn, no drawn line of any kind, no white ink or white paint, no
  shading, gradient, glow or cast shadow, nothing paler or bluer with distance.
```

③ **안 쓸린 채**(눈이 문턱까지 그대로 — 16 p1 의 아침).

### §2.1 Stove — 실제 프롬프트 (🔴 3권 공유)

```
PROP SHEET - Stove   (nono-tornpaper · SCENE token: Stove)

The cast-iron stove in the corner shop. Three books sit in front of it, so its shape and - more
importantly - ITS LIGHT are fixed here.

FORM: a squat body of OVERLAP (two papers pasted), a torn round door on the front, a flue rising
  and bending once into the wall, three short legs. All torn, all fibrous, no straight edge, no
  moulding, no drawn detail. A wood basket beside it in WOOD BROWN.
🔴 THE FIRELIGHT IS A TORN PIECE OF WHITE, not a glow. It is pasted ON TOP of whatever it falls on,
  with its own fibrous edge, and it never fades at its border. Draw THREE SIZES of that piece and
  they are the instrument of a whole volume:
  SMALL - reaches one side of one body only.
  MEDIUM - reaches the front edge of two bodies, their backs left as bare paper or overlap.
  LARGE - covers the fronts of three bodies evenly, and 🔴 NO OVERLAP IS LEFT BETWEEN THEIR
    SHOULDERS - that absence is the ending of one book and must be drawable.
  A body far from the stove gets NO white piece at all. It does not get a dimmer one.
STATES: 1 stove alone, door shut · 2 door open, the mouth a torn white hole · 3 the three light
  sizes shown against a single seated shape, side by side at the same distance.

PLATE: the three states, plus the three light sizes as their own strip.

NOT: no character faces on this sheet, no wool green, no lettering or numerals, no fourth paper, no
  cut edge, no drawn line, no white ink or paint, no glow, gradient, halo or soft edge on the fire -
  the light has a torn edge like everything else.
```

### §2.2 Sledge — 실제 프롬프트

```
PROP SHEET - Sledge   (nono-tornpaper · SCENE token: Sledge)

A short wooden sledge. One book spends nine of its ten pages on it.

FORM: WOOD BROWN - a flat deck of three boards torn as one piece, two runners under it torn as one
  more, a rope of the same brown at the front. The runners' underside is OVERLAP. No nails, no
  grain, no drawn line. Every edge fibrous and slightly wobbly; the deck is NOT a rectangle.
STATES:
  1 STANDING on snow, side on, the rope slack in front.
  2 FROM ABOVE, deck seen whole, so its three boards are countable.
  3 IN USE, tipped forward on a slope, rope taut.
  4 UPSIDE DOWN, both runners up.
🔴 SLEDGE TRACKS ARE NOT DRAWN - they are at most 5 lines TORN OUT of the white floor piece so the
  STONE GREY shows, the tear edge fibrous, running the way the sledge went and never crossing
  themselves.

PLATE: the four states plus one panel of the tracks alone on a white floor piece.

NOT: no character, no wool green, no lettering or numerals, no fourth paper, no cut edge, no drawn
  line, no white ink or paint, no motion streaks, no shading, gradient or cast shadow.
```
