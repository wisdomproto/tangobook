# 탱고북 삼국지 — 개체 규격 (cast sheet)

> 시리즈 16 `samgukji` · **시트 54장** — 고정 캐스트 10인(얼굴색 축을 쓰는 사람들) + 조역 44인.
> 🔴 **나이·복장은 여기 없다.** 권 번호가 정하고 SSOT 는 `build-samgukji.mjs` 의 `STAGES` 다 —
>   시트를 뽑을 때 빌더가 `AGES & COSTUMES` 블록을 붙여 주고, 쪽 발주서에는 그 권 한 줄만 나간다.
>   두 곳에 적으면 갈라진다(예전엔 발주서에 네 단계가 통째로 나가 삽화가가 골랐다).
> 🔴 **그림체 SSOT 는 `samgukji-anchor.md`**(수묵 산수 위의 4등신). 여기는 **한 사람씩 무엇이 다른가**만 적는다.
> 🔴 여기 쓰는 것은 **권이 바뀌어도 안 변하는 것**뿐이다 — 얼굴색 · 실루엣 · 어깨 폭 · 악센트 자리.
> 🔴 **등신 비율(4등신)은 앵커가 정한다 — 시트에 다시 쓰지 마라.** 시트가 재는 것은 어깨 폭이다.
> 매체 어휘(획·판·워시·겹수)는 앵커가 정하므로 **여기 쓰지 마라 — 두 곳이 갈라진다.**

## 왜 이 문서가 생겼나 (2026-08-20)

기획서의 캐스트 카드가 **「시그니처 한 줄 + 의상 단계 한 줄」**이었다. 그건 발주서가 아니라 메모다.
`_SERIES-ANCHORS.md` 가 기록한 사고와 같은 자리 — 시리즈 앵커 15개가 캐릭터 지정 없이 만들어졌더니
**15개의 얼굴이 하나**로 수렴했다. 이 라인은 **한 그림체로 24권 600여 컷**을 가고 등장인물이 수십 명이라,
같은 사고가 나면 되돌릴 비용이 다른 어느 라인보다 크다.

🔴 **시트는 마젠타 배경에 인물 하나다.** 「가장 크다」·「제일 작다」는 시트에 비교 대상이 없어 아무 뜻이 없다.
아래 숫자는 전부 **혼자 있어도 읽히는 값**(어깨가 머리 폭의 몇 배인가 · 수염이 몸의 어디까지 오는가 · 색 이름)으로 적었다.

## 열을 가르는 축 — 🔴 얼굴색이 배역을 말하고, 수염 길이가 나이를 말한다

> 🔴 `## ` 는 인물 제목 전용이다.

**축의 출처는 경극 검보(臉譜)다.** 붉은 얼굴 = 충의, 흰 얼굴 = 꾀 많고 찬 사람 — 관우의 「대추빛 얼굴」은
연의의 묘사이기 전에 무대의 약속이고, 우리 캐릭터 바이블이 이미 그걸 받아 적고 있었다. 이 축을 고른 이유 셋:

1. **아이가 색으로 사람을 센다.** 24권에 수십 명이 스쳐 가는데 이름을 다 기억할 수 없다. 얼굴색은 이름보다 먼저 읽힌다.
2. 🔴 **50년이 흘러도 얼굴색은 안 변한다.** 캐릭터 바이블 원칙 1(시그니처 불변)이 **공정에서 저절로 나온다.**
3. **시리즈 15개 어느 축과도 안 겹친다** — 시선·비례과장·못난구체·포즈·크기격차·소품둘·간격·소지품·부품불일치·겹침·형태분해·네거티브·늘임·재질·매체가몸 어디에도 「얼굴에 칠해진 색」이 없다.

🔴 **의상으로 가르지 않았다.** 이 라인은 신분이 오를 때마다 옷이 바뀌는 것(원칙 3)이 곧 성장의 신호라,
옷을 개체 식별에 쓰면 **유비가 왕이 되는 순간 유비를 못 알아본다.**

### 축 ① 얼굴색 — 열 중 여섯이 여기서 갈린다

| 얼굴색 | 누구 | 뜻 |
|---|---|---|
| **대추빛 붉은색** | 관우 | 이 책에서 얼굴이 붉은 사람은 **하나뿐**이다 |
| **검정** | 장비 | 이 책에서 얼굴이 검은 사람은 **하나뿐**이다 |
| **분을 바른 흰색** | 조조 · 동탁 | 둘이 같은 색이므로 **어깨 폭으로 갈린다**(축 ④ · 1.5 ↔ 2.6) |
| **회흑색** | 사마의 | 흰색과 검정 사이 — 조조의 흰 얼굴 옆에 서면 한 단 어둡다 |
| **옥빛 흰색** | 여포 | 조조의 분칠과 달리 **광이 있는 흰색**, 눈썹만 짙다 |
| **안 칠한 맨 얼굴** | 유비 · 제갈량 · 조운 · 손권 | 🔴 넷은 **수염으로 갈린다**(축 ②) |

### 축 ② 수염 — 나이계이자, 맨 얼굴 넷을 가르는 자

🔴 **수염 길이는 시계다.** 얼굴색이 안 변하는 대신 나이는 전부 수염이 진다.

| | 청년 | 장년 | 노년 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **유비** | 수염 0 | **얇은 콧수염 한 줄** | 콧수염 + 짧은 턱수염, 희끗 |
| **관우** | 가슴 위까지 | **가슴 아래까지** | **배꼽까지** + 흰 올이 섞인다 |
| **장비** | 사방으로 뻗침(짧다) | 사방으로 뻗침(길다) | 사방으로 뻗침 + 흰 올 |
| **제갈량** | 수염 0 | **짧고 곧게 다듬은 턱수염** | 같은 수염이 희끗 |
| **조운** | 🔴 **수염 0** | 🔴 **수염 0** — 이 책에서 장년인데 수염이 없는 유일한 사람 | 흰 수염 |
| **조조** | 짧은 검정 팔자 | **팔자 + 턱수염** | 흰 수염 |
| **손권** | 수염 0 | 🔴 **자줏빛 수염** — 이 책에서 색이 있는 수염은 하나뿐 | 자줏빛에 흰 올 |
| **여포** | 수염 0 (끝까지) | — | — |
| **동탁** | — | 짧고 두툼한 검정 | — |
| **사마의** | — | 좁고 길게 늘어뜨림 | 같은 수염이 희끗 |

🔴 **맨 얼굴 넷이 이 표에서 갈린다** — 유비(콧수염 한 줄) · 제갈량(다듬은 턱수염) · 조운(수염 0) · 손권(자줏빛).

### 축 ③ 실루엣 — 통째로 검게 칠했을 때

🔴 **검사 조항이다.** 시트를 뽑았으면 인물을 통째로 검게 메우고 **이 줄과 대조한다.** 안 맞으면 틀린 시트다.

| | 검게 칠했을 때 남는 것 |
|---|---|
| **유비** | 머리 양옆에서 윤곽을 깨는 **큰 귀 두 개** + 허리 양옆에 **칼 두 자루**(이 책에서 칼을 둘 찬 사람은 하나뿐) |
| **관우** | 가슴에서 아래로 늘어진 **수염 덩이 하나** + 어깨 위로 솟은 **긴 자루 하나** |
| **장비** | 🔴 **윤곽이 삐죽삐죽하다** — 수염이 사방으로 뻗어 얼굴 둘레가 매끈하지 않은 유일한 사람 |
| **제갈량** | 🔴 **손에 든 부채 하나** — 이 책에서 무기를 안 든 유일한 어른 · 소매가 손을 덮어 윤곽이 종처럼 넓다 |
| **조운** | **말 위** + 창 하나 + 투구 꼭대기에서 뻗은 술 한 가닥 |
| **조조** | 🔴 **좁은 세로 기둥 + 넓은 네모 소매** — 어깨가 머리 폭의 1.5배뿐이라 동탁 옆에서 가늘다 |
| **손권** | 허리에 **네모난 인장 주머니**(이 책에서 무기 대신 도장을 찬 유일한 사람) |
| **여포** | 🔴 **머리 위로 두 가닥이 길게 솟는다**(투구의 꿩깃) — 열 중 유일 |
| **동탁** | 🔴 **낮고 넓은 둔덕** — 어깨가 머리 폭의 2.6배, 열 중 유일하게 2를 크게 넘는다 |
| **사마의** | **좁은 세로 기둥** + 소매 안에 두 손을 넣어 팔의 윤곽이 없다 |

### 축 ④ 어깨 폭 — 머리 폭의 몇 배인가

🔴 **전원이 4등신이다**(그림체가 그렇게 정했다 — `samgukji-anchor.md`). 그러니 **키로는 아무도 못 가른다.**
대신 **어깨가 머리 폭의 몇 배인가**로 잰다. 이 값은 시트에 인물이 하나뿐이어도 자로 잴 수 있다.

동탁 **2.6** · 여포 2.2 · 장비 2.2 · 관우 2.0 · 조운 1.8 · 유비 1.7 · 손권 1.6 · 사마의 1.5 ·
조조 1.5 · 제갈량 **1.4**

🔴 **동탁이 2.6 으로 혼자 떨어져 있는 것이 조조와 갈리는 값이다** — 둘 다 흰 얼굴이고 4등신이라, 남은 축이
이것 하나다. 🔴 **둘을 나란히 그릴 때 둘 다 날씬하게 그리지 마라.**

**전체 크기**(여럿이 한 화면에 설 때만 쓴다) — 여포·관우가 가장 크고, 조조·동탁이 가장 작다. 🔴 시트에는
비교 대상이 없으므로 **크기로 그리지 말고 어깨 폭으로 그린다.**

### 축 ⑤ 악센트 — 사람마다 한 자리, 옷이 바뀌어도 안 옮긴다

## Liubei

```
LIUBEI - the eldest brother, the one the other two follow. SHOULDERS = 1.7 head-widths across.
FACE: 🔴 UNPAINTED. His is one of four bare faces in this book and the only one that is bare AND
  has a moustache. Broad, calm, wide-set eyes, a mouth that rests almost closed.
🔴 EARS: THE LARGEST EARS IN THE BOOK - each ear is as tall as from his eyebrow to his chin, and
  they stand clear of the head on both sides so that they BREAK HIS OUTLINE. Blacked out, the ears
  are how you know him.
ARMS: long - his fingertips reach below the knee when he stands with arms down.
🔴 TWO SWORDS, ONE ON EACH HIP - he is the only figure in this book carrying two blades. They stay
  through every costume stage, including the king's robe.
🔴 ACCENT: A VERMILION SASH AT THE WAIST (#B8352A). It survives EVERY stage below, and no one else
  in this book wears a sash of that colour. 🔴 IT WAS YELLOW UNTIL A TEST RENDER - and yellow is the
  rebel host's colour, so with a head cloth on he read as one of the men he fights in book 1.
  Vermilion also suits him: he is Han royal kin, and the Han honoured red.
```

## Guanyu

```
GUANYU - the second brother, and the tallest figure in the book. SHOULDERS = 2.0 head-widths.
🔴 FACE: DEEP JUJUBE RED, a flat even red across the whole face and neck. HE IS THE ONLY RED FACE
  IN THIS BOOK. It never changes - not with age, not with weather, not with lamplight.
EYES: long and narrow, the outer corners lifted; heavy level brows above.
🔴 BEARD - THE CLOCK OF THIS BOOK: youth = it reaches his CHEST. prime = it reaches BELOW HIS CHEST.
  old = it reaches HIS NAVEL and pale hairs run through it. It hangs as ONE mass, never in strands,
  and he strokes it with the back of his hand.
WEAPON: a long-handled blade whose shaft is TALLER THAN HE IS, so it rises past his shoulder in
  every pose. Carried, never sheathed.
🔴 ACCENT: GREEN. He is the only figure in green, and the green is on him from book 1 to the end.
🔴 SILHOUETTE: blacked out, one hanging mass at the chest and one long shaft over the shoulder.
```

## Zhangfei

```
ZHANGFEI - the youngest brother. SHOULDERS = 2.2 head-widths, the broadest of the three.
🔴 FACE: BLACK. A flat even black across the whole face. HE IS THE ONLY BLACK FACE IN THIS BOOK.
EYES: 🔴 PERFECT CIRCLES, wide open, the whites showing all the way around - against Guanyu's
  narrow lifted eyes on the same page, the two brothers are told apart by eye shape alone.
🔴 BEARD: IT GROWS OUTWARD, NOT DOWNWARD - stiff black bristles radiating from jaw and upper lip in
  every direction, so THE OUTLINE OF HIS HEAD IS NOT SMOOTH. He is the only figure in this book
  whose blacked-out silhouette has a spiky edge. Age lengthens the bristles and greys some; it never
  makes them lie flat.
WEAPON: a spear longer than Guanyu's shaft, with a snake-curved head.
🔴 ACCENT: BLACK - his face, his beard and his armour are the same black, so he reads as one dark
  mass with a spiky edge and two white circles in it.
VOICE MADE VISIBLE: when he shouts, the mouth is a wide open square and the bristles lift.
```

## Zhugeliang

```
ZHUGELIANG - the strategist. 🔴 SHOULDERS = 1.4 head-widths, THE NARROWEST FIGURE IN THE BOOK.
  Enters in book 12 and never wears armour.
FACE: 🔴 UNPAINTED, and the narrowest face in the book. Level brows, eyes half-lidded and calm,
  a small closed mouth that rests in a faint smile. He is never drawn shouting.
HEAD: a soft cloth band tied around the head, its two short ties hanging at the back.
🔴 HIS HANDS: ONE HAND ALWAYS HOLDS A WHITE FEATHER FAN, in all thirteen books he appears in.
  🔴 HE IS THE ONLY ADULT IN THIS BOOK WHO NEVER HOLDS A WEAPON. That is the whole point of him.
COSTUME: ONE robe for thirteen books - a wide pale crane-robe with sleeves that hang past the hands.
  🔴 HIS RANK NEVER CHANGES, SO HIS CLOTHES NEVER CHANGE. He is the only fixed costume in the book.
🔴 SILHOUETTE: blacked out, a tall smooth column with wide hanging sleeves and ONE fan shape held
  out from the body. He is the only figure with no weapon line anywhere in his outline.
```

## Zhaoyun

```
ZHAOYUN - the rider. SHOULDERS = 1.8 head-widths.
FACE: 🔴 UNPAINTED and 🔴 BEARDLESS. He is the only grown fighter in this book with no beard at all
  through his young and prime stages - that beardless face is what makes him read as the young one
  standing among bearded men. In his last books (from 23) he gets a WHITE beard, and that white
  beard arriving is the only way the reader is told he grew old.
EYES: level and open, brows straight.
ARMOUR: 🔴 SILVER, the only silver in the book, worn from his first page to his last. One long
  spear. A helmet with ONE tassel rising from its crown.
🔴 HIS HORSE IS WHITE and he is drawn mounted more often than not. Horse and armour are the same
  white-silver, so blacked out he is one large mass with a spear line and one tassel above it.
🔴 ACCENT: SILVER-WHITE. No other figure and no other horse in this book is white.
```

## Caocao

```
CAOCAO - the ruler of the north. 🔴 SHOULDERS = 1.5 head-widths - narrow, and the smallest figure
  in the book when others stand beside him.
🔴 FACE: POWDERED WHITE, flat and even, with no red anywhere in it. Two figures in this book have a
  white face - he and Dongzhuo - and they are told apart by outline, not by colour (see below).
EYES: 🔴 NARROW AND LONG WITH THE OUTER CORNERS LIFTED, the lids drawn as single thin lines; the
  eyes are the sharpest thing on him. Brows angled up and in.
MOUTH: a thin line lifted at ONE corner only - 🔴 he half-smiles; he never laughs with the mouth open.
🔴 ACCENT: BLUE-BLACK AT THE COLLAR AND CUFFS, in EVERY stage below.
🔴 SILHOUETTE: blacked out, a narrow upright column with wide square sleeves. Against Dongzhuo's
  blacked shape he is the narrow one - the two white faces must never be tellable by colour alone.
```

## Sunquan

```
SUNQUAN - the lord of the south. SHOULDERS = 1.6 head-widths.
FACE: 🔴 UNPAINTED, square-jawed and young. 🔴 HIS EYES ARE BLUE - the only blue eyes in this book,
  and they stay blue at every age.
🔴 BEARD: PURPLE. From his prime on, his beard and moustache are a clear reddish-purple.
  IT IS THE ONLY COLOURED BEARD IN THIS BOOK - every other beard here is black, white or grey.
  As a youth (book 7) he has no beard yet and the blue eyes carry him alone.
🔴 AT HIS BELT HANGS A SQUARE SEAL POUCH - he is the only figure in this book who carries a seal
  where the others carry a blade, and it is in his blacked-out outline.
🔴 ACCENT: RED. His robe is red in every stage.
```

## Lvbu

```
LVBU - the strongest fighter alive, and the least reliable man in the book. SHOULDERS = 2.2
  head-widths. Appears books 3-8 only, always in his prime - he never ages.
FACE: 🔴 JADE-WHITE WITH A SHEEN, not the flat powder white of Caocao - his is the handsome white of
  a young hero, with strongly drawn black brows and no lines anywhere. 🔴 NO BEARD, EVER.
🔴 HELMET: TWO LONG PHEASANT PLUMES RISE FROM IT AND CURVE OUT AND BACK, each as long as he is tall
  from waist up. HE IS THE ONLY FIGURE IN THIS BOOK WITH ANYTHING RISING ABOVE HIS HEAD. Blacked
  out, those two lines are him and nothing else needs to be read.
ARMOUR: the most decorated armour in the book - scaled, layered, with a red tassel at the throat.
WEAPON: a halberd with a crescent blade on ONE side of the shaft.
🔴 HIS HORSE IS RED. It is the only red horse in this book (Zhaoyun's is white, all others brown).
🔴 ACCENT: THE RED TASSEL AT HIS THROAT.
```

## Dongzhuo

```
DONGZHUO - the first darkness of the series. 🔴 SHOULDERS = 2.6 head-widths, THE WIDEST FIGURE IN
  THE BOOK. Appears books 2-5, one age only.
🔴 FACE: POWDERED WHITE like Caocao's - but 🔴 HIS IS A FAT FACE: cheeks that push the eyes into
  slits, a heavy jowl under the chin, and a flush of red across the nose that Caocao never has.
EYES: half-buried in the cheeks, the pupils small.
BEARD: a short thick black beard, wide rather than long.
🔴 SILHOUETTE: blacked out he is a broad low mound - 2.6 head-widths across the shoulders, where
  Caocao is 1.5. That ratio is the ONE thing that tells the two white faces apart. 🔴 NEVER DRAW
  THEM BOTH SLIM.
COSTUME: one robe for four books - a heavy, over-decorated court robe stretched by the body inside it.
🔴 ACCENT: GOLD - rings, buckles, the fittings of his belt. He is the only figure wearing gold
  ornaments, and there are 🔴 AT MOST 5 GOLD PIECES ON HIM in any one picture.
```

## Simayi

```
SIMAYI - the last opponent, and the one who outlives everybody. SHOULDERS = 1.5 head-widths.
  Books 22-24.
🔴 FACE: GREY-BLACK - one step darker than Caocao's white and far lighter than Zhangfei's black, so
  in a picture with either of them he sits between the two. Long and narrow.
EYES: 🔴 HALF-CLOSED IN EVERY PICTURE, even when he is surprised. He is the only figure in this book
  whose eyes are never fully open. Brows level and low.
BEARD: narrow and long, hanging straight down in one thin fall; grey-flecked in book 24.
🔴 HIS HANDS ARE INSIDE HIS SLEEVES unless he is giving an order - so his outline has no arms, just
  a column with two sleeve-ends meeting at the front. Where Zhugeliang's sleeves hang open with a
  fan held out, his are closed and empty.
COSTUME: one grey-black robe and armour set for three books. His rank does not visibly change.
🔴 ACCENT: NONE - he is the only figure in this book with no accent colour anywhere. That absence is
  the design: the man who wins is the one nothing marks.
```

## 조역 시트 — 어떻게 읽는가

> 🔴 **조역은 고정 10인의 얼굴색 축을 쓰지 않는다.** 색으로 갈리는 자리는 열 명이 이미 다 썼고,
> 조역까지 색을 쓰면 그 체계가 흐려진다. 조역은 **소지품 하나 또는 몸의 비율 하나**로 읽힌다.
>
> 🔴 **나이·복장은 여기 적지 않는다.** 그건 권 번호가 정하고 `build-samgukji.mjs` 의 `STAGES` 가 SSOT다.
> 여기 적는 것은 **권이 바뀌어도 안 변하는 것**뿐이다 — 얼굴, 실루엣, 악센트, 몸의 비율.
> 시트를 뽑을 때는 빌더가 `AGES & COSTUMES` 블록을 자동으로 붙여 준다.
>
> 🔴 **같은 화면에 서는 인물끼리 갈리는지가 전부다.** 문서 끝의 「부딪치는 짝」을 반드시 볼 것.

## Zhangjue

```
ZHANGJUE - the man who started the rebellion. SHOULDERS = 1.5 head-widths.
FACE: bare, hollow cheeks, eyes set deep. A thin grey beard in three separate strands.
🔴 HE IS THE SAME COLOUR AS HIS FOLLOWERS - yellow kerchief, yellow-brown robe, exactly like the
  hundreds behind him. NOTHING on him is a different colour from the crowd.
🔴 WHAT SEPARATES HIM: ONE STAFF, as tall as he is, with a single cloth knot near the top. He is
  the only figure in that crowd holding anything vertical. Blacked out, the crowd is a low mass and
  he is the one upright line in it.
POSTURE: both hands on the staff, never raised above his own head.
🔴 ACCENT: none of his own - his colour belongs to the crowd. That is the point of this character.
```

## Duyou

```
DUYOU - the inspector who comes to the small county with his hand out. SHOULDERS = 1.4 head-widths.
FACE: bare, round, a small mouth pushed forward. Eyebrows lifted so the eyes look half-lidded.
🔴 HE IS DRAWN ON HORSEBACK IN EVERY PANEL where others stand on the ground - his eye-line is above
  everyone else's even though he is the shortest man in the scene.
COSTUME: an official's robe of expensive cloth, too clean for the road.
🔴 WHAT READS: ONE WHIP held across the saddle and never put down. That whip is how the reader
  knows he did not dismount to greet anyone. Blacked out: a small torso on a big horse, one short
  bar across it.
🔴 ACCENT: none. Nothing on him is a strong colour - his rudeness is a posture, not a hue.
```

## Sushuang

```
SUSHUANG - one of the two horse-traders. SHOULDERS = 1.6 head-widths.
🔴 HE IS NEVER DRAWN ALONE. He and his partner are always in the same panel, the same height, the
  same clothes, one half-step apart. They read as a PAIR, not as two people.
FACE: bare, wide, laughing - open mouth, eyes squeezed shut by the cheeks. Short round beard.
COSTUME: a travelling merchant's short coat, sleeves tied back with cord.
🔴 WHAT READS: A PACK ON THE BACK, high and square, taller than his own head. Blacked out, the pair
  is two blocks with two square lumps on top and a line of horses behind.
🔴 ACCENT: none.
```

## ChildEmperor

```
CHILD EMPEROR - ten years old, the one being led. SHOULDERS = 1.2 head-widths.
FACE: bare, round, mouth open a little. 🔴 HIS EYES LOOK AT AN ADULT IN EVERY PANEL, never at the
  reader and never at what he is being shown.
🔴 THE COSTUME IS THE CHARACTER: the robe and the crown are made for a grown man. The sleeves cover
  his hands entirely. The hem lies on the ground behind him for the length of one of his own legs.
  The crown sits low enough to touch his eyebrows.
🔴 SILHOUETTE: a small head, and below it a shape far too wide that ends in a puddle of cloth. He is
  the only figure in the book whose outline is wider at the floor than at the shoulders.
🔴 ACCENT: none - imperial yellow is on the robe, but the robe is not his.
```

## Liuxie

```
LIUXIE - the younger brother, nine years old, who becomes the emperor of the rest of this book.
SHOULDERS = 1.2 head-widths.
🔴 HE IS DRAWN BESIDE THE CHILD EMPEROR AND MUST NOT BE MISTAKEN FOR HIM. Both are small, both are
  in oversized clothes. What separates them is ONE THING AND IT IS NOT CLOTHING:
  🔴 THE OLDER BOY'S BACK IS BENT AND THIS ONE'S BACK IS STRAIGHT. Draw the spine, not the face.
FACE: bare, thin, a mouth that stays closed. He answers when spoken to.
COSTUME: a prince's plain robe, also too big, but he has gathered the sleeve in one fist so his
  hand shows. 🔴 ONE VISIBLE HAND is the second thing that separates the two boys.
🔴 ACCENT: none.
```

## Hejin

```
HEJIN - the emperor's uncle, a butcher who became a general. SHOULDERS = 2.1 head-widths - the
widest shoulders in book 2.
FACE: bare, heavy jaw, a short blunt beard. Small eyes for the size of the face.
🔴 HANDS: THE LARGEST HANDS IN THE BOOK, each palm as wide as his own chin is long. He is a butcher
  in a general's coat and the hands are what say so.
COSTUME: armour under a red cloak that hangs to the back of the knee.
🔴 SILHOUETTE: a wide block with a cloak falling straight from it, and two oversized hands hanging
  clear of the body.
🔴 ACCENT: THE RED CLOAK. In book 2 no one else wears red.
```

## Dingyuan

```
DINGYUAN - the older officer whom Lvbu calls father first. SHOULDERS = 1.7 head-widths.
FACE: bare, long, deep lines from nose to mouth. A full grey beard, combed flat, reaching the chest.
🔴 HIS ARMOUR IS OLD AND CLEAN - the lacing is mended in two places and the plates are dull, but
  nothing hangs loose. That pairing (worn but tidy) is the whole character.
POSTURE: stands with both feet planted the same distance apart in every panel.
🔴 SILHOUETTE: a straight column - no cloak, no plume, nothing that moves. Beside him Lvbu is all
  motion, and that is the contrast the panel is built on.
🔴 ACCENT: none.
```

## Chengong

```
CHENGONG - the young magistrate who lets Caocao go, and pays for it for six books.
SHOULDERS = 1.5 head-widths.
FACE: bare, narrow, level brows, a mouth held in a straight line. NO BEARD in any book.
COSTUME: a blue civil officer's robe with a wide sash, never armour, not even inside a besieged city.
  🔴 HE IS THE ONLY MAN INSIDE THE WALLS OF BOOK 8 WHO IS NOT IN ARMOUR.
POSTURE: 🔴 HIS BACK IS STRAIGHT IN EVERY PANEL INCLUDING THE LAST ONE. Kneeling, bound, standing -
  the spine does not change.
🔴 SILHOUETTE: a plain vertical with two wide sleeves; nothing on his head, nothing in his hands.
🔴 ACCENT: BLUE. He is the only blue robe in books 2 and 8.
```

## Lvbosha

```
LVBOSHA - the old man at the farmhouse. SHOULDERS = 1.5 head-widths.
FACE: bare, soft, deeply wrinkled around the eyes from smiling. A thin white beard to the collarbone.
COSTUME: a farmer's padded coat, one shoulder patched with a different cloth.
🔴 WHAT READS: A WINE JAR CARRIED IN BOTH ARMS AGAINST HIS CHEST, big enough that he must lean back
  a little to hold it. He is drawn walking toward the reader with it.
🔴 SILHOUETTE: a round mass at chest height held by two arms - unmistakable, and it is the last
  thing the reader sees of him.
🔴 ACCENT: none.
```

## Wangyun

```
WANGYUN - the old minister who cannot sleep. SHOULDERS = 1.4 head-widths - 🔴 THE NARROWEST
SHOULDERS OF ANY ADULT MAN IN THIS BOOK.
FACE: bare, thin, long. A white beard in two thin points. Eyes that stay lowered when he speaks to
  powerful men and lift only when he is alone.
COSTUME: a court official's dark robe, correct in every fold.
🔴 WHAT READS: A STAFF HE LEANS ON, and 🔴 he is drawn WALKING IN A CIRCLE in his own courtyard more
  than once. When he sits, both hands rest flat on his knees.
🔴 SILHOUETTE: a thin vertical bent very slightly forward, with a second thin vertical (the staff)
  a hand's width in front of it.
🔴 ACCENT: none. In a book of gold and red he is the one figure with no colour on him at all.
```

## Diaochan

```
DIAOCHAN - the young woman who walks into it on her own feet. SHOULDERS = 1.1 head-widths.
🔴 FACE: THE ONLY PAINTED FACE IN THIS BOOK - colour at the outer eye and on the lips, and nowhere
  else on any other figure, man or woman. Oval, with the chin drawn to a soft point.
HAIR: gathered high with two pins; two strands left down in front of the ears.
COSTUME: a pale jade robe with sleeves that reach past the fingertips.
🔴 WHAT READS: A ROUND FAN held at chest height. She lowers it when she is telling the truth and
  raises it when she is not - 🔴 the fan is her only tell, so it must be visible in every panel.
🔴 SILHOUETTE: the narrowest figure in the book, with one circle held in front of it.
🔴 ACCENT: PALE JADE. No one else in the book wears it.
```

## Lijue

```
LIJUE - one of the two officers left over when their master falls. SHOULDERS = 1.7 head-widths.
🔴 HE IS ALWAYS DRAWN WITH GUOSI AND THE TWO MUST NOT BE INTERCHANGEABLE:
  LIJUE IS THE TALLER, and his eyebrows are so pale they nearly vanish - 🔴 a face with almost no
  brow line. He stands upright.
FACE: bare, square, a wide flat nose.
COSTUME: campaign armour with no decoration, a cloak stiff with dust.
🔴 SILHOUETTE: of the pair, the tall straight one.
🔴 ACCENT: none.
```

## Guosi

```
GUOSI - the second of the two. SHOULDERS = 1.6 head-widths.
🔴 HE IS THE SHORTER OF THE PAIR BY ONE HEAD-HEIGHT, and 🔴 HIS SHOULDERS ARE ROUNDED FORWARD so
  that his neck disappears. Beside Lijue he reads as a comma beside a stroke.
FACE: bare, narrow, heavy black brows - 🔴 the opposite of Lijue's, which is how the pair is told
  apart in a small panel.
COSTUME: the same campaign armour as Lijue, the same dust.
🔴 SILHOUETTE: of the pair, the short hunched one.
🔴 ACCENT: none.
```

## Taoqian

```
TAOQIAN - the old governor who has to give his province away. SHOULDERS = 1.3 head-widths.
FACE: bare, hollowed, a sparse white beard through which the chin shows. Eyes wet at the lower lid
  in every panel, without weeping.
🔴 THE BODY IS THE CHARACTER: he is drawn SEATED in all but one panel, and when seated his back
  makes a curve, not a line. 🔴 HIS HANDS SHAKE - draw the seal he offers with both hands, because
  one hand could not hold it steady.
COSTUME: a governor's robe grown too big at the shoulders.
🔴 SILHOUETTE: a low curve with two arms extended forward - the only figure in book 5 reaching out.
🔴 ACCENT: none.
```

## Yuanshao

```
YUANSHAO - the tallest man at the alliance table and the one who decides last.
SHOULDERS = 2.0 head-widths.
FACE: bare, handsome, a beard combed to a clean point and 🔴 EYEBROWS THAT SLOPE DOWN AT THE OUTER
  END - the face is grand and the eyes are uncertain, and both must be there at once.
COSTUME: the most decorated armour in the book - but 🔴 COUNT THE ORNAMENTS AND STOP AT SIX.
POSTURE: 🔴 IN EVERY GROUP PANEL HIS HEAD IS TURNED TOWARD WHOEVER SPOKE LAST. He is never the one
  looking straight ahead.
🔴 SILHOUETTE: broad shoulders over a narrow waist, with a tall crest on the helmet - the crest is
  the tallest thing in any panel he stands in.
🔴 ACCENT: none of his own. His armour catches light; nothing on him is one colour.
```

## Yuanshu

```
YUANSHU - Yuanshao's younger brother, and the one who puts the crown on himself.
SHOULDERS = 1.8 head-widths.
🔴 HE MUST READ AS HIS BROTHER'S RELATIVE AND HIS BROTHER'S LESSER: the same face shape, but the
  cheeks and jaw carry more weight, and the beard is not combed to a point.
🔴 THE CHIN IS ALWAYS LIFTED. His eye-line passes over the head of whoever he is speaking to. In the
  one panel where his chin comes down, that is the event of the page.
COSTUME: silk, always new, always more of it than the occasion needs.
🔴 SILHOUETTE: a wide soft mass with the head tipped back on it.
🔴 ACCENT: none.
```

## Gongsunzan

```
GONGSUNZAN - Liubei's old schoolmate, who arrives with white horses. SHOULDERS = 1.8 head-widths.
FACE: bare, broad, sunburnt across the nose. A short full beard.
🔴 WHITE HORSE AND WHITE ARMOUR - and 🔴 THIS IS THE SAME WHITE ZHAOYUN WILL WEAR NINE BOOKS LATER.
  They never share a panel (this man is gone by book 5, Zhaoyun begins at book 10), but if they ever
  did, the separation is THE BEARD: this man has one, Zhaoyun has none, ever.
POSTURE: one hand always on the reins, even standing.
🔴 SILHOUETTE: a rider whose horse is as bright as he is - the pair reads as one white shape.
🔴 ACCENT: WHITE, shared with the horse.
```

## Sunjian

```
SUNJIAN - the tiger of the east. SHOULDERS = 1.9 head-widths.
FACE: bare, square, a short hard beard cut straight across the bottom. Eyes wide open.
🔴 THE RED HEADSCARF IS THIS MAN. It is tied at the back with two ends hanging to the shoulder
  blade, and it moves in every panel. 🔴 IT IS THE ONLY RED IN A PANEL HE STANDS IN.
🔴 HIS SON WILL WEAR THIS SCARF FROM BOOK 7. In book 4 the two appear together and the son does NOT
  have it yet - that absence is deliberate and must not be drawn in.
🔴 SILHOUETTE: a compact body under a head with two ribbons streaming off the back of it.
🔴 ACCENT: THE RED SCARF.
```

## Huaxiong

```
HUAXIONG - the gate-keeper nobody can pass. SHOULDERS = 2.1 head-widths.
🔴 HIS FACE IS DARK RED AND GUANYU'S FACE IS RED, AND THEY MEET IN BOOK 3. This is the most
  dangerous pair in the book. Separate them like this and only like this:
  🔴 GUANYU: a FLAT EVEN red, the same value across the whole face, no shadow. Long narrow eyes.
  🔴 HUAXIONG: a BROWNISH red that is DARKER IN THE HOLLOWS - and 🔴 CHEEKBONES THAT STICK OUT far
    enough to catch a highlight. Guanyu has no bone showing anywhere.
  If a reader must check the beard to tell them apart, the faces are wrong.
BEARD: short, black, bristling sideways - nothing hangs.
🔴 HELMET: a fringe of animal fur around the rim. Guanyu wears no helmet, ever.
🔴 SILHOUETTE: a wide block with a shaggy crown; Guanyu is a tall column with one long shaft.
🔴 ACCENT: none.
```

## Simahui

```
SIMAHUI - the old scholar who tells Liubei he has no people. SHOULDERS = 1.4 head-widths.
🔴 HE WEARS WHITE AND SO DOES ZHUGELIANG, AND THEY ARE ONE BOOK APART. Separate them:
  🔴 THIS MAN IS OLD - a white beard to the chest, and Zhugeliang's beard is short, dark and cut
    square at the chin. 🔴 THIS MAN HAS NO FAN. The fan belongs to the other one and to no one else.
FACE: bare, round, 🔴 SMILING IN EVERY PANEL so that the eyes are two curves and the pupils barely
  show. He does not stop smiling even when he refuses to answer.
COSTUME: an undyed scholar's robe, sleeves wide enough to swallow both hands.
🔴 HANDS: TUCKED INTO THE OPPOSITE SLEEVES. He is never drawn pointing at anything - that is the
  character: he tells Liubei what is wrong and will not tell him where to go.
🔴 SILHOUETTE: a soft column with no hands showing and no object anywhere on it.
🔴 ACCENT: none.
```

## Xushu

```
XUSHU - the first strategist Liubei ever has, and the one he loses. SHOULDERS = 1.5 head-widths.
FACE: bare, young, NO BEARD - 🔴 he is the only man in a strategist's robe with a bare chin.
COSTUME: a plain civil robe with sleeves narrower than a court official's, because he walks.
🔴 THE HANDS COUNT THINGS. In every panel one hand is doing something with number: fingers half
  folded, a tally on a strip of bamboo, three stones set on a table. 🔴 Never an empty gesture.
POSTURE: leans forward from the waist when he is thinking; stands upright only when he is leaving.
🔴 SILHOUETTE: a narrow figure bent slightly at the middle, one hand raised to chest height with the
  fingers spread and not closed.
🔴 ACCENT: none.
```

## Liubiao

```
LIUBIAO - the old kinsman who holds a rich province and never uses it. SHOULDERS = 1.6 head-widths.
FACE: bare, long, a white beard that lies flat down the chest without a single stray hair.
🔴 HE IS SEATED IN EVERY PANEL WITHOUT EXCEPTION. If a scene needs him to move, the scene is wrong.
  🔴 A CUSHION AND AN ARM-REST are always drawn with him - he is the only figure in the book who
  has furniture.
COSTUME: a governor's robe of good cloth, the knees smoothed flat from sitting.
🔴 SILHOUETTE: a low wide triangle. Everyone else in his room is a vertical.
🔴 ACCENT: none.
```

## Adou

```
ADOU - the infant carried out of an army. SHOULDERS: not applicable - he is a bundle.
🔴 HE IS THE ONLY FIGURE IN THIS BOOK WHOSE FACE IS NEVER FULLY SHOWN. At most one cheek, one closed
  eye and one fist appear above the cloth. Never two eyes.
🔴 THE BUNDLE IS ALWAYS THE SAME SIZE - as long as a grown man's forearm and no longer, so that
  Zhaoyun's armour can close over it.
CLOTH: undyed, wrapped so that one corner hangs free and moves.
🔴 SILHOUETTE: a smooth oval with one loose corner. It must read as a bundle, not as a sack.
🔴 ACCENT: none - 🔴 he must NOT be a bright spot in the panel. He is what everyone is looking for,
  and the reader should have to look for him too.
```

## Liushan

```
LIUSHAN - Adou grown up, and the last king of his father's country. SHOULDERS = 1.6 head-widths.
🔴 HE HAS HIS FATHER'S EARS - the same size, standing clear of the head, breaking the outline. That
  inheritance is the only reason this character reads at all.
🔴 AND EVERYTHING ELSE IS THE OPPOSITE OF HIS FATHER: the eyes are round and soft where Liubei's are
  wide-set and level; 🔴 THE MOUTH RESTS SLIGHTLY OPEN where his father's rests closed.
FACE: bare, round, no beard at any age.
🔴 NO WEAPON, EVER. Liubei carries two swords through every costume; this one carries nothing, and
  in the panel where the two stand together that difference is the page.
🔴 SILHOUETTE: his father's ears on a rounder, softer body.
🔴 ACCENT: none.
```

## Pangtong

```
PANGTONG - the second of the two great strategists, and the one nobody looks at twice.
SHOULDERS = 1.6 head-widths.
🔴 NOT BEING HANDSOME IS THIS CHARACTER, AND IT MUST NOT TIP INTO UGLINESS OR COMEDY. Build it from
  three measurements and no adjectives:
  🔴 the nose is SHORT AND BLUNT, ending above the line of the mouth corners;
  🔴 the hairline sits ONE FINGER LOWER than everyone else's, so the forehead is small;
  🔴 the hair is not combed - three or four strands stand away from the head in every panel.
🔴 AND THE EYES ARE THE BRIGHTEST IN THE BOOK: larger than Zhugeliang's, with a clear highlight.
  Draw the eyes last and draw them best. The whole character is that the face is plain and the eyes
  are not.
COSTUME: a civil robe, the hem dusty, worn without a sash.
🔴 SILHOUETTE: a compact figure with an uneven crown of hair.
🔴 ACCENT: none.
```

## Machao

```
MACHAO - the young general from the west. SHOULDERS = 1.9 head-widths.
🔴 SILVER ARMOUR AND A WHITE WAR-COAT - AND ZHAOYUN WEARS SILVER ARMOUR TOO, AND THEY STAND SIDE BY
  SIDE IN BOOK 19. Separate them:
  🔴 MACHAO'S HELMET HAS A LION'S HEAD AT THE BROW and a RED PLUME. Zhaoyun's helmet is plain with a

  🔴 MACHAO CARRIES A SPEAR WITH A PLAIN SHAFT; Zhaoyun's spear has a white tassel below the head.
  🔴 MACHAO IS THE YOUNGER FACE AND THE ANGRIER ONE - brows drawn together in every panel until
    book 19. Zhaoyun's brows are level always.
FACE: bare, no beard, a straight nose, eyes wide and fixed.
🔴 SILHOUETTE: a rider with a bristling crest; Zhaoyun's crest is a single hanging line.
🔴 ACCENT: THE RED PLUME.
```

## Huangzhong

```
HUANGZHONG - the old man who takes the mountain. SHOULDERS = 1.8 head-widths - 🔴 as wide as a young
general's, and that contradiction is the character.
FACE: bare, weathered, a white beard to the chest, cut square.
🔴 THE BACK IS STRAIGHT. He is over sixty and 🔴 HE IS NEVER DRAWN STOOPED, not once, not even
  sitting. Age is in the beard and the skin; it is never in the spine.
🔴 THE BOW: he is THE ONLY ADULT IN THIS BOOK WHO CARRIES A BOW. It is longer than he is tall and it

COSTUME: old armour, plates darkened, worn over a plain coat.
🔴 SILHOUETTE: a straight vertical with a long curve crossing it diagonally. Nobody else in this
  book has a curve in their outline.
🔴 ACCENT: none.
```

## Masu

```
MASU - the young staff officer who talks well. SHOULDERS = 1.5 head-widths.
FACE: bare, narrow, quick - 🔴 THE MOUTH IS OPEN IN ALMOST EVERY PANEL because he is almost always
  speaking. Eyebrows lifted.
🔴 THE CHIN IS UP when he is being given the job, and the head is DOWN in the last panel. Those are
  the only two poses this character has, and the book turns between them.
🔴 WHAT READS: A BOOK HELD IN THE HAND WHERE A WEAPON SHOULD BE. New armour that has not yet taken

🔴 SILHOUETTE: armour that is slightly too big, with a flat rectangle in one hand.

🔴 DO NOT CONFUSE WITH JIANGWEI: Jiangwei has a spear AND a book, Masu has ONLY the book.

## Dianwei

```
DIANWEI - the bodyguard who holds a doorway. SHOULDERS = 2.2 head-widths - 🔴 THE WIDEST FIGURE IN
THE ENTIRE BOOK, wider than Huaxiong.
FACE: bare, blunt, a beard that grows up onto the cheekbones. Small deep-set eyes.
🔴 FOREARMS: as thick as an ordinary man's calf, and always bare from the elbow down.
🔴 TWO SHORT HALBERDS, ONE IN EACH HAND, held low and apart at hip height. He is the only figure in
  this book holding TWO of the same weapon.
COSTUME: heavy armour with the sleeves cut away at the shoulder.
🔴 SILHOUETTE: a broad slab with two short bars angled down and out - blacked out, he fills a
  doorway and the doorway shows on either side of him.
🔴 ACCENT: none.
```

## Xiandi

```
XIANDI - the boy from book 2 grown up, and emperor of a country that is not his.
SHOULDERS = 1.5 head-widths.
🔴 HE IS THE SAME PERSON AS LIUXIE AND MUST BE RECOGNISABLE AS HIM: the same thin face, the same
  closed mouth, the same one hand gathering the sleeve.
🔴 THE HABIT THAT DOES NOT CHANGE: BEFORE HE SPEAKS, HE LOOKS AT SOMEONE ELSE'S FACE. In group
  panels his eyes are on a minister or on Caocao, never on the person he is answering.
FACE: bare, no beard until book 18, then a thin one that does not fill in.
🔴 THE ROBE IS ALWAYS SLIGHTLY WRONG FOR HIM - too big as a boy, correct but unfilled as a man. The
  shoulders of the robe are always wider than the shoulders inside it.
🔴 SILHOUETTE: a straight figure inside a slightly larger straight shape.
🔴 ACCENT: imperial yellow on the robe - 🔴 which no one may read as HIS colour. It is the office.
```

## Zhangliao

```
ZHANGLIAO - the officer who serves two masters well. SHOULDERS = 1.8 head-widths.
FACE: bare, level, a short beard trimmed to a straight edge. 🔴 ALMOST NO EXPRESSION IN ANY PANEL -
  when he climbs a hill to talk a friend into surrendering, and when he takes eight hundred men into

🔴 THE ARMOUR IS ALWAYS TIDY. Every strap is done up; nothing hangs; the cloak is squared on both
  shoulders. After a night battle it is dirty but still squared.
POSTURE: 🔴 HE PUTS HIS WEAPON DOWN BEFORE HE TALKS. In the panel where he goes up the hill, both
  hands are empty and visible.
🔴 SILHOUETTE: a clean rectangle of a torso with no loose edge anywhere.
🔴 ACCENT: none.
```

🔴 HE IS DRAWN LEANING FORWARD even when standing still, as though about to move off. Nobody else

COSTUME: light armour, less of it than other generals wear.
🔴 SILHOUETTE: a slanted vertical - the only figure in this book whose standing outline is not

## Caopi

```
CAOPI - Caocao's eldest son. SHOULDERS = 1.7 head-widths.

## Zhouyu

ZHOUYU - the young commander of the east. SHOULDERS = 1.8 head-widths.

🔴 ACCENT: none of colour. The flute is the mark.

## 🔴 부딪치는 짝 — 같은 화면에 서면 헷갈리는 자리

> 시트를 다 뽑은 뒤 **이 표의 짝만 나란히 놓고 다시 본다.** 다른 데서 아무리 잘 갈려도
> 여기서 안 갈리면 그 시트는 틀렸다. 🔴 「보면 안다」는 검사가 아니다 — **통째로 검게 칠해서** 본다.

| 짝 | 만나는 권 | 갈리는 것 |
|---|---|---|
| **관우 ↔ 화웅** | 3 | 관우 = 평면 붉은색·뼈 안 보임·투구 없음 / 화웅 = 그늘 지는 검붉은색·광대 튀어나옴·털 두른 투구 |
| **조운 ↔ 마초** | 17·19 | 조운 = 민 투구에 **흰** 술·창에도 흰 술·수염 0 / 마초 = 사자 이마에 **붉은** 깃·민 자루 창·미간이 좁다 |
| **제갈량 ↔ 수경선생** | 12·13 | 제갈량 = 검은 짧은 턱수염 + **부채** / 수경 = 흰 수염 + **부채 없음** + 두 손이 소매 안 |
| **손견 ↔ 손책** | 4 | 4권의 손책에겐 **붉은 수건이 없다**(아버지가 쓰고 있다). 7권부터 물려받는다 |
| **조조 ↔ 조비** | 20 | 조조 = 눈꼬리 **올라감** + 한쪽만 웃는 입 / 조비 = 눈꼬리 **처짐** + 곧은 입, 절대 안 웃는다 |
| **안량 ↔ 문추** | 9 | 안량 = 목이 없다시피 + **넓은 칼** / 문추 = 목이 길다 + **긴 창** |
| **이각 ↔ 곽사** | 5·6 | 이각 = 한 머리 더 크고 등이 곧다 + 눈썹이 옅다 / 곽사 = 어깨가 말렸다 + 눈썹이 짙다 |
| **어린 황제 ↔ 진류왕** | 2 | 형은 **등이 굽고** 손이 소매에 묻혔다 / 아우는 **등이 곧고** 한 손이 나와 있다 |
| **강유 ↔ 마속** | (안 만남) | 강유 = 창 **과** 책 / 마속 = 책만. 같은 권에 안 서지만 두 권이 붙어 있다 |
| **유비 ↔ 유선** | 21 | 귀는 같다. 유비 = 칼 두 자루 + 노란 띠 / 유선 = **아무것도 안 들었다** + 입이 조금 열려 있다 |
| **노숙 ↔ 수경선생** | (안 만남) | 둘 다 소매에 손. 노숙 = **검은 수염** + 여행 망토 / 수경 = 흰 수염 + 망토 없음 |
| **공손찬 ↔ 조운** | (안 만남) | 둘 다 흰 말·흰빛. 공손찬 = **수염 있음** / 조운 = 끝까지 수염 0 |

## 🔴 이 문서를 고칠 때

1. **그림체 규칙은 여기 쓰지 마라** — 앵커가 SSOT. 여기는 색·수염·실루엣·숫자·악센트·나이 여섯뿐이다.
2. 🔴 **시트를 뽑았으면 축 ③(실루엣)으로 검사해라.** 통째로 검게 메워 열을 나란히 놓고, 위 표대로 갈리지
   않으면 그 시트는 틀렸다. 「한눈에 읽힌다」는 검사가 아니다.
3. 🔴 **두 흰 얼굴(조조·동탁)은 반드시 실루엣으로도 갈려야 한다.** 흑백 축소·역광 컷에서 색이 죽는다.
4. **나이 단계를 바꿨으면 축 ②의 수염 표와 캐릭터 바이블(기획서 3장)을 같이 본다.**
5. ⚠️ **대본이 이미 정한 것은 못 바꾼다** — 유비 = 짚신·베옷(1권) + 쌍고검 · 관우 = 청룡언월도 · 장비 = 장팔사모.
