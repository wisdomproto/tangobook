## §0. 🔴 매체 번역 한 줄 — 무대와 공정이 같은 말을 한다

앵커: `NOTHING FLOATS - every black shape stays joined to the rest of the black somewhere, so the
picture lifts as one connected piece` · `ONE WHITE CHANNEL RUNS FROM THE TOP EDGE TO THE BOTTOM EDGE
ON EVERY PAGE AND NEVER BREAKS`.

**논둑은 그리는 것이 아니라 검은 종이가 안 잘린 자리다.** 물은 오려 낸 흰 자리이고, 논둑은 **오리다 만
것**이다. 🔴 **그러므로 논둑을 다 오리면 그림이 조각으로 떨어진다** — 「논둑은 물리적으로 다 이어져
있어야 한다」와 「위 논의 물이 아래로 흐른다」가 **한 문장**이다. 15시리즈 중 매체와 무대가 이렇게 겹치는
곳은 여기뿐이다.

🔴 **시트가 정해야 하는 것**: ①그 자리에서 **흰 물길이 어디로 나가나**(위 가장자리 → 아래 가장자리,
한 번도 안 끊긴다) ②**검은 것이 어디서 이어져 있나**. 자리 시트에 `THE WHITE CHANNEL` 절과
`WHERE THE BLACK STAYS JOINED` 절을 둘 다 쓴다. 하나만 쓰면 나머지 하나가 반드시 깨진다.

### §1.1 Terraces — 실제 프롬프트 (가장 먼저)

```
STAGE SHEET - Terraces   (dingding-papercut · SCENE token: Terraces · bake FIRST)

The stepped rice fields the whole series stands in. Nearly every named place in these fifty books is
somewhere on this one hillside, so this drawing decides it once and for all.

🔴 SCENE TOKENS: this ONE sheet answers to `Terraces` and to the spot names `Terraces/A`,
  `Terraces/B`, `Terraces/C`, `Terraces/D` and `Terraces/E`. Those are NOT other places - they are
  the five camera positions listed under SPOTS below, and nothing extra is baked for them.

🔴 SEVEN BANKS, NUMBERED. The hillside carries EXACTLY SEVEN black banks, and they are numbered 1 at
  the top to 7 at the bottom - fixed forever. Each bank is ONE unbroken cut piece. The bands get
  THINNER toward the top and never greyer. 🔴 DINGDING'S OWN FIELD IS THE TOP TERRACE - the water
  ABOVE BANK 1 - and that never changes either, so a reader can always say whether a page is above
  it or below.
  A page shows at most seven banks; when it shows fewer, it shows a NUMBERED RUN of them (3-4-5).

🔴 THE WHITE CHANNEL: one continuous cut of SHEET WHITE runs from the TOP EDGE of the picture to the
  BOTTOM EDGE and never breaks - it is the water going down the hill, and it passes through every
  bank at a notch. A NOTCH is at most 3 knife cuts wide, and how much water moves is said by that
  width and by nothing else. On a page where the water has stopped, the channel is still cut - it is
  the notch that is closed.

🔴 WHERE THE BLACK STAYS JOINED: every bank must remain attached to the black at BOTH ends where it
  meets the hillside edge, so the whole sheet lifts as one connected piece. Nothing floats anywhere.
  Check this on the sheet by naming, for each panel, the two places each bank is still joined.

FIXED PARTS: the water in each terrace is cut away and is SHEET WHITE, 🔴 RIPPLES ONLY AS THE
  WaterMarks SHEET DEFINES THEM - a ripple here is not decoration, it is the BRIDGE that keeps a
  black shape on white water joined to the rest of the black (anchor `Style`), and one whole book
  turns on "three rings against smooth", so a blanket "0 ripples" would kill it. Smooth water is
  smooth because nothing has touched it, not because ripples are banned · the earth path
  along the top of each bank is black · dry cracked ground, when a terrace has none, is a set of
  hairline cuts, at most 9, never in a row, 🔴 AND IT HAS A WETTING STATE - as water comes back the
  cuts get SHORTER AND FEWER from the middle of each crack outward until the ground is one unbroken
  black again; that dissolving is the last picture of a book, so draw the ladder (9 cuts → 5 → 2 → 0)
  on this sheet · rice in a band is one cut comb shape, at most 9 presses
  · 🔴 THREE CLUMPS OF WATER WEED, fixed fixtures and not props: one in the lower terrace near the
  notch, one against the bank on the left, one out in the open water - always the same three, always
  in the same places, because two pages of one book are the SAME ANGLE compared and only work if the
  clumps have not moved · ONE FAR RIDGE behind everything is the FAR GREY second sheet, BEHIND the black, 0 holes in it,
  at most one shape per page.

SPOTS:
  A THE WHOLE HILLSIDE, wide, from below: all seven banks, thinning upward, the house at the very
     top, the white channel running the full height of the frame.
  B ON A BANK, eye level along it: the path running away, water on the left, water on the right at
     a lower level - so the two heights are read at once.
  🔴 E THE PATH GOING DOWN, wide, high: the bank path is NOT level - it crosses the banks and falls,
     bending back on itself several times as it goes, and several terraces are visible at once at
     different heights. Use this whenever a page walks from one terrace to another.
  C A NOTCH, close: the gap in one bank, at most 3 cuts wide, water passing through.
  D ONE TERRACE from its edge, medium: the black bank in the foreground, the cut-away water beyond.

PLATE: A, B, C, D, E once each, plus 🔴 ONE NUMBERED DIAGRAM of the seven banks with the house and yard
  above bank 1 and Dingding's field as the top terrace, and one panel showing the white channel traced from top edge to bottom
  edge as a single unbroken white line.

NOT: no character of any kind, no red anywhere on this sheet (the red piece is Dingding's apron
  only), no lettering, numerals or field numbers drawn in the picture, no grey except the one far
  ridge sheet BEHIND the black, no torn or soft edge - every edge is a knife edge, no floating
  piece, no shading, gradient or glow.
```

### §1.2 House · Yard · TreeOnBank — 요약 명세

`House` = 방은 검정, 바닥이 흰 슬롯 하나, 면마다 반복 칼자국 하나. 🔴 **등불은 오려 낸 흰 구멍**이고 그
쪽에서 가장 밝다(18권 등불). 🔴 **방에는 흰 물길이 없다** — 앵커 관통 줄이 「계단 논이 화면에 있을 때」로
조건이 붙었으므로(2026-09-04), 방 안에서 창·문틈을 억지로 위아래로 잇지 않는다. 창은 창이고, 그 쪽에서
가장 밝은 구멍일 뿐이다. 🔴 `Yard` = 마당 흙은 검정, **마당은 언덕 꼭대기이고 마당 옆이 곧 `Terraces` 의 bank 1**.

`TreeOnBank` = 밑동 하나 + 가지, **밑동이 논둑 검정과 이어져 있어야 한다**(안 이어지면 나무가 뜬다).
🔴 **그림자를 두 방향으로 굽는다** — 한 권이 「오전에 한쪽으로 누운 그림자 ↔ 오후에 반대쪽으로 누운
그림자」를 같은 물·같은 나무로 비교하는 이야기라, 시트에 **오전판과 오후판을 한 장에 나란히** 그린다.
그림자는 앵커대로 **또 하나의 오려 낸 검은 조각**(같은 칼 가장자리 · 회색 금지 · 몸과 얼굴 위 금지)이고,
물 위에 누운 그림자는 **그 자체가 나무를 물에 붙들어 주는 다리**다(앵커 `Style` 의 hairline 규칙).
두 판의 나무·논둑·수면 높이가 한 픽셀도 달라지면 두 쪽이 비교되지 않는다.

### §1.3 Village — 실제 프롬프트 (밍 아저씨네 · 🔴 2026-09-04 신규)

```
STAGE SHEET - Village   (dingding-papercut · SCENE token: Village · bake AFTER Terraces)

Everything in the valley that is NOT Dingding's own hillside: Ming the duck's place at the foot of
the terraces, and the dry field over in the next valley. Five books go here and one of them spends
six of its ten pages inside the duck house, so it is a stage, not a background.

🔴 IT SITS ON THE BOTTOM TERRACE. Ming's yard is level with BANK 7, the lowest of the seven, and the
  water of the bottom terrace lies along one side of the yard. 🔴 That is how a reader knows this is
  DOWNHILL FROM HOME without being told - from here the banks go UP and away, thinning toward the top,
  and Dingding's house is a small shape at the far top of the frame. Never draw this place with the
  terraces above the camera missing.
🔴 THE WHITE CHANNEL ENDS HERE. This is the bottom of the hill, so on a wide page the channel comes
  down through the banks and runs OUT of the bottom edge past the yard. In a close shot inside the
  duck house there is no channel at all and none is invented (anchor `RENDERING`).

FIXED PARTS: THE DUCK HOUSE - one low black shed with a shallow roof, its long side to the yard, ONE
  door in the middle with a threshold, and 🔴 the OPEN DOOR IS A CUT-AWAY WHITE HOLE and the brightest
  thing on any page here; a very small shape standing in that hole reads at thumbnail size, which is
  the whole point of the door · INSIDE, the floor is STRAW - one repeated cut comb mark, the same
  shape every time, running off the edges, never redrawn, with the black left between the marks; a
  hollow where something has been sitting is that mark PRESSED FLAT AND SPREAD, not a different mark ·
  THE YARD - black earth, ONE flat area with a repeated scratch mark, wide enough to spread a white
  cloth on · ONE WOODEN CHEST standing at the edge of the cloth · at most 7 ducks as separate shapes,
  🔴 and a FILE of ducks is a RUN and is exempt from the cap (anchor) · the bottom terrace water along
  one side, cut away white.
🔴 EVERY BLACK SHAPE ON THE WHITE FLOOR OR THE WHITE WATER IS BRIDGED by something the scene has -
  a duck on water by its own wake, a duckling on straw by the straw it has flattened, the chest by
  the cloth edge it sits on. Nothing here is an island.

SPOTS:
  A THE PLACE FROM THE HILL, wide, high: the yard and the duck house small at the bottom, the seven
    banks climbing away above them, the house at the top. This is the geography panel.
  B THE YARD, wide, eye level: the white cloth spread on the black ground, the chest beside it, the
    duck house behind, the bottom terrace water at the left edge.
  C THE DOOR, medium wide, eye level: the shed side, the open door as one white hole, the threshold,
    a little of the straw floor showing inside.
  D INSIDE, wide, high: the straw floor filling the frame, the threshold at one edge, the door hole
    behind - the room read from above.
  E THE NEXT VALLEY'S FIELD, wide, eye level: dry black earth in rows with 0 water anywhere, a low
    ring of stones round the edge - 🔴 the ONE page in this series with no cut-away water in it at
    all, and it must feel wrong.

PLATE: A, B, C, D, E once each, plus 🔴 ONE STRAW PANEL - the same patch of straw floor plain and
  then with a hollow flattened in it, at one size, to fix that the difference is the same mark
  spread, not a second mark.

NOT: no character of any kind, no red anywhere on this sheet, no lettering, numerals or signs, no
  grey except the one far ridge sheet BEHIND the black, no torn, soft or feathered edge - every edge
  is a knife edge, no floating piece, no shading, gradient or glow, 🔴 no second village, road, gate,
  fence or building beyond the shed named here - the valley is otherwise terraces.
```

### §2.1 Baskets — 실제 프롬프트

```
PROP SHEET - Baskets   (dingding-papercut · SCENE token: Baskets)

Two lidded bamboo baskets. 🔴 THEY ARE THE SAME BASKET TWICE - same weave, same size, same handle -
  and one whole volume turns on nobody being able to tell them apart. Cut ONE block of shapes and
  use it for both.

FORM: the basket body is a black cut shape; the WEAVE IS HOLES - a grid of small holes cut clean
  through the black, at most 9 across the front, all the same shape, none mirroring its neighbour.
  🔴 Every hole must leave the black around it connected; no cut may separate a piece.
  The lid is a separate black shape resting on the rim with the same holes. The handle is an arc of
  black joined to the body at both ends - never a free-standing loop.
🔴 SIZE IS FIXED BY THE ARM THAT CARRIES IT: an adult carries one in each hand at her side, or one
  on her head; the child carries one in two arms against the belly and it reaches from chin to
  knee. Keep that ratio in every panel - this basket is in seventeen books.
STATES - draw all four at the same size:
  1 THE PAIR, lids on, standing side by side on the bank. Indistinguishable.
  2 CARRIED, hanging one from each end of a shoulder pole, symmetrical.
  3 LID TIPPED, one lid pushed a little out of true so a crescent of the rim shows - this is the
    only difference between them that ever exists, and it is temporary.
  4 OPEN - the lid laid back, and the contents rising above the rim. Steam from cooked rice is CUT
    AWAY WHITE, at most 5 shapes, never grey and never soft.
  🔴 5 NO LID, HEAPED WITH RICE SEEDLINGS - the blades stand up out of the rim, and the ones lower
    in the basket lie sideways. Draw the basket TIPPED so both are seen at once. Most books use it
    this way; the lid only comes out when a book needs it.
  🔴 6 EMPTY AND FLOATING, seen from straight above, resting on cut-away white water: the inside
    of the basket is the brightest thing in the frame and the weave of its floor is fully visible.
    It does not float free - the water ripple around it is its bridge (see WaterMarks).
  🔴 7 KNOCKED OVER, one side crushed flat, lying on the yard earth, and 8 UPSIDE DOWN, shaken by
    two hands with a few leaf scraps falling out.

PLATE: the seven states, plus one close-up of the weave large enough to count the holes and to see
  that the black between them is continuous.

NOT: no character, no hands, no red anywhere, no lettering or numerals, no floating piece, no grey,
  no torn or soft edge, no shading, gradient or sheen on the bamboo.
```

### §2.2 Straw — 실제 프롬프트

```
PROP SHEET - Straw   (dingding-papercut · SCENE token: Straw)

Cut rice - as stubble left standing, as sheaves lying down, and as bundles stood up leaning together.

FORM: 🔴 STRAW IS NOT DRAWN, IT IS THE COMB BLOCK - one cut comb shape of at most 9 teeth, used
  again and again. A sheaf is that comb bound at the waist by one black band; a stubble field is the
  comb repeated low along the ground.
STATES:
  1 STUBBLE - short rows across the field, the near two rows as separate combs and the rest carried
    on as one joined black band (a RUN is exempt from the repeat cap).
  2 SHEAVES DOWN - eight sheaves lying at different angles, 🔴 no two parallel and none mirroring
    another. Each sheaf is one piece joined to the ground black at its lower edge.
  3 STOOD UP - three bundles leaning against each other, meeting at the top, feet apart. The white
    triangle of sky between them is CUT AWAY, and it is the shape that says they are standing.
  4 ONE SHEAF alone, close, its binding band and the cut ends of the stalks.
  🔴 5 ONE STANDING BUNDLE, TALL - a bound bundle set upright on its own, TALLER THAN THE CHILD by
    a head, the waist band at the child's shoulder. One book looks up at it from below and the
    scale is the point. Also draw it FALLEN FORWARD, the head splayed open with loose ears spilling.
  🔴 6 A ROW OF SIX TRIPODS along a bank path, same triangle at the same interval, seen from above
    at an angle - the repeat cap does not apply because this is a RUN.
  🔴 7 STRAW ROPE - a rope twisted from the same straw: one black cord with a repeated twist notch,
    shown (a) tight around a sheaf waist so the waist is visibly pinched, (b) slack in an open palm
    with the twist coming undone. One book blames the rope, so the undone twist must read.
  🔴 8 STRAW BEDDING - a floor of straw inside a shed: the comb laid flat and repeated as a RUN, with
    two parted furrows where a hand has swept through it, and one round pressed hollow.
  🔴 9 LOOSE STRAWS ON WATER - single straws floating: each is one thin black stroke on cut-away
    white water and must touch a ripple or another straw (see WaterMarks). Draw both the scattered
    state (eight, all directions) and the gathered state (eight bunched at ONE point).

PLATE: the nine states, plus one panel of the comb block on its own so it can be reused unchanged.

NOT: no character, no hands, no red, no lettering or numerals, no floating piece, no grey, no torn
  or soft edge, no individual straws drawn one by one, no shading or gradient.
```

### §2.3 Ducks — 🔴 이 시리즈에서 가장 자주 나오는 것 (21권 68쪽)

> 밍 아저씨는 **인물**이고(얼굴이 있다) 논의 오리 떼는 **사물**이다(얼굴이 없다). 대본이 40·44·48·50 에서
> `얼굴 없음`을 손으로 써 두었다 — 그 규칙을 전 권에 편다. 안 그러면 배경 오리가 매번 조연이 된다.

```
PROP SHEET - Ducks   (dingding-papercut · SCENE token: Ducks)

The flock that lives on the bottom terrace, and one duckling. They are in twenty-one of the fifty
books, mostly at the far edge of the picture, so this sheet decides what a duck is once.

🔴 A FLOCK DUCK HAS NO FACE. It is one cut black silhouette with 0 notches - no eye, no nostril, no
  open bill. The bill is the black that runs out to a point on the head outline. This is what
  separates the flock from Ming, who is a character and has a cut-away face.
FORM: body one unbroken piece, neck a black stroke, bill on the outline. THE COUNT IS SEVEN and the
  flock is the same seven everywhere; fewer on a page means the rest are outside the frame. No duck
  mirrors the duck beside it.
🔴 A DUCK ON OPEN WATER WOULD FLOAT (it is black inside white). It never floats: the wake behind it
  is a hairline of black joining it to the next duck, to a bank, or to the frame edge. Draw the
  wake on every duck that is not touching land - see WaterMarks. On a page where the water is left
  black (dirty), no bridge is needed.
STATES - all at the same size:
  1 THE SEVEN SITTING, gathered into one round clump on the water - it reads as a single dark
    circle from a distance.
  2 THE SEVEN SCATTERED, no two going the same way, each with its wake.
  3 HEADS DOWN - all seven with heads under the water, not one head up.
  4 NECKS UP AND STRETCHED toward one point off the near edge - all seven necks aimed the same way.
  5 WINGS BEATEN ON THE WATER - two ducks with wings spread; the thrown water is black splinters
    that stay joined at the wing tip, never free drops.
  6 A LINE CLIMBING A BANK - two already in the upper water, three on the bank top, two still below.
  7 A SINGLE DUCK IN PROFILE, close, so the bill and the vest-less bare body are clear (Ming wears
    a vest; a flock duck wears nothing).
🔴 8 THE DUCKLING - one, small enough to sit inside a child's two cupped hands with a hairline of
  white sheet all round it so it is never crushed by the fingers. Also draw it (a) stepping over a
  threshold, one foot up, neck forward; (b) walking away over straw, body tilted; (c) held wrongly,
  gripped in one fist with a wing bent out between the fingers. It has NO face either.

PLATE: states 1-7 in a row at one size, then the duckling at three times that size, then one panel
  of a single duck blacked out beside Ming blacked out - the flock duck must still read as the one
  without a face.

NOT: no eye, notch or open bill on a flock duck, no red anywhere, no lettering or numerals, no
  feather detail, no floating piece, no grey, no shading, gradient or soft edge.
```

### §2.4 WaterMarks — 🔴 매체 규칙 · 이 시리즈의 다리(bridge)

> 앵커: `NOTHING FLOATS`. 무대: 화면 절반이 오려 낸 흰 물. **그 위에 놓인 것은 전부 금지된 섬이다.**
> 진짜 전지가 이 문제를 푸는 방법은 하나뿐이다 — **다리**. 그리고 이 시리즈는 운이 좋다: 대본이 이미
> 물 위의 모든 것에 **물 주름**을 붙여 두었다(31쪽 14권). 주름이 곧 다리다.

```
PROP SHEET - WaterMarks   (dingding-papercut · SCENE token: WaterMarks)

Everything that happens on the surface of water: ripples, bubbles, thrown water, drips, and mud.
This sheet is a RULE, not an object. Every other sheet that puts something on water calls it.

🔴 CLEAN WATER IS CUT AWAY (sheet white). DIRTY WATER IS LEFT BLACK. Nothing else says a thing got
  dirty in this series - not grey, not texture. A basin of clean water is a white hole; the same
  basin after two muddy hands is solid black with only a hairline white ring inside its rim. A
  flooded terrace full of silt is black while the terrace above it is white.
🔴 THE BRIDGE: anything black sitting inside cut-away white must stay joined to the rest of the
  black by at least one hairline, and in this series that hairline is a WATER MARK. It is a real
  line the story already asks for, not a cheat.
FORMS - all are knife-edged black lines on white water, never grey, never soft:
  1 RIPPLE RINGS - concentric arcs, at most 3, the outer ones THINNER than the inner. The rings are
    joined to each other by one hairline spoke, and the outermost ring is joined to whatever made
    it (a foot, a mouth, a bank). One book compares three rings against a surface with none, so the
    smooth state must be truly empty.
  2 A WAKE - one long thin V opening behind a moving thing, joined to it at the point. This is the
    bridge for every duck, basket, leaf, straw and shoe on open water.
  3 BUBBLES - a CHAIN. Each bubble is a small black ring; every ring is joined to the next by a
    hairline, and the end of the chain is joined to a bank. 🔴 A single free bubble is forbidden.
    Draw two states: the chain running in ONE direction across the frame top to bottom, and the
    chain closed into a RING turning on itself, joined to the bank at one point.
  4 THROWN WATER - splinters of black radiating from the thing that threw it (a wing, a palm, a
    foot), all joined at the root, at most 7, none mirroring its neighbour. Never a free droplet.
  5 A HANGING DROP - a drop about to fall keeps a hairline thread up to the black it hangs from
    (an eave, a hem, a fingertip). A drop already falling keeps a short hairline TAIL pointing back
    to where it came from. 🔴 THE TAIL LENGTH IS THE GAP: three drops close together read as heavy
    rain, one drop with a long tail reads as rain ending. One whole book counts that gap.
  6 MUD SPREADING - a fan opening from ONE point, the point touching the bank it leaked from, the
    far edge broken into fine teeth. The fan is BLACK on white water and it is the only thing in
    the frame that is both.
  7 A ROUND MUDDY MARK - one small black ring at the base of a thing pushed into the bed. This is
    the bridge for a planted seedling.

PLATE: the seven forms at one size, plus one panel showing the same terrace twice - clean (cut away)
  and silted (left black) - at the same angle.

NOT: no character, no red, no lettering or numerals, no grey water, no white foam painted on top of
  white, no free-floating black piece anywhere, no gradient, glow or soft edge.
```

### §2.5 Sky — 🔴 매체 규칙 · 하늘은 뒤집혀 있다

> 낮 하늘은 오려 낸 흰 자리다. 그러니 **구름을 검게 그리면 하늘에 섬이 뜬다.** 뒤집는다 —
> **하늘 띠를 검게 두고 구름 사이를 오려 낸다.** 21권이 「구름이 서로 떨어져 있다 → 붙었다」로
> 날씨를 재는데, 뒤집으면 그것이 「흰 틈이 넓다 → 닫힌다」가 되어 **어두워지는 것과 같은 그림**이 된다.

```
PROP SHEET - Sky   (dingding-papercut · SCENE token: Sky)

Weather. Six books read the sky to know what to do, so what a cloud is must be settled once.

🔴 A CLOUD IS NOT A BLACK SHAPE ON WHITE SKY - IT IS THE BLACK SKY WITH THE GAPS CUT OUT. The sky
  band along the top of the picture is black and joined to the frame at both ends; the bright
  places between the clouds are cut away. Clouds "apart" = many wide white gaps with crisp edges.
  Clouds "joined" = the gaps close to slivers. Nothing is added between the two states; the second
  is simply less cut away, exactly as NIGHT is in the anchor.
🔴 ANYTHING AIRBORNE MUST TOUCH SOMETHING. A bird, a leaf or a scrap in the air is cut so it either
  runs off the frame edge or touches the black it left. Nothing hangs unattached in the white.
STATES:
  1 CLEAR - no sky band at all; the sky is the same cut-away white as the water, and the far ridge
    (the one FAR GREY sheet) sits behind the hill.
  2 FOUR CLOUDS APART - a black band with four wide white gaps, edges crisp, gaps not mirroring.
  3 CLOUDS CLOSED - the same band with the gaps down to slivers, the band reaching further down the
    frame so the white water below it is narrower.
  4 RAIN - straight lines drawn from the black sky band to the black ground, edge to edge, never
    short free dashes. Slanted lines for a driving rain, upright for a steady one. 🔴 Rain THINS by
    the lines being FEWER and further apart, never by being fainter, and when it stops the sky band
    stays but the lines are gone.
  5 LIGHTNING - ONE white crack cut clean through the black sky, from the top edge down. 🔴 One book
    puts the same crack in the same place on two pages, so cut it as a single reusable shape.
  6 NIGHT - the black covers the whole sky; stars are round holes, at most 6, none in a line, each
    leaving the black around it joined.
🔴 THE WHITE CHANNEL STILL RUNS. On a black-sky page the unbroken white channel starts at the top
  of the topmost cut water rather than at the picture's top edge, and the rain lines carry the eye
  down to it. It never breaks.

PLATE: the six states in a row at one size, plus one panel of states 2 and 3 laid over each other so
  it is plain that only the gaps changed.

NOT: no character, no red, no lettering or numerals, no grey cloud, no soft or feathered cloud edge,
  no rainbow, no sun disc drawn as an object (a high sun is a single cut-away corner shape), no
  floating piece, no gradient or glow.
```

### §2.6 RiceSeedling — 🔴 아홉 권이 여기 걸린다 (그리고 작업표에 「포기」로만 남아 있었다)

> 어린 벼 한 포기가 **50·43 두 권의 전부**이고 12권은 「잎이 곧다 ↔ 누웠다」 하나로 열 쪽을 간다.
> 그런데 후보 목록엔 「포기」라는 **잘린 조각**으로만 있었다. 아래 「작업표가 틀린 자리」 참조.

```
PROP SHEET - RiceSeedling   (dingding-papercut · SCENE token: RiceSeedling)

One young rice plant, pulled from the seedbed and planted by hand. Nine books handle it and two are
about nothing else. It is also the thing a weed is mistaken for, so the weed is on this sheet too.

FORM: a small clump of at most 5 blades rising from one root, with a lump of soil still on the root.
  The blades are cut as a narrow comb (the same comb family as Straw, fewer and thinner teeth).
  It is one piece: the blades must stay joined to the root, the root to the soil lump.
🔴 THE WHOLE SERIES IS IN TWO STATES AND THEY MUST BE TELLABLE AT THUMBNAIL SIZE:
  UPRIGHT - every blade rises, tips above the waterline, the clump reads as a vertical mark.
  LIMP - every blade lies sideways ON the water surface, tips touching or under it, the clump reads
    as a horizontal mark. There is no half state.
🔴 A SEEDLING STANDING IN CUT-AWAY WATER WOULD FLOAT: at its base draw the round muddy mark that is
  its bridge (WaterMarks form 7). Every planted seedling has one.
STATES:
  1 ONE HELD UP by the root at eye height, blades hanging a little.
  2 UPRIGHT IN THE WATER, alone, with its muddy ring.
  3 LIMP ON THE WATER, alone, blades laid flat.
  4 A ROW OF NINE, upright, evenly spaced, running away from the viewer into the frame - the row is
    a RUN and is exempt from the repeat cap. Draw the same row with THREE GAPS in it.
  5 A ROW SEEN FROM ABOVE where every blade lies flat - the whole row is one horizontal band.
  6 A HEAP, eight of them thrown loose on a bank path, roots pointing every way.
  7 FLOATING AWAY, one lying on the water with a wake behind it, heading for a notch.
🔴 8 THE WEED THAT LOOKS LIKE IT - beside the seedling at the same size: from above the two are the
  SAME shape and must be drawn so. The whole difference is at the root - the weed's leaf is
  narrower and a straight bare stem runs down to the root with no soil lump. Draw them (a) side by
  side upright, (b) tangled together in a pulled heap of seven where two of them are the weed and a
  reader cannot pick them out. That failure is the point of one book; do not make it easy.

PLATE: states 1-7, then the weed comparison at double size, root end enlarged.

NOT: no character, no hands, no red, no lettering or numerals, no floating piece, no grey, no
  individual leaf veins, no root hairs drawn one by one, no shading or gradient.
```

### §2.7 Bowls — 그릇 넷이 고리로 놓인다

```
PROP SHEET - Bowls   (dingding-papercut · SCENE token: Bowls)

The family's eating vessels: four bowls, a spoon, and the tall water jar. Seven books use them and
one is about the order they are arranged in, so their shapes must be tellable from each other.

🔴 WHAT IS IN THE BOWL DECIDES WHAT IS CUT. RICE IS CUT AWAY - the inside of a rice bowl is white
  and is the brightest thing on an indoor page. GREENS ARE LEFT BLACK - a bowl of greens is a solid
  black mass. One book's whole first page is that contrast in one frame, so never soften it.
  Single grains of rice are small notches bitten from the black rim INTO the white, never black
  specks laid on the white.
FORM - four vessels, tellable blacked out:
```

## §3. 🔴 미결 「논둑」 17갈래 — 이름 흔들림이 아니라 **축이 안 읽히는 것**

> 🔴 **이 §3 은 판정 근거만 적는다. 이름→토큰 변환의 SSOT 는 `_stage-tokens.json` 이다.**
> 표 출력·검사 = `node packages/client/scripts/extract-series-stages.mjs --tokens dingding` (**미매칭 0** 이어야 한다).
> 🔴 **쪽 목록은 폐기했다** — 목록과 변환표를 따로 두니 어긋났다(변환표엔 있는데 목록엔 없는 이름이 나왔다).
> 규칙이 전 이름을 덮으므로 목록이 필요 없고, 「목록 밖이라 안 붙인다」가 생기지 않는다.
> 🔴 **SPOT**: `_stage-tokens.json` 의 `spots` 가 `null` 인 시트는 **시트명만** 붙인다. 값이 있는 시트도

지푸라기 · 떡 위의 손자국 · 하늘의 새와 구름이 **전부 금지된 섬**이다.

→ **해결책은 §2.4 `WaterMarks` 에 넣었다: 물 주름이 곧 다리(bridge)다.** 대본이 이미 물 위의 거의 모든
것에 주름·자국을 붙여 두어서, 매체 제약과 대본이 부딪히는 게 아니라 **같은 말을 하고 있었다**.
🔴 **앵커 `Style` 절에 한 줄을 넣는 것이 옳다** — 그러면 시트 스무 장이 각자 되풀이하지 않아도 된다:

```
A BLACK SHAPE INSIDE CUT-AWAY WHITE IS JOINED BY ONE HAIRLINE OF BLACK - on water that hairline is
the ripple or the wake it made, in the air it is the frame edge it runs off. Nothing is ever an
island, and the bridge is always a thing the scene already has.
```

### ③ `Terraces` 의 `0 ripples` 가 대본과 정면으로 부딪힌다

§1.1 `FIXED PARTS`: `the water in each terrace is cut away and is SHEET WHITE, 0 ripples`.
대본: **물 주름이 31쪽 14권**에 있고, 41권은 **「동그란 주름 세 겹 ↔ 매끈함」이 그 권의 눈금**이다
(41 p5·p6·p9). → `0 ripples` 를 **`ripples only as WaterMarks defines them`** 으로 고칠 것.

### ④ `Terraces` 에 더 넣을 것 둘

- **물풀 무더기 셋**(48) — 48 p2 와 p5 가 **같은 각도에 무더기 자리도 같아야** 성립한다. 붙박이라
  `FIXED PARTS` 몫이다.
- **마른 논바닥의 금이 젖어 풀리는 상태**(23 p9) — 지금은 `dry cracked ground` 뿐이라 「금이 사라지는
  중」을 그릴 근거가 없다. 23권은 그 한 장이 착지다.

### ⑤ `TreeOnBank` 에 나무 그림자 두 방향

앵커 §3 이 「그림자 = 또 하나의 오려 낸 검은 조각」을 이미 넣었는데, 14권은 **그 그림자가 통째로
이야기**다(p2 한쪽으로 누움 → p8 반대쪽으로 누움, 같은 물 같은 나무). 자리 시트에 **오전/오후 두
방향**을 못 박지 않으면 두 쪽이 비교되지 않는다.

### ⑥ 반복 상한 9 를 넘는 자리가 하나 있다

앵커: `Repeats are capped: … a crowd at most 7 silhouettes`. 21 p2 는 **개미 열둘**이다.
→ `Straw` 시트가 이미 쓴 말(`a RUN is exempt from the repeat cap`)로 푼다. §2.29 에 그렇게 썼다.
🔴 그 예외가 지금 `Straw` 시트 안에만 있어서 다른 시트가 못 본다 — **앵커로 올릴 것.**

### ⑦ 검은 하늘 페이지의 흰 물길

앵커: `ONE WHITE CHANNEL RUNS FROM THE TOP EDGE TO THE BOTTOM EDGE ON EVERY PAGE`.
21·38·44 는 하늘이 검다(§2.5 에서 하늘 띠를 검게 두고 구름 사이를 오려 내기로 정했다). 그러면 채널이
위 가장자리에 못 닿는다. → `NIGHT` 조항이 이미 「밤엔 하늘도 검고 채널은 그대로 오려져 있다」로
답해 두었으니, 그 조항을 **밤에서 「검은 하늘 페이지」로 넓히면 된다**(비 오는 낮이 그 안에 든다).
§2.5 는 그 전제로 쓰여 있다(빗줄기가 하늘에서 물까지 눈을 끌고 내려온다).
