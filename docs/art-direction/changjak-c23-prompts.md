## C-23 §1. 앵커 배정

**권**: `c23` 「이번엔 안 잘게」 (14쪽 · 4~6세 · 주제군 **C 자연·계절·동물** · 엔진 **옛이야기 비틀기** · 무대 토스카나 언덕의 흙길 하나 · 주인공 어린 토끼 + 거북)

**한 줄**: 갈대펜 떨림 선 + **평칠 두 색**(황토 볕 / 검푸른 그늘). 🔴 **흰 것이 없다 — 종이가 한 뼘도 안 드러나고, 화면의 모든 자리가 두 색 중 하나로 덮인다.** 앵커 슬러그 `changjak-shadeline` — **신규 민팅**(배정표 지정, 변경 없음).

**이 권이 그림에 요구하는 것** 셋.

1. 🔴 **매 쪽 화면을 가르는 것이 볕과 그늘의 칼 같은 경계 한 줄이고, 그건 두 평칠이 맞닿는 자리다.** 해가 높아지면 그늘이 좁아지고 기울면 다시 길어진다 — 그림이 하는 일 중 가장 중요한 것이 **경계선의 위치와 그늘의 폭**이다. 그러려면 그늘이 「어두운 색조」가 아니라 **면적을 가진 도형**이어야 한다.
2. 🔴 **몸의 성질이 이야기다.** 토끼는 더위에 약해 그늘마다 멈추고, 거북은 볕에 등이 데워져야 걷는다. 둘 다 진짜다. 실루엣이 정반대인 둘(늘어진 귀·긴 몸 ↔ 납작한 닫힌 등딱지)을 선이 져야 하고, 상태는 **귀 안쪽의 붉기**와 **등딱지 무늬의 또렷함** 두 계기판이 진다.
3. 🔴 **온도가 화면에 있어야 한다.** 열네 쪽이 한여름 정오를 향해 올라갔다 저녁으로 내려온다. **흰색이 한 점이라도 들어오면 그 순간 화면이 시원해진다** — 이 권에서 흰색은 장식이 아니라 온도계를 부수는 것이다.

**왜 이 공정인가.** 🔴 **어길 방법이 없는 매체를 골랐다**(§2.16.3). 「시원해 보이게 하지 마라」는 검수할 수 없지만 **「종이가 보이면 그 자리는 안 칠한 것이다」는 한 장에서 눈으로 센다.** 두 색이 종이를 통째로 덮으면 흰색·회색·하늘색이 들어올 자리가 물리적으로 없고, 그래서 볕은 식지 않는다. 그 위에 두 가지가 공짜로 딸려 온다 — ①**펜은 늘 반대 색을 문다**(오커 위에서는 검푸르게, 검푸름 위에서는 오커로 긋는다). 경계를 넘는 윤곽선이 몸 한가운데서 색을 바꾸므로, p7 의 「한 몸이 두 빛에 걸쳐 있다」가 문구가 아니라 공정이 된다. ②**선의 개수가 볕의 세기다.** 볕 안에서는 윤곽 하나와 안쪽 마크 셋까지만 그려 오커가 눈부신 공백으로 읽히고, 그늘 안에서는 같은 것이 해칭을 갖는다. 「하얗게 탄다」를 **「아무것도 안 그려져 있다」**로 옮기면 흰색 없이 눈부심이 생기고, 배경 예산도 저절로 지켜진다(§2.16).

**🔴 라인 충돌 확인 (§7.2).**

## C-23 §2. STYLE ANCHOR

```
STYLE ANCHOR - changjak-shadeline   (a young hare and a tortoise / a Tuscan hill road)

Style: broad reed-pen line over FLAT FILLS IN EXACTLY TWO COLOURS, 4-6 year old picture book. The
  reed nib is wide, dry and unsteady - every line wavers, runs thin and skips where the nib dries
  out, and blots where it has just been dipped. 🔴 NOTHING IS WHITE AND NO PAPER IS EVER LEFT
  BARE: every part of every page is covered by one of the two fills, and if bare paper shows, that
  area is unfinished.

RENDERING (finish hierarchy): 🔴 ONE HARD EDGE splits each page - the line where SUN OCHRE meets
  SHADE BLUE-GREEN. Cut clean, never blended, never soft.
  🔴 THE PEN CARRIES THE OTHER COLOUR: on ochre it draws in shade blue-green, on blue-green it
  draws in ochre, so one contour crossing the edge changes colour halfway along a body. 🔴 LINE
  COUNT IS THE HEAT: in the sun a thing gets its contour and AT MOST 3 marks inside it, so the
  ochre reads as blinding emptiness; in the shade the same thing carries hatching. Heat shimmer is
  made by BREAKING a far contour into wavering dashes, never by blur. FINISHED THINGS PER PAGE = 2
  - the animal and the one thing it touches. Cypress = 1 flat shape, at most 7 notches, 0 needles.
  Dry grass = at most 20 short strokes for a whole foreground, 0 outlined blades. Far ridges = at
  most 3 wavering lines. DENSITY RATION = none; all fourteen pages stay at 2.

PALETTE: SUN OCHRE #C9903C - every sunlit surface, road, dry grass and sky. SHADE BLUE-GREEN
  #2C4038 - every shaded surface, cypress, cast shadow, and the whole of the last page. Those two
  cover the paper and nothing is ever mixed between them. THE ONE ACCENT is the hare's ear
  insides, a single red laid flat in three steps and appearing NOWHERE else on any page, not on a
  tongue, a nose, a flower or the sun: pale #E6B7A6, flushed #D0664A, scarlet #B22B18. No white,
  grey, black, blue, pink, brown or green anywhere.

CHARACTER DESIGN LANGUAGE: both animals are made exactly as the page is - a flat fill with a
  reed-pen contour in the other colour - so a body in the sun is an ochre shape with almost nothing
  inside it, and the same body in the shade carries ochre hatching. 🔴 THE TWO SILHOUETTES ARE
  OPPOSITES and must separate at thumbnail size: the hare long and loose, the tortoise one low
  closed oval. Each has a drawn eye with a SEPARATE pen brow above it. FIXED GRADE: the hare walks
  on four legs and rises only to lift a forepaw or pull its own ear down; the tortoise walks on
  four legs and only pushes its neck out and draws it back - no gestures. No clothes, no props.

CANVAS: 16:9 double-page spread. One dirt road climbing a Tuscan hill and nothing else on all
  fourteen pages. Bottom 18% quiet for a caption. No lettering, numerals, signs or markers.

NOT: no digital slickness of any kind - airbrush, gradient, glow, 3D CG, cel-shading, photographic,
  or a texture filter laid over the flat fills / never a third flat colour, never a blended or
  shaded edge between the two, and no white or grey anywhere / not blurred, hazy, faded or spotlit
  / one sheet thick - no wool fibre, no felt fuzz, no stitching, no sculpted clay.
```

## C-23 §3. 캐릭터 시트 (🔴 이것부터 굽는다)

```
CHARACTER SHEET - HareHeat   (bake FIRST, before any scene)

Same make as the book: a flat fill with a broad reed-pen contour drawn in the OTHER fill colour,
dry skipping line. Draw this sheet on a SPLIT GROUND - the left half sun ochre #C9903C, the right
half shade blue-green #2C4038 - so both states of the animal are settled at once. No white
anywhere on this sheet, and no bare paper.

FACE: young hare, long head, small round muzzle. A drawn eye with a SEPARATE pen brow above it -
  the brows do the acting and must lift, pull together and tilt unevenly. Whiskers 3 strokes a
  side. The tongue, when it hangs out panting, is CONTOUR ONLY with no colour inside it.
EARS: 🔴 THE GAUGE, and the only place the accent red is ever used. Two tall ears; when they fold
  flat sideways the inside faces the reader. Draw the ladder as three heads in a row, same size
  and same angle, differing only in the flat step inside the ear: pale #E6B7A6 / flushed #D0664A /
  scarlet #B22B18. A child must be able to name which is hottest with the sheet across a room.
BODY: long and loose, four heads tall standing, low and stretched when it lies down. Long hind
  legs, small forepaws, short lifted tail.
🔴 IN SUN / IN SHADE (draw the same pose twice, once on each half of the split ground): in sun the
  body is an ochre shape with a blue-green outline and AT MOST 3 marks inside it; in shade it is a
  blue-green shape with ochre hatching along the flank and haunch. Same drawing, opposite make.
SHEET: on hind legs with one forepaw thrown up / four legs braced skidding to a stop, forepaws
  ploughing forward / lying flat on the belly with all four legs stretched out and the ears spread
  wide sideways / one forepaw pulling its own ear down so the inside faces us / leaping with all
  four feet off the ground; plus two close-ups - eyes screwed shut with the mouth wide open
  panting, and eyes wide open having just understood something. Plain split ground, no road.
SCENE token: HareHeat.
```

```
CHARACTER SHEET - TortoiseSun   (bake SECOND)

Same make and same reed nib as HareHeat - attach the approved HareHeat sheet so the two are drawn
by one hand and so the size between them is settled. Split ground again, sun ochre left, shade
blue-green right. 🔴 NO ACCENT RED ANYWHERE ON THIS ANIMAL, on any page of the book.

FACE: old-looking tortoise, blunt beak-like mouth, heavy lids. A drawn eye with a SEPARATE pen brow
  above it, but the brows barely move - this animal is calm on every page. Loose folded skin on the
  neck drawn as 3 or 4 lines.
BUILD: 🔴 ONE LOW CLOSED OVAL on four short posts, the exact opposite of the hare's loose length.
  The shell reads as a single dome shape with a plate pattern of 5 or 6 flat facets on it - never
  more, and the plates are shapes, not texture.
🔴 SHELL LADDER (two shells side by side, same size, same angle): WET-DULL - hatching laid across
  the shell so the plate pattern is muddled and hard to count. DRY-CRISP - the hatching gone, so
  the plates stand out as clean flat shapes with one confident unbroken contour. 🔴 That is the
  whole difference: no white highlight, no shine, no gloss mark, ever. The reader reads "warmed
  up" as "you can suddenly count the plates".
🔴 SCALE PLATE: the tortoise beside HareHeat at THE SAME DEPTH - the shell is about as long as the
  hare's body without its ears, and it sits far lower. Four pages put them side by side and
  perspective must not flip that.
SHEET: walking with the neck stretched right out and the legs long and straight, body lifted clear
  of the ground / walking slowly with the neck half out and eyes half shut / stopped with the neck
  turned to look sideways / pressed flat to the ground with the neck drawn right back inside and
  the legs folded under; plus one close-up of the head coming out of the shell. Plain split ground.
SCENE token: TortoiseSun.
```

```
CHARACTER SHEET - HillSet   (bake THIRD - the stage, used on every page)

The road, empty of animals, exactly as it sits on all fourteen pages. Two flat fills, reed-pen
line in the other colour, and no bare paper anywhere.

THE PLACE: one ochre dirt road climbing from the bottom of the frame to a big tree at the top of
  the hill, curving as it goes. Dry grass either side as at most 20 short strokes. Cypresses stand
  apart along the road, each ONE flat blue-green shape with at most 7 notches on its edge, 0
  needles, 0 trunk lines. A low stone wall and rolling ridges far off as at most 3 wavering lines.
🔴 THE EDGE IS THE SUBJECT OF THIS SHEET - draw the road four times in a row, same viewpoint,
  changing only where the sun/shade edge falls:
  A · EARLY MORNING - long blue-green bands lying right across the ochre road, three of them.
  B · LATE MORNING - one clean diagonal edge cutting the frame, wide shade on one side.
  C · NOON - the shade shrunk to the width of a cypress trunk at its foot; the rest all ochre.
  D · EVENING - wide bands again, alternating, neither colour winning.
  Every edge is cut clean. 🔴 Put A and C side by side and check that the shade is a SHAPE WITH
  AN AREA, not a darker mood.
🔴 SUN HAS NO LINES IN IT: on the ochre, things get a contour and at most 3 marks. On the
  blue-green, the same things carry ochre hatching. Show one cypress standing half in each so the
  difference is settled.
ALSO ON THIS SHEET: a hare-belly hollow pressed into the shade earth · a short line of hare prints
  on the ochre · one cicada on a branch as 4 marks · a single ochre disc for the low evening sun,
  sitting inside a blue-green ground. 0 lettering, 0 numerals, 0 signs.
SCENE token: HillSet.
```
