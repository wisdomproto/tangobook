# 미오네 유치원 — 개체 규격

> 🔴 이 파일은 **프롬프트로 그대로 들어간다.** `build-series-html.mjs` 가 `## <별칭>` 을 찾아 그 사람의
> 시트 프롬프트에 `[이 인물만의 규격]` 으로 붙인다. 제목은 `_series-config.mjs` 의 별칭 중 하나와
> **글자까지 같아야** 한다. 🔴 **마지막 `##` 뒤에 무엇을 적든 그 사람의 프롬프트에 붙는다**(맺음말 금지).
>
> 🔴 앵커(`mio-anchor.md`)는 **그 세계 전체**를 말한다. 여기는 **한 사람이 남과 무엇이 다른가**만 쓴다.
> 없으면 여섯이 이름만 다른 같은 지시를 받는다 — 타로·미나에서 「가족들이 너무 비슷하게 생김」이 나온 자리다.
>
> 🔴 **가르는 축은 「윤곽 밖으로 무엇이 나가는가」 하나다.** 앵커의 축은 `THE GAP BETWEEN FIGURES IS THE
> SENTENCE OF THE PAGE` — 간격을 재려면 각자의 실루엣이 또렷해야 하므로 이 축은 앵커와 안 부딪히고 오히려
> 앵커를 돕는다. 🔴 **여섯 다 통째로 까맣게 칠해도 서로 다른 모양이어야 한다** — 리노컷이라 판정이 특히 잘 맞고,
> 앵커 `FINISHED THINGS PER PAGE = 2` 때문에 **주인공 아닌 넷은 실제로 내부가 없는 덩어리로 그려진다.**
>
> | | 윤곽 밖으로 나가는 것 | 실루엣 한 줄 |
> |---|---|---|
> | 미오 | **위로 뾰족한 둘 + 가는 것 하나** | 삼각 귀 둘, 팔뚝보다 가는 꼬리 |
> | 보보 | **옆으로 둘, 뒤로는 아무것도** | 귀가 머리 폭 밖으로 — 유일하게 가로로 긴 머리, 목 없음 |
> | 라라 | **아무것도 — 대신 테두리가 톱니** | 정수리부터 어깨까지 오톨도톨, 직선이 한 군데도 없다 |
> | 가가 | **위로 하나(목)** | 대 하나에 구슬 하나, 옆으로는 아무것도 안 나간다 |
> | 두리 | **아래로 굵은 것 하나** | 낮고 둥근 귀, 제 다리만큼 굵은 꼬리 |
> | 바우 선생님 | **가로로 셋(주둥이+귀 둘)** | 가로로 긴 머리 + 무릎을 덮는 치맛단 |
>
> 🔴 **첫 렌더(`comic-assets/mio-plan`, 2026-08-17 확인)에서 실제로 무너진 자리**: ①미오↔두리가
> 실루엣에서 같은 모양이었다(둘 다 삼각 귀 + 검은 덩어리) ②두리의 정체가 **가면과 꼬리 무늬** = 실루엣에서
> 사라지는 내부 마크뿐이었다 ③선생님이 파란 원피스를 입은 **큰 아이**였다(라라·가가와 같은 옷) ④가가가
> **손도 옷도 없는 거위**였다 ⑤보라 손수건이 **손에 들려** 있었다(SCENE 84곳은 「목에 보라 손수건」).
> 이 파일은 그 다섯을 각각 막는다.

## Mio kitten

```
THE ONLY POINTED HEAD IN THE CLASS, AND THE ONLY THIN TAIL.
EARS - two sharp upright triangles, taller than they are wide, standing clear of the skull with
  cleared paper between them. NOBODY ELSE HAS A POINTED EAR. They prick forward, lay back and swing
  when the head turns; the shape stays a triangle whichever way it points.
TAIL - ONE tail, as long as he is tall and THINNER THAN HIS OWN FOREARM along its whole length,
  never thickening toward the root. It lies straight back, sweeps aside for balance, rises in a hook
  or droops - the DIRECTION is the acting and the THINNESS is the constant. It is on the page in
  every view including front and back, because it is half of what tells him from Duri.
BUILD - the compact one. A small round head, a short body, clothed neck to ankle in SOOT so the whole
  figure is one dark block and only the two ears and the one thin tail leave it.
ACCENT - a PURPLE satchel on a strap worn diagonally across the chest, so the purple reads as a line
  across the dark block and not as a dot. Where the story takes it off him it is set down inside the
  frame, never out of the picture.
🔴 AGAINST DURI, and the drawing fails if either is missing: pointed ears against low round ones ·
  a thin whip tail against one as thick as a leg.
```

## Bobo piglet

```
THE ONLY HEAD WIDER THAN IT IS TALL, AND THE ONLY ONE WITH NO NECK.
EARS - two ears folding OUT and DOWN from the sides of the skull, each reaching past the width of the
  head, so his head silhouette is a HORIZONTAL shape while every other head in this book is round or
  upright. They flop when he moves; they never stand up.
🔴 HEAD AND BODY ARE ONE OUTLINE - no notch, no shoulder step, no cleared paper between skull and
  shoulders. The other four all show that notch, and this is the fastest way to find him in a group.
SNOUT - a round flat disc cut as its own small block on the front of the face, set low.
TAIL - a short screw that STAYS INSIDE HIS OUTLINE. He is the one child with nothing hanging behind
  him at all, and that absence is his mark in the silhouette row.
BUILD - the widest of the five, and the width is in the chest and belly, not in loose clothes.
ACCENT - a PURPLE cap sitting flat on the crown between the two folded ears. It can be shoved back or
  knocked crooked; it never leaves the page.
```

## Lala lamb

```
🔴 HER OUTLINE HAS NO STRAIGHT EDGE ANYWHERE - the only toothed silhouette in the book.
FLEECE - cut as a border of small round bumps running over the crown, down both sides of the head and
  across the shoulders in ONE continuous serrated line. AT MOST 20 bumps around the whole outline,
  unequal, and no bump a mirrored copy of its neighbour. The fleece is soft: it flattens against the
  head when wet and swings when she moves, and the bumps get fewer and lower as it flattens.
🔴 SHE HAS EARS AND THEY WORK. Two small ears set LOW at the side of the head, BELOW the top of the
  fleece, so nothing points up above the bumps - they read as two notches interrupting the serrated
  edge. They lay flat back, prick up, and can be COVERED BY HER OWN TWO HANDS. One whole volume is
  about her covering them, so an ear that cannot be found and covered is a failure.
BUILD - narrow through the body under all that fleece, thin arms and legs. Below the fleece line she
  is smooth, so the toothed top and the plain bottom read as two different surfaces.
ACCENT - a PURPLE hairpin clipped INTO the fleece at one side of the head, on the same side in every
  volume.
```

## Gaga gosling

```
🔴 ONE VERTICAL STALK AND NOTHING SIDEWAYS.
NECK - as long as her body is tall, the same narrow width from shoulder to skull, carrying a small
  head with a blunt beak cut as its own block. When she stretches it the head goes up and the
  SHOULDER LINE DOES NOT MOVE - she stands the same height at the shoulder as the other four and is
  taller only by the neck.
BODY - a wide teardrop standing on two feet. 🔴 THE WINGS STAY INSIDE THE BODY OUTLINE when she is
  not using them, so nothing at all protrudes at her sides. At thumbnail size she is a stalk with a
  bulb on top of a pear, and no other child in the book is a stalk.
🔴 HANDS - HER WINGS END IN THREE BLUNT FINGERS AND THEY DO THE WORK: holding, folding paper,
  pressing a fold flat, taking a thing out of another child's hand. Never a paddle, never a feather
  tip where a hand should be.
🔴 CLOTHES - she wears cloth like the other four, a smock over the body. A naked bird standing among
  dressed children reads as an animal that wandered in, not as a child of this class.
ACCENT - a PURPLE ribbon tied where the neck meets the body, the one place on her that never goes
  behind anything.
```

## Duri raccoon

```
🔴 LOW ROUND EARS AND A TAIL AS THICK AS HIS OWN LEG.
EARS - two low rounded arcs on the corners of the skull, WIDER THAN THEY ARE TALL, NEVER POINTED.
  The pointed ear belongs to Mio and to nobody else in this book. They turn forward, back, sideways
  and two different ways at once; the shape stays a low arc whichever way it turns.
TAIL - ONE tail AS THICK AS HIS OWN LEG along its whole length and about as long as his body,
  carrying AT MOST 5 rings. It stands up, streams out behind him when he runs, or lies along the
  ground - the DIRECTION is the acting and the THICKNESS is the constant.
🔴 THE DARK BAND ACROSS HIS EYES AND THE RINGS ON HIS TAIL ARE INTERIOR MARKS AND NEITHER SURVIVES A
  SILHOUETTE. They are not what tells him apart, and a sheet that leans on them has not done the job.
  The ears and the weight of the tail are what tell him apart.
EYES - the gouged chip is cut INSIDE the dark band, so each chip has dark on every side of it.
BUILD - the same height as the rest, ordinary width. His hands are the busiest in the class: he is
  drawn mid-reach, mid-run and half-turned-away more often than anyone.
🔴 ACCENT - a PURPLE handkerchief TIED ROUND HIS NECK. Never held in a hand, never in a pocket. His
  hands are always full of something else, so an accent he has to carry would leave the page.
```

## Teacher Bau big dog

```
SHE IS NOT A BIG CHILD, AND HER SIZE IS NOT WHAT SAYS SO. She stands twice a child's height, but she
is kneeling, crouching or sitting on half the pages she appears on - including the one page in every
volume that is hers - and on those pages the height is gone. THREE THINGS CARRY HER INSTEAD, and all
three survive her sitting down on the floor:
① 🔴 HER HEAD IS WIDER THAN IT IS TALL. A long muzzle projects forward past the front of the skull
   and two heavy ears hang down past the jaw on either side, so the head reads as a HORIZONTAL shape
   with three lobes. Every child's head is round or upright, and no child has a hanging ear.
② 🔴 HER HEM COVERS HER KNEES. A plain working smock to mid-calf whose hem is one wide curve hiding
   where her legs bend; every child's legs show from the hip down. Kneeling, the hem pools on the
   floor and she becomes a low broad mound with a head on it. Sleeves rolled above the elbow so the
   forearms are bare - always, on every page, standing or sitting.
③ 🔴 HER HANDS ARE AS WIDE AS A CHILD'S HEAD. Whatever she holds, she holds it in a hand that is
   plainly the largest shape in the picture. This is the one measurement that does not shrink when
   she kneels.
🔴 CROWN TO SHOULDER IS ONE UNBROKEN DIAGONAL - no neck, no notch, sloping shoulders. Every child has
a notch between head and shoulder. From behind she is one closed dome with both ears inside the
outline and nothing standing off it.
🔴 HER SMOCK IS NOT A CHILD'S SMOCK. If a child on the page wears RIVER cloth, hers is cut longer,
plainer and with a different hem line, so the two are not the same garment at two sizes.
NOT - 🔴 SHE WEARS NO PURPLE AND CARRIES NO ACCENT OF ANY KIND. She has no errand of her own and NO
TOOL OF HER OWN: no pointer, no stick, no bundle, nothing tucked under an arm. What is in her hands
is always the thing the children are working on at that moment.
```
