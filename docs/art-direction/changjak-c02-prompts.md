## C-02 §1. 앵커 배정

**권**: `c02` 「소가 아는 길」 (12쪽 · 4~6세 · 주제군 **C 자연·계절·동물** · 엔진 **관찰과 성장** · 무대 알프스 목초지 해 질 녘 비탈 · 주인공 새끼 개)

**한 줄**: 젖은 종이에 번지게 해 놓고 **완전히 마른 뒤에 가는 마른 선만 얹는다**(2단계). 🔴 **선이 있는 자리 = 표시가 있는 자리**이고, 나머지는 영원히 테두리 없는 번짐이다. 앵커 슬러그 `changjak-mistbleed` — **신규 민팅**.

**이 권이 그림에 요구하는 것** 넷.

1. 🔴 **안개가 형태를 삼키는 것 = 종이가 물감을 빨아들이는 것.** 안개를 흐림·뿌옇게로 그리면 그건 초점이 나간 그림이지 안개가 아니다(§7.21). 번짐은 **가장자리가 솜털이고 blooms 가 있는 젖은 흔적**이라 흐림과 다르다.
2. 🔴 **개가 「보는」 순간이 화면에서 정확히 그 자리여야 한다.** 표시(반들반들한 바위 · 밤색 털 · 눌린 풀 두 줄 · 발굽 자국)에만 마른 선이 있고 나머지엔 없다 — 그래서 독자는 p3 에서 개보다 먼저 본다.
3. 🔴 **선의 개수가 이 권의 계기판이다.** 안개가 짙어질수록 선이 줄고 걷히면 돌아온다. 그래서 **쪽마다 선 예산을 숫자로 못 박았다**(p1 14 → p5 2 → p9 30 → p12 전부). 형용사로 쓰면 열두 장이 다 같은 밀도로 온다(§2.16.1).
4. **개가 열두 쪽을 연기한다** — 신남 → 큰소리 → 답답 → 겁 → 안도 → 갸웃 → 알아봄 → 환희 → 따라감. 눈썹이 필요하다.

**후보 3.**

① **C8 워시 · 2단계 번짐(신규 앵커)** — 요구 1·2·3 을 매체 하나가 동시에 푼다. 배정표가 이 권에 준 공정 그대로다.
② **C6 단색조+여백** — 안개엔 최적이지만 **탈락**: 여백은 「아무것도 없다」이고 이 권의 안개는 「있는데 안 보인다」다. 여백으로 그리면 p12 에서 되살아날 것이 없다.
③ **C7 회화적 톤(과슈 산 풍경)** — **탈락**: 알프스 목초지는 관광 엽서의 정본이고, 붓 톤은 밤색 등에 하이라이트와 반사광을 뿌린다. 그 순간 「화면에서 유일한 따뜻한 색」이 무너져 p6·p9 의 색 잇기가 죽는다.

**🔴 c10 과 같은 C8 인데 어떻게 갈랐나** — 배정표 §3.1 이 정한 축(공정)을 여덟 겹으로 굳혔다.

## C-02 §2. STYLE ANCHOR

```
STYLE ANCHOR - changjak-mistbleed   (puppy and cow / Alpine pasture in fog)

Style: two-stage watercolour on cold soft-white cotton rag #F4F5F3 with a heavy woolly grain,
  for a 4-6 year old picture book.

RENDERING (finish hierarchy - the one rule): STAGE ONE, the paper is soaked and colour is
  dropped into the wet so it spreads and stops by itself. Every shape on the page is made this
  way and has NO edge - fur, grass, rock, slope and cow all end in a furred bleed with blooms.
  STAGE TWO, only after the paper is bone dry, a fine dry brush lays a few thin lines ON TOP,
  and it touches ONLY what the story calls a mark. Everything else stays an unlined bleed for
  ever. That is why this picture is a fog: an unlined shape reads as swallowed. Never outline a
  whole animal. Never re-wet a dried line. Depth = paler and wetter, never blur.

PALETTE: paper #F4F5F3 - fog #E4E7E5 - wet green #7C8C72 - deep grass #4F5D4C - stone grey
  #A6A9A4 - CHESTNUT #8A5A34 - dry-line pigment #3E4440 - pup cream #EDE7DA, fog-beaded #DCD8CE.
  CHESTNUT is the only warm colour in the book and exactly three things carry it: the cow's
  back, the hairs caught on the rock, the insides of the ears. Evening light on pages 1 and 12
  is the palest tint of that same chestnut, #C9A277 - it is not a new colour. No orange, no
  blue, no red, no pink anywhere. The pup is cream and must sink into the fog; it never stands
  out by colour.

COUNTS (upper limits - count them): grass = 5-9 bled strokes a clump, 0 blades drawn - fur = 0
  hairs drawn, a coat is one bled shape - rock = 1 bled mass - hoof print = 1 dry oval plus 1
  shadow line - fog = 0 strokes, bare paper and one pale wash. DRY LINES ON A PAGE = exactly the
  budget written in that page's LINE line (page 1 = 14, page 5 = 2, page 9 = 30, page 12 = every
  shape in frame); never spend a line on something the story does not call a mark.
  FINISHED THINGS PER PAGE = 2 (the pup and the one thing it is looking at). DENSITY RATION =
  page 9 only, where finished things rise to 4. Extras: 0 birds, 0 flowers, 0 huts, 0 people,
  0 fences except the one drystone wall.

CHARACTER DESIGN LANGUAGE: each body is a single bled silhouette, readable from shape alone at
  thumbnail size; eyes are two dry dots with a SEPARATE dry brow line above them so the pup can
  act. FIXED GRADE: both animals stay on four legs with no clothes, no fingers, no shoes. The
  pup barks and talks. The cow only lows - give it no open-mouth talking pose and no bell.

CANVAS: 16:9 double-page spread, one scene, the bottom 18% left quiet for a caption. No text,
  letters, numerals, speech bubbles or watermark anywhere in the image.

NOT: no digital slickness of any kind - airbrush, smooth gradient, glow, 3D CG, cel-shading,
  photographic, or a grain filter laid over flat colour (the wet paper must MAKE the edges) /
  not blurred, hazy or out of focus - a bleed has furred pigment edges and hard-rimmed blooms,
  a blur has neither / not ink-outlined cartoon shapes / not felt, wool, stitching or clay.
```

**🔴 매 컷 확인하는 세 줄** — `FOG:`(화면 몇 %가 선 없는 번짐인가) · `LINE:`(마른 선 예산과 쓸 자리) · `ORDER:`(누가 앞인가).

## C-02 §3. 캐릭터 시트 (🔴 이것부터 굽는다)

```
CHARACTER SHEET - PupCream   (bake FIRST, before any scene)

Same make as the book: two-stage watercolour on cold soft-white cotton rag #F4F5F3. The body is
ONE bled shape with no edge; a fine dry brush adds only the few lines listed below, after
drying. Do not render smoothly just because there is no background behind it.

FACE: young sheepdog pup, round skull, short muzzle. Two dry dark dots #3E4440 for eyes with a
  SEPARATE short dry brow line above each - the brows are the acting and must lift, pull
  together and tilt unevenly. Nose = one small dark bleed. Mouth = one dry line. No dot-eyes
  without brows, no catchlight, no drawn hairs.
COAT: cream #EDE7DA as a single soft bleed, one deeper cream bloom along the back; grey ears
  #A6A9A4 bleeding into the head with no join; ear insides CHESTNUT #8A5A34 - the only warm
  colour on this animal. FOG-BEADED STATE: the whole shape drops to #DCD8CE and a scatter of
  tiny bare-paper dots sits along the back where droplets caught. Matte - wet is value, never
  shine.
DRY LINES ALLOWED ON THIS ANIMAL: at most 4 in any view - two eyes, one brow pair, one mouth,
  or one forepaw pad. Never an outline of the body.
BUILD: small, four legs, plump chest, tail that flags straight up, folds flat, or drops between
  the hind legs; silhouette readable at thumbnail size.
SHEET: side view trotting with tail up / three-quarter mid-leap, forelegs off the ground, head
  turned back barking / front view frozen stiff, tail tucked down / low crouch with nose flat to
  the ground, rump raised; plus three close-ups - delighted, frightened (ears back, brows
  together), puzzled (one brow up, head tilted). Plain paper, no scenery, no cow.
NO ORANGE, BLUE, RED OR PINK anywhere. SCENE token: PupCream.
```

## C-02 §4. 12컷

각 컷은 `STYLE ANCHOR + @image1(PupCream) + @image3(TrailMarks) + 아래 블록`.
소가 나오는 쪽(p1·p3·p6~p12)은 `@image2(CowChestnut)` 를 더 붙인다.
🔴 **p5 → p9 순으로 먼저** 굽는다(안개 판 · 선 판). p12 는 반드시 **p1 승인본을 ref 로**(같은 비탈 · 같은 거리 · 앞뒤만 반대).

### p1 — 개가 앞장서고 소가 따라온다

```
CAMERA: medium wide, child's eye level, the slope falling away to the right; sun sitting on the
  ridge behind.
SUBJECT: lower LEFT and nearer, PupCream mid-bounce with both forelegs off the ground, head
  twisted back over its shoulder barking, mouth wide, tail straight up. Upper RIGHT and further
  back, CowChestnut walking slowly with its head half lowered, one ear turned toward the pup.
FOG: 0 percent - this is the only page with no fog at all. Far ridge, drystone wall and slope
  are all visible to the horizon. Evening light = the palest chestnut tint #C9A277 washed across
  the upper slope only; no orange, no gold, no pink.
LINE: 14 dry lines - 4 on the pup, 5 on the cow, 3 along the top edge of the drystone wall, 2 in
  the near grass. Nothing else in frame has a line, including the far ridge.
ORDER: the pup is in front, the cow behind, and the gap between them is wide and obvious. Page
  12 will hold this exact framing with the two swapped, so keep the distance measurable.
FINISH: 2 (the pup + the cow). Wall and ridge are unlined bleeds. The cow's chestnut back is the
  darkest, warmest thing on the page and the eye should land there first.
```

### p2 — 골짜기에서 안개가 올라와 돌담을 끊는다

```
CAMERA: wide, eye level, looking down the slope into the valley; the frame splits horizontally.
SUBJECT: centre-left, PupCream planted on all fours with its chin thrown up, chest pushed
  forward, tail stiff, mouth a round bark. Right and behind, CowChestnut keeps walking downhill
  without stopping, head low, already half into the pale.
FOG: 45 percent - the LOWER half of the frame is fog, the upper half is still wet green slope.
  The fog is a soaked pale wash #E4E7E5 with a furred upper edge and two blooms, not a soft
  gradient and not a cloud shape.
LINE: 10 dry lines - 4 on the pup, 3 on the cow, and 3 THAT STOP DEAD where the drystone wall
  enters the fog. The wall has line above that point and none below it; that break is the whole
  page.
ORDER: pup in front and shouting, cow behind and already moving past him.
FINISH: 2 (the pup + the wall it is standing beside). Beads of water on the grass tips = bare
  paper dots, 0 drawn drops.
```

### p3 — 소가 바위에 코를 대고, 개는 앞에서 뱅뱅 돈다

```
CAMERA: medium, eye level. RIGHT, the cow stopped at a boulder; LEFT and nearer, the pup
  circling. Fog to the knee.
SUBJECT: right, CowChestnut stopped square on all four legs, head turned sideways and muzzle
  pressed flat against the boulder's flank, nostrils flared, eyes half shut. Left, PupCream bent
```
