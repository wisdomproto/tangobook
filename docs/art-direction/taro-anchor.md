## §1. taro-simple-batik

```
STYLE ANCHOR - taro-simple-batik (Taro and Mumu's island village / simplified batik)
TARO SIMPLE BATIK STORYBOOK — new cast reference, replacing all old patterned cast sheets.
Flat calm indigo/blue and warm cream shapes, gently handmade contours, subtle quiet textile grain.
Large uninterrupted colour areas; no ornamental spots, diamonds, flowers, filigree, repeating motifs,
crackle lines or dotted outlines on bodies or scenery. No photoreal anatomy, glossy plastic or 3D.
Readable cream faces, cream inner ears and one broad plain cream chest/belly patch. Exactly two eyes;
no forehead marks. Natural soft rounded animal bodies, flexible elbows/knees, sturdy limbs.
Match the attached NEW cast reference for face, species, colour blocks, proportions and accessories.
Eyes, brows, mouths and ears may act naturally; do not copy the reference smile into every scene.

PALETTE: CLOTH = warm cream #EFE3CC; MID = muted blue #4C6E8C; DEEP = indigo #22406B.
These names describe broad colour shapes, not mandatory dye passes or hard limits on eye readability.
ACCENT = burnt orange #E2711D, only Taro's waist cord and Mumu's headband. Adults carry no orange.
Faces and chest/belly patches are broad cream shapes; dark eyes/noses remain readable. No all-over
patterning on skin, fur, ears, tails, trees or buildings. Shadows are simple flat blue shapes.
Grass bracelets, fruit, sand and lamps may use cream; keep story-relevant shapes and object counts.

CHARACTER DESIGN LANGUAGE: two upright child friends and two naturally proportioned adults.
Taro is a small indigo monkey, round ears, soft arms and short legs, one curled tail, orange waist cord.
Mumu is a taller medium-blue deer, long leaf ears, naturally longer legs, tiny tail, orange headband.
Taro's mother is a medium-blue adult monkey with a gently curving tail without a closed loop.
Mumu's grandmother is an indigo adult deer with a normal short neck and gently stocky body.
Adults have appropriately sized heads and natural joints; no endlessly long necks, tiny heads,
noodle limbs or forced crouches. Hands can hold objects; never replace working hands with hooves.
No clothing or jewellery beyond the children's signature cords unless a story explicitly requires
an object. Both adults may stand, sit or walk as scripted; a reference pose never fixes their action.

STAGE CLAUSES:
  VILLAGE - open cream yards, blue walls, indigo roofs/tree masses. Broad calm areas with a few
    structural details. WellTree and CreekTree have the same silhouette; preserve village geography.
  RAIN AND AFTER - blue wet ground, sparse slanted cream rain strokes, simple puddles without
    duplicate faces. Shelter and wetness remain readable. No bright magenta or missing background.
  WATER - calm broad blue surface, cream sand, few readable ripples only where action needs them.
    Preserve submerged/visible objects, stepping-stone counts, water levels and current direction.
  NIGHT - indigo surroundings, blue planes and cream lamp pools; keep faces readable where lit.
    Simplify distant scenery. Do not turn faces into dark featureless masks.

FACIAL ACTING: eyes/lids, brows, mouth tension and ear angle convey the page's actual feeling.
Concern: attentive tense eyes and lowered mouth corners. Refusal: firm mouth, no cheerful grin.
Concentration: gaze toward the task, a lightly pressed mouth. Sadness: lowered gaze and soft
inner-raised brows. Relief/joy: relaxed or smiling eyes and mouth appropriate to the moment.
Speech is not automatically a grin or round O mouth. Change expression without redesigning faces.
Exactly two anatomical eyes, no third eye/forehead spots. Respect hand/prop close-ups, rear views
and empty scenes; never add faces to show speech. One captured moment, no expression row.

COMPOSITION: 16:9 picture-book spread, generous quiet areas, characters and the story object read
first. Keep camera, characters, object counts, positions and chronology. No text, numerals, labels,
patterns as filler, magenta background, 3D, airbrush glow or excessive texture. A ground shadow needed
by the story keeps its exact length and direction. Natural shadow length is not a stretched body.
```

## §2. 가족이 아닌 네 등장인물 시트

```
CHARACTER SHEET - Taro and Mumu redesigned cast
[여기에 §1 의 앵커를 붙인다]
Four full-body characters on one ground line: Taro, Mumu, Taro's mother, Mumu's grandmother.
Taro is shortest, Mumu about a head taller, adults larger with comfortable head proportions.
Match the supplied new cast reference; no old patterned sheets. Plain cream, no labels or scenery.
```

## §3. 유지하는 이야기 정보

타로와 무무는 서로 다른 집에 사는 단짝이다. 말린 꼬리/긴 귀·키 차이/허리끈·머리끈은 유지한다.
어른의 목·등을 비정상적으로 늘여 구분하는 옛 규격은 폐기했다. 뒷모습은 SCENE이 요구할 때만 쓴다.
그림자 밟기·그림자 길이·돌 줄·조개 개수·풀팔찌 등 줄거리 정보는 장식과 구분해 보존한다.
