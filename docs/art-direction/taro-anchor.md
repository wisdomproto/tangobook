## §1. 앵커 — `taro-batik`

```
STYLE ANCHOR - taro-batik   (Taro and Mumu's island village / wax resist, dipped twice)

Style: wax-resist dyeing on woven cotton, ONE indigo dye bath used twice and one small second dye,
  4-6 year old picture book. 🔴 THE CLOTH STARTS PALE AND ONLY GETS DARKER - nothing is ever painted
  light. Wax is laid on, the cloth is dipped, more wax is laid on, it is dipped again, so there are
  exactly THREE values: waxed before any dip is the palest, waxed after the first dip is the middle,
  never waxed is the deepest. Every value shows the weave running through it. SHADING IS ZERO - no
  modelling, gradient, cast shadow or glow.

RENDERING (finish hierarchy): 🔴 EVERY BRIGHT THING IN THIS BOOK IS A PLACE THAT WAS BLOCKED, NOT A
  PLACE THAT WAS LIT - lamplight, wet leaves and the shine on water are CLOTH areas with a hard
  edge, never a soft halo. 🔴 RAIN IS DRAWN WITH THE WAX PEN, at most 13 hairline CLOTH lines all
  leaning one way, and it is the only thing allowed to cross another shape. Cracked wax lets dye in
  as hairline DEEP lines: at most 9 per page, only inside CLOTH areas, never across a face, never in
  a row. Repeats are capped: leaves at most 9 of one stamped shape · houses behind at most 4 flat
  DEEP silhouettes with 0 windows · stars at most 14 CLOTH dots; no repeat mirrors its neighbour.
  FINISHED THINGS PER PAGE = 2. DENSITY RATION = none.

PALETTE: CLOTH #EFE3CC, waxed before any dip - faces, light, rain, steam, the brightest thing
  anywhere · MID #4C6E8C, one dip - sky, water, walls, cloth, most of the world · DEEP INDIGO
  #22406B, two dips - animal backs, shade, tree masses, night, the ground of the whole book · ACCENT
  ORANGE #E2711D, 🔴 a separate small dye touching nothing but Taro's sash and Mumu's head band. No
  white, no black, no green, no pink.

STAGE CLAUSES (the stage changes where the wax goes, not the dyes):
  VILLAGE - walls and ground MID, roofs and tree masses DEEP, the yard left as one broad CLOTH area
    the two of them stand on.
  RAIN AND AFTER - MID covers the whole page including the ground, everything under a roof stays MID
    and everything out in it is DEEP, the rain lines are CLOTH and nothing anywhere is blurred. Once
    it has passed the ground stays DEEP because it is soaked and CLOTH steam rises off it, at most 5
    hard-edged shapes; puddles are flat CLOTH areas with 0 reflections.
  WATER - one unbroken MID area, 0 ripples, 0 glints; a thing floating on it keeps its whole
    outline, a thing under it is DEEP with no edge of its own. Sand is CLOTH.
  NIGHT - DEEP covers everything, MID exists only where a lamp reaches, the lamp itself is CLOTH. A
    thing being looked for is the one CLOTH shape in the page's DEEP field.

CHARACTER DESIGN LANGUAGE: animals are built from the same dipped areas as the world. GRADE:
  bipedal, upright, wearing loose light cloth, bare feet. Backs and heads are DEEP; 🔴 THE WHOLE
  FACE IS WAXED CLOTH, so a face is the palest thing on any page and the eyes, nose and mouth are
  small DEEP marks left inside it. Eyes are two solid dots set wide apart; the mouth is ONE curve. 🔴
  THE JOKE IS ON THE FACE - expression is the MOUTH CURVE alone, the eyes only open or close, their
  size NEVER changes, and nothing crosses a face, not even rain. TARO is a monkey child, thin limbs,
  one long curling tail, the smaller; MUMU is broader and heavier, no tail, short round ears.

CANVAS: 16:9 double-page spread. 🔴 No lettering, numerals or signs anywhere.

NOT: no airbrush, gradient, glow or 3D render / no soft, feathered or blurred edge anywhere / no
  white and no black pigment / no light painted on top of a dark area.
```

🔴 **컷에 붙일 때는 그 쪽 무대의 `STAGE CLAUSES` 한 조항만 붙인다** — 앵커 전문은 3656자지만 조항 넷 중 셋을 빼면 실효 ~2970자라 「그림 한 장 4,500」 예산 안이다. 한 시리즈 = 한 앵커 구조에서 앵커가 3,200 을 넘는 것은 무대를 다 덮기 때문이고, 그 초과분은 **한 쪽에 동시에 쓰이지 않는다**.

**관통 줄** (매 쪽)

```
WAX:  every bright thing is a blocked place with a hard edge - light is never painted on
PAIR: two orange marks means both are here, one orange mark means the other is off the page
RAIN: when it rains everything stops - anything still moving is out in it and therefore DEEP
```

## §2. 왜 이 공정인가

- 🔴 **다섯 중 유일하게 바탕이 어둡다**(슬레이트 §악센트). 천을 담그는 공정이라 흰 종이가 안 나오고,
  그래서 **밝은 것이 전부 「막은 자리」**가 된다. 이 성질이 골격이라 조항·관통 줄·NOT 이 전부 여기서 나온다.
- **비가 시간을 정한다**(슬레이트 §무대) → 🔴 **비를 왁스 펜으로 긋는다.** 비는 그리는 게 아니라 막는
  것이라, 이 무대 조건이 매체의 핵심 도구와 같은 동작이 된다. 「스콜이 오면 뭐든 멈춘다」는
  `RAIN` 조항에서 **지붕 밑 = MID / 밖 = DEEP** 두 값으로 갈려 문장 없이 읽힌다.
- 🔴 **얼굴만 CLOTH** 로 못박은 것이 페파형을 살린다. 어두운 바탕에서 웃음이 묻히지 않으려면 얼굴이 화면에서
  가장 밝아야 한다 — 어두운 시리즈에서 「얼굴을 못 읽는다」가 제일 흔한 실패다.
- **크랙 선이 이 앵커의 손자국**이고 다른 넷엔 없다. 단 상한(9)과 금지 자리(얼굴)를 안 적으면 화면을
  뒤덮으므로 `RENDERING` 에 개수로 적었다(형용사 금지, `_ANCHOR-SPEC` §형용사 대신 개수 상한).
- 🔴 **둘이 다 있는지 주황 두 점으로 센다.** 단짝 둘이 축이라 「누가 지금 없는가」가 매 쪽 정보다.
