# 피포 개별 시트 공통 지침

이 영문 블록만 개별 시트에 사용한다. 장면용 앵커와 다른 캐릭터의 체형은 넣지 않는다.

```
PIPO STORYBOOK CHARACTER SHEET — match the supplied approved story illustration.
The attached story character defines identity AND rendering. Extract only the named character;
exclude companions, scenery and the temporary action. Old cast-card images are not the reference.
STYLE: warm cream paper grain, lively charcoal/graphite contours, soft short fur strokes or
feather/wool marks appropriate to the species, gentle grey pencil modelling and readable dark
eyes, nose and mouth. Light fur/fleece/feathers remain warm white. Hand-drawn picture-book finish;
no photoreal anatomy, dark felt doll, plastic, 3D render or blurred ink-blob eyes. Eyelids, brows
and mouth can change expression while the character's face and natural markings stay consistent.
Only the puppy's scarf is golden yellow. Preserve the neutral colours of each other character.
Soft animal bodies and rounded limbs; no human musculature. Draw the SAME named individual in
three views: relaxed front, easy three-quarter step and relaxed back. Keep coat, ears, muzzle,
tail, accessories and proportions consistent. Infer unseen back details simply from this design.
One character only, on plain warm cream, with clear space between views. No scenery, captions,
labels, swatches, additional characters or silhouette-test rows. Do not copy the scene's pose.
```
