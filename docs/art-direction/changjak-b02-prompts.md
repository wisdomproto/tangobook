## B-02 §1. 앵커 배정

**권**: `b02` 「계단 아래 작은 문」 (12쪽 · 4~6세 · 주제군 **B 상상·변신** · 엔진 **교환·연쇄** · 무대 런던 계단집 계단 밑 · 주인공 새끼 강아지 + 아주 작은 생쥐)

**한 줄**: **스크래치보드 새김.** 검은 판을 긁어 빛을 만들고, **긁은 자리에만 색이 든다** — 안 긁은 검정에는 아무 색도 안 묻는다. 앵커 슬러그 `changjak-scratchdoor` — **신규 민팅**(배정표 지정, 변경 없음).

**이 권이 그림에 요구하는 것** 넷.

1. 🔴 **무대가 좁은 삼각 어둠이고 광원이 하나다.** 계단 널 틈으로 든 빛 한 줄 — 그게 있으면 아침이고 없으면 저녁이다. → **긁어낸 흰 줄**이 문자 그대로 그 빛이다. 저녁 쪽은 판을 거의 안 긁으므로 어둠이 공짜로 온다(§2.16 「덜 그리기」의 극단).
2. 🔴 **물건 넷이 발톱만 하다.** 실패의 실밥, 빵의 부스러기, 의자의 톱밥, 그릇의 물기 — 저쪽의 살림이 그 티끌에만 있다. → 새김의 **가장 가는 선**이 아니면 발톱만 한 물건 위에서 실밥과 톱밥이 구별되지 않는다.
3. 🔴 **크기가 이 책의 재미이고, 원근이 뒤집히면 통째로 무너진다.** 문 = 강아지 앞발 길이 · 물건 = 발톱 · 손잡이 = 좁쌀 · 생쥐 = 문보다 조금 작다. 비교되는 둘은 **반드시 같은 깊이**에 둔다.
4. **빨간 단추가 돌아온다.** p3 에 준 것이 p12 에 문손잡이가 되어 달려 있다. 화면의 빨강은 쪽마다 하나뿐이라 눈이 저절로 따라간다.

**왜 이 공정인가.** 1 과 2 는 보통 서로를 잡아먹는다 — 어두운 방을 그리면 티끌이 안 보이고, 티끌을 보이게 하려고 밝히면 어둠이 사라진다. 스크래치보드는 그 둘이 **같은 동작**이다: 긁으면 밝아지고 가늘게 긁으면 티끌이 된다. 어둠은 그리는 것이 아니라 **손대지 않은 판**이라 아무 비용이 안 든다.

**🔴 라인 충돌 확인 (§7.2).**

| 대상 | 겹치나 | 왜 |
|---|---|---|
| 호리 **니들펠트** | ✕ | 2D 판 새김. NOT 절에 `no wool fibre` · `no stitching` · `no sculpted clay` 명시 |
| 전래동화 **점눈이** | ✕ | ① 지지면 = **검정 판**(밝은 크림 종이의 정반대) ② 매체 = 감산(긁어내기)이지 색연필 가산이 아님 ③ 얼굴 = 긁은 흰 선으로 만든 눈 + 별개 눈썹 선 ④ **빨강 1점 규칙 안 씀** — 빨강이 쪽마다 하나인 건 맞지만 **그 빨강이 매번 다른 물건이고 심음–회수 변수**다(단추 → 딸기 → 잼 → 다시 단추) |

## B-02 §2. STYLE ANCHOR

```
STYLE ANCHOR - changjak-scratchdoor   (puppy and a very small mouse / under a London staircase)

Style: SCRATCHBOARD engraving, 4-6 year old picture book. The page begins as solid black
  clay-coated board #141212 and every light thing is CUT OUT OF THAT BLACK with a blade - fine
  parallel scratched lines, cross-hatched where it must be brightest, each stroke a hard clean cut
  with a crisp edge and a tiny burr. Darkness is never drawn or painted: it is board that has not
  been touched. Colour is washed as a thin transparent tint INTO the scratched areas only. 🔴 THE
  ONE RULE: unscratched black takes no colour, ever - to colour a thing you must first cut it open.

RENDERING (finish hierarchy): FINISHED THINGS PER PAGE = 2 - the puppy (or the part of it in frame)
  and the ONE object on the floor that page. Nothing else is cut into. Stair treads = 3 underside
  lines. Floorboards = 3 long grain lines for the whole floor, 0 nails, 0 knots. The little door = 3
  plank lines + 1 frame + 1 paint crack + 1 knob. Broom = 5 strokes. Dust ball = 4. Wall and the
  back of the triangle = 0. 🔴 To push a thing back you do not soften it, you CUT FEWER STROKES INTO
  IT - four lines, then two, then none. DENSITY RATION = none; all twelve pages stay at 2, because
  raising the finish means enlarging the scratched area, and that would destroy the dark.

PALETTE: board black #141212 · scratched white #F2EDE4 · morning shaft yellow #E8C24B (tinted into
  scratched lines, morning pages only) · wood ochre #B08A4E (spool, bread, chair, bowl) · brass
  #96803F (the millet-sized knob) · marble blue #3C6E9E (page 8 only) · doorway glow #E0A85A (page
  12 only). ACCENT: RED #C0342A - exactly ONE red thing on a page and never two, and it is always
  the thing being given or received. No green, pink, purple or second blue.

CHARACTER DESIGN LANGUAGE: the animals are cut out of the black like everything else - white
  scratched line for fur and form, black for every shadow, no painted grey midtone. Each has an eye
  cut open as a white shape with a SEPARATE scratched brow above it; the pupil is board left uncut.
  FIXED GRADE: the puppy walks on four legs and only sits up to use a forepaw to place or lift
  something, no clothes; the mouse stands on two legs with forepaws as hands and one small apron.
  No humans appear - the people upstairs are only footsteps.

CANVAS: 16:9 double-page spread. The narrow triangular space under a London staircase - treads
  slanting down overhead, the ceiling dropping toward the back, one small wooden door low in the
  short wall, worn floorboards, one gap between two treads that the light comes through. 🔴 The door
  is LEFT and the puppy comes in from the RIGHT on every page. Bottom 18% quiet for a caption. No
  lettering, numerals, wallpaper, newspaper, pictures or signage anywhere.

NOT: no digital slickness of any kind - airbrush, gradient, glow, 3D CG, cel-shading, photographic,
  or a scratch texture filter laid over a painted picture (the blade must MAKE the shapes) / not
  blurred or hazy / no painted grey midtones and no white paint on top of the black / one flat board
  - no wool fibre, no stitching, no sculpted clay.
```

## B-02 §3. 캐릭터 시트 (🔴 이것부터 굽는다)

```
CHARACTER SHEET - PuppyCub   (bake FIRST, before any scene)

Same make as the book: cut out of a solid black scratchboard #141212 with a blade. Fur is
scratched white line, every shadow is board left untouched, and there are NO painted grey
midtones anywhere. Do not render smoothly just because there is no room behind it.

FACE: puppy, soft blunt muzzle, curly drop ears long enough to drag on the floor. The eye is a
  white shape cut open with the pupil left as uncut board, and a SEPARATE scratched brow line

  nose left mostly black with three cut highlights; whiskers 3 a side, single cut strokes.
BODY: young and round, four heads long, loose skin at the shoulders. Fur is built from short
  curved parallel cuts that follow the form and thin out to nothing where it turns away - the
  silhouette must read at thumbnail size from the ears and the tail alone.
🔴 THE FOREPAW IS THE RULER OF THIS BOOK: draw one forepaw large and clear, from wrist to toe
  tip, with the pads and a single claw marked - the little door is exactly this long, and a
  traded object is exactly the size of one claw. Draw the paw twice more, once turned over with
  the pads up and the toes curled to hold something, once with the toes spread letting go.
SHEET: belly-crawling forward with the hind legs still behind / sitting up on the haunches with
  one forepaw held out flat / lying with the chin flat on the boards, forepaws forward / head

  (found something), nose forward with the nostrils wide and the muzzle wrinkled (smelling), and
  ears down flat with the brows drawn together (nothing to give). Plain board.
SCENE token: PuppyCub.
```

### p1 — 계단 밑에 문이 있다

```

SUBJECT: PuppyCub has its belly flat on the floorboards and has hauled itself in on its forepaws,
```
