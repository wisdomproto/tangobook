**의인화 등급 (열세 쪽 고정)**: 전부 **진짜 새** — 이족이지만 **손 0 · 옷 0 · 소품 0**. 말은 부리로 하고, 가리키는 것은 **날개 끝**, 무는 것은 **부리**(꽃은 부리로만 문다). 발은 새 발이고 기와를 발가락으로 쥔다. 포즈가 안 되면 **등급을 바꾸지 말고 까치의 몸을 더 크게 쓴다**(이 책의 사건이 바로 「몸을 크게 쓸수록 지붕이 흔들린다」이다).

## A-11 §2. STYLE ANCHOR

```
STYLE ANCHOR - changjak-tilefield   (magpie / Czech rooftop, one slope)

Style: 4-6 year old picture book. One tiled roof slope, camera never leaves it. The roof is
PRINTED from one carved stamp pressed by hand; only the birds are drawn.

RENDERING (finish hierarchy): the tile field is made from ONE carved block - a single curved
  pantile - pressed row on row BY HAND, so ink load, angle and pressure differ tile to tile:
  some heavy and wet, some starved and grainy with the warm paper #EFEAE0 showing through, some
  a hair crooked. A tile = 1 curved plane plus at most 2 short marks. 0 individual tile texture,
  0 moss, 0 drawn tile edges, 0 vector repeat. It reads as one dense field with no information
  in it, and the birds are the only hand-drawn things on the page.
  🔴 TWO SEPARATE STATES, never confused: JOSTLED = a tile still in its row, printed offset by
  at most a third of a tile (counts: p3=1, p4=1, p5=6, p6=6, p7-p10=14, p12=6, p13=0). OFF THE
  ROW = a whole tile that has left the grid, and there is EXACTLY ONE in the entire book, only
  on p9, p10 and p11 - every other page has 0. Count it before shipping any cut.
  FINISHED THINGS PER PAGE = 2 (the magpie + the one thing it touches or the birds arriving that
  page). Everything else = one printed pass, no contour. Sky = 1 flat plane, 0 clouds. Chimney =
  4-6 brick lines. Gutter = 2 lines. Loose feathers = at most 3; water drops = at most 6. Depth
  = the same stamp smaller and paler, never blur.
  DENSITY RATION: p8 and p13 ONLY, rising to 3 - p8 = magpie + swallow row + the glimpsed sill
  pots; p13 = magpie + pot row + the empty slot. The other eleven pages stay at 2.

PALETTE: tiles TERRACOTTA #B3603C (muted clay, NOT red - never pushed toward the flower) · paper
  and lime wall #EFEAE0 · magpie black #1D1B1A · morning sky #D6CDB6 · soil #4A3A2E · gutter
  zinc #8E8A80.
  🔴 GERANIUM RED #C8262A exists in two things only - the fallen flower and the dormer sill pots
  - and 0 places besides. Per page: p1 = 2 · p2-p7 and p9-p12 = 1 · p8 = at most 4 · p13 = 7
  (6 pots + the carried flower). No other red, orange or pink anywhere - not on the chimney,
  not in the sky, not in a beak.

CHARACTER DESIGN LANGUAGE: all birds are REAL BIRDS - no clothes, no hands, no held props, no
  hats. They stand on two bird feet with toes gripping the tiles; they speak with the beak, point
  with a wing tip, and carry with the beak only. Each has a dark rounded eye with a SEPARATE
  drawn brow so the face can act; no dot-eyes, no catchlight. If a pose fails, make the magpie's
  body bigger in frame - never give it hands.

CANVAS: 16:9 double-page spread. NO lettering, numerals,
  house numbers, signage, weather-vane letters or letterforms anywhere.

NOT: no digital slickness of any kind - airbrush, gradient, glow, 3D CG, cel-shading,
  photographic, or a texture filter over flat colour (the printing must MAKE the tiles) / no
  identical repeated vector motif and no perfectly regular grid - every tile was pressed by hand
  / no blur, haze or motion trail: movement is a tile out of place, never a streak / not felt,
  stitched wool or sculpted clay.
```
