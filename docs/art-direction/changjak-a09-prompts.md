## a09 §1. 앵커 배정

**권**: `a09` 「부끄럼쟁이 등불지기」 (13쪽 · 4~6세 · 주제군 **A 마음·감정** · 엔진 **관찰과 성장** · 무대 영국 해안 등대 꼭대기 불 놓는 방 한 칸 · 주인공 고슴도치 + 갈매기)

**클러스터**: **C2** · **슬러그**: `changjak-grainstroke` — 🔴 **재사용**(원본 e03, 신규 민팅 아님). 공정 문단은 원본 그대로이고 **팔레트·관통 줄·방향 한 줄만** 이 권 것이다. 앵커 원본 그림도 그대로 물려받는다.

**한 줄**: 가는 **마른 갈필 획**이 화면의 전부이고 윤곽선이 0이다. 🔴 **획의 방향이 바람이고, 한 쪽에는 방향이 하나뿐이다** — 불꽃·연기·재·바닥결·가시가 다 같은 각도로 눕는다. 그리고 🔴 **획이 있는 자리가 빛, 붓이 안 간 자리가 밤**이다.

**🔴 이 권이 그림에 요구하는 것** 셋.

1. 🔴 **불이 켜졌나 꺼졌나가 한눈에 보여야 한다.** 이 책의 값은 쪽마다 그것 하나다 — 매체가 「빛 = 획 / 밤 = 맨 판」이라 **불이 꺼지면 방에서 획이 사라진다**(p3·p5 는 창 넷 말고 아무 데도 획이 없다). 칠하는 매체였다면 「어두운 방을 그린 그림」이 됐을 자리다.
2. 🔴 **바람은 보이지 않는데 그려야 한다.** 획의 방향이 유일한 수단이다. 그래서 p8 의 「등이 바람을 막았다」가 설명 없이 성립한다 — **창 쪽 결은 눕고 불 쪽 결은 서고, 그 둘이 등에서 만난다.** p9 의 「바람이 안 닿은 재 자리」는 **획이 곧게 선 유일한 자리**다.
3. 🔴 **숨은 자리가 화면에서 물리적으로 비어야 한다.** 상자 뒤(p2·p5)는 어둡게 칠한 데가 아니라 **붓이 한 번도 안 간 데**다. 그가 나오는 것과 획이 생기는 것이 같은 사건이 된다.

**🔴 형제 권과 갈린 축** (첫 렌더에서 세어진다)

## a09 §2. STYLE ANCHOR

```
STYLE ANCHOR - changjak-grainstroke   (a young hedgehog / the lantern room of an English coastal
lighthouse, at night)

Style: picture book for 4-6 year olds. One round lantern room at the top of a very old lighthouse,
thirteen pages, camera never leaves it. Everything is thin dry-brush strokes on a near-black board;
the bristles skip, so the board shows through every mark. Form comes only from how dense the strokes
are and which way they run.

RENDERING (finish hierarchy)
Contour lines = 0; no form is enclosed by a line.
STROKE DIRECTION IS THE WIND, AND ONE PAGE HAS ONE DIRECTION: on a windy page every stroke - flame,
smoke, ash, floor, quills - lies the same way at the same angle; on a still page every stroke stands
upright. Where the gust has passed the strokes stand up again and the wind is left only in the ash,
at most 9 dots blown to one side.
THE ONE PLACE WHOSE DIRECTION DISAGREES with its surroundings is the floor behind the hedgehog's
back, on p8 and p9 only: upright strokes in a laid-over field, the size of his own body.
LIGHT IS STROKES AND NIGHT IS BARE BOARD: where no brush has been it is dark, and when the fire is
out the only strokes in the room are the four openings.
FINISHED THINGS PER PAGE = 2 - the hedgehog and the one thing it touches or looks at; the rest is
broken grain. DENSITY RATION = none.
THE ROOM HOLDS FIVE THINGS AND NOTHING ELSE, EVER: iron brazier = one mass of grain + at most 5
strokes · four square unglazed openings = 3 straight strokes each · one wooden box = at most 6
strokes · the matchbox · the stair hole. Also: sea in an opening = one direction of laid strokes, 0
waves · a boat = a gap in that grain plus 1 yellow mark, never a drawn boat · smoke = exactly 1
stroke · spent matches = at most 3 marks · wall = at most 7 strokes a page · quills = 9-14 strokes,
never individual hairs, lying or standing with the flame.

PALETTE
#14171C the board - this room's night, showing through every stroke and never covered · #D9822B fire
orange, the only warm colour · #2C4368 sea navy, only inside the four openings at night · #E9E4D6
pale, for the eye marks and for the whole room on p13 only · #B33A2E the matchbox, the only red ·
#E8C24B one boat light, at most one mark a page.
The hedgehog is not rendered grey: he is orange grain where the fire reaches him and BARE BOARD
where it does not, so on p3 and p5 the only marks on him are his two eyes.

CHARACTER DESIGN LANGUAGE
An open mouth is a gap left free of strokes. FIXED GRADE, thirteen pages: both stay animals - four
legs, no clothes, no shoes, no fingers. The hedgehog rises on his hind legs ONLY to strike a match;
the gull talks but never leaves its sill and never uses a wing as a hand.

CANVAS
16:9 double-page spread. No letters, numbers or signage anywhere in this book.

NOT (rendering only)
- no digital slickness - airbrush, gradient, glow, halo round the flame, 3D CG, cel-shading,
  photographic or a texture filter; every mark is a dry broken stroke with the board showing through
- no motion trail, speed line, double image or blur: wind is stroke direction and nothing else
- no felted wool, stitching or fibre edge on the quills, and no sculpted clay
```
