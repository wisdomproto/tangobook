## A-09 §2. STYLE ANCHOR

```
STYLE ANCHOR - changjak-darkcut   (young hedgehog / English coastal lighthouse, lantern room)

Style: 4-6 year old picture book. One round lantern room at night, thirteen pages, camera never
leaves it. Everything is a flat opaque plane with a cut edge - the picture is made of areas you
can measure, not of light that fades.

RENDERING (finish hierarchy): the ground is night blue #1B2338 laid flat over the whole page -
  walls, glass, sea, air. THE DARK PATCH is a separate opaque shape of near-black #0E1219
  painted onto the floor, cast by the lamp base, and its edge is RULED AND HARD on every side.
  THE BEAMS are opaque pale-yellow #E8D9A8 planes laid ON TOP of floor and dark alike, long
  narrow trapezoids entering from the windows, each with straight ruled edges. Where a beam
  crosses the dark it REMOVES that area - the dark is what is left over, so the two never blend.
  0 gradients, 0 soft falloff, 0 haze, 0 halo round the flame, 0 rays, 0 glow on brass. Light is
  either laid or not laid.
  FINISHED THINGS PER PAGE = 2 (the hedgehog + the one thing it touches or looks at). Everything
  else gets one flat pass and no contour. Floor = 7-9 plank lines and nothing else, 0 knots, 0
  woodgrain texture. Each window = 3 straight frame lines. Sea outside = 1 flat plane, 0 waves;
  a boat far below = 1 dark mark plus 1 yellow dot, never a drawn boat.
  COUNT THE BEAMS - it is the book's meter: p1-p2 = 0, p3-p4 = 1, p5-p6 = 2, p7 = 3, p8-p9 = 4,
  p10 = 3 whole plus 1 cut to half length, p11-p12 = 4 held low, p13 = 4. NEVER a fifth.
  DENSITY RATION: p8 only, and it does not rise to 3 - there the second finished slot goes to
  THE FOOTPRINT-SIZED PATCH OF BLACK instead of a prop. Every other page stays at 2.

PALETTE: night blue #1B2338 · dark patch #0E1219 · beam pale yellow #E8D9A8 (dimmed #D8C79A on
  p9-p10) · brass #C9A24A · flame orange #D9822B · quills #6B7284 · muzzle and paws #8A8FA0.
  There is NO WHITE and NO RED in this book: the brightest value on any page is #E8D9A8, and
  the flame is the only warm colour. 0 pink skies, 0 white stars, 0 white foam.

CHARACTER DESIGN LANGUAGE: the hedgehog is bipedal, forepaws used as hands, wearing NOTHING -
  no apron, cap, scarf or boots. Quills are a flat plane with 9-14 spike marks along its top
  edge, and they LIE FLAT or STAND UP as the acting. A dark rounded eye with a SEPARATE drawn
  brow so the face can act; no dot-eyes, no catchlight. The gull talks but has no hands: it may
  turn its neck, open its beak and half-lift ONE wing, nothing else. If a pose fails, change the
  hedgehog's pose, never the grade.

CANVAS: 16:9 double-page spread. Bottom 18% left quiet for a caption. NO lettering, numerals,
  signage, dial faces, chart marks or letterforms anywhere - not on the lamp, the frames or the
  glass.

NOT: no digital slickness of any kind - airbrush, gradient, glow, lens flare, bloom, 3D CG,
  cel-shading, photographic, or a texture filter over flat colour / no blur, haze, motion trail
  or soft shadow: a shadow here is a cut shape or it is not there / no felted wool, stitching or
  fibre edge on the quills, and no sculpted clay / no white paint or white highlight anywhere.
```
