## G-02 §1. 앵커 배정

**권**: `g02` 「불 끄면 커지는 것들」 (12쪽 · 4~6세 · 주제군 **G 용기·두려움** · 엔진 **누적·반복** · 무대 독일 다락 · 밤 · 주인공 새끼 다람쥐 + 동생 다람쥐)

**한 줄**: 어두운 청보라 판 위, 🔴 **손전등 동그라미 안에만 그린다**(가산). 어둠에는 색도 가장자리도 없다. 앵커 슬러그 `changjak-nightboard` — **a05 와 공유**.

**🔴 a05 와의 공유 조건 — 공정이 같고 방향이 반대다.** (a05 파일 §1 의 표를 그대로 받아 이 권 쪽을 채웠다.)

| | a05 「제일 작은 목소리」 | **g02 (이 권)** |
|---|---|---|
| 방향 | **감산** — 목탄을 들어내서 밝힌다 | 🔴 **가산 — 어두운 판에 빛 동그라미 안만 그린다** |
| 지지면 | 청회 종이 #343B45 | 🔴 **청보라 판 #2B2742**(회색이 아니라 보라 쪽) |
| 무대 | 야외 학교 마당, 달빛 | 🔴 **실내 다락, 전구와 손전등** |
| 화면 | 흰 거위 하나가 가장 큰 밝은 덩어리 | 🔴 **노란 원 하나가 유일한 밝은 자리** |

| 팔레트 | 귀 안쪽 분홍 + 흐린 창 황토 | 🔴 **빛 안에서만 초록·빨강·연보라·갈색** |

**이 권이 그림에 요구하는 것** 셋.

1. 🔴 **커 보이는 이유가 「가장자리가 안 보여서」이므로, 안 그린 자리에는 윤곽이 있으면 안 된다.** 가산 매체에서는 이것이 공정 그 자체다 — 어둠에는 아무것도 얹지 않았으니 끝나는 선이 원리상 없다. 어떤 다른 매체도 「어디까지가 그것인지 모른다」를 이만큼 값싸게 못 만든다.
2. 🔴 **다시 끄지 않아도 된다.** 손전등은 동그라미 하나만 밝히므로 **빛을 옮기면 방금 본 데가 저절로 어두워진다**(대본 note). 가산 매체에서 「빛을 옮긴다」 = 「저쪽에 안 그린다」라, 「껐다 켰다」를 여섯 번 그릴 필요가 없다.
3. 🔴 **마지막 하나는 끝까지 색을 못 받는다.** 이 권의 착지가 「불을 안 켬」이라, 🔴 **곰 인형은 p1 에서 한 번 갈색으로 보이고 그 뒤 열한 쪽 동안 한 번도 색을 못 받는다.** §2.9 의 새 변형 — **악센트가 한 번도 도달하지 않는 자리가 있다.**

**🔴 이 배정에서 새로 확립한 것 — 「화면당 채도색은 손전등 원 안 하나뿐이다」.** 대본 bodyColors 에 색이 여섯이라 팔레트를 못 좁히는 권처럼 보이는데, **빛 안에서만 색이 있다**는 규칙 하나가 그것을 해결한다: 팔레트는 넓어도 **한 쪽에 켜지는 색은 언제나 하나**다. 배경을 못 좁히는 권에서 팔레트를 줄이는 대신 **색이 있을 수 있는 면적을 줄인** 사례다(§2.9 후보로 §2 에 올린다).

**🔴 C7 이웃 넷과의 분리** — §2.13 이 「C7 은 공정까지 특정할 때만 연다」고 못 박은 클러스터다.

## G-02 §2. STYLE ANCHOR

```
STYLE ANCHOR - changjak-nightboard / g02   (two squirrel kids / a German attic, night)

Style: charcoal, soft pastel and coloured pencil worked on a dark board, ages 4-6. One attic, drawn
  twelve times; the furniture never moves and only the camera and the light do.

RENDERING: soft pastel and pencil on DARK BLUE-VIOLET toothed board #2B2742. The board begins dark
  and stays dark. 🔴 THE DIRECTION OF THIS VOLUME IS ADDITIVE: DRAW ONLY INSIDE THE CIRCLE OF
  LIGHT. Inside it there is colour, texture and an edge that closes; outside it 🔴 NOTHING IS DRAWN
  AT ALL EXCEPT ONE PALE SMUDGE OF WINDOW BLUE #6E7A9B PER SHAPE, rubbed on with a fingertip, and
  🔴 EVERY SMUDGE FADES OUT AT ITS TOP INTO THE BOARD SO IT NEVER FINISHES. 🔴 IN THE DARK NO SHAPE
  CLOSES: no outline, no contour line, no rim light, no silhouette edge anywhere. That is why a
  thing in the dark cannot be measured. 🔴 MOVING THE TORCH IS ENOUGH TO PUT A PLACE BACK INTO THE
  DARK - nothing is ever erased or switched off.
  FINISHED THINGS PER PAGE = 2 (the squirrel + the one thing inside the light). 🔴 DENSITY RATION =
  PAGE 1 ONLY, where the bulb is on and finish rises to 5 (the squirrel + all four objects at their
  true size). Every other page is 2.
  Counts: rafters = at most 6 strokes - floorboards = 4 lines, inside the light only - dried flower
  head = 1 shape, 0 petals - bicycle = 1 frame line + 2 wheels + 4 spoke strokes - fur = 0 hairs -
  dust in the beam = at most 12 dots. Push a thing back by MAKING FEWER MARKS, never by blurring,
  fading, softening or throwing it out of focus.

PALETTE: board #2B2742 - window blue #6E7A9B, the ONLY colour allowed in the dark - torch yellow
  #F2C55C - warm wood inside the light #C9A063 - bulb light, pages 1-2 only, #E8B85E.
  🔴 COLOURS THAT EXIST ONLY INSIDE A CIRCLE OF LIGHT: coat green #4E6B45 - bicycle red #B0392E -
  dried flower violet #9E86AE - teddy brown #8A6039. 🔴 ONE SATURATED COLOUR PER PAGE AND NO MORE,
  because only one thing is lit at a time. 🔴 THE TEDDY BROWN APPEARS ON PAGE 1 AND NEVER AGAIN -
  the last big thing is never lit, and the reader must feel that the colour did not come back. No
  moonlight, no stars indoors, no glow around the bulb or the torch, no light spilling off the beam.

CHARACTER DESIGN LANGUAGE: animal faces, no clothes, no shoes. An eye is a drawn shape with one
  dark pupil and a SEPARATE brow mark above it so the face can act - not dot-eyes, no catchlight.
  FIXED GRADE, all twelve pages: both squirrels go on four legs and rise onto the hind legs ONLY
  while their forepaws are doing something; a squirrel grips with its forepaws, which is how the
  torch is held. Big soft tails. No fingers.

CANVAS: 16:9 double-page spread. One attic under a pitched roof, unchanged. No lettering or numerals anywhere.

NOT: no digital slickness of any kind - airbrush, gradient, glow, 3D CG, cel-shading, photographic,
  or a texture filter over flat tone / no bloom, halo or light bleeding out of the torch beam / 🔴
  nothing blurred, hazy or out of focus - a thing is pushed back by having fewer marks on it / no
  felt, wool fibre, stitching or sculpted clay.
```

MATERIAL SHEET - NightBoard   (bake FIRST - this book is this one boundary)

A test board, no characters. Soft pastel and pencil on dark blue-violet board #2B2742.

A · 🔴 INSIDE AND OUTSIDE: one wooden chair drawn twice on one board. LEFT, inside a torch circle -
  full warm colour #C9A063, wood grain, and every edge closes. RIGHT, the same chair in the dark -
  🔴 ONE PALE WINDOW-BLUE #6E7A9B SMUDGE rubbed with a fingertip, its lower part readable and its

  mark that one as the failure.
B · THE CIRCLE ITSELF: a torch beam falling on a plank floor at three angles - straight on (a round
  patch), tilted down (a squashed oval), aimed up (a circle on rafters). 🔴 Its rim is soft rubbed
  pastel that runs out over a finger's width - not a cut edge, and 🔴 not a glow: outside the rim
  there is 0 light on anything.
C · 🔴 A THING HALF IN: one shape lying across the rim of the circle. The half inside has colour
  and a closed edge; the half outside has neither. One object, two laws.
D · WHAT IS NEVER LIT: one round shape with two ears, drawn only as a window-blue smudge, its top
  and sides gone into the board. 🔴 It has no colour and no edge, and it must still be plainly
  something sitting there.
E · MOVING THE LIGHT: the same corner drawn twice - lit, then with the circle moved off it, so it
  is back to a smudge. 🔴 Nothing was erased; the light simply went elsewhere.
No characters, no room, no lettering. SCENE token: NightBoard.
