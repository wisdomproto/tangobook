## D-07 §1. 앵커 배정

**권**: `d07` 「운하를 따라간 오리」 (13쪽 · 4~6세 · 주제군 **D 모험·여정** · 엔진 **여정과 귀환** · 무대 암스테르담 운하의 갈림목 하나 · 주인공 새끼 오리 + 물닭)

**한 줄**: 가는 잉크 선 + **투명 수채**. 🔴 **흰 것은 안 칠한 종이뿐이고 값은 겹수다** — 볕은 한 겹, 그늘은 두 겹, 다리 밑은 세 겹. 그늘과 볕이 화면을 정확히 반으로 가른다. 앵커 슬러그 `changjak-canalfork` — **신규 민팅**(배정표 지정, 변경 없음).

**이 권이 그림에 요구하는 것** 셋.

1. 🔴 **선택지 둘이 곧 명암 둘이다.** 왼쪽은 아치 다리 밑 그늘이라 물이 잔잔해 개구리밥이 두껍게 끼었고, 오른쪽은 해가 곧게 들어 물낯이 말갛다. **두 물길이 똑같이 예뻐야** 하고(어느 쪽도 먼저 고르라고 말하지 않는다) 그러면서 **한눈에 다른 상태여야** 한다. 같은 물의 두 상태를 한 화면에 놓는 일은 투명 수채가 가장 잘한다 — 하나는 색을 쌓아 만들고 하나는 종이를 남겨 만든다.
2. 🔴 **원인이 색이고, 색이 두 곳에 있다.** 오리 등에 붙은 초록과 왼쪽 물길에서 밀려 나오는 초록이 **같은 초록**이라는 것이 알아봄의 전부다(p9). 그러려면 그 초록이 열세 쪽 내내 **한 가지 안료로 같은 방식으로** 찍혀야 한다 — 등의 것도 물의 것도 낱개의 점이고, 겹치면 융단이 된다.
3. 🔴 **카메라가 갈림목을 안 벗어난다.** p7~p9 에서 오른쪽 물길로 들어가지만 말뚝·물닭·빨간 화분이 계속 프레임 안에 남는다. **창턱의 빨간 제라늄 한 포기가 열세 쪽 내내 같은 자리**에 있어서, 아이가 「아까 그 자리」인 걸 p13 에서 먼저 알아본다.

**왜 이 공정인가.** §2.13 이 C7 을 **공정까지 특정할 때만** 열라고 못 박았다. 여기서 특정하는 것은 셋이다 — ①**잉크 선을 먼저 다 긋고 마른 뒤에 수채를 얹는다**(2단계). 그래서 선은 물감에 안 녹고, 물감은 선 밖으로 흘러도 된다. ②**흰 물감이 없다.** 반짝임·물빛·부리 끝 광택이 전부 **안 칠한 종이**이고, 그래서 오른쪽 물길은 그리는 게 아니라 **남기는 것**이다(§2.16.3 — 눈부신 물낯을 채워 넣을 방법이 아예 없다). ③**값 = 겹수.** 그늘은 회색을 섞는 게 아니라 같은 색을 한 겹 더 얹는 것이라, 「그늘진 물」과 「볕 든 물」이 물리적으로 같은 물의 두 상태가 된다.

**🔴 라인 충돌 확인 (§7.2).**

## D-07 §2. STYLE ANCHOR

```
STYLE ANCHOR - changjak-canalfork   (a duckling and a coot / one fork of an Amsterdam canal)

Style: fine ink line and TRANSPARENT WATERCOLOUR on cool white paper #F7F4EC, 4-6 year old picture
  book, made in TWO STAGES - 🔴 the whole drawing is inked first with a fine even nib and dried,
  then the colour is washed over it, so the line never dissolves and the wash may run past it.
  🔴 THERE IS NO WHITE PAINT ANYWHERE. Every bright thing - a sparkle on water, a drop, the shine
  on a bill - is BARE PAPER left unpainted.

RENDERING (finish hierarchy): 🔴 VALUE IS THE NUMBER OF LAYERS, never a grey mixed in: sunlit = 1
  wash, shaded = the SAME colour laid twice, under the bridge = three. Nothing is darker than three and no shadow is a
  different hue. 🔴 THE TWO WATERS ARE THE SAME WATER IN TWO STATES and are made
  by opposite operations - the shaded lane is BUILT UP from separate dabbed green dots until the
  dots touch and become a raft; the sunlit lane is LEFT OUT, one thin wash with torn scraps of bare
  paper across it and 0 dots. 🔴 TO PUSH SOMETHING BACK, LEAVE THE INK LINE OFF IT and give it one
  flat wash only - never blur it, never fade it. FINISHED THINGS PER PAGE = 2 - the bird and the
  one thing it touches or speaks to. Brick wall = at most 9 courses near the water, 0 above.
  Houses = 1 outline each, at most 4 windows in a whole frame. Bicycle = 1 outline, 0 spokes.
  Ripples = at most 6 ink arcs. DENSITY RATION = none; all thirteen pages stay at 2.

PALETTE: paper #F7F4EC, the only white in the book · ink #232A2C, line only · canal water #4E5A50
  at one layer, #333D36 at two · brick #9C6C4E with waterline slime #5E6B52 · duckling yellow
  #E9BA3E · 🔴 DUCKWEED GREEN #6E9B3E, the cause colour, always separate dabbed dots, never a tint
  or a wash · coot black #1E2226 with a bare-paper frontal shield · 🔴 GERANIUM RED
  #BE3A2A, one pot on one windowsill, THE ONLY RED IN THE BOOK and never on a bird, a boat or a
  roof. No purple, orange or pink, and no white paint.

CHARACTER DESIGN LANGUAGE: birds are birds. Ink contour first, then one or two washes inside it;
  feathers are not drawn, only the outline and at most 5 wing lines. 🔴 A bird's eye is a small
  ink ring with a dark centre and a lid line above it that can lower - no eyebrow, no human
  expression, and the acting is done by NECK ANGLE and by how far the bill is opened. The two
  silhouettes must separate at thumbnail size. FIXED GRADE: neither bird has hands. They peck,
  hold in the bill and point with the bill, and nothing else. Feet are underwater and never drawn;
  effort shows as ripples only.

CANVAS: 16:9 double-page spread. One canal fork and nothing else on all thirteen pages. 🔴 No lettering, numerals, shop signs, house numbers or name plates.

NOT: no digital slickness of any kind - airbrush, gradient, glow, 3D CG, cel-shading, photographic,
  or a texture filter over the washes / never white paint, never a mixed grey shadow, never a
  fourth layer / not blurred, hazy, faded or spotlit, and no motion blur or trailing streaks / one
  sheet thick - no wool fibre, no felt fuzz, no stitching, no sculpted clay.
```

## D-07 §3. 캐릭터 시트 (🔴 이것부터 굽는다)

```
CHARACTER SHEET - DucklingWeed   (bake FIRST, before any scene)

Same make as the book: fine ink contour drawn first and dried, then transparent washes over it, on
cool white paper #F7F4EC. No white paint anywhere on this animal.

FACE: a duckling's head - short flat bill, a small ink-ring eye with a dark centre and a lid line
  above it that lowers. 🔴 No eyebrow, no cheeks, no human mouth. All the acting is in the NECK:
  drawn short and thick when it hesitates, stretched long and low when it swims hard, bent right
  back over the shoulder when it looks behind.
BODY: small and round, yellow #E9BA3E in one wash with a second wash only under the belly and tail.
  Down is NOT drawn - the outline is slightly soft and that is all. At most 5 wing lines.
🔴 THE WEED ON ITS BACK - the whole book depends on this: DUCKWEED GREEN #6E9B3E laid as SEPARATE
  SMALL DABBED DOTS across the back of the neck and the middle of the back, thick enough to see
  from across a room, thin enough that the yellow shows between them. 🔴 It is never a green tint,
  never a glaze, never a smear. Draw it in three states, same pose: DRY (dots standing separate and
  proud) · WET (the same dots flattened against the body after swimming hard) · unchanged at the
  end (🔴 not one dot ever falls off, on any page).
🔴 SIZE PLATE: one head close-up with ONE weed scrap held on the very tip of the bill, and the eye
  right beside it in the same plane - 🔴 THE SCRAP IS EXACTLY AS BIG AS THE EYE. One page is built
  on that measurement and it must be settled here.
SHEET: swimming forward with the neck low / stopped upright on the water with the neck straight and
  the bill slightly raised / floating with the feet tucked, body tipped, bill one way and tail the
  other / neck twisted right back trying to see its own back / bill turned sharply to one side as
  if pointing; plus one close-up with the two eyes crossed inward looking at the bill tip. Plain
  paper, no canal. 0 lettering. SCENE token: DucklingWeed.
```
