# 타로와 무무 — 새 단순 바틱 캐스트

2026-09-28 사용자 재설계 요청. [새 기준 그림](references/taro/redesign-cast.png).
구 시트의 전신 문양·비정상 늘임·고정 표정은 폐기한다. 제목 별칭은 빌더용으로 유지한다.

## Taro monkey

```
TARO: smallest monkey child, round ears and small crown tuft, broad warm cream monkey face.
Indigo #22406B plain body, one cream chest/belly patch, soft slightly long arms with natural joints,
short sturdy legs and broad bare feet. ONE long tail ending in a relaxed closed curl.
ONE burnt-orange #E2711D waist cord; no other clothes or accessories. Never noodle arms or claw fingers.
Keep child proportions and the new cast reference's face. No decorative fur patterns.
```

## Mumu deer

```
MUMU: deer girl child, about a head taller than Taro. Two upright leaf-shaped ears, short deer
muzzle, cream cheeks/inner ears and a broad plain cream chest/belly patch. Medium blue #4C6E8C body.
Naturally longer sturdy legs, rounded hands with fingers, normal short neck and a tiny tail.
ONE burnt-orange #E2711D narrow headband. No antlers, spots, clothing or extra jewellery.
Ears turn with feeling and sound; legs bend naturally. Never stilt legs, giraffe neck or ribbon limbs.
```

## Taro mother monkey

```
TARO'S MOTHER: adult monkey from the new cast lineup, larger than both children, with a
comfortably sized head, round ears, small crown tuft, broad warm cream monkey face and chest/belly.
Medium-blue #4C6E8C plain fur, soft rounded torso, natural flexible arms and sturdy legs, bare feet.
ONE tail bends gently but has NO closed curl. No orange cord, clothes, jewellery or decorative motifs.
She can stand, sit, walk, reach, hug or bend naturally as scripted; never permanently folded limbs or
an elongated low back. Her TWO eyes open normally and her face shows the scene's feeling, not a fixed
sleepy squint or flat mouth. Keep species clear and match the new reference.
```

## Mumu grandmother deer

```
MUMU'S GRANDMOTHER: friendly older female deer from the new cast lineup, broad cream deer face,
cream inner ears and one broad cream chest/belly patch on a plain indigo #22406B body. Normal SHORT
deer neck, head close to shoulders, gently stocky adult torso and sturdy flexible legs. Broad leaf ears
angle outward and move naturally. Tiny deer tail. No antlers, giraffe neck, spots, cane, bag or orange.
Simple hands with fingers. She can stand, sit, bend and walk naturally, with a comfortably sized head
and attentive open eyes; never a tiny head on a long neck or permanently crouching mound.
Keep her face proportions distinct from Mumu while allowing warm, worried or concentrated expressions.
```
