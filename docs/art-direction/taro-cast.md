# taro — 캐스트 규격

> 🔴 **앵커(`taro-anchor.md`)는 이 세계 전체를 말하고, 이 파일은 한 사람을 말한다.**
> 빌더(`build-series-html.mjs`)가 `##` 제목을 **영문 별칭**으로 찾아 그 사람 시트 프롬프트에
> `[이 인물만의 규격]` 으로 끼운다 — 제목 문자열을 바꾸면 조용히 안 들어간다.
>
> 🔴 **가르는 축은 앵커의 축 하나다 — 「늘임」.** 새 축(털색·안경·흰머리·옷)을 발명하지 않는다.
> 넷이 갈리는 것은 **어디가 늘어났는가** 하나뿐이고, 그래서 **손톱만 한 카드에서도 윤곽만으로 갈린다**.
>
> | | 늘어난 곳 | 윤곽 한 줄 |
> |---|---|---|
> | 타로 | **팔 · 꼬리** | 작은 공에 가는 채찍 둘 + **말린 고리 하나** |
> | 무무 | **다리 · 귀** | 공이 **긴 다리 위에 떠 있고** 머리에 **곧게 선 뿔 둘** |
> | 타로 엄마 | **등(몸통)** | 팔다리가 짧게 접힌 **길고 낮은 덩어리** + 바닥에 **곧게 눕는 꼬리 한 줄** |
> | 무무 할머니 | **목** | 다리가 안 보이는 둔덕에서 **목 하나가 아래로 길게 굽는다** + **뒤로 누운 귀** |
>
> 🔴 **어른 둘은 250쪽 중 6쪽에만 나오고 그 6쪽이 전부 「등을 보이고 앉음」 아니면 「화면 위에서
> 내려오는 손」이다**(SCENE 05 p4·p6·p7·p8 · 15 p1 · 23 p1). 그래서 갈리는 자리는 **등선과 앉은
> 덩어리**여야 한다. 🔴 **손은 갈리지 않는다** — 05 p7↔p8 은 좌우를 뒤집은 짝이고 톤이
> 「손 모양까지 같아서 두 집이 같은 말을 한 것이 겹쳐 보인다」라, 두 어른의 손이 닮은 것이 그 쪽의 명제다.

## Taro monkey

```
THE STRETCH IS IN HIS ARMS AND HIS TAIL. Body a small round ball, legs short under it, both arms
run down past his feet at the same narrow width, and ONE tail runs longer than his whole body and
ENDS IN A CLOSED CURL - the curl is his mark and no one else in the book has a closed loop anywhere.
Fingers are thin and held APART, never bunched: his hands are the fast thing about him.
He is the SHORTEST of the four. Eyes are the upright oval, wide open.
He wears ONE cord at the waist and it is the accent dye.
```

## Mumu deer

```
THE STRETCH IS IN HER LEGS AND HER EARS. Two legs, long and straight and the same narrow
width from hip to foot, carrying a small round body HIGH OFF THE GROUND, so there is open
page between her belly and her feet where Taro has none. TWO EARS STAND UP TALLER THAN HER HEAD IS
HIGH and turn to point where a sound came from; the tail is a stub. Arms are ordinary and short.
She stands A HEAD TALLER than Taro. Eyes are the upright oval, wide open.
She wears ONE cord on her head and it is the accent dye.
```

## Taro mother monkey

```
SHE IS NOT A BIGGER TARO. THE STRETCH HAS LEFT THE LIMBS AND GONE INTO THE BODY: one long straight
back runs the whole height of her, and her arms and legs stay SHORT and FOLDED IN against it, so
her outline is a long low mass and not a ball with whips. Draw her SEATED or FROM BEHIND by default
- the long back IS the way she is recognised, and it is what the page shows.
HER TAIL DOES NOT CURL. It lies out straight along the floor as one line and stops - no loop, no
hook, nothing closed. That single difference tells her from Taro at any size.
Fingers are held TOGETHER in a flat broad palm, the opposite of his splayed ones.
Eyes are the LYING oval, narrowed and calm, and the mouth is one short flat curve.
SHE WEARS NO CORD AND CARRIES NO ACCENT COLOUR AT ALL. No apron, no bundle, no tool of her own.
```

## Mumu grandmother deer

```
SHE IS NOT A BIGGER MUMU. THE STRETCH HAS LEFT THE LEGS AND GONE INTO THE NECK: her legs fold
under her and do not show, so she sits as a low mound with no open page under her body, and out of
that mound ONE NECK RUNS LONG AND CURVES DOWNWARD toward whatever she is setting down. Where Mumu
is a body up on stilts, she is a mound with a long bent neck - the two outlines share no line.
HER EARS LIE BACK FLAT ALONG THE NECK AND DO NOT TURN, so her head reads as one smooth teardrop
instead of Mumu's two standing spikes. Arms are short.
Draw her SEATED or FROM BEHIND by default; standing, the folded legs stay short and the long neck
still carries the height.
Eyes are the LYING oval, narrowed and calm, and the mouth is one short flat curve.
SHE WEARS NO CORD AND CARRIES NO ACCENT COLOUR AT ALL.
```
