## E-03 §1. 앵커 배정

**권**: `too-long-name-dog` (e03 · 10쪽 · 4~6세 · 주제군 **E 웃음·말놀이** · 엔진 **누적·반복** · 무대 영국 시골 돌담 마당 · 주인공 새끼 양(이름 없음) + 개)

**한 줄**: 3잉크 스텐실 인쇄(리소) · **황토 잉크 한 판이 통째로 개다.** 개가 멀어질수록 그 쪽에 찍히는 황토가 줄고, 개가 없는 쪽은 **황토판을 아예 안 찍는다**(2잉크 인쇄). 앵커 슬러그 `changjak-yardprint` — **신규 민팅**.

**이 권이 그림에 요구하는 것(판정의 전제)** 셋이다.
① **쌓이는 축이 「개수」가 아니라 「거리」다.** 마당 끝 → 담 너머 꼬리만 → 아예 없음. 사라짐을 그리려면 개가 매 쪽 **같은 방식으로 만들어진 같은 물건**이어야 하고, 그래야 「줄었다」가 보인다. 게다가 이 축은 **0 에 닿는다** — 개가 아예 없는 쪽이 두 장(p4·p5)이다. 🔴 **0 을 그릴 수 있는 매체**여야 한다.
② **웃음이 얼굴에 있다.** 엉킨 입·눌린 볼·어긋난 두 눈·꺼진 가슴. 표정을 못 그리는 매체는 이 권에서 원천 탈락이다(§2.8).
③ **글자가 금지인데 이 책의 내용은 소리다.** 긴 이름은 소리로만 존재하므로, 그림은 소리를 **숨(가슴·입)** 과 **거리** 두 가지로만 써야 한다.

**후보 3.**
① **C3 프린트-크래프트 · 3잉크 스텐실/리소 인쇄(신규 앵커)** — 잉크판이 **찍히거나 안 찍히거나** 둘 뿐이라 「개가 있다 / 없다」가 공정 차원에서 표현된다. 한 판을 통째로 개에게 주면 **거리 축이 곧 잉크 양**이 되고, p4·p5 는 물리적으로 2잉크 인쇄가 된다(§2.9 의 인쇄판 변형). 검정 라인판이 있어 얼굴 연기도 그대로 산다. 리스크 = 핀 어긋남·잉크 알갱이를 모델이 「질감 필터」로 뭉갤 수 있다(§2.2) → 겹침 색(황토+초록=올리브)을 **명시된 결과값**으로 못 박아 공정을 강제한다.
② **C2 선 하나 캐릭터**(E 주제군 1순위) — 탈락. 🔴 **G-10 과 정면 충돌**이다: 영국·아일랜드 농장 마당 + 잉크 선 + 평면 색면 + 네발 개 + 작은 동물 주인공. 썸네일에서 같은 책으로 보인다. 라인 내 중복이 개별 최적보다 우선(§7.6 전례).
③ **C4 평면 형태**(E 2순위) — 탈락. §2.8 대로 표정이 없다. 이 권의 웃음은 **전부 얼굴에 있다**(p3 엉킨 입 한 장이 이 책에서 제일 웃긴 그림이다). 자세만으로는 「이름이 입에서 엉켰다」를 못 쓴다.

**🔴 추천 = 후보 ①.** 주제군 E 의 기준선(C2)을 **라인 충돌이 이겼고**, 남은 기준선(C4)은 이 권의 웃음 구조가 이겼다. 배정은 `주제군 × 엔진 → 앵커` 이고(§2.11), 여기에 **라인 내 이웃**이 세 번째 축으로 붙는다.

**왜 새 앵커인가.** A-11 도 C3 이지만 **공정이 다르다** — A-11 = 리노컷(칼로 판 요철·칼자국 채터·오트지·테라코타가 화면 절반의 바탕·키라인 없음). 여기 = **새기는 행위가 아예 없다**(스크린 그물을 통과한 알갱이 잉크 · 핀 어긋남 후광 · 검정 라인판 + 색판 2 · 밝은 오트화이트 종이 · 종이가 광원). 썸네일에서 A-11 은 **어두운 목판 세계**, 이건 **밝은 인쇄 포스터 세계**다.

## E-03 §2. STYLE ANCHOR

```
STYLE ANCHOR - changjak-yardprint  (lamb & dog / English dry-stone yard / a name too long)

Style: a hand-pulled THREE-INK STENCIL PRINT (risograph / screenprint) used as a picture-book
  page for 4-6 year olds. Bright, airy, printed - not painted, not rendered, not digital.
  The book has one event - the dog is further away every time the lamb calls - so the PRINT
  itself carries it: one of the three inks belongs to the dog and to nothing else.

MEDIUM: three flat inks pushed through a screen onto pale oat printing stock.
  - Every colour is FLAT. There is no gradient inside any shape, ever. Depth is made by
    OVERPRINTING one ink on another, never by shading or blending.
  - The ink sits ON the paper as a slightly grainy, uneven film. The screen grain, the roller
    pressure and a few dust specks and skips are visible and correct.
  - 🔴 THE PLATES ARE PRINTED SLIGHTLY OFF FROM EACH OTHER, one to three millimetres. Colour
    slides past its own drawn outline; where two inks overlap a darker halo appears. This
    misregistration is the house look and is deliberate, not an error.
  - The BLACK plate does double duty: solid black for the drawing line, the eyes, the mouth and
    small shapes - and a SCREENED TINT of the same ink (about 30 percent, visible dots) for the
    stone wall and for shadows, which prints as a cool grey.
  - Shapes end where an ink plate ends. Never a clean soft-edged vector outline.
  No airbrush, no soft glow, no digital gradient, no drop shadow, no paper-texture filter laid
  over flat digital colour.

PALETTE: exactly THREE inks exist in this book. Nothing else does.
  INK 1 - BLACK #211E1B : the drawing line, the lamb's eyes and mouth, the ball, and the 30%
    grey tint of the dry-stone wall and the shadows.
  INK 2 - MEADOW GREEN #6F8A4A : grass, the hill beyond the wall, leaves.
  INK 3 - OCHRE #C98A3C : 🔴 THE DOG, AND ONLY THE DOG. Nothing else in this book is ochre -
    not the wall, not the sunlight, not the ball, not the lamb, not a flower, not the hill.
  Expected overprints: green over black = deep pine (wall shadow, hill edge) / ochre over green
    = olive (where the dog's body crosses grass) / black over ochre = the dog's eye, nose and
    the dark of its coat.
  Paper #F2EFE7 IS THE LIGHT. Sunlight is UNPRINTED PAPER - never a printed white, never a glow.

🔴 THE OCHRE PLATE IS THE STORY. How much ochre is printed on a page is exactly how much dog is
  left in the world on that page. ON THE TWO PAGES WHERE THE DOG IS GONE, THE OCHRE PLATE IS
  NOT PRINTED AT ALL and the page is a two-ink print. Read the OCHRE line of every cut before
  drawing anything. If ochre appears on those two pages, the book has failed.

MATERIAL TRANSLATION (🔴 keep it printed - never photographic, never plastic):
  - DRY-STONE WALL = one flat 30% grey tint shape with about ten joint lines drawn in solid
    black over it. NOT every stone drawn, NOT rendered rock texture, NOT a photographic wall.
  - GRASS = one flat green field. Three or four short black tick marks near the foreground are
    all the blades there are. NOT a lawn of individual blades.
  - DOG'S SHAGGY COAT = the ochre plate cut into clumped, torn-looking lobes, with four or five
    black strokes laid over it for the fall of the fur. NOT rendered hair, NOT airbrushed fluff.
  - LAMB'S WOOL = BARE UNPRINTED PAPER inside a black contour, with six or seven short curl
    marks. The whiteness is the paper, not white ink.
  - MORNING SUN = bare paper. Shadows are short flat 30% grey shapes lying to the lower right.
    NOT a light beam, NOT a lens flare, NOT a warm glow.
  - THE BALL = one solid black circle. Flat. NOT a shaded sphere.

COMPOSITION: flat ink field first, subject second.
  🔴 THE WALL IS THE MEASURING STICK. The dry-stone wall runs across the frame as a horizontal
    band on nine of the ten pages, always at about the standing lamb's shoulder height, and the
    reader learns to read the dog's distance against it. Keep its top edge a clean straight line.
  🔴 The ground beyond the wall lies about one hand LOWER than the yard (it falls away toward
    the hill). This is why only a tail, or only two ears, shows above the wall - and why the dog
    is not suddenly bigger when it climbs onto the wall later.
  Diagonals lead the eye. Never centre the lamb in dead symmetry.
  Keep the bottom 18% of the image quiet and free of key subject matter (a caption band is laid
  over it later).
  🔴 The last page does NOT repeat the first page's frame - different distance, different eye
    level, and the two animals are touching.

FINISH HIERARCHY - read twice. This is about how FINISHED each area is, NOT about opacity.
  1. LittleLamb and MungchiDog = finished (ink plate + black drawing over it).
  2. WHAT THEY TOUCH on that page (the ball, the one strip of grass under them, the stretch of
     wall they are at) = half-finished: shape plus a few black marks.
  3. EVERYTHING ELSE = FLAT INK FIELD, printed and left alone. No pebbles, no fence posts, no
     flowers, no clouds, no individual stones, no individual blades.
  🔴 The empty yard is NOT faded, NOT hazy and NOT out of focus. It is a confident flat plate of
  ink with nothing drawn inside it.

CHARACTER DESIGN: 🔴 eyes are DRAWN, not dotted - each eye is a solid black shape with a
  SEPARATE short brow stroke above it, so the face can squeeze shut, mismatch, widen and go
  crescent. The mouth is one black line that opens wide, twists sideways and purses.
  🔴 ANTHROPOMORPHISM GRADE, FIXED FOR ALL TEN PAGES: the LAMB stands on two legs and grips with
  its fore-hooves. The DOG is on FOUR legs on every page, holds nothing, and uses only its nose,
  mouth and tail. The dog never stands up, never gestures, never speaks.
  🔴 The lamb's CHEST is a second acting system: it inflates hugely, it goes uneven, it collapses
  hollow. Draw the chest deliberately on every calling page.

SETTING: an English upland farm yard - a broad short-cropped grass yard enclosed by a
  DRY-STONE WALL, one rounded green hill beyond it, morning. Northern European.
  NO barn interior, NO picket fence, NO American ranch, NO tropical planting, NO flower beds.

LIGHT: the sun is at the UPPER LEFT on all ten pages. Shadows are short and fall to the lower
  right. Morning, and the light does not change across the book.

OCHRE SCHEDULE (how much dog is printed on each page):
  p1  MEDIUM   - one whole dog, on the same ground plane as the lamb
  p2  SMALL    - the dog walking away, mid-yard, seen from behind
  p3  TINY     - one tail tuft above the wall and nothing else
  p4  🔴 ZERO  - the ochre plate is not printed. Two-ink page.
  p5  🔴 ZERO  - the ochre plate is not printed. Two-ink page.
  p6  TINY     - two ears above the wall
  p7  TINY     - the same two ears, out of focus at the frame edge
  p8  LARGE    - the ochre returns big: head and chest on top of the wall
  p9  LARGE    - a whole dog collided into the lamb, centre frame
  p10 LARGE and CLOSEST - the dog pressed against the lamb's side

CANVAS: 16:9 double-page spread, 4-6 year old picture book.

NOT: NOT digital airbrush / NOT smooth gradients / NOT glossy 3D CG render / NOT cel-shaded
  anime / NOT a texture filter laid over flat digital colour (the ink plates must MAKE the
  shapes) / NOT photographic / NOT rendered fur or rendered stone / NOT a fourth ink or any
  second warm colour beyond the dog's ochre / NOT red, orange or pink anywhere in this book /
  NOT a fully rendered background / NOT every stone, blade, cloud or flower drawn / NOT a
  uniform finish across the page / NOT a hazy, blurry or faded background (that is blur, not
  un-printed) / 🔴 NOT any lettering, numerals, signage, name tag, collar tag or engraved plate
  anywhere in the image - the dog's long name exists only as sound / NOT wool felt, NOT stitched
  fabric, NOT sculpted clay (other lines own those) / NOT dot-eyes on warm cream paper with a
  red thing on every page (that is another line).
```

## E-03 §3. 캐릭터 시트 (🔴 이것부터 굽는다)

```
CHARACTER SHEET - LittleLamb   (bake this FIRST, before any scene)

🔴 THE SHEET IS PRINTED IN THE SAME THREE INKS AS THE BOOK. Flat screen-printed ink on pale oat
  stock #F2EFE7, with visible ink grain, a 1-3mm plate misregistration and a solid black drawing
  line. Do NOT render this lamb smoothly just because there is no background behind it.
  🔴 THERE IS NO OCHRE ANYWHERE ON THIS LAMB. The ochre ink belongs to the dog alone.

FACE: 🔴 the eyes are DRAWN, not dots - each eye is a solid black rounded shape set wide on the
  face, with a SEPARATE short black brow stroke above it. The whole expression system is
  (eye shape x brow angle x mouth line):
    calling        = eyes squeezed shut into two curved seams, brows pushed up, mouth wide open
    tangled        = one eye bigger than the other and aimed a different way, brows crooked,
                     mouth twisted with the lower lip pushed to one side
    out of breath  = eyes flicking left and right, brows crowded up, mouth small and open with
                     no sound coming out
    delighted      = eyes crescent, brows soft, mouth wide in a smile
  Mouth = ONE black line that opens, twists and purses. The tongue, when it shows, is bare paper
  inside a black outline (there is no pink ink in this book).
  Nose = one small black shape. No blush, no highlight dot, no glossy catchlight.
BODY: 🔴 the wool is BARE UNPRINTED PAPER held inside a black contour, with six or seven short
  black curl marks - nothing more. The face, the ears and the legs are the 30% grey tint of the
  black plate. Matte, flat, no shading inside any shape.
🔴 THE CHEST IS A SECOND FACE: it must be drawable hugely inflated, unevenly half-filled, and
  collapsed hollow with the belly sucked in under the ribs. Show all three states on this sheet.
FORE-HOOVES: rounded fore-hooves used like hands - they grip a ball, press both cheeks, cup
  around the mouth like a trumpet, stamp the ground.
BUILD & SILHOUETTE: a small young lamb standing on two legs, about three and a half heads tall.
  Silhouette = round wool mass + two drooping ears + a small dark face + thin legs. Readable at
  thumbnail size and instantly distinct from the dog.
FACE SEPARATION (required): the eyes and brows must read apart from the head in VALUE - solid
  black on the pale grey tint, never sunk into one flat mass. Test: shrink to thumbnail and the
  mood is still legible.
REFERENCE SHEET: full body front idle / three-quarter view walking / 🔴 the CALLING pose (chest
  hugely inflated, up on the toes, mouth wide, eyes shut, fore-hooves in fists at the sides) /
```
