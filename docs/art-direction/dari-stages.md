## §0. 🔴 이 시리즈의 매체 번역 — 셋이다

**① 밝은 것은 그리는 게 아니라 「먼저 그어 둔 것」이다.**

앵커: `everything that will stay light is drawn FIRST in white wax … THEN one blue wash is flooded over`.
그래서 사물의 밝은 자리는 **워시가 오기 전에 이미 결정돼 있어야** 한다. 「저기를 좀 밝게」가 이 매체에 없는
말이고, 사물 시트가 하는 일의 절반이 **「이 물건의 어디가 흰 왁스인가」**를 못 박는 것이다.
거품 · 램프 불 · 조끼 단추 · 이불 · 편지 · 벌어진 조갯살이 전부 그 가족이다.

**② 마크는 세어져 있다 — 사물은 0에서 4개다.**

앵커: `DEEP marks … 5 to 12 on a figure, 0 to 4 on a thing`. 그러니 사물 시트가 정할 것은 「무엇을 그리나」가
아니라 **「그 넷을 어디에 쓰나」**다. 넷을 나뭇결에 다 쓰면 손잡이가 안 남는다.
그리고 **자리인 것은 옆으로 한 번 문지르고 안에 마크가 0개**(`RUBBED ON ITS SIDE … 0 marks inside`)이므로,
식탁 · 침대 · 난로 · 판자는 시트가 필요 없고 그 위에 놓인 것만 시트가 든다.

**③ 한 쪽에 마감되는 것은 둘뿐이다.**

앵커: `TWO FINISHED THINGS A PAGE, no more`. 아이 하나 + 그 쪽이 도는 것 하나다. 그래서 사물 시트는
**「이 물건이 마감되는 쪽」과 「문지르고 지나가는 쪽」을 갈라 적어야** 한다. 안 갈라 두면 화가가 조개도,
그릇도, 요람도 다 마감해서 한 쪽에 마감이 다섯이 된다 — 이 그림체에서 그건 화면이 통째로 무너지는 것이다.

⚠️ **넷째로 지킬 것** — `SHELL PINK #EE6B8D` 는 **스물다섯 권을 통틀어 달이 목의 가리비 하나**다.
아래 시트 스무 장 어디에도 분홍이 없고, 딸기 · 목걸이 · 조개 · 램프 · 구명조끼가 전부 그 함정이다.
시트마다 `NOT` 에 박아 뒀다.

### §2.1 Shell — 🔴 개수가 이 시트의 전부 (5권)

> 01 은 여섯 장 쌓다 무너뜨리고, 16 은 아홉 장을 쌓다 흩뜨렸다가 다시 모으고, 13 은 다섯에서 **일곱**으로
> 늘린다. 세 권 다 **독자가 세는** 권이라, 껍데기 한 장을 여기서 확정하지 않으면 쪽마다 다른 것이 쌓인다.

```
PROP SHEET - Shell   (dari-waxtide · SCENE token: Shell)

The empty scallop half-shells that lie all over this island. Five books stack them, count them, scatter
them and thread them, and in three of those books THE READER COUNTS, so one shell is drawn once here and
reused exactly.

FORM: one fan-shaped half-shell, FUR wax RUBBED ON ITS SIDE - one rub, so the blue wash catches in the
  grain and speckles it. EXACTLY 3 DEEP marks in the whole shell: two ribs fanning from the hinge and one
  hinge dot. Nothing else inside it. About as wide as a young seal's fore-flipper.
🔴 THE SHELL IS NEVER PINK AND NEVER THE ACCENT. Across twenty-five books the pink is the scallop on
  Dari's neck and nothing else - every shell on this sheet is FUR, and where two are drawn together the
  reader must see they are a different colour from the one at her throat.
STATES - the stacks and rows are all drawn at ONE size so they can be laid over each other:
  1 ONE SHELL ALONE, hinge down, seen from slightly above.
  2 A TOWER OF SIX, each shell laid flat on the one below, the stack as tall as a young seal's nose. The
    tower leans a hair - it is a child's stack, not a mason's.
  3 THE SAME TOWER WITH A SEVENTH being lowered onto the top from a mouth off-panel.
  4 A TOWER OF NINE, plainly taller than state 2 at the same drawing size.
  5 NINE IN THE AIR AND ON THE ROCK, thrown outward from where the tower stood - 🔴 the count is still
    nine and the empty ring the tower stood in is left as BARE PAPER.
  6 A ROW OF FIVE on a rock, laid in a straight line at EQUAL SPACING, seen from straight above.
  7 THE SAME ROW WITH SEVEN, equal spacing kept, the row longer - 🔴 state 6 and state 7 must sit one
    above the other on the plate so five and seven can be told apart at a glance.
  8 TWO SHELLS ROLLED LOOSE on a floor beside a leaning tower.

PLATE: state 1 large, then 2 3 4 5 in a row at one size, then 6 and 7 stacked one above the other.

NOT: no character, 🔴 no pink anywhere on this sheet, no lettering or numerals, no pearl, iridescence,
sheen or sparkle, no sand or barnacle texture, no more than 3 DEEP marks inside a shell, no cast shadow,
no gradient, glow, airbrush or soft edge, no shell drawn upside down in water.
```

### §2.2 Clam — 🔴 09권의 「똑같은 두 짝」과 21권의 「두드린 그 자리」

> 두 권이 같은 물건에 정반대의 것을 요구한다. 09 = **두 짝이 정확히 같아야** 공평이 성립하고,
> 21 = **벌어진 자리가 p5에서 두드리던 그 한 자리**여야 인과가 성립한다. 둘을 한 장에서 정한다.

```
PROP SHEET - Clam   (dari-waxtide · SCENE token: Clam)

The clam that gets eaten. One book carries one home the size of a soup bowl and halves it; another book
spends eight pages opening one with a stone. It is a LIVE clam and it is not the empty shell of the Shell
sheet - it is thicker, it is shut, and it opens.

FORM: a rounded bivalve, FUR wax RUBBED ON ITS SIDE, one rub. AT MOST 4 DEEP marks: one lip line where
  the two halves meet, two short growth lines, one hinge. Its lip line is the only thing that says it is
  shut. 0 barnacles, 0 speckle, 0 sheen.
  TWO SIZES, drawn together at one scale: THE EATING CLAM, a mouthful, three or four of them on a table;
  and THE BIG ONE, as wide as a soup bowl, which a young seal must hug with both fore-flippers.
🔴 THE TWO HALVES ARE EQUAL AND THE PAGE MUST PROVE IT. Draw the big clam opened, the two halves side by
  side at the SAME size in ONE panel, the same rib count in each - a four-year-old has to see that
  neither is bigger. That comparison is the whole of one volume and cannot be left to the page.
🔴 THE OPENING IS CAUSED. Mark ONE spot on the lip - a short DEEP nick - and draw the crack, and then the
  gape, AT THAT SAME SPOT. If the clam opens somewhere else the eight pages before it mean nothing.
THE STONE: a plain river stone, FUR, one rub, 0 DEEP marks, small enough to be carried IN THE MOUTH. Two
  sizes on the sheet - a grown seal's and a child's - and neither has a point or an edge.
🔴 THE MEAT INSIDE IS BARE PAPER, not painted: when the clam gapes, the inside is the brightest thing on
  the page, read only by the FUR shell around it. NEVER outline it.
STATES:
  1 SHUT, three eating clams on a table, warm, seen from above.
  2 THE BIG ONE SHUT, hugged against a chest between two fore-flippers (flippers only, no face).
  3 SHUT, pinned flat under two fore-flippers with a stone coming down - the stone still in a mouth.
  4 THE STONE SKIDDING OFF - the clam pushed half a span sideways, 🔴 not a scratch on it.
  5 ONE THIN CRACK at the marked spot and nothing else changed.
  6 GAPED OPEN, the two halves hinged apart, bare-paper meat inside, the gape starting at the marked spot.
  7 THE TWO HALVES LAID SIDE BY SIDE on a table, equal, seen from straight above.
  8 TWO EMPTY HALVES side by side, meat gone.

PLATE: the two sizes side by side, then states 1-8, then state 7 drawn again directly under state 2 so
  the halves can be measured against the whole.

NOT: no character face, 🔴 no pink anywhere (the meat is paper, not pink), no lettering or numerals, no
pearl, sheen or wet highlight, no barnacles, no blood or juice, no sharp-edged or pointed stone, no more
than 4 DEEP marks on the shell, no gradient, glow or soft edge.
```

### §2.3 ShellCord — 끈에 꿴 조개 (딸랑이 · 목걸이)

```
PROP SHEET - ShellCord   (dari-waxtide · SCENE token: ShellCord)

Shells threaded on a cord. One book breaks a rattle and ties a new one; two other books hang a shell
necklace on a neck. Same shells as the Shell sheet, same cord in every book.

FORM: the cord is ONE thin FUR line, plaited, no thicker than a whisker, with a plain knot at each end.
  The shells on it are the Shell sheet's shell, unchanged - one side-rub, 3 DEEP marks.
🔴 NOTHING ON THIS SHEET IS PINK. A shell necklace hangs beside Dari's pink scallop on two pages and the
  two must read as different colours at thumbnail size: hers is the accent, these are FUR.
🔴 A KNOT IS A DRAWING, NOT A TEXTURE. Each knot is at most 2 DEEP marks. Two knots on this sheet are
  DELIBERATELY DIFFERENT and the difference must be visible small: the OLD knot is a tight flat loop, the
  NEW knot is a fat lumpy overhand with two tails sticking out. One volume ends on the two lying side by
  side and that is the only thing that says a child tied the second one.
STATES:
  1 THE RATTLE, WHOLE - two shells face to face on a short cord, the old flat knot, hanging still.
  2 THE RATTLE, BROKEN - the same two shells, and ONE of them split from lip to hinge by a single DEEP
    line. The split shell keeps its full outline; it has not lost a piece.
  3 THE RATTLE ON THE FLOOR at the foot of a wall, cord slack, split side up.
  4 THE NEW RATTLE - two whole shells on the same length of cord, the fat lumpy knot with two tails.
  5 🔴 THE PAIR - broken rattle and new rattle lying side by side at the SAME size, the two knots plainly
    different. Nothing else in the panel.
  6 A NECKLACE - three shells spaced along a neck loop, hanging on a market rail.
  7 THE SAME NECKLACE ON A NECK, drawn beside a single scallop on its own cord so the two hang together.

PLATE: states 1-5 in a row at one size, then 6 and 7, plus a large close-up of the two knots side by side.

NOT: no character face, 🔴 no pink shell and no pink cord anywhere, no lettering, numerals or beads, no
```
