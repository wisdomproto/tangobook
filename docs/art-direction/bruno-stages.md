## §0. 🔴 매체 번역 한 줄 — 이 매체엔 윤곽선이 없다

앵커: `Direction NEVER changes inside one area - it changes only where one thing ends and another
begins, and THAT CHANGE OF DIRECTION IS THE EDGE.`

**획이 도는 방향이 바뀌는 자리가 곧 가장자리다.** 그러므로 자리 시트가 정하는 것은 「무엇이 있나」가
아니라 🔴 **「어느 면이 어느 방향인가」**다. 방향이 겹치면 두 물건이 **한 덩어리가 된다** — 벽과 바닥이
같은 방향이면 방이 없어진다. → 모든 자리 시트에 `STROKE DIRECTION MAP` 절.

🔴 **둘째 — 하늘·물·얼음·눈은 아예 왁스를 안 얹는다.** 그러니 호숫가·개울가 시트가 정하는 것은
「물을 어떻게 칠하나」가 아니라 **「어디를 안 칠하나」**이고, 물의 형태는 **둘레의 방향들이 만든다**.
21권이 통째로 얼음 이야기라 여기가 제일 위험하다.

⚠️ 셋째 — `DEPTH IS SPACING`. 숲 깊은 데는 줄기가 **좁게 선다**(같은 왁스 무게). 흐리게·푸르게 금지.
⚠️ 넷째 — 🔴 `THE FRAME STAYS WIDE ENOUGH TO HOLD BOTH BEARS FROM THE KNEE UP`(여덟 권이 앞발로
말한다). 자리 시트의 SPOT 에 **앞발만 화면을 채우는 앵글을 만들지 않는다**.

### §1.1 Cabin — 실제 프롬프트 (가장 먼저)

```
STAGE SHEET - Cabin   (bruno-crayonwood · SCENE token: Cabin · bake FIRST)

The log cabin the two bears live in. Eight of the thirteen named places in this series are inside
it, so this drawing decides the room once - and above all it decides ITS DIRECTIONS.

🔴 STROKE DIRECTION MAP - every area is one field of one crayon laid in ONE direction, and the
  direction changes ONLY at an edge. Fix them here and never change them:
  FLOOR - BARK, strokes running LEFT TO RIGHT along the planks (at most 6 planks).
  BACK WALL - BARK, strokes running VERTICALLY with the logs.
  CEILING BEAMS - OVERLAY, strokes running LEFT TO RIGHT (at most 4 beams).
  TABLE TOP - BARK, strokes running AWAY FROM THE VIEWER, 🔴 which is a third direction and is why
    the table separates from the floor without any outline.
  SHUTTERS AND CLOTH - SPRUCE, strokes running VERTICALLY.
  THE STOVE - OVERLAY, strokes running LEFT TO RIGHT.
  🔴 NO TWO TOUCHING AREAS MAY SHARE A DIRECTION. Check this on the sheet by naming, for each pair
  of neighbours, which way each one runs.

FIXED PARTS: one table with two stools · a low shelf on the back wall · 🔴 ONE WINDOW above the
  table, and THE LIGHT THROUGH IT IS BARE PAPER, unwaxed, the brightest thing in the room · a door
  to the yard · the stove in the corner with at most 5 curls of steam · at most 4 windows in the
  whole cabin, ever.
COVERAGE: wax is at most 80 percent everywhere - the oat-grey grain of the paper shows through
  every single area, and no area is ever solid.

SPOTS:
  A THE WHOLE ROOM from the door, wide: table under the window left, stove right, shelf behind.
  B THE TABLE, medium, 🔴 wide enough to hold two bears from the knee up.
  C THE WINDOW, medium: the bare-paper light and the sill.
  D THE FLOOR, close, high: plank direction and whatever is on it.

PLATE: A, B, C, D once each, plus 🔴 ONE DIRECTION DIAGRAM - the same room drawn as flat areas with
  an arrow in each one showing which way its strokes run.

NOT: no character of any kind, no boot blue anywhere on this sheet (the blue is Nils's boots only),
  no lettering or numerals, no outline of any kind - edges are changes of stroke direction, no
  third crayon, no solid area, no waxed sky, water, ice or snow, no shading, gradient, glow or
  soft edge, nothing darker or paler with distance.
```

🔴 **SCENE 이 찍는 좌표 토큰** — `Cabin/C` (22 p9·p10 · 23 p5·p7·p10) = 아래 `Cabin` 시트의 **SPOT C
(THE WINDOW)** 다. 다른 이름이 아니라 **같은 시트의 카메라 자리**이므로 시트를 새로 만들지 않는다.
🔴 다만 23권은 창 **너머**에 모이대가 있으므로 `Cabin/C` 와 `FeedBoard` 가 **한 화면에 겹친다**
(23 p10 「창을 사이에 두고 안팎이 한 프레임」) — 두 시트의 창턱 높이를 같은 값으로 굽는다.

### §1.2 Lake — 요약 명세

🔴 **호수는 안 칠한 종이다.** 물가 선은 **땅 쪽 획이 멈추는 자리**이고 물 쪽엔 아무것도 없다.
21권 얼음도 **같은 안 칠한 자리** — 얼음과 물이 같은 종이라 **구멍만이 둘을 가른다**(21권 구멍 6쪽).

### §1.3 Path — 실제 프롬프트 (신설 · 8쪽 · 5권)

> 04(봄) · 06(봄) · 24 · 19 · 46(겨울). 🔴 **이 시리즈에서 길은 「바닥이 다져졌나」로만 존재한다** —
> 06 은 다져진 큰길을 두고 **풀에 덮인 좁은 틈**으로 들어가는 것이 사건이고, 24 는 그 바닥을 눈에서
> **파내는** 것이 사건이며, 04 는 바닥에 남은 **젖은 점 자국**이 사건이다. 셋 다 「길가에 무엇이 있나」가
> 아니라 **발밑**이다.

```
STAGE SHEET - Path   (bruno-crayonwood · SCENE token: Path)

The one path that leaves the cabin clearing and goes out through the wood. Five books use it and in
every one of them THE THING THAT MATTERS IS UNDERFOOT, so this sheet decides the floor of it.

🔴 STROKE DIRECTION MAP - every area is one field of one crayon laid in ONE direction, and the
  direction changes ONLY at an edge (there are no outlines in this book):
  THE BEATEN PATH - BARK, strokes running ALONG the path, away from the viewer. This is a third
    direction and it is what makes the path read as a path and not as ground.
  THE GROUND EITHER SIDE - BARK too, but strokes running ACROSS. 🔴 The path has no edge of its own:
    the change of direction IS its edge, and that is the whole picture in 06.
  GRASS AND UNDERGROWTH - SPRUCE, strokes running UPWARD.
  TRUNKS - BARK, strokes running UP. The canopy takes whatever the season clause says.

FIXED PARTS:
  THE MAIN PATH runs from the bottom of the frame and BENDS ONCE, out of sight, so the far end is
    never shown. It is about two grandfathers wide.
  THE FORK - at one point the grass closes over a SECOND, NARROW way, no wider than a cub's
    shoulders. It shows only as a gap in the SPRUCE, and 🔴 THE BEATEN PATH CARRIES ON PAST IT to
    the right of frame, so both ways are in one picture and a reader can see which one was refused.
  ONE FALLEN TRUNK lies ACROSS the narrow way, chest-high on the cub, its strokes running ALONG it,
    at most 4 broken branch stubs, SPRUCE moss on its upper side.
  THE END OF THE NARROW WAY opens on a gap between trunks with THE CABIN ROOF AND CHIMNEY seen
    through it, small - the path is a loop and it comes home.
  Trunks at most 9. Nothing else stands beside this path: no gate, no post, no bench, no marker.

SEASON - use ONE season only, the one that book is in.
  SPRING: the canopy is bare PAPER; SPRUCE only as new growth low down and in the grass; the beaten
    path is BARK at its most open. 🔴 A WET MARK ON THE PATH IS NOT WAX - it is the path left
    UNWAXED, a bare-paper shape with a hard edge, and a line of them running away from the viewer is
    the whole subject of 04. Never a darker patch.
  WINTER: ground and sky are BARE PAPER edge to edge and THE PATH DOES NOT EXIST until something
    clears it. 🔴 A CLEARED PATH IS A BARK FIELD ON BARE PAPER with a hard edge each side and its
    strokes running away from the viewer; ITS LENGTH IS THE CLOCK of 24 - draw it three times in the
    same frame, reaching a third of the way, two thirds, and out of frame.
  A DINT WHERE A BODY HAS LAIN DOWN is bare paper with at most 3 BARK marks along its lower lip and
    nothing at all inside it.

SPOTS - four, do not invent a fifth. 🔴 None of them crops closer than the cub from the knee up.
  A ALONG THE PATH, wide, eye level: the beaten way running away and bending once.
  B THE FORK, medium wide, high: the wide way going right, the narrow gap in the grass at the left,
    both in one frame.
  C UNDERFOOT, close, high: only the path floor and what is on it - the change of stroke direction
    at its edge, and prints or a cleared line running away.
  D THE FALLEN TRUNK, medium wide, low: the trunk crossing the frame horizontally, ground on both
    sides of it, the narrow way carrying on beyond.

PLATE: A, B, C, D once each, plus 🔴 C DRAWN THREE TIMES at one size - bare spring path, path with a
  run of unwaxed wet prints, winter path cleared a third of the way - so the floor reads as one
  floor in all three. Plus one small direction diagram (path away / ground across / grass up).

NOT: no character of any kind, no boot blue anywhere on this sheet, no lettering or numerals, no
  outline of any kind - edges are changes of stroke direction, no third crayon, no solid area, no
  waxed sky, water, ice or snow, no white crayon, no shading, gradient, glow or soft edge, nothing
  darker or paler with distance, no gate, post, fence, bench, marker or sign of any kind.
```

### §1.4 SnowSlope — 실제 프롬프트 (신설 · §3-a 신고 2 의 답)

> 19 · 46 · 47. 🔴 **세 권이 같은 비탈 하나에 걸려 있고, 그 비탈에 남는 「한 줄」이 세 권의 착지다** —
> 19 p10 「아침에 자국 없던 언덕에 이제 줄이 하나 났다」 · 46 p10 「꼭대기부터 발치까지 한 줄」 ·
> 47 p9 「올라간 자국과 내려온 자국이 같은 비탈에 나란하다」. 정할 것은 경치가 아니라 **길이와 경사**다.
> 🔴 47 p3 은 **꼭대기의 아이와 비탈 끝의 할아버지를 한 화면**에 놓으므로 비탈 전체가 한 프레임에
> 들어가는 각이 반드시 있어야 한다.
> ⚠️ 50권의 「눈밭」은 여기가 아니다 — 오두막에서 헛간으로 가는 **평평한 눈밭**이고 비탈이 없다.

```
STAGE SHEET - SnowSlope   (bruno-crayonwood · SCENE token: SnowSlope · winter only)

The hill in front of the cabin. Three books slide down it. 🔴 THE SLOPE IS A RULER: what gets drawn
on it is a TRACK, and the LENGTH of that track is what each of those books is about, so the slope's
length and steepness are fixed here once and never change again.

🔴 IT IS ALL BARE PAPER. Ground and sky are unwaxed edge to edge, so the slope has no surface of its
  own - what tells the eye there is a hill is ONLY where the four fixed things below sit, and the
  track. Never wax the snow. No white crayon, no blue shadow on snow.

FIXED PARTS - the only waxed things on this stage, and they are the scale:
  TWO SPRUCES AT THE CREST, BARK trunks with SPRUCE crowns, standing close together.
  THREE SPRUCES AT THE FOOT, same build, standing further apart. 🔴 DEPTH IS SPACING - the crest
    pair stands closer together at the same weight of wax, and that spacing is the only thing that
    says one pair is further off.
  THE CABIN ROOF AND CHIMNEY below and beyond the foot, small, BARK, at most 4 windows.
  ONE SHOULDER partway down where the fall eases: steep above it, gentle below it. That break is a
    named place - two of the three books cross it, and it is where being afraid stops.
STEEPNESS: side on, the steep part falls about one in two and the gentle part about one in five.
  Seen wide from the foot, the crest sits in the upper third of the frame.

🔴 THE TRACK - the one thing that changes, and it is drawn, never implied:
  A SLED TRACK is TWO parallel BARK lines a sled's width apart, unbroken, running the length of the
    slope. A SKI TRACK is the same with the two lines a body's width apart.
  A FOOT TRACK is separate BARK marks, at most 14, in ONE line, none a mirrored copy of its
    neighbour - and it runs UP the slope where a sled track runs down.
  LENGTHS ARE THE CLOCK: draw the same wide frame FOUR times - no track at all, a track a third of
    the way down, a track from crest to foot, and a down-track with an up-track beside it.
  Flung snow is at most 14 bare-paper points, and there are none of them on a page with no track.

SPOTS - four, do not invent a fifth. 🔴 None of them crops closer than the cub from the knee up.
  A THE WHOLE SLOPE FROM THE FOOT, wide, low: the three spruces at the near edge, the crest and its
    two spruces in the upper third. 🔴 THIS IS THE SPOT THAT HOLDS ONE FIGURE AT THE CREST AND
    ANOTHER AT THE FOOT IN ONE FRAME, both legible.
  B AT THE CREST looking down, medium wide, low: the two spruces close, the slope running out of the
    bottom of the frame, the cabin roof far below.
  C SIDE ON, wide: crest at one edge, foot at the other, the shoulder plainly between them, so the
    two steepnesses can be compared.
  D THE SHOULDER, medium, eye level: the gentle fall ahead, the steep part behind and above.

PLATE: A, B, C, D once each, plus 🔴 A DRAWN FOUR TIMES at one size for the four track lengths, plus
  one small side diagram of the slope with the shoulder marked.

NOT: no character of any kind, no boot blue anywhere on this sheet, no lettering or numerals, no
  outline of any kind, no third crayon, no solid area, 🔴 no waxed snow and no waxed sky, no white
  crayon, no blue on snow, no shading, gradient, glow or soft edge, nothing darker or paler with
  distance, no motion line, speed line or spray behind a sled.
```

> 🔴 **작업표(`_PROP-SHEETS.md` `## bruno`)는 컷 라벨만 읽은 기계 산출이라 이 시리즈에서 크게 틀렸다** —
> 11권(손전등 9쪽)·47권(나무판 9쪽)·46/19권(썰매)·07권(큰 통 vs 작은 통)이 통째로 빠져 있고,
> 03권은 「건너편」만 올라오고 정작 **징검다리 돌 다섯**이 없다. 아래 목록은 **대본을 읽고** 다시 세운 것이다.

### 🔴 §2-a. 머리말 ① — 밝은 것·젖은 것은 칠하는 게 아니라 **안 칠하는** 것이다

앵커: `SKY, WATER, ICE AND SNOW ARE NEVER WAXED`. 그래서 이 시리즈의 사물 시트 아홉 장이 **한 가족**이다 —
수액 방울(01) · 물뿌리개의 물(04) · 손전등 빛(11) · 등불과 난로 불(19·27·49) · 김과 입김(9권) ·
눈(19·24·46·47·50) · 얼음 구멍(21) · 물통 속 물(28). 🔴 **이 중 하나라도 흰 크레용이나 번짐으로 그리면
그 권이 아니라 매체가 무너진다.** 각 시트가 「그 밝은 것이 무엇으로 읽히는가」를 못 박아 뒀다.

### 🔴 §2-b. 머리말 ② — **센 것과 안 센 것**

앵커가 캡을 걸어 놨다(`berries at most 9 dots · seeds at most 9 dots · logs in a stack at most 6 ·
falling snow at most 14 · steam at most 5`). 그런데 일곱 권이 **「수북한」·「쏟아지는」·「사방으로 흩어진」**
것을 요구한다(07 열매 · 13 사과 수십 알 · 14 버섯 산더미 · 15 장작 벽 · 02 씨앗 · 23 씨앗 자루).
🔴 **규칙 하나로 둘 다 지킨다 — 흩어져 따로 있는 것은 세고(캡), 그릇·바구니·자루·더미에 담긴 것은
가장자리만 삐죽한 한 덩어리로 칠한다(안 센다).** 퐁이의 「가까운 갈대 7획 ↔ 갈대밭 한 면」과 같은 자리다.

### 🔴 §2-c. 머리말 ③ — 사물에도 **획 방향**이 있다

§0 이 자리 시트에 건 규칙이 사물에도 그대로 온다. 🔴 **모든 사물 시트는 제 획이 어느 쪽으로 도는지를
한 줄로 적는다** — 안 적으면 그 사물이 놓인 바닥·벽과 같은 방향이 되어 **한 덩어리로 붙는다**(윤곽선이
없는 매체라 그게 곧 사물이 사라지는 것이다).

### §2.2 Firewood — 실제 프롬프트 (🔴 15권은 획 방향이 곧 줄거리다)

> 이 시리즈에서 §0 의 매체 규칙이 그대로 이야기가 된 유일한 권이다. **눕힌 장작 = 획이 가로 · 세운 장작
> = 획이 세로**이고, 무너지는 쪽은 「세로가 안 버틴다」이며 착지는 「마구리가 가지런히 한 방향」이다.
> 45권의 가시가 여기서 나오므로 같은 시트에 든다.

```
PROP SHEET - Firewood   (bruno-crayonwood · SCENE token: Firewood)

Split firewood. Volume 15 is a whole book of stacking it two ways; volume 45 gets a splinter out of
it; volume 49 keeps six sticks by the stove.

FORM: one billet is ONE flat field of BARK with the STROKES RUNNING ALONG ITS LENGTH, at most 3 bark
  lines, and the sawn end (the end grain) is a second field with the strokes running ACROSS - that
  change of direction is the end of the stick and there is no outline. A billet is as long as the
  cub's arm. Wax at most 80 percent everywhere; the oat grain shows through.
🔴 TWO WAYS OF STACKING, AND THE DIRECTION IS THE WHOLE VOLUME:
  LAID DOWN - billets lying, all strokes running LEFT TO RIGHT, sawn ends facing the reader in a
    grid of end-grain patches. This stack is WIDE and LOW and it holds.
  STOOD UP - billets standing, all strokes running VERTICALLY, no end grain showing. This stack is
    NARROW and TALL and it opens at the top like a splayed cone. Draw it plainly leaning.
  🔴 Never mix the two directions inside one stack. A page that shows both stacks side by side is
  ONE field running one way beside ONE field running the other, and that is the only thing that
  separates them - not a line, not a shadow.
🔴 COUNTING (anchor cap 6 in a stack): the near five or six billets are drawn as separate sticks with
  their own end grain; everything behind them is ONE flat BARK field with a ragged top edge and 0
  interior sticks. A wall of firewood is a field, not a count.
🔴 A COLLAPSE EMPTIES THE PAGE. When the tall stack falls, the field is GONE - at most 7 separate
  billets scattered on bare ground and nothing else. Do not draw a heap of rubble: the picture of
  that page is the empty wall where the field used to be.
🔴 THE SPLINTER (volume 45) is ONE OVERLAY point in the middle of an open paw, no bigger than a
  bark line, with 0 redness, 0 swelling and 0 blood - the volume has no red and no third colour. It
  moves once, from the paw to the grandfather's palm, at the same size. Draw both panels here.
STATES:
  1 ONE BILLET alone, side on, with its end grain.
  2 THE LAID STACK against a wall, knee high, wide, end grain facing out.
  3 THE STOOD STACK, three billets, splayed and leaning.
  4 THE SAME TWO STACKS side by side at one scale - one wide and low, one narrow and tall.
  5 AFTER THE COLLAPSE - bare ground, 7 scattered billets, the wall empty.
  6 THE FINISHED WALL, shoulder high, every sawn end facing the same way.
  7 SIX BILLETS in a tidy row beside a stove.
  8 THE SPLINTER, twice: in a small round paw / on a broad flat palm.

PLATE: states 1-8, plus a close-up of one sawn end showing the two stroke directions meeting.

NOT: no character face (paws only for the splinter panels), no boot blue anywhere, no lettering or
numerals, no outline of any kind - edges are changes of stroke direction, no third crayon, no solid
area, no bark texture beyond 3 lines, no sawdust cloud, no motion line for a falling billet, no
shading, gradient, glow or soft edge.
```

### §2.3 Pails — 🔴 이 시리즈에서 제일 급한 시트 (「통」이 다섯 물건이다)

> 여덟 권이 「통」에 기대는데 **매번 다른 물건**이다. 라이브 퐁이 08 에서 「쪽마다 다른 의자」가 나온 자리와
> 정확히 같고, 여기는 그게 여덟 권으로 퍼진다. **구조는 하나, 크기가 다섯.**

```
PROP SHEET - Pails   (bruno-crayonwood · SCENE token: Pails)

Every wooden vessel in the wood and the yard. Eight volumes use one, the Korean text calls them all
by one word, and they are FIVE DIFFERENT OBJECTS - so this sheet fixes one construction and five
sizes, drawn together in one row at one scale.

ONE CONSTRUCTION FOR ALL FIVE: BARK, staves shown as VERTICAL strokes (at most 7 on the largest,
  fewer on the smaller ones), two hoop lines running ACROSS - and that crossing direction is the
  only thing that reads as a hoop, never a drawn band. The mouth is a wide ellipse and 🔴 ITS INSIDE
  IS OVERLAY WITH THE STROKES RUNNING THE OTHER WAY; that direction change is how a mouth reads as
  open, and there is no outline anywhere.
🔴 FIVE SIZES IN ONE ROW, at one scale, each beside a plain cub silhouette for height:
  1 THE TAP PAIL (vol 01) - wide-mouthed, hung on a peg by a rope, comes to the cub's knee.
  2 THE SMALL PAIL (vol 07, 21) - one arched handle, carried in one paw, comes to the cub's shin.
    The grandfather's, and in 21 there are TWO OF THIS SIZE side by side and they must be identical.
  3 THE BIG PAIL (vol 07) - AS TALL AS THE CUB HIMSELF, iron-hooped, no handle, dragged by its rim.
    🔴 If it is not plainly taller than the small one by a whole body, that volume has no joke.
  4 THE WELL BUCKET (vol 24) - narrow and deep, a rope bail across the mouth, hung on the well rope.
  5 THE WASH TUB (vol 04, 28, 32, 45) - LOW AND WIDE, chest height on the cub, standing on the
    ground beside the well; both paws go in it at once.
🔴 WHAT IS INSIDE IS BARE PAPER, NOT WAX. Water, sap and melt are never waxed, so the level in a
  pail is HOW MUCH OF ITS OVERLAY INSIDE IS LEFT UNPRINTED, with a hard straight edge and 0 ripples,
  0 glints, 0 reflections. Draw the ruler once, on size 1: empty · one drop · a finger · half · full.
🔴 DIRTY WATER IS NOT A COLOUR (vol 28). There is no brown paint here - the paper is the brown. Mud
  coming off a paw is drawn as an OVERLAY area growing outward from the paw with a HARD edge against
  the still-bare-paper water. Clean and dirty split inside ONE tub. Foam and suds are BARE PAPER, at
  most 5 rounded clumps, never outlined.
🔴 WHAT IS INSIDE THAT IS NOT WATER IS ONE MASS (see the counting rule): berries or apples in a pail
  are ONE OVERLAY field with a ragged top edge and at most 9 separate pieces breaking that edge -
  never a counted heap.
STATES: 1 the five sizes in a row · 2 size 1 hung under a spout, the five levels as five panels ·
  3 size 3 dragged, tipped forward, its mouth pouring · 4 sizes 2 and 3 side by side, the small one
  full and the big one a third full · 5 size 5 with the clean/dirty split inside it · 6 size 4 coming
  up on a well rope, full · 7 size 3 on its side, empty, mouth toward the reader (deep OVERLAY hole).

PLATE: the five-size row, the five-level strip, then states 3-7.

NOT: no character face (plain silhouettes for scale only), no boot blue anywhere, no lettering or
numerals, no outline, no third crayon, no solid area, no waxed water, sap, foam or ice, no metal
sheen on a hoop, no drips or splash marks, no shading, gradient, glow or soft edge.
```
