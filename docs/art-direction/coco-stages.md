## §0. 🔴 매체 번역 한 줄 — 무대 시트는 사물 목록이 아니라 **블록 목록**이다

앵커: `the world is built from repeated blocks - one block cut once and pressed again and again.
The block is the SAME shape every time` · `Clutter is made by REPEAT, not by variety` ·
🔴 `A THING BEING SEARCHED FOR IS CUT AS ITS OWN BLOCK while everything it hides among is the
repeated one - that is how the eye finds it.`

**그러므로 이 시리즈의 자리 시트가 정하는 것은 「거기에 무엇이 있나」가 아니라 「그 자리의 반복 블록이
무엇이고 몇 번 찍나」다.** 골목 하나 = **아치 블록 1개 × 12번**. 벽 하나 = **점 블록 1개**. 선반 하나 =
**빵 블록 1개 × 9번**.

🔴 **이게 곧 사건이다.** 코코는 「찾기」 권이 많은데(13 노란 천막 · 11 해바라기 · 23 모자), **찾는 것만
제 블록을 갖고 나머지는 전부 그 자리의 반복 블록**이라야 눈이 그걸 찾는다. 자리 시트가 반복 블록을 확정해
놓지 않으면 **배경이 저마다 다르게 그려지고, 그 순간 찾는 것이 안 튀어나온다** — 이 시리즈에서 무대 시트가
없으면 못 그리는 권이 최소 셋이다.

⚠️ 같이 지킬 것 — `Ink coverage is 90 percent, never solid` · `the paper is visibly dented where a
block bit`. 시트에도 **가장자리에 잉크가 몰리고 안쪽으로 종이가 비쳐야** 한다. 꽉 찬 면을 그리면
컷이 그걸 따라간다.

### §1.1 Bakery — 실제 프롬프트 (가장 먼저)

```
STAGE SHEET - Bakery   (coco-pressalley · SCENE token: Bakery · bake FIRST)

The mouse bakery. Twelve of the twenty-seven named places in this series are inside this one room,
so this drawing decides it once - and above all it decides ITS BLOCKS.

🔴 THE BLOCK LIST - cut each of these ONCE and press it again and again. A block may run off an
  edge but is NEVER redrawn, and clutter is made by repeating a block, never by variety:
  PLANK BLOCK - the floor. One plank shape, pressed in rows, INK1.
  DOT BLOCK - the plaster wall. One small dot, pressed as a field, INK1 at a light bite.
  LOAF BLOCK - the shelf. One loaf shape, at most 9 presses per shelf, INK1.
  JAR BLOCK - the sill and the corner. One jar shape, at most 6 presses, INK2.
  BEAM BLOCK - the ceiling. One beam, at most 4 presses, INK2.
  Nothing on this stage is drawn one at a time except the three FIXED PARTS below.

FIXED PARTS (their own blocks, cut once, not repeated):
  THE OVEN, back wall, INK2 with an OVERLAP mouth. 🔴 THE OVEN GLOW IS BARE PAPER, not printed, and
    it is the brightest thing on the page.
  THE KNEADING TABLE, 🔴 STANDING AT THE WINDOW - one table, one place, fixed for the series (the
    window light falling across the dough is the picture of this book). INK1, at most 4 seam lines.
  THE WINDOW above it, INK2 frame, 🔴 THE LIGHT THROUGH IT IS BARE PAPER.
  Flour dust is bare paper showing through a thinner pull - never a white ink.

🔴 THE SOUGHT-THING RULE lives on this stage: in a volume built around finding one thing, that thing
  is cut AS ITS OWN BLOCK and everything it hides among is the repeated block. Draw a demonstration
  panel: a shelf of nine loaf-block presses with ONE differently cut loaf among them, so the eye
  goes straight to it without any change of colour.

REGISTRATION AND BITE: ink sits heaviest at the edge of every shape and the paper shows through
  inside it at about ten percent. The press dents the sheet. Where INK1 and INK2 overlap, OVERLAP
  appears - that is the only dark, and it is never mixed as a third ink.

SPOTS:
  A THE WHOLE ROOM from the doorway, wide: table at the window left, oven back right, shelves along.
  B THE TABLE, medium, from the side: window above, the light on the tabletop as bare paper.
  C THE TABLETOP, close, high: the surface and what is on it.
  D THE OVEN, medium low: the mouth in OVERLAP, its glow bare paper.

PLATE: A, B, C, D once each, plus 🔴 THE BLOCK SHEET - each of the five blocks printed alone at large
  size so it can be reused unchanged - plus the sought-thing demonstration panel.

NOT: no character of any kind, no accent red anywhere on this sheet (the red plate touches only
  Coco's kerchief), no lettering, numerals, shop signs or price cards, no third mixed ink, no solid
  100-percent area, no white ink anywhere, no airbrush, gradient, glow or soft edge, and no object
  drawn individually where a block exists for it.
```

### §1.2 Alley — 요약 명세

### §2.1 Dough — 실제 프롬프트 (🔴 3권 공유 · 높이가 사건)

```
PROP SHEET - Dough   (coco-pressalley · SCENE token: Dough)

The dough in its bowl under a cloth. One whole volume is ten pages of looking at it, so its FOUR
HEIGHTS are the instrument and are fixed here.

FORM: THE BOWL is its own block, INK1, a wide shallow shape with a rim line, ink heaviest at the
  edge, paper showing through inside. THE CLOTH is INK2, a soft square laid over the rim with its
  corners hanging, at most 3 fold marks. THE DOUGH is a plain domed shape, bare PAPER with only its
  edge bitten, so it is the palest thing in the bowl.
🔴 FOUR HEIGHTS, and a reader must be able to tell them apart at a glance. Draw all four in a row,
  same bowl, same distance, same eye level:
  1 FLAT - the dome sits BELOW the rim; the rim line runs unbroken across the picture.
  2 LEVEL - the dome touches the rim; the rim line still unbroken.
  3 OVER - the dome rises past the rim so the rim line is BROKEN on both sides. 🔴 This break is the
    event of the book and nothing else says it.
  4 DOUBLE - the dome twice as tall as the bowl, its edge lipping down over the outside.
STATES ALSO: the cloth ON (a square with the dome's shape faintly raising its middle) and the cloth
  LIFTED (held at one corner, the bowl open).

PLATE: the four heights in a row, plus cloth-on and cloth-lifted, plus one close-up of the rim line
  at height 2 and height 3 side by side.

NOT: no character, no hands, no accent red, no lettering or numerals, no third mixed ink, no solid
  area, no white ink, no steam rising from unbaked dough, no shading, gradient, sheen or glow.
```

### §2.2 Pots — 실제 프롬프트 (🔴 「찾는 것」 블록의 실례)

```
PROP SHEET - Pots   (coco-pressalley · SCENE token: Pots)

Flowerpots on a sill and along a wall. Two books use them, and in one of them a single sunflower is
the thing being searched for - so this sheet has to make BOTH the repeat and the exception.

FORM: 🔴 THE POT BLOCK - one pot shape, cut once, INK1, ink heaviest at the rim and the foot, paper
  showing through the body. Pressed at most 6 times in a row along a sill; a seventh pot is the same
  block again, never a new one. Soil at the top is one short OVERLAP band.
  THE PLANT BLOCK - one small leafy shape, INK2, pressed once per pot, and 🔴 IT IS THE SAME PLANT IN
  EVERY POT. A row of pots is a row of the same thing, and that is the point.
🔴 THE SOUGHT ONE: the sunflower is CUT AS ITS OWN BLOCK - a tall stem, one round head, leaves that
  do not match the plant block at all. In the volume that is about finding it, it may carry the
  page's ONLY warm-yellow pull, CRUST printed at half strength, and nothing else on that page gets
  it. In any other volume it takes INK1 or INK2 like everything else.
STATES: 1 the row of six identical pots on a sill · 2 the same row with the sought one among them ·
  3 one pot alone, close, so the block's bite is visible · 4 the sunflower alone, its own block.

PLATE: the four states, plus the pot block and the plant block printed alone.

NOT: no character, no hands, no accent red, no lettering or numerals, no third mixed ink, no solid
  area, no white ink, no pot drawn individually where the block exists, no shading, gradient or glow.
```

## §3. 🔴 미결 — 「골목」 6갈래(10쪽) · 「반죽대」 3갈래(7쪽) · 「창가」 2갈래(6쪽)

> 🔴 **이 §3 은 판정 근거만 적는다. 이름→토큰 변환의 SSOT 는 `_stage-tokens.json` 이다.**
> 표 출력·검사 = `node packages/client/scripts/extract-series-stages.mjs --tokens coco` (**미매칭 0** 이어야 한다).
> 🔴 **쪽 목록은 폐기했다** — 목록과 변환표를 따로 두니 어긋났다(변환표엔 있는데 목록엔 없는 이름이 나왔다).
> 규칙이 전 이름을 덮으므로 목록이 필요 없고, 「목록 밖이라 안 붙인다」가 생기지 않는다.
> 🔴 **SPOT**: `_stage-tokens.json` 의 `spots` 가 `null` 인 시트는 **시트명만** 붙인다. 값이 있는 시트도
> **A/B/C/D 는 카메라가 정하므로 경로표(§4)에서 정한다** — §3 에서 비워 둔 건 빈칸이 아니라 경로표 몫이다.
> 🔴 **일부 이름은 권을 알아야 정해진다**(`byBook`). 앞 쪽을 읽어야 하는 종류이고, 규칙 파일이 그걸 들고 있다.


미나 방식(상태로 묶기)은 여기서 안 듣는다 — 코코의 수식어는 상태가 아니라 **위치**다. 딩딩 방식(좌표)이 맞다.

**골목 = 한 골목망의 네 목. 폭 하나로만 가른다.**

| 목 | 폭 | SCENE |
|---|---|---|
| 가게 앞 골목 | 넓다 (양옆 문 넷) | 「가게 앞 골목」·「골목」 대부분 |
| 골목 가운데·모퉁이 | 중간, 한 번 꺾인다 | 「골목 모퉁이」·「아까 지나간 골목 모퉁이」 |
| 좁아지는 골목 | 좁다 (문 둘) | 「좁아지는 골목」·「골목 구석」 |
| 막다른 골목 | 가장 좁고 **끝이 벽** | 「막다른 골목」·「골목 끝」 |

🔴 **이웃 골목은 `Alley` 가 아니다** — 다른 골목이므로 `GrandmaHouse` 쪽 접근로로 둔다.
🔴 **시장 안 골목도 `Alley` 가 아니다** — `Market` 이다(천막이 있고 자갈 블록이 안 보인다).

🔴 **맨 「골목」의 기본값은 `AlleyShop`(넓은 목)이고, 한 권 안에서 뒤집히는 쪽은 규칙이 못 푼다.**
19권이 그 권이다 — **p4 는 출발해서 나가는 다리**(「이리 꺾이고 저리 꺾이는 골목」 = 꺾이는 목이니
`AlleyMid`)이고, **p9 는 결승선으로 돌아오는 다리**(다음 쪽이 빵집 앞이라 `AlleyShop`)다. 같은 낱말이
같은 권에서 두 자리를 가리키므로 **byBook 으로 못 박을 수 없다** → 붙인 토큰을 `pageWins` 에 등록해
자동 채움이 그 판정을 되돌리지 못하게 했다(15 p9 와 같은 처리).

**반죽대 3갈래 = 위치가 흔들린 것. 하나로 못 박는다** → **창가 반죽대**로 확정(§1.1 `FIXED PARTS`).
창 빛이 반죽에 드는 것이 이 시리즈의 그림이라, 구석에 두면 18권이 성립하지 않는다.
**창가 2갈래** = 「반죽대 옆 창가」·「빵집 창가」 → 둘 다 `Bakery` / SPOT B. 같은 창이다.
