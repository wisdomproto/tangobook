## §0. 🔴 매체 번역 한 줄 — 무대 시트는 사물 목록이 아니라 **블록 목록**이다

앵커: `the world is built from repeated blocks - one block cut once and pressed again and again.
The block is the SAME shape every time` · `Clutter is made by REPEAT, not by variety` ·
🔴 `A THING BEING SEARCHED FOR IS CUT AS ITS OWN BLOCK while everything it hides among is the
repeated one - that is how the eye finds it.`

**그러므로 이 시리즈의 자리 시트가 정하는 것은 「거기에 무엇이 있나」가 아니라 「그 자리의 반복 블록이
무엇이고 몇 번 찍나」다.** 골목 하나 = **아치 블록 1개 × 12번**. 벽 하나 = **점 블록 1개**. 선반 하나 =
**빵 블록 1개 × 9번**.

🔴 **이게 곧 사건이다.** 코코는 「찾기」 권이 많은데(13 노란 천막 · 11 해바라기 · 23 모자), **찾는 것만
제 블록을 갖고 나머지는 전부 그 자리의 반복 블록**이라야 눈이 그걸 찾는다. 자리 시트가 반복 블록을 확정해
놓지 않으면 **배경이 저마다 다르게 그려지고, 그 순간 찾는 것이 안 튀어나온다** — 이 시리즈에서 무대 시트가
없으면 못 그리는 권이 최소 셋이다.

⚠️ 같이 지킬 것 — `Ink coverage is 90 percent, never solid` · `the paper is visibly dented where a
block bit`. 시트에도 **가장자리에 잉크가 몰리고 안쪽으로 종이가 비쳐야** 한다. 꽉 찬 면을 그리면
컷이 그걸 따라간다.

### §1.0 🔴 자리 시트 열 장이 함께 쓰는 마지막 스팟 — 닿는 자리 (2026-09-05 신설)

> 열 장의 SPOT 은 **전부 「자리를 보는 카메라」**다 — 방 전체 · 선반 정면 · 문턱 · 위에서 · 골목 목.
> 그런데 이 시리즈의 앵커는 **`FINISHED THINGS PER PAGE = 2, Coco and the one thing she touches`** 이고,
> 권마다 두세 쪽은 **빵 하나를 사이에 둔 두 얼굴 · 굽힌 큰 몸과 든 작은 얼굴 · 품 안**이며 **대개 그게 착지**다
> (41 p5 빵을 사이에 둔 두 얼굴 · 41 p7 문 앞에서 높이가 같아진 두 얼굴 · 41 p10 품 안 · 37 p5 앞발과 천 사이).
> 🔴 **사물 시트는 이 구멍을 못 메운다** — 사물 시트는 `STATES` 만 들고 **카메라를 안 든다**.
> 🔴 고칠 곳은 그 쪽들이 아니라 **시트마다 한 줄**이다. 아래 문단을 열 장 전부의 **SPOTS 마지막 항목**으로
> 붙였고, 시트마다 다른 것은 **배경 한 줄**뿐이다.

```
ADDED TO EVERY STAGE SHEET IN THIS SERIES - THE LAST SPOT

🔴 WHERE TWO BODIES TOUCH, close, at the height of the touch itself. Two heads over one loaf, a bent
  grown-up's face brought down level with a small one, a paw on a shoulder, arms round a back: the
  frame holds the point of contact and nothing else, and neither body is whole in it.
  🔴 THE TWO BODIES ARE THE PAGE'S TWO FINISHED THINGS - the ration is spent here and on nothing
  else, so no prop in this frame is finished and none is named.
  🔴 THE POINT WHERE THEY TOUCH IS THE ONLY PLACE PRINTED AT FULL BITE, ink heaviest right at that
  edge. Everything around it is the SAME block pulled at a LIGHT BITE, paper showing through, and
  🔴 THE DEEPEST OVERLAP IN THE FRAME IS WHERE THE TWO BODIES OVERLAP - that overprinted seam is what
  says they are together, and it is the only dark.
  🔴 THE BACKGROUND IS ONE FLAT FIELD OF THIS STAGE'S OWN REPEATED BLOCK at a light bite, running off
  every edge, with 0 fixed parts in it; each sheet's spot line below names which block that is.
  🔴 THE PRESS STILL DENTS THE SHEET. This spot is CLOSER, not BIGGER: registration and bite do not
  change because the camera came in.
🔴 AND WHEN ONE OBJECT FILLS THE WHOLE FRAME (a loaf, a button, an egg, a lantern), it keeps the
  block its prop sheet cut for it AND TAKES ONE MARK MORE - one, not a new finish: a scored line on
  a crust, the chip on a claw, the thread through a buttonhole. 🔴 IT DOES NOT BECOME A DIFFERENT
  OBJECT WHEN IT IS BIG, and above all 🔴 A BLOCK IS NOT RECUT AT A LARGER SIZE. Print it once at
  full-frame size and once at hand size on the same panel so the two can be laid over each other.
```

🔴 **상한 문장 주의** — 다른 시리즈 시트에는 `Do not invent a fifth spot` 류가 있어 스팟을 더해 놓고 같은
문장이 그걸 금지한다. **이 시리즈 시트에는 그 문장이 없다**(2026-09-05 전수 확인). 새로 쓸 때도 넣지 말 것.

### §1.1 Bakery — 실제 프롬프트 (가장 먼저)

```
STAGE SHEET - Bakery   (coco-pressalley · SCENE token: Bakery · bake FIRST)

The mouse bakery. Twelve of the twenty-seven named places in this series are inside this one room,
so this drawing decides it once - and above all it decides ITS BLOCKS.

🔴 THE BLOCK LIST - cut each of these ONCE and press it again and again. A block may run off an
  edge but is NEVER redrawn, and clutter is made by repeating a block, never by variety:
  PLANK BLOCK - the floor. One plank shape, pressed in rows, INK1.
  DOT BLOCK - the plaster wall. One small dot, pressed as a field, INK1 at a light bite.
  LOAF BLOCK - the shelf. One loaf shape, at most 9 presses per shelf, INK1.
  JAR BLOCK - the sill and the corner. One jar shape, at most 6 presses, INK2.
  BEAM BLOCK - the ceiling. One beam, at most 4 presses, INK2.
  Nothing on this stage is drawn one at a time except the three FIXED PARTS below.

FIXED PARTS (their own blocks, cut once, not repeated):
  THE OVEN, back wall, INK2 with an OVERLAP mouth. 🔴 THE OVEN GLOW IS BARE PAPER, not printed, and
    it is the brightest thing on the page.
  THE KNEADING TABLE, 🔴 STANDING AT THE WINDOW - one table, one place, fixed for the series (the
    window light falling across the dough is the picture of this book). INK1, at most 4 seam lines.
  THE WINDOW above it, INK2 frame, 🔴 THE LIGHT THROUGH IT IS BARE PAPER.
  Flour dust is bare paper showing through a thinner pull - never a white ink.

🔴 THE SOUGHT-THING RULE lives on this stage: in a volume built around finding one thing, that thing
  is cut AS ITS OWN BLOCK and everything it hides among is the repeated block. Draw a demonstration
  panel: a shelf of nine loaf-block presses with ONE differently cut loaf among them, so the eye
  goes straight to it without any change of colour.

REGISTRATION AND BITE: ink sits heaviest at the edge of every shape and the paper shows through
  inside it at about ten percent. The press dents the sheet. Where INK1 and INK2 overlap, OVERLAP
  appears - that is the only dark, and it is never mixed as a third ink.

SPOTS:
  A THE WHOLE ROOM from the doorway, wide: table at the window left, oven back right, shelves along.
  B THE TABLE, medium, from the side: window above, the light on the tabletop as bare paper.
  C THE TABLETOP, close, high: the surface and what is on it.
  D THE OVEN, medium low: the mouth in OVERLAP, its glow bare paper.

PLATE: A, B, C, D once each, plus 🔴 THE BLOCK SHEET - each of the five blocks printed alone at large
  size so it can be reused unchanged - plus the sought-thing demonstration panel.

NOT: no character of any kind, no accent red anywhere on this sheet (the red plate touches only
  Coco's kerchief), no lettering, numerals, shop signs or price cards, no third mixed ink, no solid
  100-percent area, no white ink anywhere, no airbrush, gradient, glow or soft edge, and no object
  drawn individually where a block exists for it.
```

### §1.2 Alley — 실제 프롬프트 (🔴 네 목이 한 장 · 두 번째로 굽는다)

> 후보 아홉(골목 · 가게 앞 골목 · 골목 끝 · 골목 가운데 · 골목 모퉁이 · 골목 구석 · 막다른 골목 ·
> 좁아지는 골목 · 이웃 골목)이 SCENE 에서 토큰 넷이 됐다. **넷을 한 장에** 굽는다 — 폭이 눈금이라
> 따로 그리면 눈금이 안 선다.

```
STAGE SHEET - Alley   (coco-pressalley · SCENE tokens: AlleyShop / AlleyMid / AlleyNarrow / AlleyDead
                       · bake SECOND, after Bakery)

The back alley the bakery opens onto. It is ONE alley, and the four tokens above are four NECKS of
it, not four places. Everything on this sheet is drawn once and the necks differ by WIDTH ALONE.

🔴 THE BLOCK LIST - cut each ONCE and press it again and again:
  ARCH BLOCK - the cobbles. One arc shape, at most 12 presses across a spread, INK1. This block is
    the ground of the whole series and it must be boring on purpose.
  WINDOW BLOCK - at most 8 down one wall, INK2, and 🔴 NEVER MIRRORED against a neighbour - a
    mirrored press reads as a different building.
  SHUTTER BLOCK - one shutter, INK2, pressed open or pressed shut, never redrawn.
  TILE BLOCK - the roofs, INK2, at most 9 presses per roof line.
FIXED PARTS: the two walls lean slightly toward each other. 🔴 THE STRIP OF SKY BETWEEN THE ROOFS IS
  BARE PAPER and it is the light of the whole page - by day. On a night page it is printed in OVERLAP
  with at most 14 bare-paper stars, and the lanterns carry the page instead.

🔴 THE FOUR NECKS, DRAWN IN A ROW, SAME CAMERA, SAME EYE LEVEL, SAME DEPTH - only the gap between the
  two walls changes. This row IS the sheet:
  1 AlleyShop - WIDEST. Four doors, one of them the bakery's, and room for a queue of seven
    silhouettes to stand along one wall with people still passing. Windows up to 8. This is the
    default neck and most of the series happens here.
  2 AlleyMid - MIDDLE, and it BENDS ONCE, so the far part of it is hidden behind the bend. Windows
    about 5. The bend is what lets a volume say "the corner we passed a while back" and mean a place
    the reader has already seen.
  3 AlleyNarrow - NARROW, two doors, windows about 3, the sky strip pinched to a thin line. An
    umbrella opened here touches both walls; a corner here keeps snow a broom cannot reach.
  4 AlleyDead - NARROWEST, and 🔴 IT IS SHUT: a high blank wall closes it, taller than the frame,
    0 windows, 0 doors on the wall itself, and 🔴 THE SKY STRIP STOPS AT THAT WALL. At most 3 flat
    SLATE silhouettes. This same drawing serves any high blank wall a lane runs into - a cat sitting
    on top of it, a flowerpot in its corner - because it is the same wall.
  🔴 If a reader cannot rank the four by width at thumbnail size, this sheet has failed and the
  volumes that walk from one to another have no geography.

FIXTURES OF THE ALLEY - each drawn once, in its own place, and it is ALWAYS in that place:
  🔴 THE WELL, in AlleyMid at the bend: one round stone kerb, one bucket rope, nothing else. A race
    volume ends up here by mistake, so it has to be a place the reader can be wrong about.
  🔴 FIVE LANTERNS on brackets down the wall, evenly spaced, the same block five times. Draw the row
    ALL LIT, and draw it with the FAR ONE STILL DARK - the four-lit-one-dark row is the clock of a
    whole volume and it cannot be improvised. A lit lantern is bare paper; an unlit one is printed.
  🔴 TWO WASHING LINES across the alley, at most 6 pieces on them, all one block, ALL AT ONE ANGLE.
    The angle is the wind gauge - draw the row leaning left and leaning right side by side.
  🔴 THE BURROW MOUTH in the cobbles: a hole in the ground where two arch blocks are missing, its lip
    slanting down. Draw it from above (a hole in the floor of the alley with a round head coming up
    out of it) and note that the alley's own cobbles run right up to its edge.
  THE LONG FEAST TABLE: one plain trestle table standing DOWN THE MIDDLE of AlleyMid, long enough
    that plates run end to end. Draw it empty, in place - what goes on it is the Dishes sheet.
  THE EAVES: a shallow overhang along both walls, deep enough to stand under out of the rain, and in
    winter 🔴 ITS ICICLES ARE BARE PAPER while the cobbles below stay printed.
  THE ROOF RIDGE above, where a bird sits: drawn once from the alley looking up.

SPOTS: A the bakery front, wide · B the bend with the well · C the narrow throat · D the shut end ·
  🔴 E WHERE TWO BODIES TOUCH - §1.0. Background here: the ARCH BLOCK of the cobbles at a light bite
  if the touch is low, the WINDOW/SHUTTER wall if it is standing height - one field, no fixtures.

PLATE: the four necks in a row FIRST, then each block printed alone at large size, then the fixtures
  each in place, then the sky strip by day beside the sky at night.

NOT: no character of any kind, no accent red anywhere on this sheet, no lettering, numerals, shop
  signs or price cards, no third mixed ink, no solid 100-percent area, no white ink, no puddle
  reflection that mirrors anything, no cobble drawn individually where the arch block exists, and no
  airbrush, gradient, glow or soft edge.
```

### §1.3 AlleyMouth — 🔴 한 권(36) 아홉 쪽이 이 한 줄에 걸려 있다

```
STAGE SHEET - AlleyMouth   (coco-pressalley · SCENE token: AlleyMouth)

Where the alley ends and the main road begins. One volume lives here for nine pages and 🔴 THE WHOLE
BOOK IS ONE LINE ON THE GROUND - so the line is what this sheet is for.
```

### §2.1 Dough — 실제 프롬프트 (🔴 3권 공유 · 높이가 사건)

```
PROP SHEET - Dough   (coco-pressalley · SCENE token: Dough)

The dough in its bowl under a cloth. One whole volume is ten pages of looking at it, so its FOUR
HEIGHTS are the instrument and are fixed here.

FORM: THE BOWL is its own block, INK1, a wide shallow shape with a rim line, ink heaviest at the
  edge, paper showing through inside. THE CLOTH is INK2, a soft square laid over the rim with its
  corners hanging, at most 3 fold marks. THE DOUGH is a plain domed shape, bare PAPER with only its
  edge bitten, so it is the palest thing in the bowl.
🔴 FOUR HEIGHTS, and a reader must be able to tell them apart at a glance. Draw all four in a row,
  same bowl, same distance, same eye level:
  1 FLAT - the dome sits BELOW the rim; the rim line runs unbroken across the picture.
  2 LEVEL - the dome touches the rim; the rim line still unbroken.
  3 OVER - the dome rises past the rim so the rim line is BROKEN on both sides. 🔴 This break is the
    event of the book and nothing else says it.
  4 DOUBLE - the dome twice as tall as the bowl, its edge lipping down over the outside.
STATES ALSO: the cloth ON (a square with the dome's shape faintly raising its middle) and the cloth
  LIFTED (held at one corner, the bowl open).

PLATE: the four heights in a row, plus cloth-on and cloth-lifted, plus one close-up of the rim line
  at height 2 and height 3 side by side.

NOT: no character, no hands, no accent red, no lettering or numerals, no third mixed ink, no solid
  area, no white ink, no steam rising from unbaked dough, no shading, gradient, sheen or glow.
```

### §2.2 Pots — 실제 프롬프트 (🔴 「찾는 것」 블록의 실례)

```
PROP SHEET - Pots   (coco-pressalley · SCENE token: Pots)

Flowerpots on a sill and along a wall. Two books use them, and in one of them a single sunflower is
the thing being searched for - so this sheet has to make BOTH the repeat and the exception.

FORM: 🔴 THE POT BLOCK - one pot shape, cut once, INK1, ink heaviest at the rim and the foot, paper
  showing through the body. Pressed at most 6 times in a row along a sill; a seventh pot is the same

🔴 THE SOUGHT ONE: the sunflower is CUT AS ITS OWN BLOCK - a tall stem, one round head, leaves that

STATES: 1 the row of six identical pots on a sill · 2 the same row with the sought one among them ·

NOT: no character, no hands, no accent red, no lettering or numerals, no third mixed ink, no solid
```

### §2.3 Loaves — 🔴 이 시리즈에서 제일 급한 시트

> 앵커는 `LOAF BLOCK - one loaf shape, at most 9 presses` 로 **빵을 블록 하나**로 못 박았다. 그런데
> 열한 권이 서로 다른 빵을 요구하고, **그 차이가 곧 사건**이다 — 03권은 줄지은 같은 빵 속의 달팽이
> 하나, 08권은 아이보다 긴 빵, 25권은 제일 작은 빵 셋, 38권은 점 난 빵과 갓 구운 빵.
> 🔴 **그러니 이 시트가 정할 것은 「빵을 몇 가지 그리나」가 아니라 「어느 것이 제 블록을 갖나」다.**

```
PROP SHEET - Loaves   (coco-pressalley · SCENE token: Loaves)

The bread. Eleven books turn on it, and in every one of them the event is the SAME: a row of
identical loaves with ONE loaf that is not identical. This sheet settles which is which.

🔴 THE PLAIN LOAF BLOCK - cut ONCE, INK1, ink heaviest at the crust edge, paper showing through the
  body at about ten percent. It is pressed again and again along a shelf, at most 9 presses per
  board. Every press is the SAME shape; never redraw it, never mirror it, never vary it. This block
  is the background of the whole series and it must be boring on purpose.
🔴 SIX LOAVES GET THEIR OWN BLOCK, and only these six. Each is cut once, at large size, and each
  differs from the plain block in ONE readable way - so that it can be found among nine presses of
  the plain block without any change of colour:
  1 THE SNAIL - one flat spiral of exactly 2 turns. Volume 03: it sits in a row of plain loaves.
  2 THE LONG LOAF - 🔴 LONGER THAN COCO IS TALL BY A HEAD. Draw it beside a plain child silhouette
    at the same depth to fix that; volume 08 is built on it and nothing else says it.
  3 THE CRESCENT - a plain curve with two blunt ends. This is the everyday one from volume 28 on.
  4 THE STAR - a flat five-point cutter shape, drawn small; seven of them on one tray.
  5 COCO'S OWN LOAF - 🔴 SMALLEST OF ALL, the size of her own fist. Three of them stand in one shelf
    slot among the big ones in volume 25, and being small is the point.
  6 THE SPOILED CRESCENT - the same block as 3 with COLD-INK SPOTS on the crust, at most 9 presses
    of one tiny dot block. 🔴 Draw it beside a fresh crescent at the same size; the ONLY difference
    is the spots, and volume 38 depends on that being true.
🔴 A TOWER OF LOAVES IS NOT A SHELF, AND THE CAP OF NINE DOES NOT REACH IT. The anchor caps the
  plain block at nine presses PER SHELF BOARD; one volume stacks the same block into a free-standing
  column on the shop floor, twice, and 🔴 THE HEIGHT IS THAT VOLUME'S WHOLE EVENT - the grown-up who
  knocks it over builds it back HIGHER. A column of identical discs gets taller by COUNT and by
  nothing else, so the count is the ruler and it is settled here:
  TOWER ONE - NINE presses. It stands exactly as tall as the child: with both arms straight up she
    just reaches the top one.
  TOWER TWO - TEN presses, one press higher, and she cannot reach it. 🔴 THE TALLEST GROWN-UP PUTS
    THE TOP ONE ON with his arm straight up while she watches from under it. Same block, same base,
    same camera as tower one.
  🔴 Draw the two side by side at one scale with the same child silhouette beside both. Do not get
  the second tower's height by widening the base, by standing it on a sack, by adding a second column
  or by drawing the loaves bigger - 🔴 nine against ten of the SAME disc is the entire difference, and
  a child counts it. (Judgment, 2026-09-05: the script said "nine" on both pages and "one notch
  higher" on the second, which cannot both be drawn. The cap was a shelf cap, the height was the
  event, so the count moved. SCENE 41 p8 now reads ten.)
🔴 SUGARED AND SEEDED are surface, not shape: sugar is at most 11 tiny bare-paper flecks on a plain
  loaf, seeds are at most 9 tiny INK2 dots. Neither gets a block of its own.
🔴 A HALVED LOAF: cut straight across, the two halves plainly EQUAL, and 🔴 THE CUT FACE IS BARE
  PAPER - it is the only part of any loaf that is not printed, and that is how the inside reads.
🔴 CRUMBS: at most 9 separate dots. THREE SIZES, drawn in a row - a plate of crumbs for a kitten, a
  scatter on an empty dish, and 🔴 ONE CRUMB SIZED TO A BABY BIRD'S BEAK, which must be plainly
  smaller than a beak that is itself plainly smaller than a whole loaf. Volume 49 is that ladder.
STEAM: at most 5 curls, each drawn on its own, bare paper, rising and stopping. Never a cloud.

PLATE: the plain block printed alone at large size FIRST, then a shelf of 9 presses of it with ONE
  of the six set among them, then the six own-blocks in a row at one scale, then the halved loaf,
  then the three crumb sizes.

NOT: no character face (silhouettes only), 🔴 no accent red anywhere, no lettering, numerals, price
  cards or maker's marks, no third mixed ink, no solid 100-percent area, no white ink, no glaze,
  sheen or highlight on a crust, no loaf drawn individually where the plain block exists for it, no
  gradient or soft edge.
```

### §5.2 🔴 `LOAF BLOCK` 이 하나인데 빵은 여섯 가지다 — 앵커가 사물 때문에 깨지는 자리

앵커: `LOAF BLOCK ... loaves on a shelf = one loaf block at most 9 presses`. 이 한 줄만 지키면
**03(달팽이) · 08(긴 빵) · 14(별) · 25(제일 작은 빵) · 38(점 난 빵) · 47(마지막 하나)** 여섯 권이
전부 못 그려진다 — 그 여섯 권의 사건이 「같은 빵 줄 속의 **다른 빵 하나**」이기 때문이다.

🔴 앵커에 조항을 하나 더 둔다:

spotted crescent). Everything else on a shelf is the plain loaf block repeated. A loaf that a
volume is ABOUT gets its own block; a loaf that is scenery never does.`
이게 없으면 규칙이 옳게 지켜질수록 책이 못 그려진다. (도도 21권이 `no cast shadow` 때문에 통째로
막힌 것과 같은 종류다 — **금지 조항이 아니라 「하나만」 조항이 범인**이라는 것만 다르다.)

### §5.7 🔴 빨강 규칙은 맞지만 **문장이 하나 모자란다**

관통 줄 `RED: the red plate is printed last and touches nothing but Coco's kerchief` 는 옳다.
그런데 **코코가 늘 그걸 쓰고 있다는 뜻으로 읽힌다.** 30권은 벗어서 못에 걸고 여섯 쪽을 맨 머리로

on a nail**`. 그래야 화가가 「빨강이 없네, 빼먹었나」 하고 도로 씌우지 않는다.
