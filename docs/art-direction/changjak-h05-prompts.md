## h05 §1. 앵커 배정

**권**: h05 풍차는 무엇을 하나 · 관찰과 성장 · 네덜란드 풍차 맷돌방 한 칸 · 14쪽
**클러스터**: **C7** · **앵커 슬러그**: `changjak-onethick` (신규 민팅)
**한 줄**: 회색 돌가루 필드는 얇게 한 겹이고, 두껍게 쌓아 올린 것은 맷돌 하나뿐이다(국소 임파스토). 열네 쪽 내내 같은 구석에 있고 안 움직인다.

**🔴 형제 권과 갈린 축 — 첫 렌더에서 세어진다**
**두꺼운 것이 몇 개이고 움직이나.** a02(C6)는 앞발 세 겹이 두껍고 그 자국이 화면을 **옮겨 다닌다** / d03(C7)은 버릴 물건 셋이 두껍고 **떨어져 없어진다**(두께 = 무게) / **h05 는 두꺼운 것이 하나이고 붙박이이며, 사건은 p12 에 손바닥이 그 표면을 짚는 것**이다(두께 = 만져지는 것).
- **a08 을 피한다** — 밀가루를 「화면의 흰 것 전부」로 만들면 그 책이다. h05 의 흰 것 주인은 가루가 아니라 **햇빛 네모**이고, 가루는 홈통 끝과 자루 안에만 있다.
- **f10 과 갈림**(둘 다 바닥의 노란 사각형) — f10 은 **개수**가 변하고 h05 는 **크기가 안 변한다**. 🔴 **h05 의 사각형을 쪼개지 마라.**
- **d07(같은 네덜란드·C7)과 갈림** — 저쪽은 투명 수채의 겹수가 값이고 야외 물길이다. h05 는 실내이고 값은 **두께**다.

## §2. STYLE ANCHOR

```
STYLE ANCHOR - changjak-onethick

Style: picture book, ages 5-7. One room - the stone floor of a Dutch windmill - for all fourteen spreads. Oil paint on a warm grey primed board.

RENDERING (finish hierarchy)
The whole room is laid in ONE thin pass: board grain shows through, no glazing, no blending, no second coat. EXACTLY ONE thing in this book is built up in thick paste - the millstone (knife-loaded impasto, standing ridges that catch raking light). It is the only object with physical relief. It stays in the same corner of the room on every spread and never travels; from p12 its upper stone stands tipped on edge in that same corner, face toward camera. Sacks, chute, beams, floor, flour and both animals are thin.
FINISHED THINGS PER PAGE = 2 - the hedgehog plus the one thing it touches on that page. Everything else stays raw paint.
DENSITY RATION = p8 only (3 finished things there). Every other page = 2.
Hard caps: floor seams at most 7 - ceiling beams at most 3 - dust motes at most 12 and only inside the door light - grains at the chute lip at most 5 - spare sacks at most 2 - tools on the wall = 0 - windows = 0, light enters by the open door only.
Flour appears at the chute mouth and inside the sack, nowhere else. The white of this book belongs to the sunlight, not to the flour.

PALETTE
#8E8C86 stone grey - #BAB8B0 the paler worn grey of the polished patch - #EFEBE0 flour white - #E8C468 door sunlight. Wood is the board's own warm grey-brown, kept low. One accent in the book: the yellow rectangle of sun on the floor. No green, no blue, no red.

CHARACTER DESIGN LANGUAGE
Two animals, upright on two legs, animal proportions kept - head 1/4 of body for the child, 1/5 for the adult. Faces are painted, not outlined. Eyes are round with a dark iris and one small highlight, never flat dots. Mouths open wide when shouting. One garment in the whole book: the badger's flour-dusted apron. No other clothing.

CANVAS
16:9 double-page spread, full bleed. The image runs to all four edges - no caption band, no border, no vignette.

NOT (rendering only)
1. no airbrush, no smooth gradients, no glossy 3D, no photographic finish
2. no thick paint anywhere except the millstone - every other surface is one thin pass
3. no machine diagram: no gears, shafts, cogs, cutaways, arrows or wind marks
4. no letters, numbers or signage on sacks, wood or walls
```

```
CHARACTER SHEET - Hedgehog child   (bake this FIRST)

FACE: round muzzle, small black nose, round eyes with dark iris and one highlight (never dots). Cheeks full. Mouth opens wide and square when shouting.
QUILLS: short blunt quills over the back and crown, painted as at most 20 flat strokes of two greys (#8E8C86 over #BAB8B0) - never individual spines, never a fibre or wool edge.

BUILD: head 1/4 of the standing body, shoulder height about two thirds of the sack at p8. No clothes at all.
REFERENCE SHEET: full-figure front idle - 3/4 turn - back view - close-ups: (a) mouth wide open shouting upward, (b) one forepaw flat and spread, palm to camera, (c) eyes round with mouth slightly open, ears up.
NOT: no clothing, no accessories, no letters or numbers anywhere on the sheet.
```
