## C-37 §1. 앵커 배정

**권**: `who-emptied-the-pool` (c37 · 10쪽 · 4~6세 · 주제군 **C 자연·계절·동물** · 엔진 **오해와 반전** · 무대 콘월 조수웅덩이 · 주인공 어린 게)

**한 줄**: 회청-은빛 냉단색조 워시 필드 + 유일한 빨강 1점(늙은 불가사리) + **물감의 젖음/마름이 조수를 따라간다.** 앵커 슬러그 `changjak-tidepool` — **신규 민팅**.

**이 권이 그림에 요구하는 것(판정의 전제)** 세 가지다. ① **값(value)이 서사다.** 물이 빠졌다(밝음→창백) 돌아온다(창백→밝음). 이 책에서 그림이 해야 할 가장 중요한 일은 「물이 나갔다가 왔다」를 명도·채도의 흔들림만으로 읽히게 하는 것이다. ② **독자가 게보다 먼저 안다.** p2 배경에 물러난 바다가 밝은 은빛 선으로 심기고 p6 에서 그것이 화면 절반으로 회수된다 — 심음과 회수가 **같은 화면 요소의 크기 변화**라서, 그 요소가 매 쪽 같은 물성으로 그려져야 대구가 성립한다. ③ **악센트 1점이 발견 비트다.** 회청·은빛 세상에서 딱 하나의 빨강(늙은 불가사리)이라 p6 에서 시선이 거기 먼저 닿고 곧 멀어진 바다로 흐른다. 대본가가 이미 심어 뒀다(§2.9).

**후보 3.** ① **C6 단색조+악센트 1 · 해양 잉크/과슈 워시(신규 앵커)** — 젖은 워시와 마른 붓이 조수 상태를 그대로 진다. 매체가 곧 서사라 내용-형식 필연성이 최고점이다(§2.3). 리스크는 냉단색조 10쪽이 4~6세에 밋밋해질 수 있다는 것 → p6 한 장에 밀도·빨강·시원한 개방을 몰아 배급해 리듬을 만든다(§2.10). ② **C8 수채 번짐** — 물이 주제라 가장 유혹적이다. 탈락: wet-in-wet 이 화면 전체를 지배하면 **마른 쪽을 그릴 매체가 없다**(조수 대비가 사라진다). 게다가 게가 10쪽에 걸쳐 오해→분개→기다림→환희를 연기해야 하는데 번짐은 인물을 뭉갠다. ③ **C7 회화적 톤**(§7.3 주제군 C 1순위) — 탈락: 예쁜 바닷가 수채를 주지만 **색을 화면 전체에 퍼뜨려 값 규율을 안 세운다**. 물이 빠진 쪽과 찬 쪽이 둘 다 「예쁜 바다 그림」이 되면 이 책의 유일한 사건이 화면에서 사라진다(§7.5 교차관찰 1 — C7 이 가장 평범해지기 쉽다).

**🔴 추천 = 후보 ①.** 주제군 C의 기준선 1순위는 C7 이지만 **엔진이 기준선을 이긴다**(§2.11, A-11 이 주제군 A에서 C3 을 택한 것과 같은 논리). 주제군은 정서를 정하고 엔진은 **그림의 과업**을 정한다. 이 권의 과업은 「값으로 조수를 그리기 + 유일 악센트로 발견 찍기」이고 그건 C6 의 정의다.

**왜 새 앵커인가.** 기존 창작동화 C6 앵커는 A-01(냉회록 안개, 석판 크레용·오일 파스텔)과 A-04(흑연 + 앰버 1점)이고 **둘 다 마른 왁스·흑연 매체**다. 마른 크레용으로 물을 그리면 물이 안 된다. §7.1 이 「앵커 = 팔레트·매체 변주, 앵커당 6~10권」이라 했으므로 **매체가 근본적으로 다르면(마른 왁스 ↔ 젖은 워시) 새 앵커**다. 팔레트도 갈린다(냉회록+주홍 / 회백+앰버 / **해양 은청+올리브+브릭레드**).

**🔴 라인 충돌 확인 (§7.2 분리 규칙).**

| 대상 | 겹치나 | 왜 |
|---|---|---|

## C-37 §2. STYLE ANCHOR

```
STYLE ANCHOR - changjak-tidepool   (a young crab / a Cornish rock pool)

Style: ink and gouache WASH on cool grey-toned toothed paper #E4E5DF, laid with a soft round brush,
  4-6 year old picture book. Shapes are made by a wash running out or drying, never by a clean line.

RENDERING (finish hierarchy): 🔴 THIS VOLUME: THE WETNESS OF THE PAINT TRACKS THE TIDE.
  WATER-PRESENT pages are worked WET-IN-WET - colour bleeds into damp paper, edges melt, washes
  pool and granulate as they dry. WATER-GONE pages are worked DRY-BRUSH - the brush skips over the
  tooth, colour sits thin and grainy, bare paper shows through the middle of every stroke, edges
  scratch and break off; the page looks parched.
  FINISHED THINGS PER PAGE = 2 - the crab and the ONE thing it touches or speaks to. Seaweed =
  4 wash strokes a clump, 0 strands drawn. Ripples = 3-4 darker strokes on the water, 0 elsewhere.
  Barnacle colony = at most 9 grey dots and half-rings, never drawn one by one. Mudflat and open
  sand = 2 brush passes and bare paper, 0 pebbles, 0 shells, 0 ripples, 0 footprints. Bubbles and
  spray = tiny circles of BARE PAPER painted round, never white dots.
  DENSITY RATION = page 6 only, where it rises to 3, spent on the two characters - NEVER the flat.

PALETTE: paper #E4E5DF · wet rock #6B7A80 · crevice #45535A · dry mudflat #C7C9C2 · water #4E8C8A ·
  bright shallow #7FB6AE · seaweed olive #5B7A4E · starfish red #C43A2B. 🔴 EXACTLY ONE warm
  saturated colour exists, a brick-red, and it belongs to the OLD STARFISH on ONE page only; on the
  other nine there is no red, orange, pink or warm accent anywhere - not on the crab, the weed, the
  sky or a pebble. 🔴 THE NARRATIVE COLOUR IS VALUE, NOT HUE: water-present = brighter,
  more saturated blue-green with deeper darks; water-gone = pale, chalky, desaturated silver-grey
  with the contrast squeezed flat.

CHARACTER DESIGN LANGUAGE: no face on the shell. Two EYE-STALKS rise from the carapace, each ending
  in a small dark oval, and 🔴 THE ANGLE OF THE TWO STALKS IS THE ENTIRE EXPRESSION SYSTEM - not
  dot-eyes on a face, no blush, no catchlight. The stalks read apart from the shell in VALUE at
  thumbnail size. FIXED GRADE: every creature stays an animal - the crab walks sideways on eight
  legs, wears and carries nothing, and its claws only point, droop and wave.

CANVAS: 16:9 double-page spread. A Cornish rocky shore tide pool - blue-grey rock shelves,
  barnacles and periwinkles, green weed, wet sand, a wide low mudflat and the distant sea. British
  Atlantic, cool northern light. Vast negative space first, subject second; the crab is about 1/7 of
  page height on the wide pages and comes forward only on the close pages. Diagonals lead the eye,
  never dead symmetry. No lettering or numerals anywhere.

NOT: no digital slickness of any kind - airbrush, gradient, glow, 3D CG, cel-shading, photographic,
  or a paper-texture filter over flat colour / no glassy mirror reflection, wet-plastic sheen on
  rock, metallic mudflat, rendered caustics or sparkle / no fully rendered background, and no hazy
  or blurred one either / not felt, stitched fabric or sculpted clay.
```

**🔴 매 컷 확인하는 세 줄** — 컷마다 `TIDE:` `SEA:` `RED:` 를 먼저 읽는다. 이 책의 반전 장치는 **같은 은빛 바다가 화면에서 커지는 것** 하나뿐이다.

## C-37 §3. 캐릭터 시트 (🔴 이것부터 굽는다)

```
CHARACTER SHEET - LittleCrab   (bake FIRST, before any scene)

Painted in the same medium as the book: ink and gouache wash on cool grey-toned toothed paper
#E4E5DF with a soft round brush. Brush grain visible on the shell, contour left open in places,
a darker wash pooled along the shell edge where the paint dried. Do not render smoothly just
because there is no background. There is NO red anywhere on this sheet.

FACE: there is no face on the shell. Two EYE-STALKS rise from the top of the carapace, each
  ending in a small dark oval #2B2320. THE STALK ANGLE IS THE ACTING:
    eager = both tall and tipped forward · indignant = pulled in close and pushed forward like a
    jab · puzzled = splayed, each aimed somewhere different · waiting = level, still, both aimed
    at the horizon · joyful = curved outward and lifted.
  A tiny mouth line under the front of the shell, with bubbles of BARE PAPER when happy. No
  dot-eye on the shell, no blush, no catchlight.
BODY: cool shore-crab grey-green carapace #6F7A66, slightly warmer underside #8A8B72, darker
  joints #4E5548. Round low shell, wider than tall, ONE flat wash with the darker wash pooled
  along the rim - matte, no shine, no rendered chitin texture.
CLAWS & LEGS: eight thin legs, one or two brush strokes each, and two front claws deliberately a
  little oversized and expressive - they point, droop and wave. Not a jointed anatomical study.
BUILD: a small young crab. Silhouette = round shell + two raised claws + two eye-stalks, readable
  at thumbnail size and instantly distinct from a snail, a shrimp and a starfish.
SHEET: full body from slightly above (the book's main angle) / three-quarter walking sideways,
  legs mid-step / front view with both claws raised / back view sitting still, both stalks aimed
  away at a horizon; plus four stalk close-ups - eager, indignant (one claw pointing), doubtful
  waiting (stalks level, claws folded), joyful (stalks curved, claw raised, bubbles at the mouth).
  Plain cool grey paper, no scenery, no red.
SCENE token: LittleCrab.
```

```
CHARACTER SHEET - ShoreCast   (bake SECOND, attach as @image2)

Same medium - ink and gouache wash on cool grey toothed paper, brush grain visible, contours left
open. Each appears on exactly ONE page, so each must be recognisable from silhouette alone.

PERIWINKLE (p4): a small pointed grey-blue spiral snail clamped flat to the rock, only the lip of

SCENE tokens: Periwinkle, Shrimp, OldStarfish. Never write "starfish" alone.

### p1 — 웅덩이가 세상의 전부다 (젖은 판 ref)

```
TIDE: WET. Wet-in-wet throughout - soft bleeding edges, washes pooling and granulating as they
  dried. This and the last page are the two wettest in the book.
CAMERA: medium, child's eye level tipped slightly down so we look INTO a round pool in the middle
  of the frame.
SUBJECT: LittleCrab submerged in the middle, both claws thrown wide above it, legs kicking
  loosely, both eye-stalks tall and tipped up in delight, a trail of bubbles reserved as BARE
  PAPER rising from its mouth.
SETTING: wet blue-grey rock forms the whole rim; 4 olive seaweed strokes sway inside the water
  with their tips bleeding into the wash; a few broken light shapes on the surface left as paper.
SEA: NOT VISIBLE. The world of this page is this one pool; past the rim the wash softens off
  before it reaches the frame edge.
FINISH: 2 (crab + the rock rim it plays against). Past the rim, 2 brush passes and nothing else -
  0 pebbles, 0 barnacles, 0 far scenery.
RED: none. The brightest, most saturated blue-green in the book; the pool reads as a lit island.
```

### p2 — 물이 없다 · 은빛 선을 독자만 본다 🔴 심음

```
TIDE: DRY - the first parched page. Dry-brush over the whole surface, colour skipping on the
  tooth, bare paper showing through the middle of the strokes.
CAMERA: medium wide, eye level, deep space. The empty pool sits low in the near foreground and
  the frame runs back over a huge flat mudflat to a high horizon.
SUBJECT: LittleCrab stands in the dry bowl at lower centre, head down, both eye-stalks splayed at
  different angles studying the ground, one claw poking a crack in the bed. It never looks up and
  its whole body faces DOWN and AWAY from the horizon.
SETTING: the pool bed cracked into dry plates, one shallow smear of leftover water, the seaweed
  collapsed flat and dulled.
SEA: THE PLANT, AND THE POINT OF THE PAGE. High in the frame, far behind the crab, the withdrawn
  sea is ONE narrow bright silver line with a vast pale mudflat between. Clearly visible to the
  reader and clearly outside the crab's attention. Do not enlarge it, do not draw waves in it,
  do not put the stalks anywhere near it.
FINISH: 2 (crab + the cracked bed under its feet). The mudflat is bare paper with 2 flat dry
  passes: 0 pebbles, 0 ripples, 0 shells.
RED: none. Chalky, flat, drained - the page should look thirsty.
```

### p3 — 범인을 찾아내겠다

```
TIDE: DRY. Dry-brush, the grain scratching hardest along the rock edges.
CAMERA: medium close-up, low angle from ground level so the small crab towers in the frame.
SUBJECT: LittleCrab centre with both claws thrown up above its shell and spread wide, both
  eye-stalks pulled in close and shoved forward - the indignant angle. Eight legs braced
  mid-stride to one side, body tilted, about to scuttle sideways.
SETTING: the cracked rim of the empty pool, one rock ledge behind it, a short row of small
  sideways prints already pressed into the damp sand, one strand of dulled seaweed.
SEA: out of frame. Nothing bright anywhere - this is a page about being sure.
FINISH: 2 (crab + the rim it stands on). The ledge and the ground beyond are open dry wash
  strokes only; everything behind the crab is held down and quiet.
RED: none.
```

### p4 — 붙박이 총알고둥 (@image2)

```
TIDE: DRY. Dry-brush; the rock face is all grain and skipped paper.
CAMERA: close-up, eye level, the two characters facing each other across the frame. Even flat
  daylight, both kept the same brightness so the reader compares postures, not colours.
SUBJECT: right, LittleCrab leans in with one claw pointing straight at the snail and both stalks

SETTING: a wet-dulled blue-grey rock wall carrying at most 9 grey dots and half-rings for the
  whole barnacle-and-periwinkle colony - never drawn one by one - and a dry water stain running

SEA: out of frame.
FINISH: 2 (crab + Periwinkle, with the patch of rock they stand on and the stain at one pass).
  The rest of the wall, the crevice and the ground are open wash.
RED: none.
```

### p5 — 손바닥만 한 물 (@image2)

### p6 — 바위 꼭대기 · 바다가 화면 절반 🔴 회수 + 밀도 배급 (마른 판 ref, @image2)

```
