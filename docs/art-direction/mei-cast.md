## Mei

- 아기 염소 — 흰 몸, 짧은 뿔 두 개, 늘어진 귀. 다섯 중 제일 작다. 겁이 많아 어깨가 자주 움츠러들어 있다. 🔴 주황 목도리 하나.

## Rudi

- 다람쥐 — 등 뒤로 말려 올라간 큼직한 꼬리 하나가 실루엣을 만든다. 앞치마를 둘렀고 🔴 앞치마 주머니가 주황.

```
🔴 THE TAIL IS WIDER THAN HIS BODY AND IT IS THE ACTING. It is a FIELD with one outline, CROSS-HATCH,
its strokes running ALONG its length; it is never cropped, never behind another figure and never
smaller than his own head. STATES, one row, one size:
  1 UP AND FULL - curled over the back, at its widest, the whole silhouette twice his body.
  2 CAUGHT - pressed narrow where it meets a doorway or a gap, the outline dented by the thing that
    holds it, and 🔴 the dent is on the thing's line, not smoothed away.
  3 WRAPPED - brought round the front and closed over his own body, so he is one rounded mass with
    the tail's outline on the outside and no arms showing.
  4 LAID DOWN - stretched flat along the ground behind him, at its full length, still one field.
  5 SWUNG WIDE - thrown to one side in a long curve that leaves the frame.
🔴 STATES 3 AND 4 ARE HOW VOLUME 37 IS READ: the same tail as a wall round himself, then as a floor
under somebody else. Draw them side by side.
```

## Ppino

- 토끼 — 곧게 선 긴 귀 두 개. 아이들 중 제일 키가 크다. 늘 앞으로 기울어진 자세. 🔴 주황 장화.

```
🔴 HIS TWO EARS ARE AS TALL AGAIN AS HE IS AND THEY ARE COUNTED, NOT DESCRIBED. Volume 41 asks one
question ten times - HOW MANY EARS ARE OUT OF THE HAT - so the answer must be readable at a glance
from the silhouette alone: 0, 1 or 2, and never "sort of".
STATES, one row, one size:
  1 TWO OUT, STRAIGHT UP - both clear of the hat, parallel, the tallest thing in the frame.
  2 ONE OUT - one straight up, the other gone, and 🔴 THE ONE THAT IS GONE IS NOT INVISIBLE: the side
    of the hat BULGES IN THE SHAPE OF THAT EAR, one outline pushed out where it is folded inside.
    This medium never shows an inside, so an outline is how an inside is told.
  3 NONE OUT - the hat's two sides bulging, and his head reads as a plain round mass. He then leans,
    because a pressed ear tips him, and the lean is why the others get ahead of him.
  4 CREASED - out again but bent at the fold it was pressed into, the crease staying in the outline.
  5 THE HAT ALONE, HELD OPEN - the two EAR HOLES cut in it, facing the reader, drawn at the same
    height and the same width apart as his two ears in state 1. 🔴 That pairing is the ruler of the
    volume: holes and ears in one frame, at one depth, at one size.
```

## Soso

- 고슴도치 — 짧은 가시가 덮인 낮고 둥근 등. 손을 앞으로 모으고 있다. 🔴 머리에 주황 리본.

```
🔴 HIS BACK AND HIS BELLY ARE TWO DIFFERENT SURFACES, AND THE WHOLE OF HIS TWO BOOKS IS THAT
DIFFERENCE. THE BACK is a FIELD with THINGS STANDING ON IT: short EARTH spines drawn one stroke
each, each with its own outline, standing off the back edge so the silhouette is toothed. THE BELLY
is a FIELD AND NOTHING ELSE: one bed of short strokes all lying ONE WAY, no spine, no outline
inside it, and it is the softest surface any character in this series has.
STATES OF THE BACK, drawn in one row at one size:
  1 UP - every spine standing clear, the toothed edge at its widest.
  2 HALF - the spines leaning back at one angle, the edge shallower.
  3 DOWN - all of them lying one way, flat against the back, the edge nearly smooth; the back then
    reads as a field like the belly, and that is the point.
  🔴 4 SPLIT BY A HAND - a hand laid on the back, spines LYING where the hand has passed and STILL
    STANDING where it has not, with the hand itself as the line between the two states. 🔴 TWO
    VOLUMES DRAW THIS SAME PICTURE (28 and 48) AND IT IS ONE PICTURE, not two - the hand may be a
    child's or a grown-up's and nothing else about it changes.
  5 CLOGGED - dried earth caught at the base of the spines: CROSS-HATCH filling the angle where
    each spine meets the back, at most 6 of them, so those spines CANNOT reach state 3. That is
    volume 33's whole ruler; it goes clogged, pulled from the root, beaded, and clean.
🔴 A SPINE IS NEVER DRAWN LONGER THAN HIS OWN HEAD and the back never becomes a ball of quills.
```

## Leo

- 여우 — 뾰족한 주둥이와 길게 뻗은 꼬리 하나. 턱을 든 자세. 🔴 주황 목깃.

```
🔴 HIS TAIL IS AS LONG AS HE IS TALL AND IT IS STRAIGHT, NOT BUSHY - one narrow EARTH shape with a
single outline, the same width its whole length. 🔴 IT IS NOT RUDI'S TAIL AND THE TWO ARE NEVER THE
SAME SHAPE IN ONE FRAME: Rudi's is wider than his body, Leo's is narrower than his own arm.
STATES, one row, one size:
  1 STRAIGHT OUT AND LEVEL - carried behind him, the default, its line continuing the line of his back.
  2 DOWN - hanging, the tip touching the ground.
  3 SWUNG WIDE - one long curve to one side, the tip past the edge of his own body.
  4 CURLED ROUND A SPACE - brought forward and closed into a ring on the ground, MAKING A PLACE for
    somebody smaller to sit inside. 🔴 The ring is closed and the space inside it is bare PAPER; that
    empty middle is what volume 39 is about, so nothing is drawn in it until somebody sits there.
  5 HELD - taken in another's hands and pulled straight, the outline TAUT: no curve anywhere along it.
```
