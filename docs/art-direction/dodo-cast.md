## 🔴 렌더 실물을 먼저 봤다 — 진단이 타로·미나와 다르다

`comic-assets/dodo-plan` 에 네 시트가 이미 구워져 있다. 넷을 나란히 놓으면:

| | 실물 | 판정 |
|---|---|---|
| **엄마** | 목이 몸통만큼 길게 **위로 곧게 서고**, 몸은 좁고 세로로 높고 다리가 길다 | ✅ 혼자 있어도 읽힌다 |
| **아빠** | **목이 안 보이고**(머리가 어깨에 바로 얹혔다) 몸이 낮고 넓다, 챙이 어깨보다 넓다 | ✅ 혼자 있어도 읽힌다 |
| **도도 · 무무** | 🔴 **같은 그림이다.** 몸·날개·배 쐐기·다리·머리가 전부 같고 **파란 천만 다르다** | ❌ |

🔴 **그래서 이 시리즈에서 갈 데는 아이 둘이다.** 「넷이 다 오리라 넷 다 갈라야 한다」는 브리프의
전제는 실물과 다르다 — 어른 둘은 이미 갈려 있었다.

🔴 **다만 엄마의 긴 목은 규격에 한 줄도 없다.** 앵커가 엄마에게 준 것은 「도도보다 머리 하나 큼 ·
크림빛 앞치마 · 날개에 밀가루」뿐이고, 목은 **모델이 알아서 뽑은 것**이라 다시 뽑으면 사라진다
(미나 엄마의 발끝까지 오는 옷과 같은 종류의 우연이다). 아빠의 「넓음」만 앵커에 있다.
→ **된 것을 규격으로 못 박고, 아이 둘에게 같은 등급의 축을 준다.**

### 가르는 축 = **목과 몸통의 비례** — 앵커의 축 안이다

이 그림체의 캐릭터 축은 **「해부는 정확한데 자세가 코믹」**이다. 그래서 개체를 가르는 것도
**실제 오리가 실제로 다른 자리**여야 한다 — 마당의 오리는 한 모양이 아니고, 목을 세우고 선 오리와
몸이 낮고 넓은 오리는 **둘 다 정확한 오리**다. 새 축(털색·안경·옷)을 발명하지 않았다.

| | 목 | 몸통·다리 | 검게 칠하면 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **무무** 막내 | **쉴 때 목이 없다** — 머리가 몸에 바로 얹힌다. 볼 때만 제 몸 길이만큼 가는 줄기로 나온다 | 다리가 제일 짧아 배 밑에 종이가 거의 없다 · 머리가 키의 1/3 | **조약돌** — 큰 머리 얹힌 달걀, 거기서 가는 목 하나만 나간다 |
| **도도** 누나 | 늘 나와 있고 **뒤로 젖은 얕은 S**(턱이 들려 머리가 가슴 뒤에 앉는다) | 다리가 길어 배 밑에 종이 띠가 뚜렷 · 날개가 넷 중 몸에 비해 제일 길다 | **한쪽으로 기운 십자** — 긴 다리 위 몸에서 한 날개가 밖으로 |
| **엄마** | **넷 중 제일 길고 위로 곧게 선다.** 일할 땐 그 목이 통째로 아래로 굽는다 | 몸통이 좁고 세로로 높다 · 다리 사이 틈이 제일 크다 | **국자** — 세로 막대 위에 얹힌 긴 목, 앞에 맨 종이 한 장 |
| **아빠** | **없다** — 넷 중 목이 안 보이는 유일한 인물 | 몸통이 **높이보다 폭이 크다** · 다리가 짧고 벌어졌다 | **버섯** — 넓고 낮은 덩어리 위에 가로 막대(챙) 하나 |

🔴 **어른과 아이가 크기로 갈리지 않는다** — 엄마는 목이 제일 길고, 아빠는 목이 없다.
큰 아이를 그리면 둘 중 어느 쪽도 안 된다.

### SCENE 250쪽이 이미 이 축으로 그리고 있었다 (한 글자도 안 고쳤다)

인물 지문을 인물별로 갈라 세었다.

- **무무 = 목이 그의 연기다.** `목을/목이` **42쪽**(빼고·젖히고·숙이고·길게 뻗고). 그리고 `두 날개` 74 vs `한 날개` 14 — **두 날개를 몸에 붙여 대칭**으로 쓴다.
- **도도 = 턱과 한쪽 날개다.** `턱` **28** · `한 날개` **43** · `뻗` 28 · `눈이 반쯤 감겼다` 12. 넷 중 **비대칭인 유일한 인물**.
- **엄마 = 굽는다.** 19쪽 중 `굽혀/숙이` **7**(목을 숙이고 · 허리를 굽혀 · 몸을 굽혀).
- **아빠 = 받친다.** 13쪽 전부가 누르고 받치고 잡고 번쩍 든다, `기울` 3. 목 지문 **0**.
- 🔴 **11권 p1 이 날개 크기를 못 박아 뒀다** — 「아빠의 두 날개가 셋 중 가장 넓고 그 아래 도도의 펼친 날개가 무무 것보다 한 뼘 크다」. 날개 길이 축은 내가 만든 게 아니라 **대본에 있던 것**이다.
- 🔴 **도도 리본은 끈이 길다** — 「뒤로 눕는다」 3 ·「늘어졌다」 5 ·「위로 튄다」·「옆으로 날린다」. 짧은 띠로 그리면 이 지문 스무 곳이 안 그려진다.

### 일부러 안 정한 것

- **눈썹·눈의 각도** — SCENE 이 그걸로 감정을 그린다(「눈썹 자리가 위로」·「가운데로 모였다」·「눈이 옆으로 흘렀다」). 기본각을 박으면 250쪽이 안 그려진다.
- **목의 방향** — 무무는 앞으로도 위로도 아래로도 뺀다. 정한 것은 **길이와 굵기**뿐이다.
- **도도가 늘 한 날개를 내밀고 있다** — 안 박았다(도도도 `두 날개` 48쪽). 박은 것은 **날개가 길어서 접어도 꼬리를 넘는다**는 몸 규격이고, 그건 자세와 무관하게 남는다.

## Dodo duck

BUILD: the elder sister, half a head taller than Mumu. Head is 1/4 of her standing height.
  🔴 SHE IS NOT A BIGGER MUMU. HER LEGS SHOW: there is a clear band of open page between her belly
  and her feet, TWICE AS DEEP as the gap under Mumu, and it is there even standing still. The body
  rides high on those legs and is tipped forward so the chest leads.
NECK: always out, and CURVED BACK IN A SHALLOW S so the chin is lifted and the head sits BEHIND the
  chest, never on top of it. The neck is medium - shorter than Mommy's, and it does not fold away.
WINGS: THE LONGEST AGAINST THE BODY OF THE TWO CHILDREN - folded they reach past the tail, spread
  they are a hand-span wider than Mumu's, and the tips come to a point. She works with ONE wing out
  away from the body while the other stays down, so HERS IS THE ONLY OUTLINE THAT IS NOT THE SAME ON
  BOTH SIDES.
BILL: long and narrow, clearly longer than Mumu's.
CLOTH: one BLUE ribbon over the crown, knotted at one side, with TWO LONG LOOSE ENDS that hang past
  her cheek - long enough to lie back when she moves, flick up when she jumps and droop when wet.
  She wears nothing else.
SILHOUETTE: filled in solid she is a lopsided cross - a body up on two long legs, chin up, one wing
  thrown out. Nobody else in the book is asymmetrical.

## Mumu duck

BUILD: the younger brother, the smallest of the four. 🔴 HIS HEAD IS THE BIGGEST PART OF HIM - a
  round ball that is 1/3 of his standing height and wider than his own shoulders.
  🔴 HE IS NOT A SMALLER DODO. HIS LEGS ARE THE SHORTEST OF THE FOUR: the belly hangs so low that
  the open page under him is no deeper than one webbed foot, and he reads as a body sitting on its
  feet. Body a short upright egg.
NECK: 🔴 FOLDED AWAY AT REST - the head sits straight down on the body and NO NECK SHOWS AT ALL. He
  is the only one whose neck disappears. When he looks at a thing it comes OUT AS A THIN STALK AS
  LONG AS HIS OWN BODY, the same thin width all the way, and it can point forward, up, down or bend
  into an arch. That stretch is what he does on more pages than anything else.
WINGS: the shortest and roundest at the tip, and they stay held against the body so they do NOT break
  his outline. He holds a thing with BOTH wings cupped against his chest, and both wings move the
  same way at the same time - he is symmetrical where Dodo is not.
BILL: short and blunt.
CLOTH: one BLUE square kerchief knotted at the throat, knot in front, two points hanging over his
  chest. It can be untied and carried. He wears nothing else.
SILHOUETTE: filled in solid he is a pebble - a big round head on a short egg, no legs and no neck -
  and the only thing that ever leaves that pebble is one thin neck.

## Mommy duck

BUILD: the adult, a full head taller than Dodo. Head is 1/6 of her height, the smallest head against
  the body in the book. The body is NARROW AND UPRIGHT, deeper front-to-back than it is wide, carried
  high on LONG LEGS with the widest open page under her of anyone.
NECK: 🔴 THE LONGEST NECK IN THE BOOK, as long as her body is deep, AND IT STANDS UP VERTICAL so that
  a third of her whole height is neck. At work it BENDS OVER IN ONE SMOOTH ARCH from the shoulder,
  the head coming down to whatever her wings are doing, and the arch is her working shape.
WINGS: long, held close to the body, and dusted with flour - the flour is BARE UNPAINTED PAPER laid
  along the top of the wing, never a white pigment.
CLOTH: one APRON and it is BARE UNPAINTED PAPER, a plain panel from chest to knee with two straight
  ties - 🔴 SHE IS THE ONLY CHARACTER WITH UNPAINTED PAPER ON HER BODY, and that pale panel is how
  she is found on a crowded page. No pattern, no pocket detail, no other cloth. NO BLUE ANYWHERE.
SILHOUETTE: filled in solid she is a ladle - a long standing neck rising off a narrow upright body on
  long legs - with one pale rectangle cut out of her front.

## Daddy duck

BUILD: the adult, the tallest and by far the widest. 🔴 THE HEIGHT IS ALL BODY AND NO NECK. His body
  is WIDER THAN IT IS TALL - the only figure in this book built as a lying oval instead of a standing
  one - with a broad low chest and a back that runs nearly level. Head is 1/5 of his height.
NECK: 🔴 NONE SHOWS. The head sits straight down onto the shoulders and stays there; he turns the
  whole body to look at something. Where Mommy is a third neck, he is none.
LEGS: short and SET WIDE APART, so he stands square and planted - the open page under him is wide but
  very shallow, the opposite of Mommy's tall narrow gap.
WINGS: THE WIDEST OF THE FOUR. He braces, presses down and carries with both of them at once.
CLOTH: one straw hat, WHEAT, and 🔴 ITS BRIM IS WIDER THAN HIS SHOULDERS - one flat horizontal bar
  laid across the top of him, sitting directly on the head with no neck under it. He wears nothing
  else. NO BLUE ANYWHERE.
SILHOUETTE: filled in solid he is a mushroom - a wide low mass with a flat bar across the top and
  nothing between the two.

## Guest goat (36권 · 수레를 끄는 염소)

BUILD: 🔴 NOT A DUCK AND NOT BIPEDAL - this is the one four-legged body in the series. A goat,
  standing and walking ON FOUR LEGS, back running level, adult size: 🔴 its back is at Mumu's eye
  height, so the cart it pulls is a thing a duckling has to look UP the side of. That size is the
  danger the book is about.
HEAD: horns curving BACK over the neck, a long straight muzzle and 🔴 A MOUTH, NOT A BILL - the book
  says so in as many words, and it is the fastest way a reader sees this is not one of the family.
  Ears hang out sideways. Eyes are two plain marks like everyone else's.
PULLING: the cart shafts are carried BETWEEN THE TWO FORELEGS against the chest, head down, body
  leaning forward. When it stops it turns halfway round and 🔴 PUTS ONE FORELEG ON THE WHEEL - that
  foreleg on the wheel is its one held thing.
CLOTH: 🔴 NONE AT ALL. No hat, no cloth, no harness decoration.
COLOUR: WHEAT body, OVERLAP where the anchor puts backs and heads. 🔴 NO BLUE ANYWHERE - not on the
  cart, not on the shafts, not on anything it carries.
SILHOUETTE: filled in solid it is a low horizontal bar on four posts with two backward hooks at one
  end - the only silhouette in fifty books that is longer than it is tall.

## Guest granny (50권 · 할머니 오리)

BUILD: an adult duck, 🔴 Mommy's height class but read as older by ONE thing: THE NECK IS FOLDED
  FORWARD AND LOW and stays there. Where Mommy's neck stands vertical and arches only at work, this
  one is carried down and forward on every page, so 🔴 HER HEAD IS ALWAYS LOWER THAN MOMMY'S WOULD BE
  AT THE SAME DISTANCE. Back rounded rather than upright.
🔴 SHE IS NOT MOMMY AND NOT A BIGGER DODO - the two things that separate her are THE FOLDED NECK and
  BARE PAPER NOWHERE ON HER BODY (Mommy is the only one with an unpainted apron, and that pale panel
  is how Mommy is found). If this one is drawn with a pale front the two adults swap.
HELD THINGS: a WALKING STICK carried the duck way - pinched between one wing edge and the body - and
  a BASKET held against the chest with the other wing. At rest the stick lies on the ground beside
  her and the basket sits in front. 🔴 When she takes both children in at once, the stick stays
  pinched under the wing; it is never set down for that.
CLOTH: none. No shawl, no apron, no hat.
COLOUR: 🔴 NOT ONE BLUE THING ANYWHERE - the script says it outright, and in this series blue is the
  children's mark, so an adult with no blue is the rule being confirmed rather than an omission.
  Stick and basket are WHEAT.
SILHOUETTE: filled in solid she is a question mark - a rounded body with the neck coming forward and
  down off the top of it, and one straight line (the stick) leaning out of her side.
