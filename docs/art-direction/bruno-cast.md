# bruno — 캐스트 규격

> 🔴 **앵커(`bruno-anchor.md`)는 이 세계 전체를 말하고, 이 파일은 한 사람을 말한다.**
> 빌더(`build-series-html.mjs`)가 `##` 제목을 **영문 별칭**으로 찾아 그 사람 시트 프롬프트에
> `[이 인물만의 규격]` 으로 끼운다 — 제목 문자열을 바꾸면 조용히 안 들어간다.
>
> 🔴 **가르는 축은 앵커의 축 하나다 — 「크기 격차」.** 새 축(털색·모자·수염)을 발명하지 않는다.
> 대신 **크기 말고 무엇이 두 배가 됐나**를 정한다. 답 = **머리는 안 커졌다.**
>
> | | 머리 | 윤곽 한 줄 | 🔴 바닥이 끝나는 모양 |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | 닐스 | 키의 **1/3** · 어깨만큼 넓다 | 정수리에 귀 둘이 서서 **그 사이로 종이가 보인다** · 턱부터 발까지 허리 없는 **한 덩어리** | **장화 = 다리보다 넓은 네모 둘** |
> | 브루노 | 키의 **1/5** · 어깨가 머리의 **두 배** | 머리와 귀가 **끊김 없는 한 돔** · 조끼가 어깨에서 밑단까지 곧게 떨어져 **옆선이 직선** · 밑단 아래 두 다리 사이에 종이 | **맨발 = 다리보다 좁게 뾰족** |
>
> 🔴 **키는 두 배인데 머리는 1.2배다.** 둘을 나란히 세우면 몸만 커지고 머리는 거의 그대로다 —
> 이 비율 하나가 「어른」과 「큰 아이」를 가른다. 렌더된 두 시트가 갈리지 않은 진짜 이유가 여기 있다
> (조끼·안경·장화를 벗기면 **같은 곰 인형을 두 크기로 뽑은 것**이었다).
>
> 🔴 **닐스는 250쪽 중 149쪽을 혼자 선다**(브루노 혼자 6쪽 · 둘 다 88쪽). 그래서 **크기 격차가 한 화면
> 안에 없는 쪽이 60%**이고, 개체 규격은 **옆에 비교 대상이 없을 때** 작동해야 한다.
>
> 🔴 **손이 66쪽에서 클로즈업된다**(익스트림 4쪽 포함). 두 앞발이 **다른 물건**이어야 한다 —
> 크롭이 팔목 위로 올라가면 크기도 조끼도 장화도 화면 밖이고 남는 건 앞발뿐이다.
>
> 🔴 **표정은 나누되 동작은 안 나눈다.** 원 수상작의 장치가 「큰 쪽은 무표정, 작은 쪽이 팔로 과장한다」였고
> (`collins-bearchair` styleSpec `character`), 앵커가 크기 격차만 가져오고 이 절반을 버렸다.
> 팔다리 각도는 앵커대로 같이 가되 **얼굴이 움직이는 양**이 갈린다.

## Nils bear cub

```
HIS HEAD IS AS WIDE AS HIS SHOULDERS and stands ONE THIRD of his whole height. Nothing on him is
wider than that head.
TWO EARS SIT UP ON THE CROWN, each about a third of the head wide, WITH BARE PAPER SHOWING BETWEEN
THEM, so the top of his outline is two clear bumps against the sky.
HIS FACE IS FLAT: the muzzle is a BARK patch lying inside the head circle and does NOT stick out
past the brow, so from any angle the head still reads as one circle.
ONE ROUND MASS runs from chin to foot - no waist, no hem, NO BARE PAPER BETWEEN THE LEGS. The arms
are short stubs held close against that mass.
🔴 HIS OUTLINE ENDS IN TWO SQUARE BLOCKS. The BOOT BLUE boots are WIDER THAN THE LEG above them, so
the widest part of his lower half is the very bottom. Boots in every season, and they are the only
blue in the book.
FOREPAW: a small ROUND MITT, toes not separated, no wider than his own muzzle patch. In a close-up
that mitt - and the blue cuff when it is a foot - is how the page says the paw is his.
🔴 HE DOES ALL THE ACTING. The mouth curve swings wide, the two brow strokes tilt hard, the arms go
up and out away from the body. Both eye strokes stay long and open.
```

## Bruno grandfather bear

```
HE IS NOT A BIGGER NILS. He stands TWICE the cub's height, but THE TWO HEADS ARE NEARLY THE SAME
SIZE - his head is ONE FIFTH of his height and HIS SHOULDERS ARE TWICE HIS HEAD WIDE. All the extra
height went into the body and none into the head. Stood side by side the height doubles and the
head barely changes; THAT ONE RATIO is what makes him an adult instead of a large cub.
🔴 HIS EARS SIT AT THE SIDES OF THE HEAD, small, and head and ears carry on as ONE UNBROKEN DOME -
no bare paper between them, where the cub has two bumps with sky between.
🔴 HIS MUZZLE STICKS OUT PAST THE BROW as a blunt snout, and it is the ONE FACE IN THE BOOK LEFT AS
BARE UNWAXED PAPER - the grey of the paper is his grey muzzle. The cub's muzzle is BARK and flat.
THE VEST HANGS STRAIGHT. Shoulder to hem is a STRAIGHT-SIDED BLOCK ending in a FLAT HORIZONTAL HEM,
never a round belly. BELOW THAT HEM TWO SEPARATE LEGS WITH BARE PAPER BETWEEN THEM - the cub has no
such gap anywhere.
🔴 HIS OUTLINE ENDS IN TWO TAPERS: bare paws NARROWER THAN THE LEG above them. The cub's bottom is
wider than his legs and his is narrower - the two silhouettes end in opposite shapes.
FOREPAW: a BROAD FLAT PALM with FIVE BLUNT TOES HELD APART, the open paw AS WIDE AS THE CUB'S WHOLE
FACE. Eight volumes turn on this paw at close range with no other bear to compare it to.
ROUND GLASSES, two rings on one straight bar - the only closed rings drawn on either face.
🔴 HIS FACE BARELY MOVES. The mouth is ONE SHORT FLAT CURVE and the two brow strokes stay LEVEL
whatever is happening. He does not point, does not throw both arms up, does not lean in. The cub
over-acts and he does not; that gap is the other half of the size gap.
🔴 NO BLUE ANYWHERE ON HIM, no walking stick, and nothing of his own in his hands on the sheet.
```
